# VINE RUNNER — Art Prompts (paste-ready for Midjourney or ChatGPT)

Every prompt already includes the flat **magenta #FF00FF background** so it can be
knocked out to transparent (neither tool makes real transparent PNGs). Prompts are
self-contained — copy one, paste, generate. The `--ar` / `--v` at the end are for
Midjourney; ChatGPT ignores them.

**Character consistency:** generate **#1 first**, then for #2–#5 reuse that character
— Midjourney: add `--cref <url of #1> --cw 100`; ChatGPT: attach #1 and say "same
character." Sprites are theme-neutral (one render each, they play on 6 palettes).

Drop finished art into the `ART {}` object at the top of
`satellites/vine-runner/index.html`, or send them to me and I'll matte + wire them.
