## 1. The deal

Lucid Winds is a single-developer botanical collection game. It uses procedurally-generated SVG art tied to deterministic hashes. Each plant you mint is unique. The Game runs in your browser and on Pi Browser (when launched on Pi Network).

The Game is currently in **beta**. We may reset plants, currencies, or progress before public launch as we balance the economy. Nothing in your collection is guaranteed permanent until full launch.

## 5. In-game currencies

- **Sunbeams** are earned by playing pattern-matching mini-games. They are NOT cryptocurrency. They cannot be withdrawn, sold, or converted to fiat or Pi.
- **Dew** is earned via in-game activity (wild tending, login streaks). Same — not cryptocurrency, not withdrawable.
- **Pi** is real cryptocurrency on the Pi Network. When you spend Pi inside the Game, the transaction is processed by Pi Network — we receive only a payment ID and fulfill the upgrade. All Pi transactions are governed by Pi's Terms. Refunds are at Pi's discretion.
- We may adjust earn rates, costs, or in-game economy at any time as the Game evolves.

## 6. Plants are unique but not necessarily rare

- Each plant is procedurally generated from a hash; visually it's one-of-one.
- The "rarity grade" (Common through Cosmic) reflects the trait bank scoring at mint time. Future trait bank updates may shift grade distributions for newly minted plants.
- Plants you mint are bound to your account. We may add cross-account trade or chain-extraction features in the future; if so, separate terms will govern those.

## 7. The Wild map

- You can remove a plant by harvesting or composting; the location is removed with it.
- Don't plant in places where revealing the location would be a safety issue (e.g., your home address). Use the Game responsibly.
