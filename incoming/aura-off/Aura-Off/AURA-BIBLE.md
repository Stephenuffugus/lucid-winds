## 1. WHAT THIS ACTUALLY IS — VERIFIED CULTURE

### 1.1 The term

"Aura farming" describes cultivating an appearance of effortless charisma through repetitive "cool" actions. The expression was popularized in **2024**, largely in reference to anime characters and celebrities. It's synonymous with charisma or "rizz," minus rizz's romantic connotation. **[V1]** (Wikipedia/NYT)

"Farmear" comes from gamer slang for repeating actions to accumulate resources. Latin American usage fused *farmear* + *aura*. **[V1]** (La Nación, Infobae)

Aura is scored socially in imaginary points: "+1000 aura" for a win, "lost aura" for an embarrassment. **[V1]** (Infobae)

### 1.2 The origin dance — Pacu Jalur

This is the single richest vein of material in the project and it is **fully documented**.

- **Pacu Jalur** is a traditional boat race from **Kuantan Singingi regency, Riau province, Sumatra, Indonesia**, traceable to the **17th century**. Long canoe-like boats crewed by teams of up to ~60 paddlers. **[V1]**
- Each boat carries a child dancer called the **Togak Luan** (also **Tukang Tari**) whose job is to hype the crew and signal spectators when the boat is leading. **Children are chosen because the role demands extreme balance.** **[V1]**
- **Rayyan Arkan Dikha**, age 11, from Kuantan Singingi, performed in a **Teluk Belanga** traditional outfit with a **Malay Riau headcloth** and sunglasses. Calm, repetitive, rhythmic hand movements and body waves. **[V1]**
- The clip was **filmed in 2024**, posted to TikTok, went viral **mid-2025** once paired with **"Young Black & Rich" by Melly Mike**. **[V1]**
- Dikha said in interviews he **created the dance himself, spontaneously**. **[V1]** (BBC)
- He had trained three times a week since age 8–9; his father is a paddler. **[V1]**
- Outcome: appointed **cultural and tourism ambassador for Riau province**, met national Ministers of Culture and Tourism in Jakarta, received a government scholarship. **[V1]**
- Recreated by **Travis Kelce** (14M+ views), **Paris Saint-Germain**, F1's **Alex Albon**, footballer **Diego Luna** (as a goal celebration), **KSI**, **Steve Aoki**. **[V1]**

> **Design note:** the Togak Luan is the best character concept in this entire project. A child whose *job* is to generate aura for a team, standing on the prow of a moving boat, where losing balance means losing. That is already a video game.

### 1.3 The battles — how they actually run

Format, as practiced **[V1]**:

- Two people face off. **No speaking. No physical contact.** Poses, gestures, walks, dance moves only.
- Surrounded by a crowd. **Single-elimination bracket.**
- Winner decided by **crowd reaction**, sometimes a **panel of judges**, sometimes both.
- Participants are mostly students **aged 14–20**.
- **There is no official scoring system.** Sources are explicit: no scientific scale, no universal points. The joke is treating charisma as a game stat.

Organizer Aldhir González (22), who ran one of the first Mexico City battles, states the criteria plainly: whoever looks best, whoever demonstrates it, **whoever causes the most laughter**, whoever has the most confidence. **[V1]**

Critically: **it is essential to reference as many memes and viral videos as possible through gesture.** **[V1]** That is the actual win condition, and it is the mechanical heart of our game.

Fit counts as an opening move. A judge said Kaled Rosales had aura the moment he saw his red Lightning McQueen shorts and clogs. **[V1]**

Documented lineage/comparison: **voguing battles** and 2000s dance battles. **[V1]**

### 1.4 Backlash — real, and useful for campaign stakes

- A mayor in **Honduras banned the battles** outright, remarking he'd prefer compulsory military service. **[V1]**
- A **Barcelona** battle escalated from gestures to shoving and blows. **[V1]**
- Battles spread amid a Mexican policy debate on regulating social media and phones in schools. **[V1]**
- Venezuelan psychologist **María Eugenia Tovar** defended it, noting digital communication is genuinely real for adolescents and can drive in-person gathering. **[V1]**

> **Design note:** "the town that banned it" is a free campaign act. So is "the battle that turned into a real fight." Both are true.

### 2.1 SIX-SEVEN — the single most-used move in battles

**Evidence: [V1]** — AFP names it in the CDMX final. Metrópoles reports it was **the most present movement in every battle** at Suzano.

**Verified origin chain:**
1. **1 Dec 2024** — Philadelphia rapper **Skrilla** releases **"Doot Doot (6 7)"** feat. G Herbo (drill). Lyric: "6-7, I just bipped right on the highway."
2. **Jan 2025** — the sound explodes on TikTok/Reels via bait-and-switch basketball edits of **LaMelo Ball** (Charlotte Hornets), who is listed at **6 ft 7 in**.
3. The **gesture** was added by **Taylen Kinney ("TK")**, an Overtime Elite basketball player and TikToker, forcing the numbers into interviews and streams.
4. **Dictionary.com named "6 7" its 2025 Word of the Year** while conceding it has **no fixed meaning**.

**Physical description [V1, cross-verified 4 sources]:**
Both hands raised to roughly chest height, **palms facing upward**, moving up and down in a loose alternating **see-saw / balance-scale** motion. Elbows stay in near the ribs. Face is usually deadpan or smug.

> ⚠️ **[?] CONFLICT FLAGGED.** One Infobae piece describes palms facing **downward** at chest height. Know Your Meme, CNN, and Fox all independently say **palms up, like a balance scale**. **Ruling: build palms up.** Majority + closer-to-primary sourcing. Logged in §11.

**Upper/lower decomposition:**
- **Upper (carries 100% of the move):** `sL/sR ≈ -35/+35` (upper arms held close, slight abduction), `eL/eR` alternating between `-95` and `-60` out of phase — this is the see-saw. `lean` micro-counter-rotates ±3° against the hands. `head` static or 2° tilt.
- **Lower (carries 0%):** dead still. **This is the point.** Six-seven is a pure upper-body move — the stillness below is what makes it read.

**Game role:** the universal neutral. Low base, near-zero risk, never fully stale. The jab.
**IP risk: LOW.** Gesture is folk-authored and unregistered. Do **not** use the Skrilla recording or the phrase "6-7" as a product name.

### 2.2 MEWING

**Evidence: [V1]** — AFP documents a 14-year-old firing it in the CDMX final, describing it as a meme used to convey superiority.

**Verified origin chain:**
- Tongue-posture technique named for British orthodontist **Mike Mew** (orthotropics, developed with his father John Mew). Mew created the Orthotropics YouTube channel **27 Oct 2011**.
- Spread through looksmaxxing/lookism communities; went viral on YouTube/TikTok **2018–2019**; entered brainrot vocabulary by **2024**.
- **The American Association of Orthodontists says it is not scientifically supported. [V1]**

**Physical description [V1]:**
The **classroom variant** is the one used in battles: index finger vertical over the lips in a "hush" sign, then **slid along the jawline** from chin to ear, with a fixed stare and a locked jaw. Means "I can't talk, I'm mewing." Silence is part of the gesture — which is perfect, because battles are silent.

**Upper/lower decomposition:**
- **Upper (100%):** `sR ≈ -124 → -88` (hand travels up to face then out along the jaw), `eR ≈ +66 → +26`, `head` rotates **into** the hand `+4 → +11` then holds. `lean ≈ -3` (subtle recline = superiority).
- **Lower (0%):** locked. Weight even. Any lower-body motion kills it.

**Game role:** high-value **counter/reversal**. Thematically it's a silence move — should interrupt the opponent's combo.
**IP risk: LOW.** Concept is unownable. Never name-drop Mike Mew or make health claims.

⚠️ **Content-safety note for the whole project:** mewing/looksmaxxing/sigma originate in manosphere and lookism communities and can act as an on-ramp to body-image and appearance-obsession content. **Our game must use these as absurdist comedy, never as aspiration.** No stat called "attractiveness." No character rewarded for "improving their looks." Play the jaw thing as a joke, which is how the kids in the plaza are playing it.

### 2.3 THE BOAT DANCE (Togak Luan)

**Evidence: [V1]** — extensively documented, see §1.2.

**Physical description [V1]:**
Calm, repetitive, **rhythmic hand movements and body waves**. Performed on the narrow prow of a moving racing boat, so the defining quality is **composure under instability**. Sunglasses, deadpan face, unhurried tempo. Arms sweep in wide slow arcs; torso undulates; the lower body is doing constant micro-corrections for balance.

**Upper/lower decomposition — this is the most important split in the game:**
- **Upper:** slow wide arcs. `sL/sR ≈ ±75`, `eL/eR` trailing the shoulders by ~15% of the cycle (whip/follow-through), `lean` sways ±9°, `head` counter-rotates against the lean.
- **Lower:** **constant micro-correction.** `kL/kR` alternating small bends (−8 to −14), `bob` ±4, `hL/hR` shifting weight. **Never still, never large.**

> **This is the move that justifies the whole upper/lower architecture.** Serene above, working hard below. Build the rig around this and everything else falls out.

**IP risk: MEDIUM — read carefully.**
- Pacu Jalur and the Togak Luan role are a **centuries-old public tradition**. Not ownable. Safe.
- Dikha stated he **improvised** the dance, so there is no registered choreography behind it. Safe under *Hanagami*.
- **BUT:** Dikha is a **minor** and a **government-appointed provincial ambassador**. Using his name, likeness, silhouette, or a recognizable one-to-one copy is a right-of-publicity problem plus a real reputational one — there have already been documented scams exploiting his fame. **[V1]**
- **Ruling:** build the *tradition* (boat, river, prow, headcloth-and-shades silhouette of the role) and an **original** dance in that register. Do not build the boy. If this game gets traction, the correct move is a **licensed partnership routed through the Riau provincial tourism office**, not a DM to a child.

### 2.4 THE AURA WALK

**Evidence: [V1]** — Infobae describes it as the walk that represents domination of space.

**Physical description [V1]:** Slow, deliberate pace. Straight back. Fixed gaze. **Relaxed shoulders.** Unhurried to the point of confrontation.

**Upper/lower decomposition — the inverse of six-seven:**
- **Upper (~20%):** deliberately minimal. `sL/sR` swing ±14 only. `lean ≈ -2`. `head` locked forward. Relaxation *is* the content.
- **Lower (~80%):** carries the move. `hL/hR` alternating ±22, `kL/kR` −26 on the swing leg, `bob` −5 at midstride. Tempo roughly **half** of a normal walk cycle.

**Game role:** the pair-opposite of six-seven. Teaching these two back to back is how the tutorial should introduce the upper/lower system.
**IP risk: NONE.** It's walking.

### 2.5 SIGMA STARE

**Evidence: [V1]** — La Nación lists sigma poses in the battle repertoire: fixed gaze, calculated indifference, crossed arms. Metrópoles confirms "sigma" and "beta" as active battle vocabulary.

**Origin [V2]:** "Sigma male" is a pseudoscientific archetype from manosphere culture, built off the alpha/beta framing. Went mainstream via ironic memes (notably the SpongeBob "Erm, what the sigma?" edit).

**Physical description [V1]:** Arms crossed high on the chest. Unbroken eye contact. Chin slightly down. Total stillness.

**Upper/lower decomposition:**
- **Upper (100%):** `sL/sR ≈ ∓60`, `eL/eR ≈ ∓70` (forearms stacked across chest), `head +5`, `lean -4`.
- **Lower (0%):** planted, feet slightly wider than shoulders.

**Game role:** defensive stance. Should reduce incoming damage rather than deal it.
**IP risk: NONE.** Same content-safety caveat as mewing — play it as a joke about a joke, which is how it already reads.

### 2.6 THEATRICAL COLLAPSE

**Evidence: [V1]** — Primera Hora documents competitors throwing themselves on the ground; other coverage cites "theatrical falls."

**Physical description [V1]:** A deliberate, committed fall to the ground, held. Comedy through total commitment.

**Upper/lower decomposition — the only move where lower body leads:**
- **Lower (70%):** `rot` drives to −88 (whole-body rotation about the feet), `bob +58`, `hL/hR −18`.
- **Upper (30%):** arms fly wide late in the fall (`sL/sR ≈ ∓96`), `head −16`. The upper body is **reacting**, not initiating. Delay upper by ~120ms behind lower — that lag is what sells the physics.

**Game role:** high risk, high reward, guaranteed **crowd-meter** swing (laughter is an explicit win criterion). Should be punishing to whiff.
**IP risk: NONE.**

### 2.7 PROP WORK — THE LASSO

**Evidence: [V1]** — Kaled Rosales Salazar (16) used **a large white sandal** as a prop, miming lassoing his opponent and leading him away like a horse. Won the CDMX battle and **3,000 pesos (~$177 USD)**.

**Physical description [V1]:** Overhead circular arm rotation as if twirling a rope, then a throwing/pulling motion directed at the opponent, then a walk-away tug.

**Upper/lower decomposition:**
- **Upper (85%):** `sR` sweeping −146 → −166 in a full cone, `eR` counter-rotating to keep the "rope" level, `lean` ±6 following the arm.
- **Lower (15%):** weight shifts foot to foot under the twirl; on the pull, one step back with `hL +16`.

**Game role:** **the only move that directly targets the opponent.** Should reduce *their* score, not raise yours. Introduces the interaction layer.
**IP risk: NONE** for the lasso itself. **Do not** replicate his Lightning McQueen shorts and clogs — that's Disney/Pixar.

## 5. CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE [V3 — all original]

Five acts. Each act unlocks moves; each boss teaches a mechanic you cannot beat without.

**Act 1 — The Plaza.** Tutorial. Unlock Six-Seven, Aura Walk, Look Away. Boss teaches the FLEX/FLOW/BAIT triangle.
**Act 2 — The Park Bracket.** First real elimination ladder (models Suzano). Unlock Dead Drop, Lasso. Boss punishes repetition — forces the freshness system.
**Act 3 — The Banned Town.** Based on the Honduran ban. Underground battles, no crowd meter — **judges only**, different scoring rules. Boss teaches counters.
**Act 4 — The Capital.** Bellas Artes analogue. Judge panel + crowd. Fit Check matters most here. Boss is a prop specialist.
**Act 5 — Upriver.** Kuantan Singingi. The boat. **Balance becomes a live mechanic** — the lower body is now fighting the water while the upper body performs. Everything the game taught about upper/lower splits converges here.

**Unlock economy:** beat an opponent → learn *their* signature move. Classic, readable, and it makes every fight worth studying.

**Post-campaign:** online duels, weekly rotating "meta" (which category the crowd currently favors), community-submitted moves with a review queue.

## 6. COMBAT SYSTEM [V3]

Carried forward from the prototype, with fixes.

**Categories:** FLEX beats BAIT · BAIT beats FLOW · FLOW beats FLEX

**Score** = `base × timing × matchup × freshness × amplitude × combo × pattern`

- **Timing:** Perfect 2.0 / Clean 1.35 / Shaky 0.7 / Whiff 0.3
- **Matchup:** 1.5 advantage / 1.0 neutral / 0.7 disadvantage
- **Freshness:** 1.0 → 0.68 → 0.42 → 0.25 on repeat. *Directly models the verified "reference as many memes as possible" win condition.*
- **Amplitude:** 1.0–1.6, set by hold duration
- **Combo:** +14% per link
- **Pattern:** named 3-move chains (FULL SPECTRUM 1.5×, etc.)

**Fixes for v2:**
1. **Upper/lower is now a first-class stat.** Each move carries `upperWeight`/`lowerWeight`. Blending takes upper tracks from A and lower from B — but a blend of two upper-dominant moves (six-seven + mewing) should look and score *worse* than a genuine split (six-seven upper + aura walk lower). **Reward the split. Punish the stack.** This is the mechanical answer to Stephen's note.
2. **Specials:** interrupt (mewing), guard (sigma), debuff (lasso), feint (side-eye), counter (eye roll), high-risk (dead drop). Move roles, not just numbers.
3. **Judges vs crowd.** Verified: some battles use crowd, some judges, some both. Make them **different scoring functions** — crowd rewards laughter and surprise, judges reward technique and freshness. Act 3 is judges-only. Act 4 is both.

## 7. RIG & ANIMATION SPEC

Current prototype: 12-joint 2D SVG rig, keyframe-interpolated at runtime.
Production target: same joint names on a Meshy/Blender humanoid, exported glTF.

**Rules for Claude Code:**
1. **Do not change joint names.** `rot, bob, lean, head, sL, eL, sR, eR, hL, kL, hR, kR` map to real bones later. Renaming breaks the port.
2. **Every move must declare `upperWeight` + `lowerWeight` summing to 1.0.** No untagged moves.
3. **Amplitude is a multiplier on joint deltas, never on absolutes.** Keeps rest pose stable.
4. **Blending = bone masking**, upper set from clip A, lower set from clip B. Same in 2D and 3D.
5. **Follow-through:** upper-body joints lag lower by 80–140ms on any move where lower initiates (Dead Drop, Aura Walk). This single rule does more for perceived quality than any polish pass.
6. Keep the pure-data move library in **one file, no logic**, so it stays authorable without touching the engine.

**Pipeline:** Meshy base mesh → Blender retarget to the 12-joint skeleton → author clips against the same keys → export glTF → three.js `AnimationMixer` with masked blending.

## 12. THE HONEST STRATEGIC READ

Two things worth saying plainly.

**The timing is a real risk.** This trend is roughly two months into mainstream press saturation. A massive campaign game may land after the peak. **Mitigation:** the mechanics — upper/lower splits, meme-reference-as-combat, crowd-versus-judge scoring — are genuinely good and outlive the trend. Almost nobody is building on **Pacu Jalur** — a 17th-century boat race with a child dancer whose literal job is generating aura for a crew, where the skill is composure on an unstable surface. That is a better game premise than anything in the TikTok layer, it is public tradition rather than someone's IP, and it gives the whole project a spine that isn't disposable.

Build the memes to get attention. Build the river to be remembered.

# ADDENDUM v1.1 — 28 Aug 2026, second research pass

## A1. THE FINDING THAT CHANGED THE GAME [V1]

**Frederick Luis Aldama**, humanities professor at the University of Texas at Austin, witnessed an aura battle at **Parque México** and told CNN that the result contradicted what he expected. He had assumed the winner would be whoever performed the highest-intensity move. Instead the winner was the one who brought **total composure** — the glow of absolute calm.

**This inverts the v1 scoring model.** v1 rewarded amplitude linearly: hold longer, score higher. That is wrong, and it is wrong in a way that was making the game worse.

**Implemented in v2 as the composure curve.** Every move now carries an `idealAmp`. Score falls off on **both sides** of it, and falls off **harder above than below** — overplaying is punished more than underplaying, because trying too hard is the cardinal sin of this culture. FLEX moves want restraint (`idealAmp` 0.90–1.10). BAIT moves want full commitment (1.35–1.50). FLOW sits between.

This is the best mechanic in the game and it came directly from a sourced observation. It also makes all 18 moves feel physically different in the hand, which no amount of tuning was going to achieve otherwise.

## A2. THE LINEAGE — CNN NAMED OUR GENRE FOR US [V1]

CNN observes that these events resemble earlier youth "battles" where competitors were armed with **Pokémon cards, Beyblades, or rap verses**.

Stephen's original instruction was "almost like an old school Pokémon battle on Game Boy." **The press independently reached for the same comparison.** That is unusually strong validation that the turn-based duel frame is the right one — we're not imposing a genre, we're matching the one the culture already sits in.

## A3. WHY IT'S LATIN AMERICA [V1]

Aldama's explanation, which is the best cultural read available and should shape the art direction: the region has **a long history of the plaza, the courtyard, the gathering in parks**. These battles are collective, public, person-to-person. He adds that they offer a **free, nonviolent space** for young people to find human connection, and may reflect a **desire to disconnect from technology and be present**.

> **Design consequence:** the crowd is not set dressing. Build the ring of people as a real system. And the game's emotional core is not "get famous online" — it's "everyone came outside." Every environment should be a public square, never a stage.

## A7. BALLROOM — STRUCTURE TO STUDY, VOCABULARY TO LEAVE ALONE

Multiple outlets compare aura battles to **voguing battles**, so the parallel is theirs, not ours. Ballroom is the most developed competitive-performance format in existence and it has solved problems we're facing:

- **Categories**, not just move types — competitors "walk" a named category rather than freestyling
- A **preliminary round** where judges either pass a competitor or eliminate them, before the head-to-head battle even begins
- **Judge panels** scoring out of ten, and the crowd as a parallel force
- An **MC** who introduces competitors, announces results, and drives the room
- **Houses** — chosen-family crews competitors represent
- A **reputation ladder** where sustained winning earns standing over time

**What to take:** the two-stage structure (qualify, then duel), the crew system, the MC as a live voice, and reputation that persists across the campaign. Those are generic competition mechanics.

⚠️ **What NOT to take: the vocabulary or the aesthetic.** Ballroom is Black and Latino LGBTQ+ culture with a specific and difficult history — it grew from drag balls as a survival space for people excluded from mainstream society. The community has its own word, **"noguing,"** for exactly this kind of uninformed borrowing. **Do not use ballroom terminology in this game.** Not "tens across the board," not "chop," not "realness," not "houses" as a label. Aura battle culture has its own native vocabulary — *farmear*, aura points, *batalla* — and that is what we use. Study the architecture, ship our own words.
