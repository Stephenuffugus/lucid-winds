Balance Flags — things to tweak

3 redundant keyword clusters (6 creatures each):
• Swift + Restless — Cat, Bat, Mouse, Silly Goose, Squirrel, Raven
• Hardy + Lush — Moth, Luna Moth, Origami Crane, Koala, Seahorse, Koi Fish
• Restless + Lush — Bee, Caterpillar, Robin, Rabbit, Cricket, Mushroom Sprite
These creatures differ only by family + temperament + art. Mechanically interchangeable. Fix: break up with Thorned or Rooted swaps — OR accept the redundancy since family + art carries identity.
Domain thinness:
• Climate signatures: 1 (Mammoth alone)
• Economy signatures: 1 (Raccoon alone)
• Mycelium family: 0 signatures (only family with zero)
Future pass: add signature creatures for each climate vector, more economy flavor, Mushroom Sprite signature.
