1. THE IDEA, IN STEPHEN'S WORDS
"The core concept of preparing yourself different gives me kind of a Kirby's Dream Land idea where I can cook or prepare
myself differently and enemies eat me so I have to figure out how to budget myself to get all the enemies and survive
the level while properly countering the enemies. My weapon is how I prepare myself and then how they eat me."

Kirby inhales an enemy and takes its power. Tofu is the other way round: the enemy eats the tofu, and what the tofu was
when it bit is what happens to the eater. The tofu is six bites. Each eater has one preparation it cannot stomach:
bitten in that state it takes one bite and is beaten; bitten in any other state it takes two and comes back happy. The
bite takes the preparation with it, so the player prepares again at a station (water, press, pan, freezer, soy, brine;
all drawn by you in round one) before the next eater. The bowl at the end wants a dish AND enough tofu left to serve.

The feel to hit: Kirby's Dream Land. Round, soft, big readable faces, enemies you like even while they eat you, a
bright storybook world. Nobody bleeds; a beaten eater is funny, never hurt. Keep everything in the look of your round
one pack: the same line weight, the same cream, soy brown, pan orange and freezer blue, one light from the upper left.
