2. The game in two minutes

Live: https://lucidwinds.com/satellites/tally/, the only thing under review. Open it on a phone, portrait. A number puzzle for all ages, part of the Sky Wolf Studio arcade.

  • A round: a target number and a ring of 4 or 5 number beads. Tap a number, an operator (plus, minus, times, divide), a number, and so on; times and divide go before plus and minus. An equation that equals the target banks itself, no submit button. Each puzzle has several different solutions shown as dots; find the required two or three to clear it, extras are bonus finds. Results must be whole numbers from 0 to 300.
  • Stars: one for reaching the target, two for a two number solution, three for finding 2 or more bonus solutions.
  • Worlds: 8 levels each: Meadow Meadows, Bubble Bay, Candy Peaks, Starlight City, Dino Valley, Space Station, then the Endless Expanse after level 48. Numbers and targets grow with the level. Today the worlds are only names and emoji; every level looks the same.
  • Daily Challenge: the same puzzle for everyone each day, a streak, streak freezes, a gem a day, and a text result to share.
  • Pals: 10 little cheerleaders who sit on the play screen and cheer in a speech bubble (5 cheer lines and a win line each): Pip the fox and Hoot the owl are free; Bram the bear, Nibble the rabbit, Sprout the frog, Cosmo the cat, Bolt the robot and Splash the whale cost coins; Flare the dragon and Star the unicorn cost gems. Today every pal is a system emoji.
  • Skins: 11 colour themes for the whole screen: Abacus (the default: cream #f2e4cf, navy #33455f beads, orange #d2622e target), Twilight, Sunrise, Deep Forest, Sumi Ink, Abyss, Sakura, Arcade, Bubblegum, Ember, Aurora.
  • Economy: coins (start 130; 14 a solve, 9 a bonus find, 12 a star), gems (start 6, one a daily), hints for 25 coins, a streak freeze for 3 gems, double coins for a day for 5 gems. Nothing is sold for real money.
  • Screens: splash ("sliding the beads into place"), the header (double coins, streak, coins, gems, how to play, settings), five tabs (Play, Map, Daily, Shop, Stats), the shop (Pals and Skins), a win screen (stars, finds, coins, share, next), how to play (4 steps), settings (sound, animations, haptics, add to home screen, reset), stats.
  • Sound: simple beeps. Art today: none inside the game. Beads and the target are CSS circles, icons are a stock line icon set, the font is the phone's own. The only painted picture is the arcade thumbnail: a painterly abacus, navy and burnt orange beads on a peach board with splatter.

3. What is weak, honestly (go past these)

  • The studio's words: "Tally improve graphics maybe some assets". It is clean and it works, and it looks like a utility.
  • The pals are the heart of the idea ("a pocketful of pals cheering you on") and they are emoji in a speech bubble.
  • Six named worlds, one look: nothing tells you that you have travelled from the meadow to space.
  • The painted abacus thumbnail and the flat app icon are two different looks, and neither is inside the game.
  • The win moment is a modal with stars; there is no celebration a kid would show a parent.

4. Part one: the review

What we want: the look and the cast. Make Tally feel like a warm, crafted place a family would keep on the home screen, and make the pals characters people want to collect. Also: the map as a journey through the worlds; the win moment; the daily; how to teach a new player (some are young, some are grandparents); whether it should cost 99 cents on Google Play or stay free with the daily as its front door; anything about puzzle feel and difficulty.

The bar every direction must respect: portrait phone first, 412 x 915 is the main viewport (also check 360 and 375 wide); every touch target at least 48 px; no text under 0.7 rem; player facing copy never uses dashes of any kind and never uses exclamation marks; every colour choice must work for colour blind players (say how: shape, icon or pattern, never colour alone); Sky Wolf Studio, singular, is the studio name. Be specific to what you saw on the live page: name the screen and the spot.

The skins recolour everything by code, so painted parts that must change with the skin should be drawn to be tinted: say which in the review.

Format, strictly: one numbered block per direction: Title / What and why (3 lines max) / Where on screen / Effort S, M or L / Priority 1 to 3. Group them under these headings: Look and feel, Pals, The worlds and the map, Puzzles and difficulty, Win moment and juice, Daily and retention, Onboarding, Bugs you saw. End the document with a json block: an array of objects with keys id, title, group, effort, priority, summary. No prose after the json.

5. Part two: the asset pack

5.1 Style

Your call, argued in the review, starting from the painterly abacus in the thumbnail: warm, tactile, a little hand made (wood, paint, paper), with chunky beads you want to touch. The pals are small friendly animals with big readable faces at 64 to 160 px. Numbers must stay crisp and readable over everything: the art sits behind and around the numbers, never under them.

5.2 Rules for every file

  • PNG with a transparent background for every sprite, prop, icon and overlay. If your tool cannot make true transparency, use a flat pure magenta #FF00FF background with no magenta anywhere inside the art, and say so in the manifest; we key it out. JPG only for full bleed plates.
  • Deliver at the sizes given. Never a side over 1600 px.
  • Animations: one picture per frame, named like cheer_01.png, cheer_02.png. Every frame of a set on the same canvas size, the subject centred, the feet or base on the same line in every frame, the same scale across every set of one character. Characters face RIGHT; we mirror for left.
  • No text baked into any picture, except a logo or wordmark where the list asks for one.
  • No real trademarks, logos or known characters.
  • One light direction across the whole pack: upper left.
  • File names lowercase with underscores, exactly as listed. If you add something of your own, put it in a folder called extras/ and list it in the manifest.
