3. Style

  • Match your own Orchard Loft set exactly: the same warm sunrise light from the upper left, the same soft painterly finish, the same saturation and outline weight as orchard_loft decor1 to decor4 and its sky.
  • An orchard, not a wild forest: fruit trees (apple, pear, plum or cherry), a tall poplar for rhythm, tidy grass, wildflowers, worn wood.
  • The camera sits behind and above the knife, looking down at about 30 degrees, on a portrait phone. Trees are seen from the side; the ground and the stump tops are seen from above at that angle.
  • No text, no logos, no baked shadows beyond a soft contact shadow at a tree's or bush's foot.

4. How each piece is used (so the sizes work)

  • The counter is a long box the course runs on: 3 units tall, 14 units deep, as long as the level (up to about 300 units). Its top is the grass; its front face is soil. Tiles repeat along it, so they must tile seamlessly left to right (and the grass top also front to back): check each tile 3 by 3 before you send it.
  • Backdrop trees stand in two loose rows behind the course, 9 to 17 units apart, 9 to 19 units tall (trunk plus crown). They are flat paintings that always face the camera, standing on the bottom edge of the image: the trunk base sits at the bottom centre, nothing hangs below it.
  • Bushes sit along the front edge of the counter, 2 to 4 units wide, low and wide. Tufts and flowers are small (about 1 unit) and scattered on the grass.
  • The stump is a cylinder the knife lands on: radius 1.7 at the top, 2.4 to 4.8 units tall. Its top is a disc seen from above (tree rings); its side is bark that wraps around.
  • The mushroom is a springboard the knife bounces off: a cap 4.4 units wide sitting on a stem 2.2 units tall. One painted mushroom facing the camera, cap and stem together.
  • The overhead branch runs horizontally across the course 8 to 11 units up, 6 to 14 units long, with leaves at one end; fruit hangs from it on a vine.
  • The cracked shelf is a plank shelf that breaks after one bounce: 4 to 6 units wide, 0.9 tall.
  • The finish flag flies from a pole on top of the score wall at the end of the course: 3.4 by 2.2 units.

6. Rules that do not bend

  • File names and folders exactly as above (lower case, underscores), so the game picks them up without anyone renaming.
  • Cut out pieces: transparent background, the subject trimmed to its edges with a few pixels of margin, standing on the bottom edge where the list says it stands.
  • Tiles: no seams when repeated 3 by 3, no vignette, no lighting gradient across the tile.
  • Nothing changes how the game plays: every painting goes over a shape that already exists, so do not change the proportions given in section 4.
