2. The game in two minutes

Live URL, the only thing under review: https://lucidwinds.com/satellites/slice-3d/ (build v6.0). Portrait phone, a three.js scene, one page of vanilla JavaScript. Sky Wolf Studio, the Lucid Winds arcade. Non botanical, kid friendly, snackable, started from Penny asking for the Slice Master feel.

The hook: one knife, flipping end over end. The BLADE cuts (fruit, brown slabs, ropes, planks) and the HANDLE bounces (walls, bumper pads). What is deadly and what is rewarding flips per mode. Tap or hold to flip.

  • Journey: a runner through a forest of fruit on tables, pergolas, towers and swinging ropes, ending at a score wall of multiplier bands x1 to x10. Blade sticks the wall and ends the level; handle bounces back for a retry.
  • Freefall: dive a canyon shaft. The side walls KILL if the blade touches them (the knife sticks, red sparks, STUCK); the handle bounces clean. Brown cuttable stacks split to reveal a bright inner colour. A bullseye floor x1 to x10. Levels get deeper and wider.
  • Endless: the pit with no floor, a live depth in metres, ramping fall speed, best depth on the menu, PIT CONQUERED if you reach the bottom clean.
  • Wall Climb: the Journey ending stretched tall: a short runway into one mega wall of 10 bands (x1 to x15) growing to 20 bands (up to x75). Blade sticks for the multiplier; handle thunks you back down.
  • Fruit (16): apple, orange, plum, banana, pineapple, watermelon, lime, kiwi, dragonfruit, starfruit, pomegranate, coconut, pumpkin, turnip, pepper, honeydew. Today each is a flat single colour sphere that splits into two halves.
  • Pit pieces (16): shelves, branch, stack, swing, shroom, bumper pad, crystal, wasp, plank, gate, thorn bramble, boom gourd (a trap fruit that explodes), golden fruit, pinata, melon wall, vine.
  • Worlds (4, unnamed palettes): a mossy ochre canyon; an ice blue chasm; an ember red kiln; a teal glow cave. Each is only a set of hex colours on flat walls.
  • Knife Forge (22 skins, currency Slivers earned every run, no lootboxes, cosmetic only): Classic Chef (free); Iron Cleaver 150, Rainbow Brush 300, Bamboo Splitter 200, Garden Trowel 280, Feather Quill 350; Midnight Katana 500, Crystal Shard 800, Obsidian Flake 620, Icicle 760, Tuning Fork 900; Golden Fortune 1500, Ember Fang 1600, Prism Edge 1900, Moonlit Scythe 2200; trophies earned by play: Cosmic Edge (50 clean dives), Wallbreaker Pick (a x30 climb stick), Depth Diver (150 m), Combo Conductor (a x25 combo), Groundskeeper (100 slabs); Support the Studio showpieces: Starforge Blade, Wolf Fang. Every skin is built from boxes in code; blade on the left, grip on the right, each side six units long.
  • Known faults from our own art audit: the shaft is one flat brown and depth reads as a smear; the menu buttons are candy coloured slabs over the scene; the HUD is grey on brown with no plate; every fruit is a flat hex sphere; the music card covers the Endless button at boot.

4. The asset pack

4.1 How art enters a three.js game

The world is geometry we build in code. You paint what wraps it: tileable textures for walls and ledges, back wall plates, sky plates, atlases that multiply over the fruit, sprite sheets for effects, decor billboards, and the UI. For the knives you make orthographic concept sheets that we rebuild in code or lift into 3D, so proportion is law there. Style: painted, chunky, warm and readable, high contrast between the knife, the cuttables and the walls; the midnight palette for UI (deep blacks, sage #7ab356, gold #c8a84b, cream #e8dcc8); each world owns its own palette inside the scene. No text baked in. No real trademarks. One light direction, upper left.

4.2 Rules for every file

  • Textures that repeat must tile seamlessly on both axes and be square powers of two: 512 x 512 or 1024 x 1024. Say TILE in the manifest for each.
  • Plates (back walls, skies, title) are JPG. Anything with transparency (sprites, UI, decor, atlases) is PNG with alpha. Never a side over 1600 px except tileable 1024s.
  • Sprite sheets: one uniform cell size per sheet, frames in reading order, no gaps, cell size in the manifest. One frame per picture is also fine, numbered.
  • Knife sheets: 1024 x 256 transparent, the knife horizontal, BLADE pointing LEFT, grip on the right, the two halves equal in length (the blade tip at x 32, the pommel at x 992, the balance point at the centre), side view only, flat lighting, no shadow on the ground. One sheet per skin, plus a 256 x 256 icon of the same knife at 45 degrees.
  • Folders and lowercase names with underscores, exactly as below.

4.8 Manifest

manifest.txt at the zip root: one line per file, path | width x height | frames | TILE or not | note; a MISSING section; then one paragraph on the style choices so we can match them in code.

5. The bar and the format

The bar: portrait phone, 412 x 915 primary (also 360 and 375 wide); touch targets at least 48 px; no text under 0.7 rem; player copy never uses dashes of any kind; Slivers are earned, the only real money is the clearly marked Support the Studio shelf; one page of vanilla JavaScript with vendored three.js; colour blind safe (hazards by shape, never colour alone); 60 fps on a mid phone.

Format for every direction, strictly: one numbered block: Title / What and why (3 lines max) / Which mode or world / Effort S, M or L / Priority 1 to 3. Group under: Worlds and levels, Things to cut, Knives and forge, Modes and progression, Economy, UI and feel. End the document with a json block: an array of objects with keys id, title, group, effort, priority, summary. No prose after the json.
