PIXEL PETRI, ROUND 1 FOR ASTRA: THE LIVING YEAR (7 Nov 2026)

Pixel Petri is Stephen's tiny world sandbox (it lives at https://lucidwinds.com/satellites/tiny-world/ and on Google
Play as Pixel Petri, $0.99; do not play it for this round, the rules below are the whole of what you need). A finger paints terrain, drops creatures, people, things and powers, and watches what
happens: 126 creatures, a village that grows, reactions the game tells back. It is his best seller so far, seven buyers
in seven countries with no marketing, and he wants a MASSIVE expansion and a commercial. The first expansion is THE
LIVING YEAR: seasons and weather over every world, ponds that freeze, snow that spreads, harvests, migrations, nocturnal
creatures that sleep by day, eggs and nests, populations that boom and crash on their own. Your round 1 is the art for
it, and your ideas first. Fable wires your zip from the zip alone, so write as if no one can ask you a follow up.

HOW TO WORK (Stephen's standing order; it binds this pack)
- No tests. No checks. No validation scripts, no pixel audits, no reports about reports. The art is the deliverable.
- Look at each sheet ONCE with your own eyes when it is done, fix what is wrong, move on.
- One assessment, one contact sheet per section, one zip. No progress notes.
- If something is ambiguous, pick the reading that ships and write one line about it in NOTES.md. Do not stop to ask.

THE RULES OF THE CUT (the game's own art law, design/05-art-audio.md; every line binds)
- Sprites are 8x8 or 16x16 STRING GRIDS: one character a pixel, '.' transparent. Nothing rotated, nothing scaled to a
  non integer. Babies draw at 6x6, giants at 10x10, by the renderer, never by you.
- ONE master palette, letters in the grid: k #1b1b26 ink, w #f4f4f0 white, l #c9c9d4 light gray, e #8c8c99 gray,
  E #55555f dark gray, s #f2c29b skin, p #f2a0b5 pink, r #d43b3b red, o #e8842a orange, y #f2d03b yellow, g #4fb04a green,
  G #26663a dark green, z #8fbf6a sick green, c #7fd4e8 cyan, b #3b6fd4 blue, n #8a5a32 brown, N #4f2e1a dark brown,
  A the species primary, B the species secondary. Recolours pick from it; a new hex needs a reason and a line in NOTES.md.
  Extended hexes exist for recolour targets only: #ffffff #e9dcb0 #d9b44a #c9a35a #b98a4a #a8703a #7a4a2a #5a4030
  #2a1e16 #a82a2a #7a1f1f #5a1010 #f28c2a #f2f060 #7be04a #3f7a3a #3f5a34 #2a9d8f #5fa8e8 #34507a #30206a #6a3fa0
  #8e44ad #e87ad4 #22222c.
- Read at 2x on a phone: the silhouette alone, filled one colour, must be identifiable against grass.
- Every living thing has an eye: one k pixel (two for front facing). Side view faces RIGHT; the renderer mirrors.
- Shading: at most one darker shade per material, bottom or right. No dithering, no anti aliasing, no outlines.
- Animation is TWO frames at 4 fps while moving, frame A when idle. Frame B is built by rule from frame A (legs shift,
  wings shift, tails flick) unless you give frames[1] yourself: give it only where the rule would be wrong.
- Terrain is not a sprite: each terrain is THREE colours the renderer noise mixes (a triple), animated terrains have
  four pre rendered phases. So a season's ground is a triple, not a tile.
- Night is the renderer's blue wash with light pools. Weather sits on top of that.
Deliver every grid as text in the manifest (the game stores sprites as text, data/sprites.json), and a PNG of each at
1:1 beside it for your contact sheets. The text is what gets imported.

YOUR IDEAS FIRST (write before you draw; one line each, up to ten, in ASSESSMENT.md)
Stephen's words: "a massive expansion ... since it's my highest grossing". The question for you: what should a child
SEE happen over a year in this world that makes them come back tomorrow? Season events, weather with consequences,
things creatures do only at night, what a village does in winter, what the first snow changes, a migration worth
watching, anything that makes a world feel alive without a score. Stephen reads these; nobody builds them without his yes.

THE PIECES
A. THE FOUR SEASONS OF THE GROUND: for each terrain that changes (grass, forest floor, meadow, farmland, the pond's
   bank, the shallow water), a colour TRIPLE per season (spring fresh, summer deep, autumn gold and rust, winter white
   with the ground showing through), in the palette or the extended hexes; plus ICE (a frozen pond triple) and SNOW
   COVER (a triple that lies over any ground). Write them as a table in seasons.json: terrain, season, three hexes.
B. TREES AND PLANTS THROUGH THE YEAR: the game's tree, pine, bush and berry sprites in four seasonal states each, 16x16
   trees and 8x8 bushes and berries, frame A only (they are things): spring blossom, summer full, autumn colour, winter
   bare (the pine keeps its needles, gains snow). Berries: bud, ripe, picked. Farmland crops: sown, grown, harvest, stubble.
C. WEATHER OVER THE WORLD, each a strip of four 8x8 frames the code scatters and moves: RAIN (streaks), SNOW (flakes),
   LEAVES on the wind (autumn), PETALS (spring), HEAT shimmer (summer, sparse). And a 16x16 two tone CLOUD (two
   frames) whose shadow the renderer casts; a LIGHTNING flash is the game's own.
D. EGGS AND NESTS: an EGG at 8x8 for five families (bird, reptile, fish roe, bug, dragon), each with a CRACKED frame;
   a NEST at 8x8 (twigs) and a dragon nest at 16x16 (stones and bones); a HATCH puff is the game's own.
E. SLEEPING: an overlay the renderer lays on any creature asleep, 8 wide: closed eye marks (two k dashes) and a small
   'z' glyph in w rising, two frames; and for the five nocturnal species the game has (owl, bat, raccoon, wolf, firefly)
   a frame A asleep pose in their own template (the owl tucked, the bat hung, the raccoon curled, the wolf lying, the
   firefly dark) at the size the species draws.
F. MIGRATION: a V of birds as one 16x16 two frame sprite (seven dots and wings), a school of fish at 16x16 two frames,
   a herd marker is not needed (the game moves herds itself).
G. THE SEASON AND WEATHER MARKS for the top bar: four 16x16 season icons (a sprout, a sun, a leaf, a flake) and four
   weather icons (clear, rain, snow, wind) in the game's UI grammar (05 section 6), two tone, no words.

WHAT NOT TO DRAW
No new creatures (the next round), no new terrains, no UI screens, no title art, nothing off the palette, nothing over
16x16 except the dragon nest.

DELIVERY
ONE zip named pixel-petri-astra-round1.zip: ASSESSMENT.md (your ideas, then a short assessment), manifest.json (every
sprite: key, size, frames as text grids in the palette letters, the frame rule or your own frame B), seasons.json (the
triples), assets/ (a_ground, b_plants, c_weather, d_eggs, e_sleep, f_migration, g_marks; a PNG per sprite at 1:1),
previews/ (one contact sheet per section), NOTES.md (what you chose differently and why). Stephen uploads it.
