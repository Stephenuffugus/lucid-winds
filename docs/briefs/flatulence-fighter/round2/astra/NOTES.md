## Sound direction

The game already synthesizes flaps and squeaks. No sound files are included. Keep an uncovered squeak brief and percussive; covered release can have a softer, longer flap without a hiss. A new call benefits from one quiet dry tap, a completed call from a small two-note cadence, and a move from a short directional pair. Keep LEAVE distinct from the failure sound so the player can still correct it during the grace period. Avoid long tails that mask the real cover-noise ending.
