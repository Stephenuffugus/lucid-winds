0. Toybox Peaks in models (live tonight)

Built in its own style, glossy painted toys. Four reviewers (name, readability, craft, style and safety) looked at every toy before it shipped; where a model lost, today’s shape stays.

0b. Crumb Country, second look (fixed tonight)

Three independent reviewers looked at every w1 model through one lens each (does it read as its name, does it stand out on its ground, is it well made). They were right where I had been too generous. Fixed: the strawberry, ant, thimble, lemonade jug, cooler, teapot and cordial bottle were made again; the butterfly's wings now fold into a V so it never vanishes edge on. Back to today's shape: the chess rook (twice a queen), the cracker, the thermos and the banner pole. And every model now has a clean texture layout: the fine pale lines across the teacup, the jam jar and the ladybird are gone.

Your idea: a style per world

Your idea, a style per world: not dumb, if every world stays one family: chunky, rounded, bright, readable from far away, no text. Only the material changes with the world's theme. The game already gives each world its own models and its own texture, so it costs no extra credits. One rule: a thing that shows up in several worlds keeps the look of the world it first appears in.
