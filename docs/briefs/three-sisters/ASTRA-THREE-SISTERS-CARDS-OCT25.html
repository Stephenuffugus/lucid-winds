3. The hard rule stays the hard rule

Four traits, each with three values, each readable on its own at 40 px with twelve cards on a 360 px phone, in greyscale. Shape by silhouette, fill by full / hatched / hollow, count by repetition, colour by ink plus the seal. A painted crop that makes a hatched squash look like a solid squash has failed, however beautiful. Your fill key sheet is the test; run it on every candidate before you send it.
