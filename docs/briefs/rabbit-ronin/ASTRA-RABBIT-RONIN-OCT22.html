2. The game in two minutes

Live URL, the only thing under review: https://lucidwinds.com/satellites/rabbit-samurai/ . Review that page and nothing else. Open it on a phone, portrait. Sky Wolf Studio, the Lucid Winds arcade.

A masterless rabbit ronin with a dagger on a vine. The dojo burned, the crows took his master's sword. He swings across bramble canyons on the vine, frees caged mice, collects carrots, and slices enemies. One HTML file, vanilla JavaScript, 2D canvas, portrait phone, everything on screen today is drawn by code (no painted art at all yet).

  • Structure: 24 dojos (levels) in 4 worlds of 6: The Crate Yards (mossy green on near black), The Burrows (warm browns, underground), The Grove (deep forest green), The Peaks (cold blue night, snow and gusts). Story cards fade in between dojos (four acts: find the sword, carry it underground, learn it, take the sheath back from the crows). The sword is found after dojo 6; from then on the DASH button exists. The mole who pops out of the ground at the end of every dojo is his informant.
  • Controls: touch, bottom of the screen. Left side: move. Right side: VINE (throw the dagger up to a vine anchor and swing), JUMP, and after dojo 6 DASH (the sword: for 0.18 s the ronin lunges and cuts anything he passes through; a kill refunds the dash, chains show x2, x3 over the button; 1.4 s cooldown drawn as a gold arc).
  • Hero: cream white rabbit, 24 px tall today, red headband with two trailing tails, dagger on a vine, a small sword on the back after dojo 6. Faces left and right (we mirror). Runs, jumps, swings hanging from the vine, dashes with a slash trail, blinks when hurt. Three hearts, four with an upgrade.
  • Enemies: hedgehog (ground patrol, walks back and forth), porcupine (a rolling spiked ball), crow (flies, dives), toad spitter (sits, throat sac swells, lobs a glob you can slice mid air). Hazards: spike strips, pits.
  • Friends and things: caged mouse (a cage with bars; free it by touching it), the mole (pops up at the end of a dojo), carrots (currency, currently the raw emoji), diamonds (bonus gems), hearts, bounce pads, cannons (launch pads that fire you up), vine anchors (the points the dagger grabs).
  • Dojo shop (spend carrots): Longer Vine (3 tiers), Mighty Fling (2), Swift Paws (2), Fourth Heart (1). Nothing is ever sold for real money.
  • Screens: title (text only today, red and cream on black with one red button), dojo map (24 buttons tinted per world), shop, help, comfort settings (sound, story cards toggle, reduced motion), story cards, clear banner, results and share.

4. Part two: the asset pack

4.1 Style

Painted 2D with a soft ink outline and simple cel shading, storybook cut out feel, chunky readable silhouettes (a sprite is seen at 24 to 64 px on a phone, so big shapes, few details, strong value contrast against dark backgrounds). One light direction, upper left. Warm rim light on the hero. The red headband is the hero's brand and must read at any size. Not pixel art, not photoreal, not 3D render. No text baked into any picture. No real trademarks, logos or known characters. Backgrounds are dark and moody per world; characters are brighter than the world behind them.

4.2 Rules for every file

  • PNG with transparent background for every sprite, prop, tile, icon and parallax layer. JPG only for full bleed plates (title plate, world cards).
  • Sizes below are what to deliver. Never a side over 1600 px (the host resizes anything bigger).
  • Animations: one picture per frame is welcome. Name frames run_01.png, run_02.png and so on. If you make a sheet instead, use one uniform cell size per sheet, cells in reading order, no gaps, and write the cell size in the manifest. We cut, fit and build the atlas on our side.
  • Characters face RIGHT in every frame; we mirror for left.
  • Keep the character's feet on the same baseline in every frame of a set, and the same scale across all sets of one character.
  • Every frame of a set at the same canvas size (the cell size given), character centered horizontally, feet at the bottom edge minus 8 px.
  • Folder layout inside the zip is fixed below. Lowercase, underscores, no spaces.
