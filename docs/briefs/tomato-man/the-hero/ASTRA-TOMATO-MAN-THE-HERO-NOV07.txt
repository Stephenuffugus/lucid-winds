TOMATO MAN, THE HERO: THE THIRTEEN PRODUCE IN HIS SHEETS, AND THE PLANET TOMATO (7 Nov 2026)

Your v2 pack gave Tomato Man his five bearing sheets (south, south east, east, north east, north; the west side is
mirrored by the code), 256 px cells, four by two: idle, four walk frames, the dash crouch, the dash stretch, the hop.
The thirteen other produce bodies in the builder (strawberry, orange, lemon, lime, peach, cherry, bell pepper,
eggplant, avocado, heirloom, pickle, golden tomato, sunburn survivor) are still one still image each with his face
laid on, so a player who buys one loses the walk, the dash and the eight bearings. This round gives every body the
same sheets, and draws the planet tomato's passing shadow that round 2 asked about. Fable wires your zip from the zip
alone, so write as if no one can ask you a follow up.

HOW TO WORK (Stephen's standing order; it binds this pack)
- No tests. No checks. No validation scripts, no pixel audits, no reports about reports. The art is the deliverable.
- Look at each sheet ONCE with your own eyes when it is done, fix what is wrong, move on.
- One assessment, written once. One contact sheet per section. One zip. If something is ambiguous, pick the reading
  that ships and write one line about it in NOTES.md. Do not stop to ask.

THE PIECES
A. THIRTEEN BODIES IN HIS SHEETS. For each produce body, the five bearing sheets in EXACTLY the tomato's cell format
   (256 px cells, four by two, the same eight frames in the same order, the same timing, the same pivot at the feet,
   the face read from the same cells). The head must sit where the tomato's head sits in every cell, because the hats
   are laid on by the tomato's per cell anchors (your HAT_ANCHOR table): a lemon's point or a pepper's stalk may rise
   above that spot, never move it. Keep each body's silhouette its own (the pickle long, the cherry two stalked, the
   avocado pitted, the sunburn survivor peeling) and its colours as the builder's cards show them. Files:
   hero/<body>_<bearing>.webp with <body> in strawberry, orange, lemon, lime, peach, cherry, bell_pepper, eggplant,
   avocado, heirloom, pickle, golden, sunburn and <bearing> in s, se, e, ne, n; sixty five sheets. If a body cannot
   keep the head spot (say the pickle), say so in NOTES.md and give its own anchor table in anchors.json.
B. THE PLANET TOMATO PASSES (Stephen's decision 1; draw it, he decides whether it is wired). Once per level in Dunes at
   Dusk and Eclipse a huge round shadow (radius 220 world px) crosses the map on a fixed path at a fixed time, with the
   planet itself drawn at the far edge of the screen as the caster. The shadow is a moving platform you can ride and a
   wall you can hide behind. Draw: the planet (a tomato the size of a moon, 480 world px across, with the strange sun
   sick look of the v2 hero, no face) in three states, approaching (three frames), passing (a four frame slow turn),
   leaving (three frames); its shadow disc as a soft 440 world px texture the code lays over the exact circle (the
   circle is the truth, as your shade pool); a 48 px mark for the cue line (tm-planet).
C. THE BUILDER'S CARDS, refreshed if A changes a body's look: the thirteen builder heads (builder/<body>_head.webp) so
   the shop matches the sheets. Skip any that already match.
