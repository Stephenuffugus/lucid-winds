THE GAME IN ONE LINE
A top down dash across a beach under a sun that burns him in two thirds of a second; shade is the only ground. Play it at
https://lucidwinds.com/satellites/tomato-man/ on a phone. Your v2 pack is the look to match: his hero sheets, the awnings, the
Angry Sun. Every size below is in world px; on a phone one world px is one CSS px.

THE PIECES
A. THE SEED TOSS
   A1 seed: 16 px, spinning in the air on a 46 px arc. Four frames.
   A2 seed shadow on the sand.
   A3 sapling: it stands on the point where the seed landed, 30 px tall at full height. Sprout (four frames over 0.2 s), alive (a slow
      two frame sway), wilt (four frames over the last 0.5 s, drooping and going yellow). The base is the anchor.
   A4 sapling shade: a soft round pool, drawn over a circle of radius 58 (the circle is the truth, as with your shade pool).
   A5 the burst: a seed thrown into the Angry Sun's ring bursts the lunge. An effect 140 px across, four frames over 0.3 s.
   A6 the SEED button: 66 px round, in the set of your DASH and SHADE buttons, with its word in code.
   A7 a landing mark: 14 px, the faint ring where a seed would land.
B. SUN CRITTERS
   B1 the sand scorpion: about 40 px long, top down, drawn facing right; it turns to any bearing. Run (four frames at 12 fps), pin (a
      seed sprouting on its back, two frames), sink into the sand and rise (four frames each, 0.4 s). It reads against sunlit sand
      at a glance, in each world's sand colour. It also has a charging tint or pose: it must be plain that it has seen him.
   B2 the heat mirage: his own shape in shimmering orange, about 32 px, walking a third of a second behind him. Four frames of shimmer,
      a dissolve (four frames over 0.35 s) and a pinned state with the same sprout. It must never be taken for him at a glance.
   B3 the sting: an effect at him, 72 px, three frames.
   B4 two marks for the cue line and the how to play card, in your icon sprite's style: tm-scorpion, tm-mirage; and a tm-seed.
