STEPHEN'S FOUR YESES (his words in quotes)
A. THE COLLAPSING STAIRCASE ("kind of sounds cool"): a run of three to five one way tabs that goes as a staircase, left
   or right; when the bug stands on its first step the steps drop one after another, bottom first, each a half second
   after the last, announced by dust and a creak before the first goes. Draw: staircase_step, 16 by 8, the frames whole,
   cracked, falling, gone, in the tab's grammar (your tab_bold is the bold film tab: keep its edge); staircase_dust, 16
   by 16, three frames of settling dust under a step that went; a creak mark for the room's cue line (tm style not
   needed, this is Shutterbug: a 16 px one colour mark in the icon grammar, key icon_creak).
B. THE SWINGING STRANDS ("swinging platforms ... sounds kind of cool"): in the Loom's arena and, from this round, as a
   room piece in Attic rooms: a web strand hung from the ceiling the bug can grab and swing on, as a footholds that moves.
   Draw: web_strand_swing, 16 by 48, five frames across a swing, far left, left, centre, right, far right (the anchor
   at the top centre, the foothold a knot at the bottom); web_knot, 16 by 16, two frames, the knot at rest and under
   a bug's weight; a snapped strand, web_strand_snap, 16 by 48, two frames (the Loom's cut strand becomes a swing: when
   it snaps it falls).
C. THE CROSSWIND ("some kind of diversity ... a feel change"): a photo's bright window regions give a short crosswind
   every few seconds that pushes the bug sideways a cell while it blows, with an audible inhale (the game's sound) and a
   sparse gust cue. Draw: window_wind, 48 by 16, three frames of a horizontal gust in sparse one pixel marks (a few
   streaks, never a filled sheet; Ash and Paper, no Sun), drawn left to right, the code mirrors it; a wind mark for the
   cue line, icon_wind, 16 px one colour.
D. THE GUARDIANS' PHASES ("two or three phases, at least two"): a guardian's attack changes as its health goes. For the
   seven guardians (your five of round 9 and the Loom and the Wasp of round 10) the body sheets stay as they are; draw
   for each a RAGE variant of its tell frame only, 80 by 64 in the boss_fallback layout, the same 5 by 4 erosion cells,
   the body under half its health: brighter eyes, a changed posture, the same silhouette so the erosion mask still fits.
   Keys: boss_<name>_rage with one frame, tell. Names: moth_mother, rime_mass, anemone, thorn_heart, cinder_kiln,
   cobweb_loom, lantern_wasp.
