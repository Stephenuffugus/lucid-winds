SHUTTERBUG, ROUND 9 FOR ASTRA: FIVE GUARDIANS AND MORE BUGS (6 Nov 2026)

Stephen on 6 Nov, after a house run on his Pixel: "shutterbug needs to have a lot more depth to the fighting ... i
just fought a boss that shot stuff upwards but i could sit next to it and hit it over and over ... i have also only
seen one boss that looks the same. we could use a ton more bad guys, bosses, depth to levels." The sitting beside it
is fixed in code (a guardian now shoves a bug that camps within two cells of it, in every biome). The rest needs art
before it can be built, and that is this round.

HOW TO WORK (Stephen's standing order; it binds this round)
- No tests. No checks. No validation scripts, no pixel audits, no reports about reports. The art is the deliverable.
- Look at each sheet ONCE with your own eyes when it is done, fix what is wrong, move on.
- One assessment, one contact sheet per section, one zip. No progress notes.
- If something is ambiguous, pick the reading that ships and write one line about it in NOTES.md. Do not stop to ask.

WHAT IS THERE TODAY
Play a house at https://lucidwinds.com/satellites/shutterbug/ (the Darkroom from the title). The last room of a house
holds the guardian: your clay body (boss_fallback, 80 by 64 px, five by four cells of 16 px, breathe and brace frames)
in every biome. Only its attack changes by biome: Attic sheds moths, Frost drops icicles down marked columns, Deep
pulses a ring out from its middle, Bramble raises thorn runs, Ember throws a fan of sparks upward. Every house ends on
the same clay lump, so to a player there is one boss. The rooms before it hold the biome's fodder and one elite kind
each, from your round 8 sheets.

WHAT WE WANT
A. FIVE GUARDIAN BODIES, one per biome, in the boss_fallback format exactly (80 by 64 px at 16 px cells, the body as
   the cells the weapons strike; the game erodes the struck cells away, so draw it as a thing that can lose pieces):
   Attic: the Moth Mother, a furred moth body with the shed on her back. Frost: the Rime Mass, blue ice with a dark
   core. Deep: the Anemone, a soft body with mouths. Bramble: the Thorn Heart, a knot of stems around a seed. Ember:
   the Cinder Kiln, a brick kiln with a mouth of fire. For each: breathe, brace, a tell frame (the half second before
   its attack), a hurt flash, and a down frame (what stays after it falls). Keep the dark mass it rests on from round 8.
B. A SECOND ATTACK PER GUARDIAN, the props only (we build the behaviour): Attic, a dust cloud that drifts across the
   room (three drift frames, 32 px); Frost, a frost breath cone from its mouth (a three frame cone, 48 by 24); Deep, a
   tentacle that whips up from the floor beside it (a 16 by 48 rise, three frames); Bramble, seed pods that roll along
   the floor (16 px, four roll frames); Ember, a flame that crawls along the floor from its base (16 px, three frames).
   Each with one tell mark the room can show before it comes.
C. THE SHOVE TELL: the game now draws an ink frame two cells out from the body while a shove winds up. Give us a biome
   coloured flash for that frame (a 1 px ring sprite we tile, three frames) so it reads as the body's own.
D. TEN NEW ENEMY KINDS, two per biome, in your round 8 enemy sheet formats (fodder 16 px, elite 24 px, with the frames
   round 8 used: idle, move, hurt, dead): Attic, a silverfish that darts along walls and a spider that drops on a
   thread; Frost, a snow flea that hops and an icicle bat that hangs then dives; Deep, a lamprey that swims straight
   and a jelly that drifts up and stings; Bramble, a bramble tick that crawls the thorns and a seed wasp that circles;
   Ember, a cinder mite that scurries and an ash moth that burns a trail.
E. ROOM PIECES for the harder rooms (he has the list, we build after his pick): a conveyor belt (a 16 px tile strip,
   four frames rolling, both directions), a crumbling shelf (16 px, four frames from whole to gone), a swinging lamp
   (a 32 px lamp on a 48 px chain, three swing frames), a window gust (a 16 by 48 gust column, three frames).

WHAT NOT TO DRAW
No new HUD, no new weapons, no new title art. Nothing over 1600 px a side. No reuse of the clay lump's silhouette for
any of the five bodies: a player must tell them apart in the dark at a glance.

DELIVERY
One zip, shutterbug-astra-round9.zip: ASSESSMENT.md (one page), NOTES.md, then A to E each in its own folder with a
contact sheet, file names in round 8's style.
