SHUTTERBUG, ROUND 10 FOR ASTRA: FILM STOCKS, RANK, THIRTY PRINTS, FOUR NEW VERBS, TWO GUARDIANS (7 Nov 2026)

Stephen, 7 Nov: "I'm loving the look. I'm loving the difficulty increase ... we could do with a whole bunch more assets.
Why not make the game real diverse? Make it grow as people play it more. It's still missing a little something whether
it's a way to change biomes or level up their character or unlock collectibles." Three systems are designed (film
stocks, rank, prints) and wait for your art before they are built; round 10 is that art, the bodies he asked for on
6 Nov ("a ton more bad guys, bosses, depth to levels"), and your own ideas first. Fable wires your zip from the zip
alone, so write as if no one can ask you a follow up.

HOW TO WORK (Stephen's standing order; it binds this round)
- No tests. No checks. No validation scripts, no pixel audits, no reports about reports. The art is the deliverable.
- Look at each sheet ONCE with your own eyes when it is done, fix what is wrong, move on.
- One assessment, one contact sheet per section, one zip. No progress notes.
- If something is ambiguous, pick the reading that ships and write one line about it in NOTES.md. Do not stop to ask.

THE RULES OF THE CUT (round 9's, unchanged): the game's 16 colour palette and nothing outside it (your round 9 files
carried it; the cutter refuses any other hex), 16 px cells, crisp pixels, 1 px outlines, no smoothing; living things
are stop motion clay and read through motion (squash, stretch, a one pixel idle wobble); strips of discrete frames, file
names in round 8 and 9's style, every sheet's frame names in the manifest. Play the live game at
https://lucidwinds.com/satellites/shutterbug/ on a phone (the Darkroom and the album are on the title).

THE NEW SYSTEMS, so you know what the art is for
- FILM STOCKS: on the develop screen, before Develop, the player picks a film: Auto (the photo's own colour decides the
  biome, as today), Attic, Frost, Deep, Overgrowth, Ember. A locked stock shows a lock and the rank that opens it.
- RANK: ten ranks in the camera register, earned by what the player has done (clears, house clears, flawless rooms,
  keys, wall jumps, album pages): Shutterbug, Snapshot, Focus Puller, Darkroom Hand, Exposure, Lensman, Developer,
  Enlarger, Archivist, The Light. Each rank up grants a stock, a hero look or a frame. The title shows the rank and a bar.
- PRINTS: a room may hide one PRINT, a tiny photograph on the photo's own furniture, off the climb. Thirty prints, six a
  biome, collected in the album on a COLLECTION page (five rows of six, silhouettes until found); a full row gives that
  biome's frame.

The three systems above are Fable's answer; yours is wanted beside it. New enemy verbs, bosses, room set pieces, biome

THE PIECES
A. SIX FILM BOXES (the stock picker): Auto plus one per biome, 48 px square, a small film box in the biome's colours
   (Auto a plain box with a question of light), each with a locked state (a 1 px lock mark in the frame's ink, not a
   grey wash). Keys film_auto, film_attic, film_frost, film_deep, film_overgrowth, film_ember, and _locked.
B. RANK: ten badge glyphs at 24 px (keys rank_01 to rank_10) in the icon grammar, a lens iris that opens one stop per
   rank; the rank up strip: a 128 by 16 px plate in the strip's style the words sit in (words in code).
C. THIRTY PRINTS, each as a 16 px pickup (the Polaroid shape, the subject a few pixels) AND a 48 px album tile (the same
   subject readable), plus one 48 px silhouette tile the code tints by biome. The names (Stephen's to rename):
   Attic: The Dusty Globe, A Doll's Eye, The Stopped Clock, Grandmother's Fan, The Tin Soldier, A Moth in Amber.
   Frost: The Frozen Key, A Snow Globe, The Blue Bottle, The Icicle Comb, A Lost Mitten, The Last Match.
   Deep: The Glass Float, A Ship in a Bottle, The Anchor Charm, A Pearl Button, The Diver's Watch, The Conch.
   Overgrowth: The Seed Packet, A Pressed Fern, The Garden Gnome, The Watering Can, A Snail Shell, The Thorn Crown.
   Ember: The Brass Lantern, A Matchbook, The Kiln Key, The Ash Jar, A Red Thread, The Coal Heart.
   Keys print_attic_1 to print_ember_6, print_tile_<same>, print_silhouette. Five frame plates for the biome frames the
   full rows unlock (the Polaroid border in that biome's colours, as your round 8 frames: frame_attic ... frame_ember).
D. FOUR NEW ENEMY KINDS, in your round 8 and 9 enemy sheet formats (fodder 16 px, elite 24 px, with the frames those
   sheets carry: idle, move, hurt, dead), each a new VERB, never a recolour:
   D1 the LOBBER: stands and lobs a pellet in an arc; add a wind up frame and the pellet (8 px, two frames). Lives in
      the Attic (a dust lobber) and Ember (a cinder lobber): two bodies.
   D2 the DROPPER: hangs under a shelf and drops when the bug passes under, then climbs back; add a hanging frame, a
      drop frame, a climb frame. Deep (a jelly dropper) and Overgrowth (a seed dropper): two bodies.
   D3 the SHELLBACK: walks with a hard front; a hit from the front bounces the bug, a hit from behind or a dive from
      above kills it; its front must read as armour at 16 px. Frost (an ice shell) and Ember (a slag shell): two bodies.
   D4 the SPLITTER: an elite body that splits into two fodder on its first hit; the elite sheet plus a split frame and
      the two halves as a fodder sheet. Any biome: one body in the palette's neutral clay.
E. TWO NEW GUARDIANS, in the boss_fallback format exactly (80 by 64 px at 16 px cells, the body on its dark mass, with
   breathe, brace, a tell frame, a hurt flash, a down frame, as round 9's five): THE COBWEB LOOM for the Attic (a loom of
   web with a spider's eyes in it) and THE LANTERN WASP for Ember (a paper lantern body with a wasp's wings). Each with
   two attacks in round 9's style: the Loom a web strand that drops from the ceiling (16 by 48, three frames) and a
   dust puff; the Wasp a stinger dart (8 px, three frames) and a flame that crawls the floor (round 9's Ember flame is
   reused; say so). The house run alternates its guardian from round 11 on; tonight they are art.
F. WEATHER, one per biome: a particle strip of four 8 px frames the code scatters over the room: dust (Attic), snow
   (Frost), bubbles (Deep), pollen (Overgrowth), embers (Ember). Keys weather_<biome>.
