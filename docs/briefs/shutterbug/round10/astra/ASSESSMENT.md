# Astra’s ideas — proposals for Stephen, not approved additions

1. Give every newly developed photo one visible, optional side route to its hidden print, so collecting asks for a deliberate detour rather than blind searching.
2. Let a cleared photo retain its best route as a faint replay silhouette, giving familiar rooms a personal time-to-beat without online leaderboards.
3. Turn repeated horizontal edges into a short collapsing staircase event, announced by dust and creaks before the first step drops.
4. Add a shutter beetle that opens its shell only after a near miss, making careful baiting a distinct alternative to repeated attacks.
5. Make the Cobweb Loom’s cut strands briefly usable as swinging footholds, so damaging the guardian also changes the route through its arena.
6. Let window-shaped bright regions produce a brief crosswind with an audible inhale and a sparse gust cue, creating a photo-specific movement puzzle.
7. Offer a voluntary “one stock, three new photos” expedition with a cosmetic reward, rewarding exploration without daily streak penalties.
8. Put a small visual clue for the last missing print on a completed biome row, so finishing collections becomes a solvable hunt instead of repetition.
9. Let a guardian’s last surviving part change its final attack, making erosion choices matter beyond reducing its health.
10. Reward a flawless room with a contact-print version of that player’s own photograph, preserving the memory rather than only increasing a number.

## Short assessment

The strongest connection is between the player’s photograph and a persistent collection: film stocks give agency, rank records mastery, and prints give an optional reason to explore off the climb. The art should make those roles distinct—boxes for choosing, an opening iris for progress, miniature photographs for discoveries—while keeping gameplay silhouettes stronger than decoration. I played the available sample room and opened the biome album in build **sb m2 12**. This browser did not expose the Darkroom route, so I did not complete a house or see its guardian; a physical-phone playthrough was not available. The supplied brief governs the three unbuilt systems and the new guardian art.
