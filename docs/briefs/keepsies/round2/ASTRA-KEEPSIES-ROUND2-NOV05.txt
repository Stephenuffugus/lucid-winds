HOW TO WORK (Stephen's standing order; it binds this pack)
- No tests. No checks. No validation scripts, no pixel audits, no scripts that prove your own files, no reports about
  reports. Your v1 zip carried a VALIDATION.json; this one carries none. The art is the deliverable. Nobody reads a
  test log.
- Look at each sheet ONCE with your own eyes when it is done, fix what is wrong, move on. Never render again a thing
  that already reads.
- One assessment, written once. One contact sheet per section. One zip. No progress notes, no plans about plans.
- If something is ambiguous, pick the reading that ships and write one line about it in NOTES.md. Do not stop to ask.

WHAT DID NOT FIT, AND WHY (each one is a row in section B)
1. The aim line. aim_line.svg is a fixed 400 px arrow; the real aim runs from 40 to 420 px at any angle and is drawn
   as a stretched strip, so the arrow could not follow it. The game still draws its own brass gradient.
2. The snap trail. No moment in the game draws a trail yet. Skip it unless you see where it belongs.
3. The player chip. player_chip.svg bakes seven grey dots at fixed places; the game draws seven 16 px pips of yours
   that never land on them, so only the frame could be used, and its edges were sliced around the dots.
4. The tier frames. Each tier frame carries its mark in the top edge; a frame has to stretch to a 96 px square tile
   and to a header pill, and a nine slice squashes the mark. The frames went on the tiles with the mark distorted.
5. The level up plate. level_up.png is a 3 by 2 card with a sun over two rules; the only level up moment today is one
   line on the result card, and the card squashed onto that line put the sun inside the words. It was taken off.
6. The pot cloth. pot_cloth's skewed shape cannot hold the ante block cleanly: two strips of 48 px stakes, a line of
   words between them, about 335 by 240 CSS px on a 375 px phone.
7. The heirloom cloth. Its three resting places a quarter of the plate apart, at your 62 px radius, give each live
   render about 46 CSS px on a 375 px phone. The marble the player is choosing is too small to choose.
8. The ground shadow. Under a marble on your dark cloth (and the shelf, and the pouch) the shadow does not show.
9. The chalk ring. One 1024 decal stretched across a three metre ring is about 3 mm a texel: from the brace camera
   the far arc is crisp and the near arc, a metre from the lens, is a broad cloudy smear.
10. The panorama. 1600 px for 360 degrees: magnified as the skyline band and at the lowest camera it goes soft.
11. The pouch reveal. The open pouch's mouth is 109 of 640 px tall, so the drawn marble inside it is small.
12. The clay bowl and the chores board. No screen names the clay pool or lists chores yet, so both plates wait.
13. The share cards. On the marble card the name crowds the window's lower edge; on the match card the band under
    the numbers is mostly empty.

THE STATUES TODAY (exact; the physics is the truth, so draw to it)
- A Ringer house rule, off by default. Each player places TWO, alternating, before the lag. On your own turn you may
  move ONE of your statues up to two feet instead of shooting; that spends the turn. Dusty places and moves his by his
  own rule (he moves his own when it blocks his best shot).
- Where: anywhere on the dirt inside the ring, but never within one foot of any mib of the lay or of the ring's
  line, and clear of the other statues. Spots snap to a half foot grid. A moved statue may never touch a marble.
- The four roles (sizes in millimetres; marbles are 16 mm, shooters 22 mm; the ring is ten foot, three metres):
    COLUMN, tall and narrow, a glancing hit deflects: a base cylinder radius 18, 8 tall, and a shaft radius 12, 52
      tall, 60 in all. Footprint radius 20.
    LOAF, wide and low, it blocks: a box 90 wide, 34 tall, 52 deep. Footprint radius 52.
    ARCH, a marble can be shot under it: two legs 16 wide, 48 tall, 24 deep, centred 28 either side of the middle,
      and a lintel 72 wide, 12 tall, 24 deep laid on top, 60 in all; the opening is 40 wide by 48 tall, through the
      depth. Footprint radius 38.
    ORB, round, a hit scatters: a plinth radius 16, 10 tall, and a ball radius 22 centred 32 up, 54 in all.
      Footprint radius 22.
  You may propose other envelopes for each role (give the millimetres and we rebuild the colliders), but a role keeps
  its job: one deflects, one blocks, one lets a marble through, one scatters.
- The two views a statue lives in (statues/ and front-door/statues/ in the materials):
    the playing view: side on, 30 degrees down, two to five metres away, where a 60 mm statue is about 15 to 25 CSS
      px tall on a 375 by 667 phone;
    the placement view: straight down on the whole ring above the tray, where a footprint is about 6 to 17 px across.
  Silhouette first, then surface.
- The input (Stephen: the controls must be impeccable): a finger drags a shape from its 48 px tile in a tray at the
  bottom; the statue follows the finger on the dirt, snapped, its chalk footprint whole where it may stand and broken,
  with the words Not here, where it may not; it lands where the finger lifts; Confirm keeps it, Undo takes it back;
  nothing is placed by a tap. A move is the same drag on one of your own statues on the dirt.
- What is wrong with them today (our read; find the rest): grey boxes and cylinders with a chalk ring; owner told
  apart only by a slightly darker stone (colour alone, against the style guide); the tray tiles are words with no
  drawing; from straight above every statue is a grey chip; the spot a moved statue left is not marked.

THE HARD LINES (round 1's still bind; these are the ones this round touches)
- Procedural by law: never paint a marble, never draw a marble where a real render goes.
- What you draw for a statue must fit its collider. A Meshy reference must keep the Arch's opening open and every
  part at least 12 mm thick.
- Never by colour alone: tier, owner, legal and illegal each carry a mark or a word too.
- Formats as round 1: SVG for icons and marks (24 unit viewBox, 1.8 unit rounded strokes, one currentColor ink, read
  at 24 and 48 px); raster UI art as PNG at 2x with transparent backgrounds where it floats; plates and boards as WebP
  or JPG; textures 1024 at most (except where a row names a size); nothing over 1600 px a side except the store and
  the rows that name a larger strip; names lowercase_with_underscores.
- One light, as your style guide set it. One hand, portrait, 375 px wide must read; every target 48 px.
- Copy: spare and wry; no exclamation marks; no dashes of any kind; no text inside plates except a wordmark and what a
  row asks for.
