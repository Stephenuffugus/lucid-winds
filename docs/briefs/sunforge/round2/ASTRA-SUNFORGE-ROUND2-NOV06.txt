the blocks at all. it also doesn't move very well." The second half is ours and is done in code (a Tetris lock delay,

WHAT IS WRONG WITH THE BLOCKS TODAY
Play it at https://lucidwinds.com/satellites/ring-stacker/ first. The board is a grid of square cells. A locked cell is
drawn as your enamel face in the colour of the RING it sits on (its distance from the sun, twelve ring colours), with a
seam and a weld trim on every side whose neighbour is not the same ring. The falling piece is one ceramic cell repeated
four times. At 26 CSS px a cell on his phone the result reads as flat coloured tiles with a border: a 2015 puzzle app.
Nothing about them says forge, sun, heat, metal or danger. The rings, which are the whole idea (a band of one colour
flowing inward when a ring breaks), do not read as rings; they read as a colour chart.

WHAT WE WANT INSTEAD
A cell language that is unmistakably Sunbreaker and reads at 26 px. Three directions, one contact sheet each showing
the REAL board (a seven ring gyre half built, one piece falling, the ghost bracket, the almost ring lit) at phone size,
then your pick built out. Directions to consider, or better ones of your own:
1. Forged slugs. Every cell a hammered metal slug: a bevelled face with a hot seam, the ring colour as the heat of the
   metal (gold white at the core out to cold slate at ring twelve), welds as actual weld beads between unlike rings.
2. Obsidian shards. Dark glass cells with a lit crack running through them in the ring colour; the sun's light inside
   the stone; a ring that is about to break glows along its cracks.
3. Ember tiles. Scorched ceramic with a glowing edge, the colour on the rim and not the face, so a ring reads as a lit
   outline around the sun.
Whichever wins, the rule is: a RING must read as one band at a glance, the FALLING piece must read as not yet part of
the structure, and the picture must still be one thing with your v1 plates and HUD.

THE SHEETS (the formats the game already loads; keep them so the wiring is a swap)
A. The ring cell face, 64 px, twelve ring colours (one row), plus the seam. Same cut as v1's cell sheet.
B. The weld trims: the side trim and the inner corner trim, 64 px, as v1 (the trim rotates, the face never does).
C. The falling cell, 64 px, and a RESTING variant: the game now holds a landed piece for half a second before it sticks

D. The ghost bracket, as v1, in the new language.
E. The core's four jaw states (idle, charged, crushing, spent), 128 px, as v1, re cut only if the cells demand it.
F. The almost ring's notch and the lit seam, the stick flash (six frames), the ring crush burst (six frames), the void

G. One How to play plate: a finger dragging the piece sideways across the board (the new drag gesture) and a tap that
   turns it. Same plate size as your four How plates.
H. The arcade thumb and the social card redrawn with the new board, since both show cells.

than 26 px to read.
