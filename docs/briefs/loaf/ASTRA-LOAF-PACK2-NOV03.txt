Your pack 1 is in the app. Milestone A ("That's her") is built and live on lucidwinds.com/loaf.html behind the dev gate:
the room now has your floor, wall and window in all three lights; two cats in one photo ask "Tap the cat to meet.";

The screens in this zip are the real app tonight. What the build taught us is below, and it decides what we ask for.

WHAT THE BUILD TAUGHT US
- Likeness is won in small marks: a blaze, two white forefeet, narrowed eyes, a muzzle line. Two tuxedos looked
  alike until the stranger's blaze was found. The owner fixes marks with text chips today; icons would read faster.
- Every Loaf cat still wears ONE head shape (big, round, big ears). Barthalomew is a broad tom; Oreo is narrower.
  The 3D head needs morph targets drawn from real face types.
- The coat painter guesses where real patterns sit. A tabby's bars, a classic's bullseye, a calico's patches and
  each white-spotting grade need true body maps to check against.
- The photo decides everything downstream. People will shoot backlit, far away and from above.

THE HARD LINES (unchanged; they bind the art)
Comfort first: nothing dies, sickens, runs away or is punished; a low need is amber "you could", never red.
Coat, never breed. Chonk never reads as body condition. Days together only count up. The owner's photo is the hero
of the card. No fixed mascot cat: silhouettes and neutral reference bodies are welcome, a finished character is not.

THE ASKS, IN ORDER (send what you finish; A to C unblock the next two milestones)

A. THE BEANS, TINTABLE (the Bean Press toy).
   Rebuild the three paw plates as separate layers so the app can colour them to each cat: fur (neutral mid grey,
   to be tinted to her coat), pads (neutral, tinted to her bean colour), shading (multiply), line art and
   highlights. Keep the 154 x 152 logical size and the pad centres exactly (the hit targets are coded to them):
   toes (32,53) (66,35) (101,38) (126,58), centre (79,90). Front left, front right (mirrored), and one "toes
   splayed" variant of each for the press. Add a chocolate bean swatch beside pink, black and mixed. PNG at 3x,
   transparent, straight alpha.

B. MARKING ICONS FOR "DOES THIS LOOK LIKE HER?".
   Your stat icon grammar: 24 unit grid, two main values, stroke 1.6 to 1.8, round joins, readable at 24 and 48.
   Narrowed eyes; round eyes; forehead lines (the tabby fork); pale eye rims; white chin; white muzzle; white
   collar; white blaze; one paw drawn for left and right (mirrored), in three heights: toes, sock, boot; an iris
   glyph and a nose glyph the app tints to the chosen colour. SVG, one file each.

C. THE PHOTO GUIDE (the first scan).
   1. A camera framing overlay: a soft outline of a sitting cat's head and shoulders with an "eyes about here"
      line, portrait 9:16, thin enough to read over any photo. SVG.
   2. Three small tips in the field card style: "Daylight from the side, not behind her", "The whole cat, close
      enough to fill the frame", "At her eye level". Neutral silhouettes only. PNG 3x or SVG.
   3. Optional: a "two cats in one photo" illustration for the chooser's help line.

D. FACE SHAPE REFERENCE (for the 3D head morphs).
   Eight real face types as clean line drawings, front and three quarter, same grid and scale: broad tomcat (wide
   cheeks), round domestic, wedge (long and narrow), kitten (big eyes, short muzzle), long muzzle, short muzzle,
   big low set ears, small high set ears. Mark what differs: eye spacing, ear base width and set, cheek width,
   muzzle length, chin depth. Reference only: PNG and PDF.

E. COAT MAPS (to check the coat painter against).
   One neutral standing cat body seen from the side, the top and the front, flat two tone (no shading), the same
   views for every map: mackerel (spine stripe, rib bars, leg bars, tail rings, necklaces, forehead M, cheek
   lines); classic (bullseye, butterfly, swirl); spotted; ticked; tortie; calico; colourpoint; and white spotting
   by grade (bib, socks, tuxedo, bicolour, harlequin, van) with the common blaze shapes. PNG and PDF.

F. THE POND FROM ABOVE (the fishing game).
   Four top down fish shadow silhouettes (soft and dark, they slide under your water texture), three lily pads,
   and a near bank edge strip that tiles left to right, so the pond has a shore she sits on. PNG 3x, transparent.

G. STORE SCREENSHOT TEMPLATE (Play listing).
   A 1080 x 1920 portrait frame with a headline band in Bricolage on ink with amber, six layout variants; we drop
   real app captures in. One 1024 x 500 feature graphic variant with a window for a real photo beside her 3D self.

H. THE DATED MEMORY (the card back).
   A small "filed" stamp and a date tab for the one dated memory the card back opens on, in the reverse's style.
