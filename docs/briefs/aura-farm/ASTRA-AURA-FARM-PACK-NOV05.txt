AURA FARM, THE WHOLE PACK FOR ASTRA (5 Nov 2026)

Stephen: "I want one for our Aura Farm game. Look at the aesthetics of people talking aura, and what they are doing and
what they look like, and then we will build like that."

This pack has a research step in front of it. The game is called Aura Farm and today it means aura in the mystical
sense: a glowing emotional energy you harvest from strangers in a park. The internet means something else by aura now,
and that meaning has a look, a vocabulary and a scene of its own. Stephen wants the game to look like THAT.
Your jobs:
  1. STUDY the culture (our dossier and pointers are below; verify them, extend them, pick the look).
  2. ASSESS the game and say how its systems map onto that culture without losing what works.
  3. PRODUCE the art.
Fable wires your zip into the game with Stephen from the zip alone, so write as if no one can ask you a follow up.

HOW TO WORK (Stephen's standing order; it binds this pack)
- No tests. No checks. No validation scripts, no pixel audits, no scripts that prove your own files, no reports about
  reports. The last rounds of work on other games spent most of their time on tests that proved nothing, and he pays
  for every minute of it. The art is the deliverable. Nobody reads a test log.
- Look at each sheet ONCE with your own eyes when it is done, fix what is wrong, move on. Never re-render a thing that
  already reads.
- One assessment, written once. One contact sheet per section. One zip. No progress notes, no plans about plans.
- If something is ambiguous, pick the reading that ships and write one line about it in NOTES.md. Do not stop to ask.

WHAT AURA FARM IS (the game as built; its systems are good and they stay)
A fourteen day harvest sim, one HTML file, canvas 2D, portrait. People in a venue give off aura, which the game calls
emotional energy, and you harvest it. Tap a person; hype them up (Radiance: lift them toward joy; slower, but they
recover and can be farmed again) or put them down (Blight: grind them toward misery; pays 1.6x and spikes higher but
it is permanent; they become a husk, and husks spread gloom to everyone nearby). A ring around the person pulses
faster as the feeling builds; at high charge a peak window opens; harvest inside it for the best specimen; too early
is a weak specimen forever, too late and it vents to nothing. Timing is the skill. Six essences by where you steered
the feeling: Hope and Joy on the lift side, Sorrow and Rage on the sour side, rare Awe at the very top and rare Dread
at the very bottom. Five quality tiers: Faint, Common, Vivid, Brilliant, Transcendent. Meet the day's quota by dusk or
the farm withers.
Around that: the Broker posts three contracts each morning (deliver essences, land clean captures, chain combos; the
hard ones pay in relics, permanent powers kept across runs: Aura Prism, Cracked Hourglass, Peal Bell, Velvet Locket,
Dawn Lantern, Thorn Crown, Broker's Quill, Gray Mirror). Mara the Gray arrives on day three as the Broker's other
harvester; she siphons whatever you leave ripening, you tap her to flare your aura and drive her off; from day six she
snipes peak windows and from day ten she tries a deep drain. Letters arrive each morning (the Broker reacts to how you
farm, the husked leave notes, Mara slips bargains under the door: tributes, joint hunts, husk trades). Husks can be
gleaned for one last handful of ash. The Specimen Case keeps one peak aura per person across every run: 68 people in
22 groups (The House Regulars, The Birthday Crew and twenty more), with learnable traits (Sunny, Spirally, Guarded,
Volatile, Steady, Bottled Up, Open Book, Empath, Loner, Resilient, Deep Well, Fragile). Weather (Clear Skies, Golden
Morning, Grey Drizzle, Static Air, Restless Winds) and fourteen venue events (Marathon Day, Birthday Night, Quarter
Close, Full Moon Crowd, The Wake, The Vows, Meteor Shower, Bad News and more). Six venues unlocked by pushing each to
its bloom threshold: Dawnlight Park, The Neon Cellar, Grindstone Offices, The Night Market, The Velvet Chapel, Skyline
Rooftop. A shop. Ten milestones. A standing with the trade that the game reads back to you (Reaper, Gray Broker,
Luminary) and that prices Mara's siphon, seeds your wallet and leans the contract board. Survive fourteen days and the
Endless Dusk opens, with quotas rising until you fall. Endings (The Farm Withers, The Night Shepherd, The Rain
Collector and others; list them from the game). Sunbeams, the arcade's currency, pay out.
It lives at https://lucidwinds.com/satellites/aura-farm/ in the Sky Wolf Studio arcade, open to players, 164 KB. The
people are simple canvas figures drawn by code; the venues are a procedural sky with a day cycle, stars, a sun and
three clouds; the UI is a violet black void with a gradient wordmark, a violet slab button, a 700 word rules wall and
168 emoji doing the work of icons (the six essences are emoji; the tiers are emoji; a lightning bolt is painted over a
charged person). There is no image anywhere in the game.
A sibling game, Aura Off (a one on one aura battle with a posed rig; dev gated, not public), was built from the same
research. The two should feel like cousins, not twins; flag anything in your look that Aura Off should share.

THE CULTURE (start here; our dossier is at https://lucidwinds.com/satellites/aura-off/docs/AURA-BIBLE.md and
https://lucidwinds.com/satellites/aura-off/docs/AURA-CULTURE.md, compiled August 2026 with evidence tiers, and both are
also in this Drive folder; read both before you draw)
What aura means now: perceived coolness, presence, composure, charisma. Explicitly not spiritual. Aura farming is
cultivating effortless cool through repeated cool actions; the word farming is gamer slang; the scene scores it in
imaginary points: +1000 aura, +10.000 aura, aura infinita for the top prize, perdió aura (lost aura) for a public
failure. Clip farming is the opposite: doing something obvious for the camera.
What it looks like, the recurring grammar across the edits, the AI portrait prompts and the street battles:
- Stillness over action. The deadpan face. Sunglasses. A slow walk with relaxed shoulders. Eye contact held too long.
  Presence is the content; movement is minimal and unhurried.
- The edit: slow motion, cinematic letterbox bars, high contrast black and white or desaturated with one hot colour,
  film grain, lens flare, chromatic fringing, a phone camera softness, bold condensed white type with a black shadow
  (the +1000 AURA callout), phonk and Brazilian funk under it (we synthesise sound; describe the beat, never a track).
- The aura drawn: a glowing outline or halo around a person, purple, gold or white; anime energy auras; the sigma
  stare; the walk toward camera; sparkles and light leaks.
- The street battles (Latin America, August 2026: three in one weekend in Mexico City, 200 entrant brackets in
  Brazil): two people face off in a plaza, no speaking, no contact, poses and gestures only, a crowd ring filming on
  phones, sometimes a judge table, the winner by cheers and laughter. Documented moves: six seven (palms up, see
  saw), mewing (the hush finger slid along the jaw), the sigma stare (arms crossed), the aura walk, the theatrical
  collapse, the closing grimace, the boat dance (serene arms, working legs). Fit counts as an opening move: red
  racing shorts and clogs, a frog suit, a giant rubber clog as a prop.
- The origin: an eleven year old boat dancer on the prow of a forty metre racing canoe in Riau, Indonesia, in a
  traditional outfit, headcloth and sunglasses, calm hands, body waves. The tradition is centuries old and public. The
  boy is a real child and a provincial ambassador: build the tradition and the silhouette of the role, never the boy.
- The register of the whole scene is ironic and warm: a kid who has somewhere to go on a Tuesday. Not fame. Not
  contempt.
Verify this against what you can see today (the trend moves fast; our dossier is from August), then write the look.

THE LINES WE DO NOT CROSS (from the dossier; they bind the art)
- No real person, no minor's likeness, no recognisable copy of any named competitor or the boat dancer.
- No brands (the racing shorts are a film character, the clogs are a brand, the music is a recording): draw the idea,

THE MAPPING (the heart of the assessment; propose, do not assume)
The systems stay; the skin changes. Propose, in a table, what each thing in the game becomes in the aura culture
register, and draw the ones that change the most:
- The people (68 of them in 22 groups, with traits): a crowd at a venue; hype gives them aura and they get cooler; the
  essences are what kind of aura they threw off (Joy, Hope, Awe on the bright side; Sorrow, Rage, Dread on the dark).
- Blight and husks, the dark path. Options to weigh: the husk is someone who lost all aura and became an NPC (grey,
  blank, standing in the background); Blight reads as making someone clip farm, cringe and lose the room, not as
  mocking them; the gloom they spread is the cringe spreading. Propose the reading that keeps Blight a real choice
  without crossing the humiliation line.
- The peak window: the moment the crowd would scream. The harvest: the clip. The Specimen Case: your clips, your best
  capture of each person at their peak.
- The Broker: the owner of the edits page who posts the daily trends (the contracts). The letters: comments and DMs.
- Mara the Gray: the rival clip farmer who steals your moments; the deep drain is her ruining someone for a clip.
- The venues (Dawnlight Park, The Neon Cellar, Grindstone Offices, The Night Market, The Velvet Chapel, Skyline
  Rooftop), each as a place where this scene would actually happen (the plaza, the club line, the office floor, the
  market, the chapel steps, the rooftop at night), with a day cycle.
- The scoring: +1000 AURA, +10.000 AURA, AURA INFINITA, PERDIÓ AURA, NPC as the callouts (the exact strings the
  dossier documents; the game's own numbers stay underneath).
- The standing (Reaper, Gray Broker, Luminary) and the relics, in this register.
- The quota and the day: the venue's night; the dusk report as the recap; the morning letter as the feed.
Say what should NOT change (the timing skill, the Radiance and Blight economy, the fourteen days, the case).
