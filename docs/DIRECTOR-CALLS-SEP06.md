## B. Rules that re grade everything a player has done (decide before anyone keeps a record)

4. **Inkswing: equal temperament or just intonation.** The sliders snap to piano notes, so a
   "fifth" is 2^(7/12) and no drawing ever closes. Just intonation closes every named interval
   and makes the drawings finite and symmetric; the note names drift slightly off a piano. My
   call: just intonation by default, equal temperament behind Settings. Changes every drawing.
5. **Gerplunk: the daily line.** Everyone throws the same line (five results compare cleanly) or
   everyone picks their own (the wind and the three faces are part of the puzzle). My call: let
   them pick; forcing the line deletes the only thing aim is for. Built that way tonight.
6. **Gerplunk: the dead band.** A release under 758 px/s is a set down, not a weak throw, which
   deletes the one to six skip band. My call: keep it; it makes changing your mind free.
7. **Gerplunk: `TURN_DEG_PER_M` 480.** 197 px of thumb covers the whole aim axis. Only your thumb
   can settle it: too fast to sit on a line, drop toward 320; needs a re grip to reach the spit,
   raise toward 640.
