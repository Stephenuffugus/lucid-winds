# THE ONE LIST: Director calls left open across the twelve, Sep 06 2026

Written by Fable at the end of the Sep 06 build. Everything below is a choice only Stephen can
make; nothing on it blocks playing any of the twelve tonight. Ordered by what changes the most
for a player, then by cost. Each line says what I would do if the call were mine.

## A. Listen to these first (nobody has heard them)

1. **Windup, `satellites/windup/docs/shots/p0-tine.wav`.** One middle C, the C above, seven notes
   of Twinkle. The design says one note has to sound like a memory. If it does not, the tine
   synth is the thing to tune, not the box.
2. **Swell, `satellites/swell/docs/shots/p0-swell.wav`.** Dawn, one finger, six seconds. Four
   numbers wait on your ear: the string brightness at the top of the sweep, the timpani at the
   cadence (the spike at ten seconds, the loudest thing in the file), the hall at 0.28 wet, and
   whether six seconds of hold is enough of an arc. Storm and Lullaby have never been rendered.
3. **Strata's clink and Gerplunk's ticks** are synthesised and unheard. The clink is the heartbeat
   moment of Strata.

## B. Rules that re grade everything a player has done (decide before anyone keeps a record)

4. **Inkswing: equal temperament or just intonation.** The sliders snap to piano notes, so a
   "fifth" is 2^(7/12) and no drawing ever closes. Just intonation closes every named interval
   and makes the drawings finite and symmetric; the note names drift slightly off a piano. My
   call: just intonation by default, equal temperament behind Settings. Changes every drawing.
5. **Gerplunk: the daily line.** Everyone throws the same line (five results compare cleanly) or
   everyone picks their own (the wind and the three faces are part of the puzzle). My call: let
   them pick; forcing the line deletes the only thing aim is for. Built that way tonight.
6. **Gerplunk: the dead band.** A release under 758 px/s is a set down, not a weak throw, which
   deletes the one to six skip band. My call: keep it; it makes changing your mind free.
7. **Gerplunk: `TURN_DEG_PER_M` 480.** 197 px of thumb covers the whole aim axis. Only your thumb
   can settle it: too fast to sit on a line, drop toward 320; needs a re grip to reach the spit,
   raise toward 640.

## C. Feel calls (play it on the Pixel, then say yes or no)

8. **Airworthy: the challenges take the throw off you.** Fold and trim are yours, the arm is the
   challenge's; it is what makes six challenges ask for six planes. The free gym keeps the sling.
9. **Airworthy: the starting plane porpoises** and needs most of the elevator slider to settle. It
   is the opening lesson working; a smaller airframe bias would make it two nudges instead.
10. **Airworthy: canyon-hang.** The best plane hangs 10.9 s on the first ridge; the builder
    thinks that may be dull to watch. Shorten the first ridge if it is.
11. **Windup: Happy Birthday opens on two of the same note** and a real box cannot play those an
    eighth apart, so the starter is punched a quarter apart. If the pickup reads wrong, the rule
    moves, not the song.
12. **Whistlestop: fit the whole railway or let the player pinch in.** Fitting keeps the puzzle
    visible and makes it small on a tall phone. The builder's tall phone fit (Sep 06 pm) is the
    middle ground; if it still reads small, pinch and pan is one switch.
13. **Whistlestop: pass through instead of bump stop for younger children.** One branch in the
    collision rule; nothing in the sim fights it.
14. **Wardian: the quiet first fortnight.** A player who never opens the pouch sees moss move and
    nothing else for ten days. Tonight the jar says once when the pouch can plant; whether the
    ladder itself should move is yours. Also: humidity cannot separate a cared for jar from a
    neglected one, because a sealed jar is damp by design.
15. **Strata: the fifty bone crate.** Most people will press ALL every time, which makes the drag
    a decoration. Group the tray by kind, or ask only for the ten bones that matter.
16. **Fathom: the world scale.** Fifteen tiles across makes every corner a big right angle and a
    cave reads as a maze. Halving the tile makes caves look like caves and doubles the face
    count. Occlusion is built now, which was the other half of that complaint.
