# The newsletter and the Discord, set up in twenty minutes (Fable, Sep 06 2026)

Two accounts only Stephen can make. Each is ten minutes on a laptop. Everything after the
accounts exist (the welcome email, the channel list, the links on every title screen and on the
portal) is Fable's and is written below, ready to wire the moment the two URLs exist.

## 1. The newsletter (Buttondown, free tier, one email per launch)

1. buttondown.com, Sign up, with the studio address (stephen@skywolfstudio.com), name it
   "Sky Wolf Studio".
2. Settings, Basics: from name "Stephen at Sky Wolf Studio", description "One email when a
   game ships. Nothing else." Reply to: the studio address.
3. Settings, Subscribing: turn on the double opt in. Leave everything else default.
4. Copy the subscribe page URL (it looks like buttondown.com/skywolfstudio) and send it to Fable.

The welcome email (paste as the automated welcome, Settings, Emails):
```
Subject: You are on the list

Thanks for following Sky Wolf Studio. One email when a game ships, nothing between. The games
are free to play in the browser at lucidwinds.com, and on Steam and Google Play when you want
one on your own machine. Reply to this if a game breaks or if one of them made your day. I
read every one.

Stephen
```

## 2. The Discord (free, one server, five channels)

1. discord.com, add a server, "Create My Own", "For a club or community", name "Sky Wolf Studio".
2. Channels, in this order and no more: welcome (read only, the rules below), announcements
   (read only), games (talk), bugs (talk), clips (talk, images and video on).
3. Server settings, Invites: make a permanent invite link (never expires, unlimited) and send
   it to Fable.
4. Moderation: verification level "Medium", explicit content filter for everyone.

The welcome post (paste in welcome):
```
Sky Wolf Studio makes small games, free in the browser at lucidwinds.com. This is the place
to say a game broke, a game was fun, or a level is unfair. Three rules: be kind, no spam, no
politics beyond the games themselves. Bugs go in bugs with the game's name and your phone.
```

## 3. What Fable wires once the two URLs exist (no action from Stephen)

- A FOLLOW line on the portal's front page and footer: the newsletter and the Discord, two
  plain links, no slabs over art.
- The same two links on the title screen of every flagship (Jimothy, Flock, then the third),
  under the studio line, 0.7 rem or larger, 48 px targets, outside the chip's corner.
- The Play listing and the Steam page carry the Discord link in the support field.
- The launch kits' posts end with the newsletter link instead of nothing.
