## 3. Finding ten more yourself, twenty minutes

The method that works: pick three games Jimothy is like, search YouTube for each by name, skip the
big channels at the top, and take the creators between five thousand and a quarter million
subscribers whose last video is under two months old. For Jimothy the three names are Crossy Road,
Shooty Skies and Frogger. A creator who covered one of those this summer is a better bet than a
bigger channel that never has. Keep a sheet: channel, size, email, date sent, reply.

## 4. Two free places that hand keys out for you

- Gamosy KeyVault: free for key campaigns of any size; you post the game, verified creators apply,
  you approve. Worth setting up once, it runs while you sleep.
- Keymailer: free tier, ten creator requests a month. Same idea, older, smaller free allowance.

## 5. What to expect, honestly

Well aimed campaigns land single digit videos per hundred emails. Ten emails is one video if you
are lucky, which is why the curators and the Reddit posts in the kit matter as much as the emails,
and why the free key platforms are worth the setup. Every video counts more than it looks: a
3,000 subscriber channel that plays ten minutes reaches more people than any post you can make.

Store page: https://store.steampowered.com/app/5043360/

Do not ask for a wishlist mention, a link or a Discord shoutout; that reads as paid promotion.
The key goes in the first email. One email per creator, never a second unless they reply.
