READ THIS FIRST (Stephen, about a minute)

What the game is: Ripcord is the spinning top game. You build a top, wind it by drawing circles, launch it, and it fights another top in a round dish seen from directly above. The tops, the markings on the dish, the score and every button are drawn by the code. Today the space around the dish is flat dark brown, and every league fights on one of four dish floors. These pictures give every league its own real place.

Concept line: every league is a real place where a ring could be drawn, seen from the ceiling, lit low and warm, with the fight in the middle.

What is in here: 3 pastes, 19 pictures.
Paste 1: the 8 worlds, the ground around the dish. This is the main ask. 8 pictures.
Paste 2: the 7 new dish floors, so each world has its own ring as well as its own surroundings. 7 pictures. Do this after Paste 1 is in the game and we have looked at it.
Paste 3: the workshop backdrop and the store art (key art, Play banner, app icon). 4 pictures.

The 8 worlds: The Chalk Yard (league 1), Market Lantern (league 2), Riverside Jetty (league 3), The Barrel House (league 4), Kelantan Ground (league 5), The Giant's Quarry (the last boss), The Foundry (the endless Field mode), Tin Roof Rain (Pass the Phone).


THE STYLE IS YOUR CALL. Three options:

A. Golden hour film still (my pick). Looks like a real photograph taken from the ceiling: warm low light, real dirt, tin, oak, stone and chalk, a little film grain. Why I pick it: the four dish floors already in the game look exactly like this, so new pictures sit next to them with no seam, and it makes the game look expensive on the store page.

B. Tabletop diorama. Every place looks like a handmade miniature model, slightly toy like, soft focus toward the edges, small warm lights. Catchy and charming, and it still sits fairly well next to the dish floors already in the game.

C. Linocut fight poster. A bold carved print look, three or four flat inks on cream paper grain, heavy shadows. The most striking store page of the three, but it clashes with the realistic dish floors, so Paste 2 becomes required and the Chalk Yard floor gets redone too (8 floors instead of 7).

The pastes below have A built in. To use B or C, in every paste replace the line that starts with "Style for every picture:" with one of these:

B: Style for every picture: a handmade tabletop miniature diorama photographed from above, slightly toy like materials, soft focus toward the edges, small warm practical lights, rich but dark colours.

C: Style for every picture: a bold linocut print, three or four flat inks on cream paper with visible paper grain and carving marks, heavy black shadows, no gradients, no photographic detail.
