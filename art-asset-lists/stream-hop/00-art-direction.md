# Stream Hop — Art Pack

> Hop the midnight stream, weave a trail of lily-lanterns, bank a flower every crossing.

**Genre:** Endless arcade hopper (Frogger / Crossy Road lineage) with a native plant-collection meta — read the shifting hazard lanes, thread one clean hop at a time, and weave a Bloom Trail that mints a keepsake flower on every clean crossing.

_The game already ships and plays procedurally — this art is an optional visual upgrade **and** the cosmetics library that powers the in-game customization economy._

## Pick a look

### 1. Moonpond Storybook
*Cozy cut-paper gouache — the softest, most kid-first read. Rounded felt-like shapes, chunky lily pads, big friendly eyes, warm firefly bokeh, gentle grain like a bedtime picture book.*

Maximum warmth and approachability; a 5-year-old instantly trusts it. Downside: reads a touch flat next to the polished satellites in the portal, and fine hazards (acorn vs pod) can blur together at 60px, so it needs the colorblind shape-cues carrying more weight.

### 2. Lantern Ink
*Midnight-garden nocturne in clean vector-gouache: crisp rounded silhouettes, painterly interior shading, soft ambient occlusion, and a warm gold rim-light as if every object is lit from below by floating lily-lanterns. Cozy but premium.*

The sweet spot — reads native to the Lucid Winds midnight-garden world, gives Stream Hop its own lantern-lit identity, and stays unmistakably kid-friendly while looking a full tier more finished than the procedural placeholder. Silhouettes stay legible at gameplay scale; hazards separate by shape AND value.

### 3. Enamel Nocturne
*The most mature option: cloisonné / enamel-pin look — dark lacquer field, thin metallic-gold cell-linework, jewel-toned fills with a glossy specular pop. Feels like a collectible pin set of botanical critters.*

Gorgeous for the cosmetics/collection fantasy and screenshots beautifully. Risk: the metallic linework and gloss push slightly toward a teen/adult toy-collector vibe and add per-asset visual weight that can fight the fast arcade read at speed. Best reserved for high-tier / rare cosmetic unlocks rather than the whole game.

**Recommended: Lantern Ink. It nails the brief's three asks at once: it sits natively inside the Lucid Winds midnight-garden palette (deep blacks, sage water, gold lantern-blooms, cream firefly glow), it hands Stream Hop a distinct lantern-lit identity so it doesn't read as a Frogger port, and it stays firmly kid-and-parent safe while looking a clear tier above the current procedural draws. Its value-plus-shape silhouettes keep hazards legible at 60px gameplay scale (critical for the 'is the gap open NOW?' read and for colorblind cues), and its soft gold rim-light literally IS the Bloom-Trail lantern fiction, so the whole board feels like one lit diorama. Use Enamel Nocturne selectively for the rarest cosmetic unlocks (e.g. The Beholder hopper, cosmic trails) to give the top of the collection ladder extra shine..** Sheets here use this look; swap the STYLE line to try another.

## Cosmetics economy

All Stream Hop cosmetics are earned by PLAYING — no loot boxes, no randomized purchases, no pay-to-win, nothing that alters hazard difficulty or hitboxes. Everything is a KNOWN unlock at a KNOWN threshold, so a kid always sees exactly what they're working toward. THREE free mastery lanes: (1) Distance mastery — hopper skins unlock at rising best-endless-row marks, extending the shipped ladder (toadling 0, newtling 40, button bug 90, firefly 160, sprout 260) out across the 85-companion roster (e.g. tree frog ~120, koi ~350, raccoon ~500, pangolin ~750, heron ~1000, and The Beholder as a far-horizon capstone), turning the roster into the long-tail collection chase the brief calls for. (2) Bloom mastery — trail styles and auras unlock by lifetime Flowers Banked (e.g. rosepetal at 25, ember at 75, frost at 150, starfield aura at 300), so the reward loop that already mints plants also dresses up the trail that mints them. (3) Streak/season mastery — the four seasonal pad + lane + trail skins unlock and ROTATE with the real-world season and the Daily Current streak (7/30/100-day milestone blooms per the retention hook), giving lapsed-friendly reasons to return. Optional soft-coin: the portal's existing DEW (never Sunbeams — Sunbeams stay the earn/collection currency) can OPTIONALLY early-unlock the CURRENT season's rotating cosmetic a little ahead of the streak gate, or re-unlock a past-season skin out of rotation — purely convenience/cosmetic, capped to the rotating set, and every item is still fully earnable free by simply playing. The one 'playstyle' unlock (baby_mammoth — slower hop, wider coyote grace) is a neutral trade, earned by distance, never bought, so it never becomes power. Feeding the meta: banked Bloom Trails keep minting real one-of-one Greenhouse plants (the native collection source), Sunbeams follow the portal standard (30/day, cap 12/run), and the cosmetic catalog gives a second, non-gambling collection wall that rewards showing up daily over grinding one long session.
