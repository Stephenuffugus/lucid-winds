# JIMOTHY SHEET 14F — Ballard Locks — zone traffic

Ballard is the marine zone and has the highest water bias of the six, so its road traffic
should feel like the stuff that lives BESIDE water. Cell 6 is the funniest hazard in the
game and I would paint that one first.

## The nine cells

1. **Small sailboat on a trailer** — wide, slow, mast laid flat along the top.
2. **Sea lion** — wide, slow, lumping along. Enormous and unbothered.
3. **Lock gate crew with ropes** — medium, medium, three people hauling.
4. **Kayak rack truck** — very wide, medium, six kayaks stacked.
5. **Fish ladder inspector with a clipboard** — narrow, slow.
6. **A rowing eight carried by eight people** — the WIDEST hazard in the game, slow, the
   whole shell overhead. Nearly a full lane. Absurd and completely real.
7. **Marine crane** — medium, very slow, hook swinging.
8. **Channel buoy on a cart** — medium, medium, bell on top.
9. **A heron taking off** — wide wingspan, fast, low.

## Format
3 x 3 grid, **magenta #FF00FF** background, **white divider lines** between cells.
Side on, same camera and the same ground line as the existing vehicles, so they drop
straight into a lane. One sheet per image. Note a rough width per item ("about as wide as
a sedan" is fine) so the hitbox can be set honestly.
