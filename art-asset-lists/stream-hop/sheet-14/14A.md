# JIMOTHY SHEET 14A — The Waterfront — zone traffic

Right now every neighbourhood draws from the SAME obstacle pool, so six zones look
different and play identical. This sheet gives the Waterfront its own traffic. A player
should know where they are without reading the banner.

## The nine cells

1. **Harbour tour boat on a trailer** — wide, slow, top heavy. Big lane blocker.
2. **Luggage cart train** — three carts linked, very wide, slow. A wall you wait out.
3. **Gull mob, low flying** — wide, fast, low to the ground. The fast lane filler.
4. **Ferry foot passengers** — a clump of six people walking together, medium width, slow.
5. **Fish crate dolly** — narrow, quick, a stack of iced crates. The nippy one.
6. **Pedicab** — medium, medium. Reads friendly, still flattens you.
7. **Cruise shuttle bus** — very wide, slow, the biggest thing on the sheet.
8. **Stack of crab pots on a hand truck** — medium, slow, tall and lattice-y.
9. **A lost tourist holding a giant unfolded map** — narrow, very slow, the map is wider
   than they are. The joke is that the map is the hitbox.

## Format
3 x 3 grid, **magenta #FF00FF** background, **white divider lines** between cells.
Side on, same camera and the same ground line as the existing vehicles, so they drop
straight into a lane. One sheet per image. Note a rough width per item ("about as wide as
a sedan" is fine) so the hitbox can be set honestly.
