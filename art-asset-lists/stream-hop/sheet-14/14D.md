# JIMOTHY SHEET 14D — Capitol Hill — zone traffic

Capitol Hill at night. Warmer light, more people than vehicles, more chaos at ankle
height. This zone should feel busy without a single new background element.

## The nine cells

1. **E bike courier** — narrow, very fast, insulated food box on the back.
2. **Someone in heels crossing carefully** — narrow, slow, glorious, mid stride.
3. **Coffee delivery trike** — medium, medium, crates of cups.
4. **Nightclub queue crowd** — wide, very slow, a shuffling line.
5. **Cat on a leash refusing to move** — narrow, VERY slow. Nearly stationary.
6. **Record crate hand truck** — medium, medium.
7. **Bendy bus** — the longest vehicle in the game, slow, with a visible articulation joint.
8. **Rideshare car with the roof light on** — medium, fast, glowing sign.
9. **Person carrying a houseplant taller than they are** — narrow person, wide leaves.

## Format
3 x 3 grid, **magenta #FF00FF** background, **white divider lines** between cells.
Side on, same camera and the same ground line as the existing vehicles, so they drop
straight into a lane. One sheet per image. Note a rough width per item ("about as wide as
a sedan" is fine) so the hitbox can be set honestly.
