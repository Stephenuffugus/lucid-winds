# JIMOTHY SHEET 14E — Interbay — zone traffic

Interbay is heavy industry and it is where the LATE and BIG speed bands finally get some
company. Right now four vehicles and two big ones carry every level past 25. Everything on
this sheet is slow, wide and heavy, which is a completely different read from Capitol Hill
and is the point of doing zone traffic at all.

## The nine cells

1. **Forklift** — medium, medium, forks out front, a pallet on them.
2. **Shipping container on a truck** — VERY wide, slow, the heaviest thing in the game.
3. **Freight pallet stack** — wide, slow, shrink wrapped.
4. **Crane counterweight block** — medium, very slow, absurdly dense looking.
5. **Hard hat crew walking** — wide, medium, four workers abreast.
6. **Fuel bowser** — wide, slow, hazard stripes.
7. **Baggage trolley train** — very wide, medium, four trolleys linked.
8. **A boat on a low loader** — very wide, slow, hull overhanging both ends.
9. **Loose oil drums rolling** — three drums, medium spread, FAST and unpredictable looking.

## Format
3 x 3 grid, **magenta #FF00FF** background, **white divider lines** between cells.
Side on, same camera and the same ground line as the existing vehicles, so they drop
straight into a lane. One sheet per image. Note a rough width per item ("about as wide as
a sedan" is fine) so the hitbox can be set honestly.
