# JIMOTHY SHEET 14H — Water set pieces

The hard end of the water game has no vocabulary. There are thirteen pads and they are
all the same idea in a different hat: a thing you stand on. These give water lanes states
and consequences instead of just spacing.

## The nine cells

1. **Thin fast plank** — narrow, clearly risky at a glance. The pad you do NOT want.
2. **Sinking pad, mid sink** — half submerged, water pouring off. So the sink can be SEEN
   coming instead of just happening.
3. **Boat wake** — a moving band of white water. A hazard that lives in the water itself.
4. **Ferry gangway** — a ramp, for the start and end of a crossing. Makes a ferry a place.
5. **Mooring buoy** — a tiny pad. One paw of margin. Terrifying and fair.
6. **Log boom, two logs chained** — they move as ONE unit, so the chain must read.
7. **Eddy or whirlpool** — a slow rotating pull. Moving hazard, not an obstacle.
8. **Lock gate, closed** — full width, the water held back.
9. **Lock gate, open** — the same gate swung. Ballard's signature moment.

## Format
3 x 3 grid, **magenta #FF00FF** background, **white divider lines** between cells.
Side on, same camera and the same ground line as the existing vehicles, so they drop
straight into a lane. One sheet per image. Note a rough width per item ("about as wide as
a sedan" is fine) so the hitbox can be set honestly.
