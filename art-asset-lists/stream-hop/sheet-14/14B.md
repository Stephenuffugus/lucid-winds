# JIMOTHY SHEET 14B — Pike Market — zone traffic

Pike Market traffic. The flying salmon is the star of this sheet and probably of the
whole pack: a fish thrown across the lane is instantly readable, funny, and unlike
anything else in the game.

## The nine cells

1. **Flying salmon mid air** — wide, FAST, arcing. Draw it in flight with motion, not
   flopping. This is the signature hazard of the zone.
2. **Flower bucket trolley** — medium, slow, tall bright blooms. The one splash of colour.
3. **Produce hand truck** — medium, medium, crates of apples stacked high.
4. **Busker with a guitar case open** — narrow, stationary-looking but drifting slowly.
5. **Cheese wheel rolling loose** — narrow, fast, comic. Round and heavy.
6. **Newspaper stand on wheels** — medium, slow, papers fluttering.
7. **Tourist clump taking a photo** — wide, very slow, phones up, oblivious.
8. **Fishmonger carrying a tray overhead** — narrow, medium, tray wider than the person.
9. **Gum wall cleaning cart** — medium, slow, steam wand and buckets. Grimy and specific.

## Format
3 x 3 grid, **magenta #FF00FF** background, **white divider lines** between cells.
Side on, same camera and the same ground line as the existing vehicles, so they drop
straight into a lane. One sheet per image. Note a rough width per item ("about as wide as
a sedan" is fine) so the hitbox can be set honestly.
