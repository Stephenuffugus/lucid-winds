# Flock the World — Art Pack

> You are the surveillance vendor. Sign the contracts, own the story, manufacture the crisis, and put every innocent person on Earth inside your frame before the public notices.

**Genre:** Plague Inc-style satire strategy sim. **Tone:** PG-13 corporate satire. No gore, no real logos, no real people's faces, invented brands only. The player is explicitly the villain and the art should sell that the way a glossy vendor brochure would: cheerfully.

_The game ships and plays with zero images today; every asset here is new. Text stays engine-rendered: no words inside the art except the wordmark sheet._

## The game's palette (use these, they are the CSS variables)
void `#05070b` · deep `#0a1018` · panel `#101822` · line `#22303f` · dim `#5b7186` · ice `#7fa8c4` · paper `#dfe9f0` · **sodium `#ffa229`** (amber, the player's color) · **scan `#39e6c3`** (teal, data and resistance tech) · grn `#7ef0a6` · **blood `#ff3b30`** (danger only, use sparingly)

## Pick a look

### 1. Sodium Vapor
*Photoreal night city under sodium streetlights, teal scanlines, wet asphalt, brutalist concrete.* Maximum atmosphere and it screenshots like a film. Downside: photoreal generation is inconsistent across 46 tiny icons, and it reads grim rather than satirical; the joke is lost.

### 2. Procurement Brochure ← RECOMMENDED
*The surveillance vendor's own pitch deck: clean flat corporate-vector illustration, soft gradients, slightly isometric hardware, confident rounded shapes, stock-photo optimism.* Amber `#ffa229` and teal `#39e6c3` accents on the void/panel darks, paper highlights, blood red only for alarms. The satire lives in the contrast: a smiling brochure selling dystopia. Icons cut cleanly, stay consistent across 60+ cells, and read at 44 px on a phone. Dossier motifs (rubber stamps, redaction bars, paperclips) are allowed as PROPS inside this style.

### 3. Redacted Dossier
*Photocopier grain, manila folders, typewriter, black redaction bars, red stamps.* Perfect for the cast and the end screens, weak for 46 tree icons that must read at thumbnail size, and it fights the game's existing dark teal UI.

**Recommended: Procurement Brochure, everywhere.** One style across trees, UI, cast, and store so the whole game reads as one product. Use dossier props for the cast portraits and end screens rather than switching style.

## Technical rules for every sheet
- Flat magenta `#FF00FF` background fills every cell (knockout). No magenta, hot pink, or hot purple anywhere inside the artwork.
- One object per cell, centered, filling ~75% of the cell, bold silhouette, no drop shadow outside the object, no cell borders, no labels, no numbers.
- Icon cells 512x512. Cards 768x1024. Backdrops and store art are single images, generated one at a time.
- Same light direction on every sheet: key light upper-left, amber; rim light lower-right, teal.
