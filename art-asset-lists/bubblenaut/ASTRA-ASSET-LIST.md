IMAGE SPEC FOR EVERY SHEET
1. PNG.
2. The whole background is one flat solid magenta, FF00FF. No gradient, no texture, no shadow and no glow on the background.

ASSEMBLY NOTE: end your reply with a note for every sheet you made: file name; number of frames; frame order; suggested speed in frames per second; loops or plays once; the frame where the key moment happens (for example the bubble leaving the mouth, the landing, the pop); and where the feet sit (the ground line, for example 12 pixels above the bottom of each frame). The builder uses these notes to put the animation into the game. If you can, also give me one zip of all the sheets named by their file names.

THE ACTIONS

hero_idle: standing still on the ground, breathing, any loose parts bobbing softly. Loops. About 4 to 8 frames, your call.
hero_run: running along the ground (the game flips it for running left). Loops. About 6 to 8 frames.
hero_jump: crouch, push off and rise to the top of the jump. Plays once, the game holds the last frame. About 3 to 5 frames.
hero_fall: falling through the air, legs dangling, loose parts trailing up. Loops. About 2 to 4 frames.
hero_land: touching down with a squash, back to standing. Plays once. About 2 to 4 frames.
hero_blow: blowing a bubble forward. Do not draw the bubble, the game draws it; tell me the frame where it leaves the mouth. Plays once, quick (the button fires about every 0.4 seconds). About 4 to 6 frames.
hero_turn: turning around from facing right to facing left. Plays once. About 3 frames.
hero_hurt: bumped by a loose enemy and a life is lost: knocked back, dizzy, funny, nothing painful. Plays once. About 4 frames.
hero_die: the last life is lost: spins, goes dizzy and sinks down, lying still with eyes closed. Gentle and funny, nothing gory. Plays once. About 6 to 8 frames.
hero_respawn: appears in a swirl of sparkles at the start point and lands ready. Plays once. About 4 to 6 frames.
hero_cheer: a happy victory dance when a room is cleared (about 2 seconds). Loops. About 6 to 8 frames.
hero_wave (optional): facing the viewer and waving, for the title screen. Loops. About 4 to 6 frames.
