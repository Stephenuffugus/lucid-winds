# Cipher Bloom — Art Pack

> A hidden verse waits under scrambled letters — crack the cipher letter by letter, and the poem blooms into a keepsake flower pressed into your Gallery.

**Genre:** Cryptogram / substitution-cipher word puzzle (single-file HTML5, 540×960 portrait stage — but this game is **DOM-based, not canvas-drawn**: the play surface is a scrollable cream `#paper` panel of DOM letter-cells plus a DOM on-screen keyboard; the `#game` canvas only paints a static ambient gradient once, and small canvases draw the procedural keepsake flowers). Tap a scrambled letter to select it everywhere, tap the keyboard to assign its plaintext (fills every matching spot at once), read the **frequency bars** under each letter for codebreaking clues. Four modes: **Daily Cipher**, **Garden of Verses**, **Sun Race** (timed), **Zen Reading** (free hints, no Sunbeams). Hints cost 3 Dew; Check gently flags wrong guesses; there is no way to lose. Solving a verse **blooms a keepsake flower** (seeded from the poem, 6 palettes × 5-9 petals) into the Decoded Gallery — 34 botanical poems to collect.

_The game already ships and plays procedurally (`satellites/cipher-bloom/index.html`) — this art is an optional visual upgrade **and** the cosmetics library for the in-game customization economy. Mechanics, letter cells, keyboard layout, frequency bars and every threshold are IDENTICAL across every theme below; only the skin changes. Critical constraint for a word game: **every letter on screen is live DOM text** — the art supplies letterless plates, papers, stamps and blooms underneath it, so NOTHING in any sheet may contain text or letterforms._

## Pick a look — theme direction

⚖️ **Director note:** no forced-botanical house style — but this is the rare mechanic where the garden IS the content: the verses are botanical poems, the win moment literally mints a flower, and the cosmetics are papers and inks. So the botanical option here is native, not forced. A non-botanical direction is still offered below for an honest choice.

### 1. Pressed Herbarium ⭐ RECOMMENDED (cozy, native)
*A keeper's midnight field-journal. The puzzle sheet is a sun-faded pressed-paper page in a lantern-lit study; guesses are hand-inked strokes, given letters are wax-sealed, wrong guesses get a thorn stamp. Solved verses press their bloom flat into an herbarium Gallery with linen mounting tapes. Warm gold lantern light over deep moss-black; everything matte, papery, deckle-edged, quiet.*

**Why this one:** it is a 1:1 fit with what the code already renders — the play surface IS a cream paper panel (`#paper`, `#e8dcc8→#dccbb0`), the cosmetics ARE five papers and four inks (`PAPERS[]`/`INKS[]`), and the keepsake flowers already press into 74px gallery cards. The reskin rides the existing midnight-study palette (`#0d100c`/`#0e140d` screens, gold `#c8a84b`, sage `#7ab356`) so integration risk is near zero, and the wardrobe's real unlockables (Birch Bark, Pressed Petal, Vellum Gilt, Midnight Slate papers; Ironwood, Sage, Dew Blue, Rose inks) become genuinely covetable objects instead of CSS gradients. Cozy, contemplative, and exactly the speed of a poem-decoding game.

### 2. Gilded Scriptorium (alt, polished / more mature)
*A candlelit monastery archive: the verse on gilt-edged vellum, illuminated flourishes in the margins, brass instruments and deep wax seals, letters revealed like gold leaf being laid. Rich, premium, museum-quiet.* Gorgeous and grown-up while staying kid-friendly, and it flatters the existing gold/cream palette — but it is heavier per asset (gilt detail everywhere), reads slightly cold next to the game's warm garden verses, and the "illuminated manuscript" fantasy overlaps the Herbarium look without beating it. Best held as the flavor for the premium **Vellum Gilt** paper and the solve-moment gold burst.

### 3. Bureau of Lost Letters (alt, non-botanical)
*A cozy midnight decoder's bureau: the verse arrives as a mysterious letter on a case-file desk — brass magnifier, ink stamps, pinned string, typewriter-style keys; cracking the code stamps the case SOLVED and files a pressed flower found inside the envelope.* The full theme jump — detective, not garden — and tap-a-letter/stamp-a-guess maps neatly onto desk furniture. But the game's content fights it: all 34 verses are nature poems and the win moment mints a flower, so the fiction has to smuggle the garden back in anyway. Fun, but it decorates around the content instead of amplifying it.

**Recommendation: Pressed Herbarium.** The mechanic and content are already a botanical journal, so the native look wins on fit and integration cost; borrow Gilded Scriptorium's gold-leaf richness for the Vellum Gilt paper and the solve celebration, and keep Bureau of Lost Letters in reserve as a future paper-and-ink set (a "Case File" paper + "Typewriter" ink would slot straight into the existing wardrobe arrays). **All sheets below bake the Herbarium look in.**

## Cosmetics economy

Every Cipher Bloom cosmetic is earned by **PLAYING** — no loot boxes, no randomized purchases, nothing that changes a puzzle, a cipher, a letter cell or a payout. Everything is a **KNOWN unlock at a KNOWN threshold** shown right on its locked wardrobe card (`renderWard()` prints `c.desc` on locked cards). Two cosmetic families, both already wired in `localStorage` key **`cipherbloom_save`** (the `PROG` object):

- **Papers** (`PROG.back`, `PAPERS[]`) — the puzzle page itself, applied by `applyPaperTheme()`: *Linen* (starter, free) → **Birch Bark** (solve **8** verses) → **Pressed Petal** (solve **20** verses) → **Vellum Gilt** (**5 hintless** solves) → **Midnight Slate** (**7-day** Daily streak).
- **Inks** (`PROG.ink`, `INKS[]`) — the color your guesses are written in (sets `--ink`): *Ironwood* (starter, free) → **Sage** (solve **12**) → **Dew Blue** (solve **30**) → **Rose** (**8 hintless**).

Three earn faucets, all real fields in `PROG`: **`PROG.solved`** (every solved verse, any mode), **`PROG.hintless`** (only hint-free solves at Standard/Master — code guards `G.hintsUsed===0 && G.diff>0`, so Zen/Gentle can never farm it), and **`PROG.streak`** (consecutive Daily Cipher days; only the first Daily solve of a day counts). `PROG.owned{}` exists as a manual-grant escape hatch but the live check (`cosmUnlocked`) is threshold-only. A fourth collection layer is free: the **Decoded Gallery** (`PROG.blooms[]`, one keepsake per unique poem, 34 poems total) is itself the completable set the blooms sheet dresses up.

**Currency lanes stay locked:** Dew is the in-run hint currency only (hint = **3 Dew**, free in Zen; earned 4 + 2×difficulty + 3 hintless + 2 race per solve, flat 2 in Zen) — it buys NOTHING in the wardrobe, and no cosmetic ever costs Dew or Sunbeams. Sunbeams remain the portal earn (3 + difficulty + 2 hintless, race speed bonus, daily-streak bonus, hard-capped **12/run** in `onSolve` and **30/day** in `_sbCapEarn`; Zen pays 0 by design). Skins are purely visual — `applyPaperTheme()` repaints the page and ink and touches nothing else.
