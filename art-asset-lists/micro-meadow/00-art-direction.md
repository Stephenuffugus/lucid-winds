# Micro Meadow — Art Pack

> One word flashes — TAP! WATER! SHOO! — you do the thing in a blink, and the next tiny game is already here.

**Genre:** WarioWare-style microgame rush (single-file HTML5 canvas, 540×960 portrait; gameplay on canvas, menus / wardrobe / gallery / results are DOM overlay screens). **15 five-second microgames**, each a one-word prompt + one gesture: TAP! · WATER! · CATCH! · POP RIPE! · COUNT! · SWAT! · GROW! · DODGE! · HOLD! · FEED! · AIM! · MATCH! · SORT! · SHOO! · TAP NOW!. The prompt beat lasts ~0.62s, rounds start at 3.0–4.0s and shave **−0.22s per speed level** (speed steps up every 4 clears) down to a **1.9s floor**; every **10th round is a boss** drawn from the harder pool (SWAT / AIM / MATCH / TAP NOW, an extra −0.3s). You have **4 hearts** (∞ in Zen); every clean win fills the **Bloom Meter** 1/8, and a full meter grows a procedural **keepsake bloom** into the Gallery. Modes: **Meadow Rush** 🎪, **Daily Dash** 📅 (date-seeded), **Boss Bloom** 🌟 (boss-only bag), **Zen Meander** 🍃.

_The game already ships and plays procedurally (`satellites/micro-meadow/index.html`) — this art is an optional visual upgrade **and** the cosmetics library for the in-game customization economy. Prompts, gestures, hitboxes, timings and the symbol-label colorblind cues are IDENTICAL across every look below; only the skin changes._

## Pick a look — theme direction

The subjects are fixed by the engine (a tap-bug, a thirsty sprout, a koi, a crow, a thistle burr…), but the STYLE is free — and per the Director's standing note, no forced cozy-botanical house style. Three directions, cozy → polished, all kid-friendly:

### 1. Storybook Gouache (cozy — the quiet painterly option)
*A soft nocturnal picture-book meadow: gouache-washed critters with rounded paper edges, moonlit sage and cream, everything gentle and hand-made.* Warmest and most native to the Lucid Winds portal, and the safest fit for the game's sleepy copy ("the meadow rests"). But it is the lane we already run everywhere, and at blink-speed pacing a soft wash reads slower than a bold silhouette — this game needs pop more than hush.

### 2. Firefly Funfair ⭐ RECOMMENDED (cozy-bold — the meadow throws a tiny nighttime carnival)
*The meadow after dark, hosting a pocket-sized funfair: bunting strung between grass stalks, fireflies as festival lanterns, a faraway big-top glow — and every microgame is a sideshow booth act. Chunky, huggable, circus-poster-rounded critters lit like lanterns on the deep meadow-night field, each built to be read in half a second.*

**Why this one:** it is a genuine theme jump — carnival energy, not garden maintenance — that still keeps every word of the code-owned copy true (the fair is IN the meadow, so "the whole meadow blooms" and "the meadow rests" still land, and the portal's 🎪 glyph and the "Meadow Rush" mode name finally mean something). WarioWare's soul is a chaotic variety show; a funfair is that soul translated kid-friendly. It sits natively on the game's real dark palette (`#0b0f0b`/`#141d12` night field) so bright sprites pop with zero integration risk, the prompt word becomes a ringmaster's call on a marquee plaque, the three unlockable prompt FRAMES become marquee garlands, and the speed ramp reads as the show getting faster. **All sheets below bake this look in.**

### 3. Sticker Pop Arcade (alt, more mature — leaves the garden mood entirely)
*Thick-outline vinyl-sticker critters in flat saturated color slapped on a dark arcade field, comic energy-burst shapes, zero botanical softness — pure WarioWare pop.* The boldest stylistic departure and genuinely great for blink-reads, but hard black keylines fight the portal's midnight glow, and the flat-sticker attitude sits colder next to the game's warm resting copy. Hold it in reserve as a future unlockable theme direction.

**Recommendation: Firefly Funfair.** Biggest fictional lift at the lowest integration risk: it keeps the real dark palette and the meadow copy intact, gives the 🎪 identity a reason to exist, and its lantern-lit chunky silhouettes are the fastest half-second reads of the three. Borrow Storybook's gouache grain for warmth; keep Sticker Pop parked as a possible later skin.

## Cosmetics economy

Every Micro Meadow cosmetic is earned by **PLAYING** — no loot boxes, no randomized purchases, nothing that touches a microgame's hitboxes, timings or symbol cues. Every unlock is a **KNOWN threshold shown right on the locked wardrobe card**, live-computed from the save (`metric()` line ~202, `ownedC()` line ~203 — unlocks can never be lost). All fields already persist in `localStorage` key **`micromeadow_save`** (line ~186); equipped picks live in `PROG.theme` / `PROG.frame`, and `PROG.owned{}` allows an optional manual grant without touching earn logic. Two cosmetic families, three earn faucets:

- **Meadow themes** (`THEMES[]`, line ~191 — the gameplay backdrop wash): *Night Meadow* (starter, free) → **Rose Dusk** (`PROG.cleared` ≥ **40** rounds) → **Amber Field** (`PROG.cleared` ≥ **80** rounds) → **Frost Glade** (`PROG.blooms.length` ≥ **6** keepsake blooms).
- **Prompt frames** (`FRAMES[]`, line ~197 — the color/dressing of the flashing prompt word): *Sprig* (starter, sage `#7ab356`) → **Gold Leaf** (`PROG.streak` ≥ **25**, gold `#c8a84b`; in-game card copy reads "25 in a row" — the counted metric is Daily Dash days completed, +1 per finished daily, endRun line ~436) → **Petal** (`PROG.blooms.length` ≥ **3**, pink `#e58fa0`).

The three faucets: **lifetime rounds cleared** (`PROG.cleared`, +1 per clean win in `resolve()` line ~421), **keepsake blooms** (`PROG.blooms[]` — every 8 clean wins fills the Bloom Meter and `doBloom()` line ~425 grows one), and the **Daily streak** (`PROG.streak`), the lapsed-friendly return loop. Cosmetics are purely visual. Sunbeams stay the earn currency only, fully separate: `_sbCapEarn` (30/day via `sw_sb_micromeadow`), 12/run via `payRun()` — +2 per bloom, +3 for the first Daily clear of the day, plus `floor(cleared/3)` capped at 10 at run end; Zen Meander pays 0. (Footnote: "12/run" is the code comment's intent, but `_sbRun` at line ~429 is never reset by `startRun()` — so live behavior is 12 per page-session across runs until a reload. Pre-existing engine quirk the art never touches; if the engine is ever fixed, the fix is `_sbRun=0` inside `startRun()`. The 30/day `_sbCapEarn` ceiling holds either way.)
