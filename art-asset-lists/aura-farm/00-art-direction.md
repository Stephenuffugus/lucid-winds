# Aura Farm — Art Direction

**Game:** `satellites/aura-farm/` (vendored; source of truth `Stephenuffugus/aura-farm`).
An emotional-harvest sim: you walk a hand-drawn crowd of 68 souls across six
venues, lift them into joy (Radiance) or grind them into misery (Blight), and
harvest the feeling at its peak. Deep purple night-garden UI, gold vs violet
economy, a rival harvester in gray.

**Status:** the game's canvas art is complete and shipped (it draws its own
characters). The ONLY requested piece right now is the **portal thumbnail** —
the live card uses a raw gameplay screenshot that sits flat next to the painted
MJ thumbs on the shelf.

## The look

Painted, moody, a little wicked but beautiful; closer to a tarot card than a
kids' game. Key colors from the game itself: deep ink violet `#0b0a14`, warm
gold `#ffd75e`, orchid `#a86bff`, mint glow `#8effc1`, rose `#ff6b90`.

## Sheet 1 — Portal thumbnail (square)

Paste-ready prompt:

> A painted square game card illustration, rich digital gouache. A hooded
> harvester seen from behind at dusk in a glowing park, holding up a small
> glass orb that swirls with two energies, warm golden light and deep violet
> smoke, perfectly balanced. Around them a soft crowd of simple rounded
> villagers, half bathed in gold light and smiling, half in violet shadow
> looking drained. Fireflies of both colors stream toward the orb. Deep ink
> purple night sky, lantern strings, painterly glow, tarot card mood,
> beautiful and slightly ominous, no text, no border. Square 1:1.

Deliver: one 1024x1024+ PNG. It gets downscaled to 400x400 for
