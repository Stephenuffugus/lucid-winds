# Star Field — Art Pack

> Ink one gold star into every row, column, and constellation — none may touch — and the solved sky blooms into a keepsake constellation.

**Genre:** Star Battle (K=1) logic puzzle (single-file HTML5, 540×960 portrait; the board is **canvas-drawn**, the title / win / Grove / Skins / Settings screens are **DOM overlays** on top of it). An N×N grid is split into N irregular regions ("constellations"); the player places exactly **one star per row, per column, and per region**, and **no two stars may touch**, even diagonally. Tap cycles empty → dot → ⭐ star → empty; **auto-mark** (◈) dots every cell a placed star rules out; a 💡 hint places one true star (and costs the "clean" solve). Four modes: **Star Garden** (5×5 ramping to 7×7 with solves), **Daily Sky** (seeded 7×7, streak-boosted Sunbeams), **Zen** (6×6, auto-mark on by default — the ◈ chip can still turn it off mid-puzzle, no reward), **Deep Field** (8×8). Every puzzle is generator-built and **solver-verified unique**. Solving mints a **keepsake constellation** — the solution stars joined by dew-blue threads — into the Grove gallery. Colorblind guarantee: **region identity is carried by THICK cream borders (3px, 4-5px in high-contrast mode), never by fill color**; the region tints are decorative low-contrast washes only, and conflicts announce themselves in text ("two stars are touching") as well as color.

_The game already ships and plays procedurally (`satellites/star-field/index.html`) — this art is an optional visual upgrade **and** the cosmetics library for the in-game customization economy. Grid logic, tap cycle, auto-mark, thresholds and the border-carries-meaning rule are IDENTICAL across every theme below; only the skin changes._

## Pick a look — theme direction

⚖️ **Director note:** no forced botanical house style. The mechanic is pure logic on a night sky — the reskin is free to leave the garden. Three directions below, cozy → polished/mature, all kid-friendly, with a recommendation.

### 1. Quilted Night (cozy)
*A bedtime quilt of the night sky: the constellations are soft felt patches stitched together with visible thread, stars are embroidered gold buttons, pencil dots are pinheads, and each solved keepsake hangs in the Grove as a little cross-stitch sampler.* **Why:** the warmest and most tactile option, instantly kid-cozy, and stitching literally IS "thick borders between regions." **Why not:** patchwork begs for bright patch colors, which fights the hard rule that borders (not fills) carry region identity; and embroidered texture muddies at the small cells an 8×8 Deep Field forces.

### 2. Midnight Seedbed (botanical — the in-fiction option)
*The game's own copy literalized: a moonlit garden bed divided into earthen plots, gold star-flowers planted into dark soil, fireflies drifting the edges, the Grove a pressed-flower album.* **Why:** native to Lucid Winds and it matches the code's strings word-for-word ("plant a star in every constellation," "Constellation bloomed!", "stars planted"). **Why not:** it keeps the game in the botanical lane the Director asked to stop defaulting to, and near-black soil plots give the least contrast headroom under the gold stars and cream borders.

### 3. Astrolabe Atlas ⭐ RECOMMENDED (polished/mature, non-botanical)
*An antique celestial cartographer's atlas: each puzzle is a plate in an old star chart, the regions inked like map provinces with hand-drawn cream coastlines, stars are gold-leaf illuminations, dots are the astronomer's graphite ticks, and every solved sky is engraved into the atlas as a gilded constellation card.* **Why:** it is the cleanest fiction-to-mechanic fit in the whole catalog — the code already calls regions "constellations," the win screen already draws a star chart, and thick inked borders between provinces is exactly how antique maps work, so the colorblind rule becomes the art style instead of fighting it. It sits natively on the game's locked near-black palette (the charts ARE `#0b0f0b` night vellum), reads polished and a little grown-up without ever being scary, and the glyph names the code owns (Marigold, Rose) absorb beautifully as gilded botanical illuminations in the chart margins — old atlases really did decorate their cartouches with flora. **Why not:** slightly less huggable than the quilt on a portal card — we buy that back with warm lamplight and rounded ink shapes.

**Recommendation: Astrolabe Atlas.** Boldest step out of the garden while keeping every gold/cream/sage anchor the engine already draws with, zero fiction rewrite (constellations stay constellations), and the border-first colorblind rule becomes the look's signature rather than a constraint. Hold Quilted Night in reserve as a future unlockable sky if the wardrobe ever grows.

## Cosmetics economy

Every Star Field cosmetic is earned by **PLAYING** — no loot boxes, no randomized purchases, nothing that changes a puzzle's grid, regions, solution or rules. Every unlock is a **KNOWN threshold shown right on its locked wardrobe card** ("unlock: 15 solves"). All of it is already wired in `localStorage` key **`starfield_save`** (`SAVE = { solves, cleanSolves, streak, lastDaily, dailyDoneOn, gallery[], theme, glyph, sound, cb, auto }`):

- **Star glyphs** (`SAVE.glyph`, `GLYPHS[]`, gated on `SAVE.solves` — total solved skies, any mode including Zen): **Marigold** (starter, free) → **Classic Star** (5 solves) → **Firefly** (15 solves) → **Rose** (40 solves). The equipped glyph is also **baked into each keepsake at solve time** (`ks.glyph`), so the Grove becomes a visible history of what you were wearing.
- **Night skies** (`SAVE.theme`, `THEMES[]`, gated on `SAVE.cleanSolves` — solves **without using a hint**): **Midnight** `#0b0f0b` (starter, free) → **Dusk** `#141018` (6 hintless) → **Frost** `#0a1016` (20 hintless) → **Ember** `#150e0a` (45 hintless). This is the mastery lane: hints are always available, but the prettiest skies belong to players who solve clean.
- **The Grove** (`SAVE.gallery`): every solve auto-mints a keepsake constellation (solution stars joined by dew threads); the save keeps the last **60**, the gallery displays the last **24** — a free-flowing collection faucet with no gate at all.

Two earn faucets feed the thresholds: **volume** (`solves` — any solve counts) and **mastery** (`cleanSolves` — hintless only). The **Daily streak** (`SAVE.streak`) does not gate cosmetics; it boosts the Daily Sky's Sunbeam payout (`4 + ⌊streak/2⌋`, capped 12/run). Sunbeams stay the earn/collection currency only (`_sbCapEarn`, 30/day cap under `sw_sb_starfield`; Star Garden/Deep Field pay 2, Deep Field's 8×8 pays 3, Zen pays 0). Skins are **purely visual** — they never touch grid size, region shapes, solution uniqueness, conflict rules or payouts.
