# Plot Bloom — Art Pack

> Read the plot, plant for good neighbours, and watch a lucky corner burst into a Harmony Bloom you keep forever.

**Genre:** Islanders-style tap-placement puzzle (single-file HTML5, 540×960 portrait stage, scaled to fit). **This is a DOM game, not a canvas game:** the board is a CSS grid (`#pb-board`) of 7×7 square `.cell` divs; the garden starts 3×3 and **grows** to 5×5 then 7×7 at score thresholds **20 / 60 / 130**; pieces are currently emoji glyphs inside cells and hand cards. You hold a hand of 3 pieces from **SEVEN types** — Flower, Tree, Pond, Hive, Bench, Veg, Hedge — and every placement scores from the **8 neighbouring squares** via a synergy matrix (flowers love bees, ponds feed veg, trees crowd trees, hives hate benches). Selecting a piece shows a live **+/− preview number on every empty square**. A single placement scoring **6+** fires a **Harmony Bloom**: a screen flash, +3 bonus, and a procedural keepsake flower saved to the Bloom Gallery. Four modes: Garden Plot, Daily Plot (date-seeded, streak), Zen Garden (full 7×7, all pieces), Harmony challenge (22 pieces, reach 90). Colorblind support is a settings toggle that adds **letter badges F/T/P/H/B/V/G** — the engine owns those letters; art must keep the seven types silhouette-distinct and NEVER bake letters in.

_The game already ships and plays procedurally (`satellites/plot-bloom/index.html`) — this art is an optional visual upgrade **and** the cosmetics library for the in-game customization economy. Board math, the synergy matrix, thresholds, preview numbers and the letter-badge colorblind system are IDENTICAL across every look below; only the skin changes._

## Pick a look — theme direction

⚖️ **Director note:** no forced childish house style. One honest constraint here: the engine's own copy names the pieces "Flower / Tree / Pond / Hive / Bench / Veg / Hedge" and the help text talks about planting, so a FULL theme exit (space, circuits) would fight code-owned text. The escape hatch is the **setting** — the garden doesn't have to sit in a flat cozy backyard. Three directions below, cozy → polished, all kid-friendly.

### 1. Skyshard Isles ⭐ RECOMMENDED (bold setting-jump, the Islanders homage)
*The plot is a cluster of small floating turf shards drifting in a calm twilight sky — a pocket world. Every piece is a chunky, toy-like miniature perched on its own mossy shard: a tulip the size of a lamppost, a pond like a dewdrop caught in turf, a skep hive glowing like a lantern. When the plot grows, new shards drift in on dew-blue sparkle dust; the board's underside tapers into hanging roots and floating motes over the void.*

**Why this one:** it is the biggest jump the code copy allows — off the ground entirely — and it is a direct, loving nod to the game's source (Islanders is literally "build on tiny islands"). It maps 1:1 onto real mechanics for free: the dark locked palette (`#0d100c`/`#0b0f0b` screens) IS the twilight void, the `.cell.locked` dashed squares read perfectly as *not-yet-arrived shards*, the expansion moment ("The plot grows") becomes new islands drifting in, and the three real wardrobe backdrops — **Meadow / Dusk Rows / Moonlit** (names owned by code) — become three skies. Chunky miniatures on bright turf pop hard against dark tiles, which is exactly what a 68px DOM cell needs. Kid-magnetic (floating islands are top-tier), yet the twilight light keeps it moody enough for adults.

### 2. Meadow Almanac (alt, cozy — the botanical option, upgraded)
*A hand-inked farmer's almanac come alive: pieces painted like seed-catalog plates in soft gouache on midnight paper, the board a pressed-earth garden bed with twine-and-stake edges, keepsake blooms rendered like pressed flowers.* Warmest and most native to Lucid Winds, and the cheapest to read at tiny sizes — but it keeps Plot Bloom inside the same cozy-botanical lane as a dozen sibling games, and a portal card in this style disappears next to them.

### 3. Tabletop Terrarium (alt, polished / more mature)
*A midnight glass terrarium on a maker's workbench: every piece a hand-built clay-and-moss miniature with visible craft charm, tilt-shift depth, soft lamp key light, condensation glints on the glass.* Premium and gender-neutral, photographs beautifully for the portal — but the miniature-model rendering costs the most per asset, glass reflections risk muddying 68px cells, and it is the least "alive" of the three (a terrarium is sealed; this game is about a garden growing).

**Recommendation: Skyshard Isles.** Boldest shift the code copy permits, a true Islanders homage, native to the locked dark palette (lowest integration risk), and it turns three real mechanics — locked cells, expansion, backdrop unlocks — into theme moments instead of UI states. Borrow Almanac's gouache warmth in the paint handling; hold Tabletop Terrarium in reserve as a future premium piece-style unlock.

## Cosmetics economy

Every Plot Bloom cosmetic is earned by **PLAYING** — no loot boxes, no randomized purchases, nothing that touches the synergy matrix, thresholds, preview numbers or scoring. Every unlock is a **KNOWN threshold shown on its locked wardrobe card**. Two cosmetic families and the keepsake collection, all already wired in `localStorage` key `plotbloom_save` (the `PROG` object):

- **Backdrops** (`PROG.bg` equipped, `PROG.bgUnlocked` gate): *Meadow* (starter, free) → **Dusk Rows** (personal best ≥ **120**) → **Moonlit** (personal best ≥ **300**). Faucet: single-run **best score** (`PROG.best`), checked in `endRun()`.
- **Piece styles** (`PROG.theme` equipped, `PROG.themeUnlocked` gate, `THEMES[]`): *Classic* (starter, free) → **Dusk** (grow **10** lifetime Harmony Blooms, `PROG.grewTotal >= 10`). Faucet: lifetime **blooms grown** — mastery of the synergy matrix, not grind.
- **Bloom Gallery** (`PROG.blooms[]`, last 60 seeds kept): every Harmony Bloom mints a deterministic keepsake flower (`drawKeepsake` from the stored seed) — the collection IS a reward surface and this pack frames it like one.
- **Wired but not yet spent:** the Daily streak (`PROG.streak` / `PROG.lastDaily`) already persists — a future streak-gated cosmetic can hang off it with zero new plumbing. Do not invent a threshold for it in art copy.

Sunbeams stay the earn currency only: `_sbCapEarn` caps **30/day** (`sw_sb_plotbloom`, ~lines 193-197). The end-of-run payout is `floor(score/40)` (+2 Daily bonus) capped at **12/run** — that `Math.min(12,…)` in `endRun()` (~line 339) applies ONLY to the end-of-run payout; the **+1 per Harmony Bloom** grants land mid-run in `harmonyBloom()` (~line 323) outside that per-run min and count only against the 30/day cap, so a bloom-heavy run can total more than 12. Skins are purely visual — they never change a tile hitbox, a synergy value, or a Sunbeam payout.
