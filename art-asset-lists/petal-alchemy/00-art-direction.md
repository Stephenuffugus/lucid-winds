# Petal Alchemy — Art Pack

> Five seeds on a midnight workbench — tap two, watch them take, and chain your way to a hundred discoveries and six one-of-a-kind True Blooms.

**Genre:** Infinite-Craft-style tap-to-combine discovery sandbox (single-file HTML5, 540×960 portrait stage). This is a **DOM game, not a canvas game**: the whole play surface is a scrolling flex **shelf of element tiles** plus a fixed **mixing tray** (slot + slot = result slot) at the bottom; the only canvases are the tiny 110×90 keepsake-bloom cards in the Grove. Players start with **Seed, Water, Sun, Soil, Air** and tap pairs to combine them through a fully **deterministic recipe tree — 106 elements, 119 recipes, NO randomness, NO LLM** — up to six rare **True Blooms** that mint keepsake flowers. Modes: Free Alchemy, Daily Recipe (seeded shared target + streak), Bloom Hunt (4 quests), Zen Lab (no earn). **Every tile always shows its written name** under its icon — the name text is code-owned and is the game's core readability/colorblind guarantee, so the art's job is a distinct **silhouette per element** that reads at 22px beside that name, never instead of it. (Honest caveat, inherited game bug worth surfacing to the campaign tracker: the settings "Big labels" toggle is a **NO-OP today** — `wireToggle('tg-labels','labels')` at ~478 persists `SET.labels` but NOTHING ever reads it; making it real is a one-line `.nm` font-size wiring, budget it if anything is to lean on that control. The always-on name label is the real guarantee, not the toggle.)

_The game already ships and plays procedurally (`satellites/petal-alchemy/index.html`) — today the icons are emoji and the frames are flat CSS borders. This art is an optional visual upgrade **and** the cosmetics library for the in-game customization economy. Element ids, names, recipes, tile hitboxes and the always-visible name labels are IDENTICAL across every look below; only the skin changes._

## Pick a look

⚖️ **Director note:** no forced childish house style. The mechanic is *crafting/alchemy* — the name is literally Petal Alchemy — so the reskin is free to trade the garden for the **laboratory**. Three directions, cozy → polished, all kid-friendly:

### 1. Pressed-Flower Codex (cozy)
*A naturalist's field journal at night: every element a soft watercolor specimen taped into a cream scrapbook page, tiles like torn-paper labels, the discovery log an actual pressed-flower album.* Warmest and most crafty-cozy, and it flatters the "Pressed Flower" and "Parchment" cosmetics beautifully. But it wants light paper backgrounds that fight the game's locked midnight palette (cream tile names need dark plates to stay readable), and it keeps the game squarely in the botanical lane the Director wants the option to leave.

### 2. Midnight Apothecary ⭐ RECOMMENDED (polished, kid-friendly, leaves the garden for the lab)
*A candlelit alchemist's workbench in a deep midnight room: every element a chunky enamel specimen token — brass bezels, glass vials, wax seals, glowing tinctures — the mixing tray a brass rack, the True Blooms blooming out of alembics in gold light.* This is the boldest fiction shift the code permits: the framing leaves the garden for the **alchemy lab** (the element *names* stay code-owned, but Seed/Water/Sun read as specimens on a workbench, not plants in a bed). It sits natively on the real palette — the midnight greens `#0d100c`/`#0e140d` ARE the dark workshop, `#c8a84b` is the candle brass, `#e58fa0` the rose tincture — so integration risk is near zero. Best of all, the shipped cosmetic catalog practically dictates it: the four node frames are literally named **Sage, Brass, Pressed Flower, Stained Glass** and the logs **Sage, Parchment, Gilt** — an apothecary already owns brass, pressed specimens, leaded glass and gilt folios. Enamel-badge rendering also solves the real art problem: running the live `EL` table, **39 of the 106 elements share an emoji across 16 duplicate groups** — five 🌳 (hedge, tree, oak, orchard, shrub), four 🌿 (shoot, vine, fern, herb), three 🪴 (plant, seedling, houseplant), three 🍯 (nectar, honey, honeypot), plus twelve two-way pairs (🌰 seed/acorn, 🍄 mushroom/toadstool, 🪱 root/worm, 🦋 butterfly/dragonfly, 🕯️ beeswax/candle, 🔮 glass/terrarium, 🌻 sunflower/sunbloom, 🌹 rose/dewrose, 🌼 flower/meadow, 🌬️ air/wind, 🪵 trunk/bog, 🐝 bee/hive) — and tokens force a unique silhouette for each.

### 3. Glyphline Lab (mature / minimal — fully non-botanical)
*A sleek dark crafting terminal: every element a flat luminous glyph on smoked glass, thin light-line frames, synthwave-quiet.* The most grown-up and gender-neutral, cheapest to render, and it reads instantly at tiny sizes. But it sheds all the warmth that makes discovery feel like a gift, makes the six True Blooms (the emotional payoff) feel like icons instead of flowers, and a glyph language for 106 distinct nouns drifts toward abstraction that hurts silhouette-vs-name readability for kids.

**Recommendation: Midnight Apothecary.** Native to the locked midnight palette, the biggest fiction refresh the deterministic recipe tree allows, a 1:1 match for the cosmetic names already shipped in code, and the strongest fix for the duplicate-emoji problem. Borrow the Codex's pressed-petal warmth inside the "Pressed Flower" frame and the Gilt log; hold Glyphline in reserve as a possible future minimal frame skin.

## Cosmetics economy

Every Petal Alchemy cosmetic is earned by **PLAYING** — no loot boxes, no randomized purchases, nothing that touches the recipe tree, tile hitboxes or name labels. Every unlock is a **KNOWN threshold shown right on its locked wardrobe card** (the card prints the requirement, e.g. "20 discovered"). Two cosmetic families and two earn faucets, all already wired and persisted in `localStorage` (`petalalchemy_save`):

- **Node frames** (`SAVE.frame`, `FRAMES[]`) — reskins of the element-tile border: *Sage* (starter, free) → **Brass** (**20** discovered) → **Pressed Flower** (**50** discovered) → **Stained Glass** (**90** discovered). Faucet: cumulative discoveries (`discCount()` over `SAVE.disc`), the game's heartbeat stat.
- **Log parchments** (`SAVE.log`, `LOGS[]`) — reskins of the discovery log / Grove panel: *Sage* (starter, free) → **Parchment** (**2 True Blooms**) → **Gilt** (**4 True Blooms**). Faucet: True Blooms grown (`bloomCount()` over `SAVE.blooms`), the mastery stat — Gilt at 4 of the 6 possible blooms is a real collector's badge.

Gating runs live in `renderWard()` via `unlocked()` (frames check `discCount() >= need`, logs check `bloomCount() >= need`), so it is free and offline-safe; the equipped choice lives in `SAVE.frame` / `SAVE.log`. Skins are **purely visual** — they never change a recipe, a tile's tap target, or the printed names. Sunbeams stay the earn currency only (`_sbCapEarn`, **30/day** cap, **12/run** via `earn()`, Zen Lab earns 0): +1 per new discovery, +3 per True Bloom, +2 daily recipe, +2 per completed hunt. `SAVE.streak` (Daily Recipe streak) and `SAVE.bestRun` also persist and are natural hooks for FUTURE cosmetic tiers, but no shipped cosmetic keys off them today — do not promise streak unlocks in art.
