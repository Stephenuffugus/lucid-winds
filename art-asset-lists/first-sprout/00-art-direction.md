# First Sprout — Art Pack

> A dark handful of soil, one dormant seed, and a single tap of warmth — the grove wakes slowly, fills the frame, and grows even while you sleep.

**Genre:** A Dark Room remake — idle/incremental (single-file HTML5, 540×960 portrait stage). This is a **hybrid canvas + DOM game**: one full-stage `<canvas>` paints the grove scene (sky, moon, stars, soil bed, the growing plant, lantern glows, fireflies, tap floats) while the DOM owns everything else — the top HUD (light ☀ / dew 💧 counters), the tap zone, and a fixed **300px bottom shop panel** (log line + unlock buttons + New Seed button), plus full-screen DOM menus (title, how, settings, wardrobe, Daily Blessing, offline-welcome). You tap the soil for **light**, spend it on five repeatable helpers (Paper Lantern 🏮, Dew Sprout 🌱, Dew Pool 💧, Firefly ✨, Luna Moth 🦋) and six one-time **reveal nodes** (Kindle the Soil 🕯️ → Wake the Seed 🌰 → Turn the Soil 🪱 / Raise a Canopy 🌳 → Open the First Bloom 🌼 → Grow the Heartwood 🪵). The grove canvas literally **fills in** as grove value rises; offline progress (up to 8h) greets you back; **New Seed** prestige folds the grove into a seed for a permanent +0.25× Heirloom multiplier. There is no losing, no timer, no fail state — an Ambient mode even hides the numbers.

_The game already ships and plays procedurally (`satellites/first-sprout/index.html`) — this art is an optional visual upgrade **and** the cosmetics library for the wardrobe economy. Mechanics, costs, gates and layout are IDENTICAL across every look below; only the skin changes._

## Pick a look — theme direction

The mechanic's fiction is baked into code-owned copy ("the seed splits with a small green sound", "Wake the Seed", "Grow the Heartwood") — so unlike a pure-abstract arcade game, First Sprout cannot fully leave the grove. What CAN move is the **material and mood** of that grove. Three directions, cozy → mature:

### 1. Candlewick Storybook (cozy)
*A picture-book nocturne in soft ink and gouache: round-shouldered seedling, button-eyed moths, a fat friendly moon, candle-glow washing warm circles onto dark paper. Everything slightly plump, hand-drawn wobble in the linework, bedtime-story gentle.*

**Why:** warmest and most instantly kid-magnetic; forgiving to generate; the "no losing here" promise reads in every stroke. **Why not:** it softens the one thing A Dark Room fans come for — the austere, patient darkness. The game's own copy is quiet and literary, and a plush look talks over it; it also risks the exact forced-childish house style the Director killed.

### 2. Wick & Loam ⭐ RECOMMENDED (polished chiaroscuro, still kid-kind)
*One small flame carving soft shapes out of a deep, kind darkness. Painterly chiaroscuro: near-black fields, a single warm candle key from the soil, silhouettes emerging edge-first — a pale stem, a moth's wing catching gold, dew beads holding tiny cold highlights. Mature restraint, zero horror: the dark is a blanket, not a threat.*

**Why this one:** it IS A Dark Room's identity — light as the first resource, darkness as the canvas — and it maps 1:1 onto what the engine already draws: the kindle-glow radial, the pulsing lantern points, the twinkle stars and firefly motes are all ALREADY light-in-darkness effects, so the art drops in with the lowest integration risk. The locked Lucid Winds midnight palette (`#0d100c` base, gold accents, sage greens) is native chiaroscuro. It's the most premium-feeling of the three on a portal card, it honors the literary copy instead of fighting it, and it stays kid-friendly because the flame is always warm and the dark always soft. **Why not:** demands discipline from the generator — blown-out glows or gritty horror shadows would wreck it (the style block below fences both off).

### 3. Emberglass Vigil (mature, leaves the garden material)
*The grove reimagined as a votive stained-glass window growing in a dark chapel of night: the stem is leading came, leaves are cut glass panes lit from behind by candlelight, the bloom is a small rose window, helpers are hanging votive lanterns and glass moths. Jewel-toned light through dark glass.*

**Why:** the boldest departure available — it swaps the material world (living plants → glass and light craft) while every code-owned noun still lands ("bloom", "canopy", "heartwood" all have glasswork readings); very handsome, gender-neutral, mature. **Why not:** faceted glass is the fussiest to keep consistent across sheets, the jewel tones fight the muted save-driven sky/soil cosmetics (which are literal gradient/dirt colors in code), and back-lit glass wants brightness everywhere when the engine's whole composition depends on a dark field with few lights.

**Recommendation: Wick & Loam.** It's the truest heir to the source material, native to the locked midnight palette, lowest-risk against the engine's existing glow-on-dark drawing, and reads premium without losing kindness. Borrow Storybook's plumpness for the moth and seedling silhouettes so nothing ever feels severe; hold Emberglass in reserve as a future unlockable sky/soil set. **All sheets below bake Wick & Loam in.**

## Cosmetics economy

Every First Sprout cosmetic is earned by **PLAYING** — no loot boxes, no randomized purchases, nothing that touches rates, costs or gates. Every unlock is a **KNOWN threshold shown right on the locked wardrobe card** ("plant 1 seed", "grove 5"). Two cosmetic families, both already wired and persisted in `localStorage` key **`firstsprout_save`** (the `COSM` table at index.html:245-253, gates in `ownedCosm()` line ~449, cards built by `refreshWard()` lines ~450-459):

- **Night skies** (`G.sky`, `COSM.sky`, engine colors `SKYCOL` line ~356): *Deep Night* (starter, free, `#0b1220→#05070a`) → **Moonrise** (plant **1** seed, `#141a2e→#080a12`) → **Aurora** (plant **3** seeds, `#10222a→#06121a`). Faucet: **`G.seeds`**, the New Seed prestige counter — sky skins are the prestige-loop reward, and each seed also permanently raises the Heirloom multiplier (+0.25× per seed, `heirloom()` line ~273), so the cosmetic and the power fantasy climb together.
- **Soil beds** (`G.soil`, `COSM.soil`, engine colors `SOILCOL` line ~357): *Loam* (starter, free, `#2a2016`) → **Mossbed** (grove value **5**, `#243019`) → **Ashen** (grove value **12**, `#241f22`). Faucet: **`G.groveMax`** — the best-ever grove value, where each helper copy counts 1 and each of the six reveals counts 2 (`groveValue()` line ~296). `groveMax` **survives New Seed** (carried in `newSeed()` line ~301), so soil unlocks are never lost to prestige.

Equipped choices live in `G.sky` / `G.soil` and both survive prestige. `G.owned{}` is persisted in the save as an escape hatch for future manual grants but the live gates read only `seeds` / `groveMax` — do not invent other gates. Additional real save fields available if new tiers are ever added (Director call): `G.streak` (Daily Blessing consecutive days), `G.totalLight` / `G.totalDew` (lifetime gathers). Skins are **purely visual** — they never change tap gain, helper rates, costs or the Heirloom curve. Sunbeams stay the portal earn currency only (`_sbCapEarn` 30/day via the `sw_sb_firstsprout` ledger, 12/run cap via `payRun`; New Seed pays 4, Daily Blessing pays 3-6 by streak) and never buy cosmetics.
