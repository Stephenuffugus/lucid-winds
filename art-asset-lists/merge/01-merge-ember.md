# MERGE 01 — EMBER FORGE (one theme, one sheet)

STYLE — bold kid-friendly layered cut-paper art: 3-5 stacked flat paper layers,
clean rounded silhouettes, soft rim-glow between layers, FLAT fills, paper
grain, NO gloss, NO photorealism; every tile reads at 44px. Palette: charcoal
black #1a1614, forge iron #3a322c, ember orange #e0662a, flame gold #f0a03c,
white-hot cream #f5e6c8, cool slate #5a544c. NO text, numbers, logos, watermarks.

Create one sprite sheet. File: merge_ember.png. Grid: 4 columns x 4 rows
(16 cells, left-to-right, top-to-bottom). Cell: 512x512. Master: 2048x2048.

KNOCKOUT: flat magenta #FF00FF fills the background of every cell EXCEPT cell
13, which is a FULL-BLEED board background with no magenta. No magenta or hot
pink inside any art.

GROWTH LADDER LAW: the eleven tiles are one story of fire being forged to
life. Size climbs from about 45% of the cell (tile one) to 90% (tile eleven).
Detail climbs every step. ONLY the last three tiles glow. Every object stands
centered on the same soft ground-shadow ellipse.

CELLS:
1.  tile-2 — a single cold black COAL LUMP, matte, one gray highlight
2.  tile-4 — two coals leaning together, one thin crack of dim orange between them
3.  tile-8 — a small EMBER, coal with veins of orange showing through
4.  tile-16 — a live ember cluster on a tiny iron dish, brighter veins
5.  tile-32 — a small forge CRUCIBLE with orange melt just visible at the rim
6.  tile-64 — a glowing INGOT fresh from the pour, gold seams
7.  tile-128 — an ANVIL with a hot ingot on top, spark flecks
8.  tile-256 — a forged ORNATE HAMMER upright, ember veins in the head
9.  tile-512 — a fire SALAMANDER curled around a crucible, first soft glow
10. tile-1024 — a PHOENIX EGG in a nest of embers, strong inner glow, spark motes
11. tile-2048 — the RISEN PHOENIX, wings up, layered flame feathers, radiant
12. empty-cell mark — a faint ghosted coal outline, barely-there charcoal on magenta
13. board background — FULL-BLEED dark forge-brick wall, very dark, soft ember
    flecks in the lowest corners, vignette edges, quiet enough that tiles pop
14. theme emblem — a small crest: crossed hammer and flame in a round badge
15. celebration burst — a radial burst of sparks and tiny flame petals
16. wilt mark — a gentle curl of gray smoke rising from a cooled coal
