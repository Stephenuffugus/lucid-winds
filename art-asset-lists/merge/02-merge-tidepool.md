# MERGE 02 — TIDEPOOL (one theme, one sheet)

STYLE — bold kid-friendly layered cut-paper art: 3-5 stacked flat paper layers,
clean rounded silhouettes, soft rim-glow between layers, FLAT fills, paper
grain, NO gloss, NO photorealism; every tile reads at 44px. Palette: abyss
navy #10202e, lagoon teal #2b7c6c, sea-glass aqua #bfe0f2, warm sand #e8dcc8,
coral pink #e58fa0, bioluminescent mint #7ce8c8. NO text, numbers, logos,
watermarks.

Create one sprite sheet. File: merge_tidepool.png. Grid: 4 columns x 4 rows
(16 cells, left-to-right, top-to-bottom). Cell: 512x512. Master: 2048x2048.

KNOCKOUT: flat magenta #FF00FF fills the background of every cell EXCEPT cell
13, which is a FULL-BLEED board background with no magenta. No magenta or hot
pink inside any art.

GROWTH LADDER LAW: the eleven tiles climb from a single bubble to the great
glowing whale. Size climbs from about 45% of the cell (tile one) to 90%
(tile eleven). Detail climbs every step. ONLY the last three tiles glow.
Every creature floats centered over the same soft shadow ellipse.
