# Seed Reel — Art Pack

> Spin the garden bed, nudge one tile with the Gardener's Hand, and let the bee find her flower — then pay the Bloom Quota before the garden goes to seed.

**Genre:** Luck-be-a-Landlord spin-and-synergy roguelite (single-file HTML5, 540×960 portrait: gameplay on one canvas, menus/results as DOM overlay screens). A **5×4 garden bed** (20 cells) scatters your tile bag every spin; each of the **24 symbols** pays base nectar plus **8-neighbour adjacency bonuses** (diagonals count) — Bee beside Flower, Rain beside Seed, Toad beside Worm — shown live as glowing link-threads. Once per spin the **Gardener's Hand** lets you tap a tile then a cell to move it and line up a synergy before you **Harvest**; after every harvest you **draft** 1 of 3 new tiles into the bag (tier odds .58 / .32 / .10). Every **4 spins** a rising **Bloom Quota** comes due (18, then +16 per season +6·season², i.e. 18 / 40 / 74 / 120…) — pay it and a new season begins (Spring→Summer→Autumn→Winter tint cycle), miss it and the run ends. Six rare tier-3 tokens crown the pool, including the Lucid Winds companions **Koi, Toad and The Beholder**. Modes: 🌻 Grove Run, 📅 Daily Plot (date-seeded), 🏵 Bloom Rush (fixed 8 spins), 🍃 Zen Garden (no quota). Colorblind support is built in: every symbol is a distinct pictogram and a **"Big symbol labels"** toggle prints each tile's name.

_The game already ships and plays procedurally (`satellites/seed-reel/index.html`, emoji glyphs on painted cells) — this art is an optional visual upgrade **and** the cosmetics library for the in-game customization economy. Board geometry, adjacency rules, payouts, quota math and the 24 silhouette cues are IDENTICAL across every theme below; only the skin changes._

## Pick a look — theme direction

⚖️ **Director note:** no forced childish-botanical house style. The mechanic here is literally a **slot reel**, so the strongest non-garden fiction is the machine itself — the symbols stay the garden critters the code names, but the world around them is free to leave the flowerbed.

### 1. Brass Nightreel ⭐ RECOMMENDED (polished, kid-friendly, leaves the garden for the arcade)
*A moonlit Victorian penny-arcade machine in a night conservatory. The 24 symbols become **cloisonné enamel-and-brass tokens** clicking into a felt-lined garden bed; the quota bar is a brass gauge, the SPIN button a warm lamplit lever plate, the bed frames carved fascia inlays. Whimsical antique arcade — never casino: no cherries, bars, sevens or card suits, and the game has no gambling economy to echo (spins are free, quota is a survival score).*

**Why this one:** it embraces the actual mechanic — the one non-garden fiction the game invites — at near-zero integration cost, because the code already speaks brass: the gold `#c8a84b` tier-3 glow, the flat-brass `#d9b85a` canvas SPIN plate (plus the same brass as a `#d9b85a`→`#b2913a` gradient on the DOM menu buttons' `.btn.primary`), the gold quota bar and the gold rare-draft border ARE a machine fascia waiting to be painted. The four FRAME cosmetics (Willow / Amberwood / Rosevine / Moonsilver) map perfectly onto a material ladder of cabinet inlays, and the four SKIES stay the conservatory's glass-roof night. Enamel token faces keep all 24 pictograms crisp at the real 84px cell size, and the rim material (plain bronze / notched sage-silver / ornate gold filigree) gives tier a **non-color** cue on top of the code's border colors. Mature "collector" energy, still warm and huggable for kids.

### 2. Almanac Garden (alt, cozy — the botanical option, upgraded)
*A farmer's-almanac seed catalog by lamplight: each symbol a hand-inked, watercolor-washed seed-packet stamp on a cream label, pinned into a dark potting-bench bed.* Warmest and most storybook, and native to the copy ("the garden went to seed"). But it keeps the game inside the cozy-botanical lane the Director wants options beyond, and twenty bright parchment rectangles on a midnight field crowd the payout numbers the engine prints on every tile.

### 3. Night Market Charms (alt, middle — collectible-pin energy)
*Glossy enamel-pin charms on dark felt, like a claw-machine prize display: chunky metal-rimmed charms, soft lamplight, gachapon delight.* Very current and the tokens pop hard, but it shares most of its DNA with Brass Nightreel while offering less fiction glue for the reel, gauge and lever HUD — and felt texture muddies at 84px.

**Recommendation: Brass Nightreel.** It is the boldest coherent shift (arcade machine, not flowerbed), it inherits the game's locked gold-on-midnight palette almost verbatim (lowest integration risk), the cosmetics families map 1:1 onto cabinet inlays and backglass skies, and the enamel-token language doubles as a tier cue for colorblind players. Keep Almanac Garden's warmth in the lamplight; hold Night Market Charms in reserve as a future token-skin idea.

## Cosmetics economy

Every Seed Reel cosmetic is earned by **PLAYING** — no loot boxes, no randomized purchases, no pay-to-win. Everything is a **KNOWN unlock at a KNOWN threshold**, shown right on each locked wardrobe card (`desc` strings like "Reach season 6"). Two cosmetic families, three earn faucets, all already wired in `localStorage` key **`seedreel_save`**:

- **Bed Frames** (`PROG.frame`, `FRAMES[]`, drawn as the bed's border): *Willow* (starter, free) → **Amberwood** (best **Season 3**) → **Rosevine** (best **Season 6**) → **Moonsilver** (best **Season 10**). One faucet: the seasons-tended personal best (`PROG.bestFloor`, `kind:'floor'`) — the game's core mastery ladder, since each season means another Bloom Quota paid.
- **Skies** (`PROG.sky`, `SKIES[]`, drawn as the full-canvas backdrop gradient): *Midnight* (starter, free) → **Dusk** (play **5 runs**) → **Aurora** (play **15 runs**) → **Streaklight** (**5-day Daily Plot streak**). Two faucets: total runs finished in any mode (`PROG.runs`, `kind:'runs'`) and the Daily streak (`PROG.streak`, `kind:'streak'`, advanced once per day on first Daily Plot completion via `PROG.lastDaily` / `PROG.dailyDone`) — the lapsed-friendly return loop.

Unlock logic is `ownedC()`: threshold met on the live metric **or** an optional manual grant in `PROG.owned{}` (`'frame'+id` / `'sky'+id`) without touching earn logic; `curFrame()` / `curSky()` fall back to the starter if an equipped item isn't owned. Cosmetics are **purely visual** — a frame changes only the bed's border, a sky only the backdrop gradient; they never touch symbols, adjacency rules, payouts, quota math, draft odds or Sunbeams. Sunbeams stay the earn/collection currency only (`_sbCapEarn`, **30/day** cap in `sw_sb_seedreel`, ≤12/run at the call sites: Grove Run `min(12, seasons×3 + nectar/60)`, Daily Plot `min(12, 3 + seasons×2 + nectar/80)` first completion per day only, Bloom Rush `min(12, nectar/40 + 2)`, Zen earns nothing). Every cosmetic stays fully earnable free by playing — the wall is a showing-up wall, not a paywall.
