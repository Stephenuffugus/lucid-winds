## Sheet layout

Generate the whole sheet at **1024x2048** (a tall vertical strip). Lay it out as a **single column of 7 evenly spaced cells**, magenta #FF00FF knockout background with equal margins and gutters so a fixed-pitch cutter can slice one clean 1024x-tall band per row. The meter renders on-table as a narrow vertical tube roughly **40px wide × 400px tall** (x≈30, y120→520), so every full-height sprite (empty tube + the three fills + erupt) must share the **exact same slim vertical footprint and baseline** — draw them all at the identical tube outline so fills stack pixel-perfect over the empty frame. The two small bubble-accent sprites are compact round pops (~22px on table) and can sit in the bottom cell side by side. Keep each sprite fully inside its cell, nothing touching the edges, no magenta spill onto the clay.

Cell order top→bottom:
1. `meter_tube_empty` (full height)
2. `meter_fill_low` (full height, potion only in bottom third)
3. `meter_fill_mid` (full height, potion to two-thirds)
4. `meter_fill_high` (full height, potion near the top)
5. `meter_erupt` (full height, foaming over the rim)
6. `bubble_accent_a` + `bubble_accent_b` (two small pops sharing the bottom cell)

- `bubble_accent_a` — A single fat round clay bubble, translucent slime-green #86d24a with a bright pinhole desk-lamp highlight and a soft teal #37b3a0 rim, one thumbprint dent on its side. The pop-frame version: plump and whole, about to burst.
