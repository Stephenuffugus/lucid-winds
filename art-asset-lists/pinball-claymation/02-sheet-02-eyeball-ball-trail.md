# Sheet 02 — Clay Eyeball Ball + Trail + Launch

The rolling clay eyeball marble, its charged multiball state, the goo trail motes it drops, and the plunge launch smear — every motion sprite the ball physics touch.

```
STYLE — "Midnight Monster Lab" claymation (Blobworks pinball). Top-down 90 degree orthographic pinball art, hand-sculpted MODELLING CLAY / plasticine, stop-motion look (Aardman / Gumby / Morph). Every object is a little clay model: visible thumbprint dents, tool-scrape seams, soft rounded edges that wobble slightly (never perfectly geometric), matte plasticine sheen with ONE soft desk-lamp highlight, tiny pressed-in dust/lint flecks, a gentle contact shadow underneath. Goofy-friendly monsters — googly eyes, buck teeth, one stubby horn, big grins; cute NOT scary, no gore, no menace. Palette: dark clay lab-bench base #22202a, slate shadow #16151d; slime green #86d24a + deep monster teal #37b3a0; goop purple #9b6fd4; eyeball cream #f1ede2 with iris #4fa3d1 + black pupil; hazard yellow #f4c93a; danger red #e5533d; brass machine #c8a84b + warm lamp cream #efe6d2; gum-pink tongue #e88ba0. Lighting: one warm desk-lamp from top-center, cool moonlight fill, dark drop-off toward the middle of the table so the moving eyeball ball + bumpers pop. Rendering: photographed-clay feel — soft studio light, subtle grain, NO vector-flat, NO neon glow-blowout, NO photoreal humans, NO text / watermark / UI baked into sprites (letters are drawn by the engine). Everything reads at small size on a 540x960 phone table. Consistent top-down view for all playfield pieces (no perspective tilt). Deliver each sprite knocked out on a flat magenta #FF00FF background.
```

## Sheet layout

Generate the whole sheet at **2048x2048**, a clean **4x4 grid** (512px cells, equal margins and gutters) on a pure flat **magenta #FF00FF** knockout background so a fixed-pitch cutter can slice on the 512px pitch. Each sprite is centered in its cell, fully inside frame, nothing touching the cell edges, with only a small tight contact shadow (no long cast shadow that would bleed into a neighbor). On the table the two eyeballs render at about **24-30px** diameter; trail motes render **6-10px**; the launch streak is a short vertical smear roughly **12x40px**. Sprites are drawn large and centered in their 512px cells for crisp downscaling — keep the eyeball marbles perfectly round in silhouette so the physics radial reads clean at tiny size.

Cell map (row-major, top-left first):
- **Row 1** — `eyeball_core`, `eyeball_lit`, `launch_streak`, `trail_mote_lg`
- **Row 2** — `trail_mote_md`, `trail_mote_sm`, `trail_mote_dot`, `eyeball_core_squash`
- **Row 3–4** — leave the remaining 8 cells as EMPTY flat #FF00FF spacer cells (reserved gutters; do not fill with art), keeping the grid pitch uniform for the cutter.

## Assets

- `eyeball_core` — The everyday ball: a hand-rolled clay EYEBALL marble, perfectly round silhouette, cream sclera #f1ede2 with faint hand-smeared red clay veins #e5533d threading across it, a fat blue iris #4fa3d1 with a black clay pupil pressed in slightly off-center, ONE crisp warm desk-lamp glint #efe6d2 pinned upper-left. Thumbprint dents and a hairline seam where the two clay halves join; matte-satin plasticine, looks like a marble you could flick. Small tight contact shadow. This is the default swappable skin (sheet 11 overrides it).
- `eyeball_lit` — The same eyeball CHARGED for GOO MULTIBALL: identical iris and pupil but ringed with a raised bead of hazard yellow #f4c93a clay around the rim like it's been dipped, the sclera warmed a touch brighter, and a WIDER, brighter double glint (main lamp spot plus a small secondary catch) so it reads as "energized." Same round silhouette and size as `eyeball_core` so it swaps 1:1 in physics. No neon glow-blowout — the charge is clay, not light.
- `eyeball_core_squash` — A single squash/impact frame of the core eyeball: the same marble momentarily flattened into a soft wobbly oval (wider than tall), veins and iris smeared with the stretch, glint still upper-left, seam pinched. For the on-hit deform beat; same colors and materials as `eyeball_core`, just deformed.
- `launch_streak` — A short vertical motion SMEAR of clay goo for the plunge launch: a tapered comet-shape of slime green #86d24a fading into deep teal #37b3a0 at the trailing end, sculpted as a stretched blob with tool-drag scrape lines running lengthwise and a faint cream glint down one edge. Blunt rounded head, wispy thinned tail. Reads as speed-blur made of plasticine, not a light trail.
- `trail_mote_lg` — The largest goo droplet the ball sheds: a soft rounded blob of slime green #86d24a with one bright cream glint #efe6d2 and a tiny darker teal underside, faint thumbprint dent. Slightly wobbly, gum-drop shaped, ~10px on table.
- `trail_mote_md` — Same goo droplet, medium size (~8px): rounder, one small glint, slime green #86d24a to teal #37b3a0 shading. Same style as `trail_mote_lg`, scaled down.
- `trail_mote_sm` — Small goo droplet (~6px): a simple slime-green #86d24a clay bead with a single pinpoint glint, barely any veining. Cutter-clean round silhouette.
- `trail_mote_dot` — The tiniest fading mote (~4-5px on table): a minimal slime-green #86d24a clay dot with the faintest cream catch, almost dissolving. Used for the far/oldest end of the ball trail so it thins out to nothing.
