# Sheet 04 — Chomp-Head Bumpers + Sling Blobs + SLIME Targets

Every clay bumper, sling, and standup target for the Blobworks pinball playfield — three chomping monster heads (idle + lit), two kicking sling blobs (idle + kick), and one letter-block standup tile in three lit states, all top-down and cut-ready.

```
STYLE — "Midnight Monster Lab" claymation (Blobworks pinball). Top-down 90 degree orthographic pinball art, hand-sculpted MODELLING CLAY / plasticine, stop-motion look (Aardman / Gumby / Morph). Every object is a little clay model: visible thumbprint dents, tool-scrape seams, soft rounded edges that wobble slightly (never perfectly geometric), matte plasticine sheen with ONE soft desk-lamp highlight, tiny pressed-in dust/lint flecks, a gentle contact shadow underneath. Goofy-friendly monsters — googly eyes, buck teeth, one stubby horn, big grins; cute NOT scary, no gore, no menace. Palette: dark clay lab-bench base #22202a, slate shadow #16151d; slime green #86d24a + deep monster teal #37b3a0; goop purple #9b6fd4; eyeball cream #f1ede2 with iris #4fa3d1 + black pupil; hazard yellow #f4c93a; danger red #e5533d; brass machine #c8a84b + warm lamp cream #efe6d2; gum-pink tongue #e88ba0. Lighting: one warm desk-lamp from top-center, cool moonlight fill, dark drop-off toward the middle of the table so the moving eyeball ball + bumpers pop. Rendering: photographed-clay feel — soft studio light, subtle grain, NO vector-flat, NO neon glow-blowout, NO photoreal humans, NO text / watermark / UI baked into sprites (letters are drawn by the engine). Everything reads at small size on a 540x960 phone table. Consistent top-down view for all playfield pieces (no perspective tilt). Deliver each sprite knocked out on a flat magenta #FF00FF background.
```

## Sheet layout

Generate the whole sheet at **2048x2048**, a strict **4 columns × 4 rows** grid of identical **512x512** cells (equal margins and gutters so a fixed-pitch cutter can slice it blind). Pure flat **magenta #FF00FF** knockout fills every cell and all the gutters — no gradient, no texture, no cast shadow on the background. Each sprite sits centered in its cell with a small tight contact shadow only, nothing touching the cell edges. 13 cells are used; leave the trailing 3 cells (row 4, cols 2-4 onward) as clean flat magenta.

Row assignment (top to bottom):
- **Row 1 — Bumper heads, IDLE:** `bumper_head_a_idle` | `bumper_head_b_idle` | `bumper_head_c_idle` | (empty)
- **Row 2 — Bumper heads, LIT/chomp:** `bumper_head_a_lit` | `bumper_head_b_lit` | `bumper_head_c_lit` | (empty)
- **Row 3 — Sling blobs:** `sling_l_idle` | `sling_l_kick` | `sling_r_idle` | `sling_r_kick`
- **Row 4 — SLIME standup tile (3 states):** `standup_idle` | `standup_lit` | `standup_done` | (empty)

On-table render sizes (draw the clay to fill its cell so it downscales crisp): heads A and B render at ~**48px** (r24), head C the big one at ~**52px** (r26); sling blobs at ~**26px** (r13); standup tiles at ~**24px** (r12). Keep all three heads roughly one visual family so they read as a set, but clearly three different critters.
