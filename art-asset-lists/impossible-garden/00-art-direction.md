# Impossible Garden — Art Pack

> Turn the arms until paths that could never meet line up, then walk the wanderer home to the bloom.

**Genre:** Monument Valley style isometric tile-graph puzzle (single-file HTML5, 540×960 portrait — a canvas play field plus DOM menu screens for title / levels / win / wardrobe / gallery / settings). Eight handcrafted "gardens" of floating iso blocks drift in a twilight void. Tapping a **mechanism dial** cycles its state; an active state opens an **impossible-seam bridge** between two tiles that are NOT adjacent in world space (sometimes not even at the same height) but visually kiss on screen — the game's whole magic trick. The little **wanderer** BFS-pathfinds across static edges + open seams when you tap a tile; reaching the **goal bloom** makes the garden flower. Three modes — **Journey** (solve each to open the next), **Daily Path** (one date-seeded garden a day, streak tracked), **Zen** (any garden, auto-hints) — and every solve presses a **keepsake bloom** into the Gallery. There is no way to lose.

_The game already ships and plays procedurally (`satellites/impossible-garden/index.html`) — this art is an optional visual upgrade **and** the cosmetics library for the in-game wardrobe. Tile graph, seam logic, tap targets, palette hex values and the shape-cues are IDENTICAL across every theme below; only the skin changes._

## Pick a look — theme direction

⚖️ The mechanic is impossible ARCHITECTURE, not horticulture — the reskin is free to leave the flowerbed. The engine keeps exactly two botanical anchors in code (the goal bloom sprite and the wanderer's leaf-sprout), so any architecture theme still reconciles with the win copy ("The garden blooms"). Three directions, cozy → polished, all kid-friendly:

### 1. Lantern Terraces (cozy, botanical)
*Hanging-garden terraces cut from layered paper and gouache: mossy stepped planters, warm paper lanterns at the seams, string-light bridges, a soft dusk wash.* Warmest and most literal reading of the name, and closest to the Lucid Winds house garden. But it keeps the game inside the botanical lane the Director wants options outside of, and a Monument Valley remake earns more prestige than another planter box — the terraces also visually busy the clean iso silhouettes the puzzle depends on.

### 2. Moonstone Monument ⭐ RECOMMENDED (polished, dreamlike, non-botanical architecture)
*A sacred-geometry dream ruin: pale carved moonstone and alabaster blocks floating in an indigo twilight void, tops glazed in the palette's color like enamel inlay, seams bridged by threads of living light, mechanism dials carved like tiny astrolabes. The only green things alive are the wanderer's sprout and the single gold bloom waiting at the goal — a garden reduced to its last flower, which IS the game's fiction.* **Why this one:** it is the true Monument Valley heir — quiet monumental architecture, not foliage — and it is **native to the shipped stage**: the play void already renders `#141526 → #0c0b14 → #08070d` with a violet title glow, so the indigo dream-void costs nothing in integration. The three cosmetic palettes (Twilight sage / Amethyst violet / Ember copper) read perfectly as three enamel glazes on the same pale stone, which makes the tint-template pipeline clean. Carved stone keeps silhouettes razor-legible at iso scale (the puzzle is READ, not reacted to), and "one flower in a stone dream" makes the solve bloom land harder than a garden already full of flowers ever could. Kid-friendly (rounded, softly lit, a big-eyed sprout hero), yet the most premium-looking option on a portal card. **All sheets below bake this look in.**

### 3. Dream Origami (bold, fully non-botanical)
*A folded-paper pocket world: every block a crisp origami form with visible fold lines, bridges as unfolding paper strips, the wanderer a little paper charm, confetti solve.* The biggest theme jump and charming at thumbnail, but sharp fold facets fight the 2:1 iso diamond the engine actually draws, paper white wants a bright field (the locked stage is a dark void), and the goal "bloom" turning to paper undercuts the living keepsake gallery. Hold it in reserve as a future palette-tier skin idea.

**Recommendation: Moonstone Monument.** Truest to the Monument Valley source, lowest integration risk (the indigo void and all six palette/skin hex families are already in code), leaves the forced-botanical lane while keeping the two botanical anchors the code literally draws, and its neutral-stone base makes all three cosmetic palettes cheap to produce as glaze tints.

## Cosmetics economy

Every Impossible Garden cosmetic is earned by **PLAYING** — no loot boxes, no randomized purchases, nothing that touches the tile graph, seam logic, tap radii or hints. Every unlock is a **KNOWN threshold shown on its locked wardrobe card** (the code prints "Solve N" right on the card). One earn currency drives everything, already wired in `localStorage` key **`impossiblegarden_save`**: **gardens solved** = `solvedCount()`, the count of true entries in `SAVE.solved` (Journey/Zen solves write `L0`–`L7`, Daily solves write `d<idx>` — all count).

- **Wanderer skins** (`SAVE.skin`, `SKINS[]`, each a body color the engine paints in `drawWalker`): **Sprout** `#eafbd6` (starter, free) → **Dewdrop** `#bfe0f2` (solve **1** garden) → **Rosewalk** `#e58fa0` (solve **3**) → **Lantern** `#ffe9a8` (solve **5**).
- **Palette glazes** (`SAVE.pal`, `PALS[]`, each retints tile tops/faces, start tile, goal bloom and seam arms): **Twilight** (starter, free — top `#7ab356`, faces `#4f7d38`/`#3a5c2a`, start `#5b9bd5`, goal `#c8a84b`, arm `#a6d77f`) → **Amethyst** (solve **2** — top `#b57de0`, faces `#7e56a0`/`#5c3f76`, start `#e58fa0`, goal `#ffe9a8`, arm `#d3b0f0`) → **Ember** (solve **4** — top `#d4842a`, faces `#9c5f1e`/`#6f4415`, start `#e56b6b`, goal `#ffe9a8`, arm `#f0b070`).
- **Keepsake Gallery** (`SAVE.gallery`, seed-deduped, **cap 60**): every solved garden presses a procedural keepsake bloom (5–9 petals) — the collection display itself is a cosmetic surface (frames/empty slots on the cosmetics sheet).
- **Tracked but gating nothing (display only — do NOT invent unlocks on them):** no-hint solves (`SAVE.noHint`, shown as ✿✿ on level cards and counted in the wardrobe header) and the Daily streak (`SAVE.daily.streak`).

Skins and palettes are **purely visual** — they never change tile adjacency, seam solutions, tap hitboxes or payouts. Sunbeams stay the earn currency only (`_sbCapEarn`: **30/day** device cap; per-run-day cap **12** via `SAVE.runEarn`; **+2** first solve of a garden in Journey/Zen, **+4** for the Daily, once per day).
