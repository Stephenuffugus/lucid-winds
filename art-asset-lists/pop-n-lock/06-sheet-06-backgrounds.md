# Sheet 06 — Backgrounds
Two full-bleed 1080x1920 portrait backdrops for Pop N Lock: the night alley battle scene that sits behind both puzzle boards, and the blank burner wall that sits behind the title menu. No characters, no readable text on either.

```

EVERY character is an animal B-BOY in SHINY NYLON PARACHUTE PANTS — baggy MC Hammer style, billowing at the thigh, cinched at the ankle, glossy wet-nylon sheen with bright specular folds — plus 80s streetwear: high-top sneakers with fat laces, kangol and bucket hats, sweatbands, gold rope chains, boomboxes, cassette tapes, windbreakers. ## Sheet layout
This sheet is NOT a grid and uses NO magenta knockout. It is TWO separate standalone generations, each a full-bleed opaque 1080x1920 portrait image that runs edge to edge with zero transparency. Generate each one from its own copy-paste prompt below, export as its own file, and keep each export under 300KB (JPEG quality ~70 to 80 is plenty at this darkness level).

- Generation 1 → `bg/battle-alley` — 1080x1920 portrait, full bleed, opaque
- Generation 2 → `bg/menu-wall` — 1080x1920 portrait, full bleed, opaque

There are no cells, no gutters, and nothing stays empty. Every pixel is painted scene.

## Assets
- **bg/battle-alley** — The versus match backdrop. A night-time brick alley seen straight on: weathered brick in deep desaturated brown-red sitting on the near-black #0c0a06 base, a chain-link fence panel catching cool moonlight along the top edge, and layered abstract graffiti throwies in hot magenta #FF2FB9, electric cyan #2FF0E0, voltage yellow #FFD21A, and neon lime #39FF14 climbing the left and right thirds of the wall — bubbly unreadable shapes only, pure abstract flow, never letterforms. Ground level dresses the outer edges: one warm streetlamp pool of voltage yellow #FFD21A spilling down the left side, a chunky cel-shaded boombox with cyan speaker glow and a stack of milk crates on the right, a curb line and a puddle catching an ultra purple #C24BFF neon reflection. The ENTIRE CENTER THIRD stays dark, low-contrast, and low-detail — plain shadowed brick fading toward #0c0a06 — so the two game boards read cleanly on top of it. No characters anywhere, cool moonlight fill overall, neon rim glow on edges only.
- **bg/menu-wall** — The title and menu backdrop. A straight-on subway-tile and brick wall prepped for a masterpiece burner: grimy off-white tile rows across the lower half, aged brick above, both dimmed onto the #0c0a06 night base. The center of the wall is a large clean BLANK patch, primed and empty like a fresh canvas, softly lit and free of detail — the engine drops the logo and buttons there, so nothing may occupy it. Around that blank zone: a hot magenta #FF2FB9 neon wash bleeding in from an off-screen sign on the left, an electric cyan #2FF0E0 wash from the right, faint abstract throwie ghosts of neon lime #39FF14 and ultra purple #C24BFF peeking at the far edges. On the ground line sit a cluster of cel-shaded spray cans with colored caps matching the palette, one tipped over, and a single cassette tape with a voltage yellow #FFD21A label patch left blank. No characters, no readable text, kid-friendly and inviting, like the wall is waiting for the crew to arrive.

## Wire
Both files land in satellites/chaff-wars/ as opaque CSS backgrounds. `bg/battle-alley` is set as the background image on `#stage` (cover, center) so it sits under both puzzle boards during a match; the existing procedural gradient stays in the CSS as the fallback layer if the image fails to load or is not yet dropped. `bg/menu-wall` is set the same way on the menu `.screen` (title, ladder select, and result screens can share it), drawn under the engine's logo, buildLadder rows, and buttons — its blank center is exactly where those elements render. Neither image feeds the opponent HUD card, ladder `.fem` tiles, or `go-emoji` slots; those pull character sprites from the character sheets. Keep both exports under 300KB and cache-bust with the usual `?v=` bump when wiring.
