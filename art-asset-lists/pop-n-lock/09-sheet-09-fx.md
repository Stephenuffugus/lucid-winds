# Sheet 09 — FX Splats, Wipes and Auras
OPTIONAL POLISH TIER (the game ships with procedural FX and never requires this sheet): 16 animation frames of paint splat bursts, grey chaff splats, the ALL CLEAR rainbow buff wipe, and the neon power cast aura, drawn tint-friendly so the engine can color them per pod and per player.

```
STYLE — "Neon Boombox" (Pop N Lock). 1987 breakdance movie poster meets Saturday-morning cartoon: chunky cel-shaded anthropomorphic ANIMAL characters with THICK black comic outlines, neon airbrush shading, spray-paint glow edges, subtle halftone dot texture. EVERY character is an animal B-BOY in SHINY NYLON PARACHUTE PANTS — baggy MC Hammer style, billowing at the thigh, cinched at the ankle, glossy wet-nylon sheen with bright specular folds — plus 80s streetwear: high-top sneakers with fat laces, kangol and bucket hats, sweatbands, gold rope chains, boomboxes, cassette tapes, windbreakers. Cheerful swagger, kid-friendly, big expressive cartoon eyes, NO menace, NO gore. Palette: night-alley near-black #0c0a06 base; hot magenta #FF2FB9; electric cyan #2FF0E0; voltage yellow #FFD21A; neon lime #39FF14; ultra purple #C24BFF; plus each character's own accent (listed per sheet). Lighting: neon-sign rim light from one side, cool moonlight fill, characters pop off dark backgrounds. Rendering: bold flat cels + airbrush gradients, NO photorealism, NO 3D render, NO text/watermark baked into character sprites (words are drawn by the engine or live on the dedicated logo sheet). Deliver every sprite knocked out on flat magenta #FF00FF.
```

## Sheet layout
Generate the whole sheet at 2048x2048 as a strict 4x4 grid of identical 512x512 cells (equal pitch in both axes so a fixed-pitch cutter can slice it blind at 512px steps). Pure flat magenta #FF00FF fills every cell background and all space between sprites. Each sprite is centered in its cell with at least 48px of clear magenta padding on every side; nothing touches a cell edge. Each row is one 4-frame animation read left to right, frames of a row sized and positioned consistently so they align when played back to back. All 16 cells are used; no cell stays empty.

Row assignments:
- R1C1 `fx/pop-splat-1` · R1C2 `fx/pop-splat-2` · R1C3 `fx/pop-splat-3` · R1C4 `fx/pop-splat-4`
- R2C1 `fx/chaff-splat-1` · R2C2 `fx/chaff-splat-2` · R2C3 `fx/chaff-splat-3` · R2C4 `fx/chaff-splat-4`
- R3C1 `fx/buff-wipe-1` · R3C2 `fx/buff-wipe-2` · R3C3 `fx/buff-wipe-3` · R3C4 `fx/buff-wipe-4`
- R4C1 `fx/cast-aura-1` · R4C2 `fx/cast-aura-2` · R4C3 `fx/cast-aura-3` · R4C4 `fx/cast-aura-4`

## Assets
No characters on this sheet, so no outfits and no parachute pants: pure effect sprites only, in the same thick-outline airbrush cartoon language as the rest of the pack. Rows 1, 2 and 4 are deliberately NEUTRAL white and grey so the engine can tint them to any pod or player accent color at runtime; only Row 3 uses the neon palette directly.

- **fx/pop-splat-1** — Frame 1 of the pod pop, TIGHT IMPACT: a compact star-shaped paint splat, 7 chunky jagged spikes radiating from a dense round core, drawn small (about 45% of the cell). Body is pure white #FFFFFF with a light grey #C7CCD2 airbrush ring near the core, thick black comic outline, a sprinkle of halftone dots inside the spikes. It reads like fresh paint smacking a wall the instant of contact. Keep it perfectly neutral in hue so code can tint it magenta, cyan, yellow, lime or purple per pod.
- **fx/pop-splat-2** — Frame 2, WIDE BURST: the splat at maximum spread, a big ragged paint star filling about 75% of the cell, spikes longer and splitting at the tips, with 8 to 10 fat round droplets flying outward between them. White #FFFFFF fill, grey #C7CCD2 airbrush shading pooled at the center, thick black outlines on the main mass and every droplet. This is the money frame, the loudest silhouette of the four.
- **fx/pop-splat-3** — Frame 3, DRIPPY BLOOM: the burst collapsing into a rounded gooey blob with 4 long glossy drips sagging off its lower edge, tips bulging like wet paint about to fall, plus 3 or 4 stray droplets still hanging in the air. White #FFFFFF with a soft grey #C7CCD2 belly shadow and one bright specular streak per drip. Outlines stay thick but the silhouette is soft and rounded now, all the energy draining downward.
- **fx/pop-splat-4** — Frame 4, FADING MIST: the splat dissolving into a loose airy cloud of small dots, thin broken outline rings and halftone speckle, mostly open magenta space between the marks. Palest tones of the row, white #FFFFFF and light grey #DDE1E6, outlines thinning and breaking apart. Reads as the last puff of spray hanging in the alley air.
- **fx/chaff-splat-1** — Frame 1 of the Chaff landing, GREY BLOB HIT: a dull concrete grey #8A8F98 paint blob squashing flat on impact, pancaked wide with 5 short blunt impact spikes, a darker grey #565B63 core and one weak flat highlight. Thick black outline, matte finish, zero neon, zero sparkle. It should feel like a wack tag next to Row 1's fresh burners: heavy, dull, unwanted.
- **fx/chaff-splat-2** — Frame 2, DRIPS: the grey blob sagging under its own weight, 3 thick sludgy drips oozing down with bulbous tips, the top edge slumping. Same greys, #8A8F98 body over #565B63 shadow, one drab #A9ADB4 highlight so it still reads on dark boards. Slow and gloopy where Row 1 was fast and juicy.
- **fx/chaff-splat-3** — Frame 3, SETTLE: the drips have landed and merged into one squat rounded wack-tag blob, edges dried and slightly crusty with a few tiny nicks in the outline, a faint darker #565B63 rim where the paint pooled. Matte #8A8F98 with a single dull highlight. This is the resting look of a Chaff pod, so keep the silhouette simple and readable at small sizes.
- **fx/chaff-splat-4** — Frame 4, DUST: a soft puff of grey dust motes, crumb specks and 2 thin broken rings dispersing outward, the blob itself gone. Light greys #A9ADB4 and #C7CCD2, sparse halftone, lots of open magenta. The fizzle-out frame for when Chaff gets cleared.
- **fx/buff-wipe-1** — Frame 1 of the ALL CLEAR celebration: a tall vertical spray wave just getting rolling on the left side of the sprite area, a glowing electric cyan #2FF0E0 leading edge backed by a band of hot magenta #FF2FB9, with a scatter of small dull grey #8A8F98 flecks floating ahead of the wave to its right, still uncolored. Spray-paint glow edge on the wave front, thick outline on the main band, subtle halftone in the glow.
- **fx/buff-wipe-2** — Frame 2: the wave a third of the way across, now a full rainbow front, bands of cyan #2FF0E0, magenta #FF2FB9, voltage yellow #FFD21A and neon lime #39FF14 stacked in the wave, with ultra purple #C24BFF mist trailing behind it. Grey flecks caught by the wave are mid-transformation, half grey #8A8F98 and half lit in neon; flecks ahead of it stay grey. Motion streaks pull left to right.
- **fx/buff-wipe-3** — Frame 3: the wave two thirds across and at peak glow, the biggest brightest frame of the row, full five-color rainbow band with a white-hot #FFFFFF crest line, colored star sparkles and neon droplets filling the area behind it, only 2 or 3 grey flecks left ahead of the front. Airbrush bloom around every sparkle, thick outlines on the wave body.
- **fx/buff-wipe-4** — Frame 4: the wave exiting the right side of the sprite, no grey left anywhere, the whole area a joyful drift of neon sparkles, thin rainbow arcs and floating droplets in cyan #2FF0E0, magenta #FF2FB9, yellow #FFD21A, lime #39FF14 and purple #C24BFF, all beginning to fade with breaking outlines. Fresh, clean, victorious.
- **fx/cast-aura-1** — Frame 1 of the power wind-up: a single thin circular ring, small (about 40% of the cell), pure white #FFFFFF line with a faint grey #C7CCD2 glow halo and 4 tiny spark ticks sitting on the ring at the cardinal points. Thick clean outline, perfectly centered, neutral hue so the engine can tint it to the casting player's accent color.
- **fx/cast-aura-2** — Frame 2: the ring grown to about 60% of the cell and doubled, an inner and outer ring with short radial tick marks between them like a clock face, 6 spark ticks now, glow slightly brighter. Still strictly white #FFFFFF and greys #C7CCD2 over #A9ADB4, thick outlines, centered.
- **fx/cast-aura-3** — Frame 3, NEAR FULL CHARGE: a thick bold ring at about 75% of the cell with a soft white-hot inner glow disc, 8 zigzag energy ticks dancing on the rim (bouncy and cartoonish, nothing scary), halftone shimmer inside the disc. Brightest whites of the row, grey #C7CCD2 only in the outer falloff. The frame should visibly vibrate with held-in power.
- **fx/cast-aura-4** — Frame 4, FULL FLARE: the ring bursting outward with 8 radiating spokes, star sparkles at the spoke tips and a blazing white #FFFFFF center flash, outline fragments of the old ring flying off. Largest frame of the row but still fully inside the padding. Keep every mark neutral white and grey #C7CCD2 so the runtime tint owns the color.

## Wire
All four rows feed `satellites/chaff-wars/index.html` as an optional polish layer behind image loaded checks, with the current procedural flashes kept as the permanent fallback. `fx/pop-splat-1..4` and `fx/chaff-splat-1..4` hook the pop sub-state inside `resolveTick`: when a group clears, the renderer plays the 4 white frames tinted to the popped pod's color (canvas tint of the neutral white art), and Chaff clears play the grey row untinted. `fx/buff-wipe-1..4` backs the ALL CITY float, sweeping the board once left to right on an all clear before the score text pops. `fx/cast-aura-1..4` winds up `firePower` in Powers mode, tinted to the casting player's accent color, with frame 4 landing on the exact tick the power resolves. Nothing on this sheet touches the opponent HUD card, ladder tiles or result screen; those pull from the character sheets.
