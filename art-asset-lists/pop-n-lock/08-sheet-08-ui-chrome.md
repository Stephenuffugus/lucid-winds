# Sheet 08 — UI Chrome Plates (Banners + Buttons + Bursts + Room Card)

Every text free UI chrome plate for the Pop N Lock shell — versus frame, three result banner plates, two mode button pills, the next pods window, the POWER cap in lit and unlit states, four escalating countdown bursts, and the cassette room card — all blank centered so the engine draws every single word.

```
STYLE — "Neon Boombox" (Pop N Lock). 1987 breakdance movie poster meets Saturday-morning cartoon: chunky cel-shaded anthropomorphic ANIMAL characters with THICK black comic outlines, neon airbrush shading, spray-paint glow edges, subtle halftone dot texture. EVERY character is an animal B-BOY in SHINY NYLON PARACHUTE PANTS — baggy MC Hammer style, billowing at the thigh, cinched at the ankle, glossy wet-nylon sheen with bright specular folds — plus 80s streetwear: high-top sneakers with fat laces, kangol and bucket hats, sweatbands, gold rope chains, boomboxes, cassette tapes, windbreakers. Cheerful swagger, kid-friendly, big expressive cartoon eyes, NO menace, NO gore. Palette: night-alley near-black #0c0a06 base; hot magenta #FF2FB9; electric cyan #2FF0E0; voltage yellow #FFD21A; neon lime #39FF14; ultra purple #C24BFF; plus each character's own accent (listed per sheet). Lighting: neon-sign rim light from one side, cool moonlight fill, characters pop off dark backgrounds. Rendering: bold flat cels + airbrush gradients, NO photorealism, NO 3D render, NO text/watermark baked into character sprites (words are drawn by the engine or live on the dedicated logo sheet). Deliver every sprite knocked out on flat magenta #FF00FF.
```

## Sheet layout

Generate the whole sheet at **2048x2048**, a strict **4 columns × 4 rows** grid of identical **512x512** cells (equal margins and gutters so a fixed-pitch cutter can slice it blind). Pure flat **magenta #FF00FF** knockout fills every cell and all the gutters — no gradient, no texture, no cast shadow on the background. Each plate sits centered in its cell, drawn big so it downscales crisp, nothing touching the cell edges. **14 cells are used; leave the last 2 cells (row 4, cols 3-4) as clean flat magenta.** No character appears on this sheet, so no parachute pants here — this is pure chrome. Sheet accents (this sheet only, per the STYLE accent rule): buffed grey #9a948c, drip grey #55524c, label cream #ece7dc.

EVERY plate on this sheet is 100 percent TEXT FREE — blank centers, blank labels, blank windows. The engine draws all words, numerals, and codes. Zero letters, zero numbers, zero tags baked into the art.

Row assignment (top to bottom):
- **Row 1 — Versus + result banners:** `ui/vs-frame` | `ui/banner-plate-win` | `ui/banner-plate-lose` | `ui/banner-plate-allclear`
- **Row 2 — Buttons + preview:** `ui/mode-plate-a` | `ui/mode-plate-b` | `ui/next-window` | `ui/power-orb-lit`
- **Row 3 — Countdown bursts, escalating:** `ui/countdown-burst-1` | `ui/countdown-burst-2` | `ui/countdown-burst-3` | `ui/countdown-burst-go`
- **Row 4 — Room card + orb sleep state:** `ui/room-card` | `ui/power-orb-unlit` | (empty) | (empty)

Both power orb cells must be the exact same geometry and size so the engine can hot swap them on one element.
