## Sheet layout

Generate the whole sheet at **2048x2048**, a strict **3 columns × 3 rows** grid of identical **~682x682** cells (equal margins and gutters so a fixed-pitch cutter can slice it blind). Pure flat **magenta #FF00FF** knockout fills every cell and all the gutters — no gradient, no texture, no cast shadow on the background. Each character sits centered in its cell, FULL BODY with feet and high-tops fully visible (the parachute pants and sneakers are the signature — never crop them), nothing touching the cell edges. **All 9 cells are used; no empty cells.** COLUMNS are the three characters (left to right: Duchess Dapple, Lady Bindweed, Miss Mildew); ROWS are the poses (row 1 idle, row 2 win, row 3 lose). Each character must be the SAME recognizable design in all three of her cells: same fur/vine/spore colors, same pants, same hat and gear. NO text, letters, tags, or throwies anywhere on this sheet.

Row assignment (top to bottom):
- **Row 1 — IDLE:** `char/deer-duchess-idle` | `char/bindweed-lady-idle` | `char/powdery-mildew-idle`
- **Row 2 — WIN:** `char/deer-duchess-win` | `char/bindweed-lady-win` | `char/powdery-mildew-win`
- **Row 3 — LOSE:** `char/deer-duchess-lose` | `char/bindweed-lady-lose` | `char/powdery-mildew-lose`

Per-character accents for this sheet: Duchess Dapple rose neon **#FF5C8A** + gold lamé **#FFD21A**; Lady Bindweed blossom magenta **#FF3BC0** + leaf lime **#39FF14**; Miss Mildew lavender grey **#B8A6C8** + toxic lime **#39FF14**.

## Wire

All nine cut sprites feed `satellites/chaff-wars/index.html` for the Pop N Lock skin. Each `char/<id>-idle` replaces the emoji drawn on the opponent HUD card during the HUD pass (keep `st.em` as the fallback when the PNG is missing) and fills that opponent's ladder row tile in `buildLadder` (the `.fem` element). `char/<id>-win` and `char/<id>-lose` swap onto the result screen in place of the `go-emoji` glyph — the loser's lose art when the player wins the match, the winner's win art when the player drops the round — and the idle/win pair is the source for the planned pre-match VS splash screen.
