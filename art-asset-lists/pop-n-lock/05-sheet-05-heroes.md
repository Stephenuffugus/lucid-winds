## Sheet layout

Generate the whole sheet at **2048x2048**, a strict **3 columns × 3 rows** grid of identical **~682x682** cells (equal margins and gutters so a fixed-pitch cutter can slice it blind). Pure flat **magenta #FF00FF** knockout fills every cell and all the gutters — no gradient, no texture, no cast shadow on the background. Each character sits centered in its cell, FULL BODY with feet and high-tops fully visible (the parachute pants and sneakers are the signature — never crop them), nothing touching the cell edges. All **9 cells are used**, no empties.

COLUMNS are the three characters, ROWS are the poses. Row assignment (top to bottom):
- **Row 1 — IDLE:** `char/baron-greymould-idle` | `char/ronin-hare-idle` | `char/keeper-idle`
- **Row 2 — WIN:** `char/baron-greymould-win` | `char/ronin-hare-win` | `char/keeper-win`
- **Row 3 — LOSE:** `char/baron-greymould-lose` | `char/ronin-hare-lose` | `char/keeper-lose`

Each character must read as the SAME design in all three of their cells — same colors, same pants, same gear, same face. In game these portraits render tiny (~52px ladder tiles, ~60px HUD card, ~96px result screen), so silhouettes must stay bold and readable at thumbnail size. This is the HERO sheet: crank the drama on the Baron (sickly green power radiating off the King) and the Ronin (one-red-sun minimalism); keep the Keeper as the calm center of the whole roster.

## Wire

These nine portraits feed `satellites/chaff-wars/index.html`. `char/<id>-idle` replaces the emoji draw in the opponent HUD pass (the `ctx.fillText(st.em, ...)` call around line 779 becomes a drawImage of the foe's idle portrait, keeping `st.em` as the fallback if the sheet fails to load) and fills the `buildLadder()` roster rows (each `.fem` tile at line 851 shows the idle portrait when unlocked, the lock glyph otherwise). The result screen swaps `go-emoji` (line 635) for art: opponent's `-win` portrait when the pest takes it, the Keeper's `-lose` for the player side, and the Keeper's `-win` on player victory. All three pose sets are also the ready-made pair art for the future VS splash screen (player Keeper vs foe, idle vs idle at intro, win vs lose at the wipe). Baron Greymould is roster #12 (final boss), The Ronin Hare is #13 (secret duel), keeper is the player-side card everywhere.
