## Sheet layout

Generate the whole sheet at **2048x2048**, a strict **3 columns × 3 rows** grid of identical **~682x682** cells (equal margins and gutters so a fixed-pitch cutter can slice it blind). Pure flat **magenta #FF00FF** knockout fills every cell and all the gutters — no gradient, no texture, no cast shadow on the background. Each sprite sits centered in its cell, nothing touching the cell edges. **All 9 cells are used** — no empty cells on this sheet.

COLUMNS are the three characters (left to right: Escargeddon, Cawlin the Crow, Gustavo the Gopher); ROWS are the poses (row 1 idle, row 2 win, row 3 lose). Every character must read as the SAME design in all 3 of their cells — same colors, same pants, same gear. Full body with parachute pants, cinched ankles, and high-tops visible in every cell EXCEPT Gustavo's manhole gag cells (idle and lose), where the manhole ring itself is part of the sprite — the hole hides him by design, the cell never crops him. No text, letters, or tag graffiti anywhere on this sheet: spray cans stay blank, the cassette door stays blank.

Row assignment (top to bottom):
- **Row 1 — IDLE:** `char/garden-snail-idle` | `char/crow-cawlin-idle` | `char/gopher-gustavo-idle`
- **Row 2 — WIN:** `char/garden-snail-win` | `char/crow-cawlin-win` | `char/gopher-gustavo-win`
- **Row 3 — LOSE:** `char/garden-snail-lose` | `char/crow-cawlin-lose` | `char/gopher-gustavo-lose`

On-screen these render tiny — 52px ladder tiles, ~34px on the HUD card, 46px on the result screen — so keep silhouettes chunky and open, one big readable shape per cell.
