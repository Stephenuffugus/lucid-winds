The first three Pop N Lock crew characters in three poses each (idle, win, lose) — full-body b-boy animal portraits, parachute pants and high-tops always in frame, cut-ready on magenta.


## Sheet layout

Generate the whole sheet at **2048x2048**, a strict **3 columns × 3 rows** grid of identical **~682x682** cells (equal margins and gutters so a fixed-pitch cutter can slice it blind). Pure flat **magenta #FF00FF** knockout fills every cell and all the gutters — no gradient, no texture, no cast shadow on the background. Each sprite sits centered in its cell, FULL BODY with feet and high-tops fully visible, nothing touching the cell edges. All **9 cells are used**, no empties.

COLUMNS are the three characters (left to right: The Aphid Swarm, Gnat King Cole, Mabel Cabbagewing). ROWS are the poses (top to bottom: idle, win, lose). Each character must be recognizably the SAME design in all three of their cells — same colors, same parachute pants, same gear.

Row assignment (top to bottom):
- **Row 1 — IDLE:** `char/aphid-swarm-idle` | `char/gnat-king-cole-idle` | `char/cabbage-moth-idle`
- **Row 2 — WIN:** `char/aphid-swarm-win` | `char/gnat-king-cole-win` | `char/cabbage-moth-win`
- **Row 3 — LOSE:** `char/aphid-swarm-lose` | `char/gnat-king-cole-lose` | `char/cabbage-moth-lose`

Character accents for this sheet: aphid body lime #39FF14, gnat body electric blue #2E8BFF, moth body cream #F2E9D8 + cyan #22E0E0. No text anywhere on this sheet — no tags, no throwies, no lettering of any kind.

## Wire

These nine cuts feed `satellites/chaff-wars/index.html` as the Pop N Lock skin's character art. The idle frame replaces the emoji draw on the opponent HUD card in the HUD pass (falling back to `st.em` if the PNG is missing), and the same idle frame fills the ladder rows in `buildLadder` as the `.fem` tile for each of these three opponents. The win and lose frames swap in on the result screen where `go-emoji` currently renders (opponent lost = their lose frame, opponent won = their win frame), and the idle/win pair is reserved for the future VS intro screen face-off.
