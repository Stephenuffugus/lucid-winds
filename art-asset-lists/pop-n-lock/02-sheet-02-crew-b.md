The second half of the Pop N Lock crew — three animal b-boys (a posh slug, a stout cutworm, an armored june beetle) each in idle, win, and lose poses, full body, cut-ready for the versus HUD and ladder.

## Sheet layout

Generate the whole sheet at **2048x2048**, a strict **3 columns × 3 rows** grid of identical **~682x682** cells (equal margins and gutters so a fixed-pitch cutter can slice it blind). Pure flat **magenta #FF00FF** knockout fills every cell and all the gutters — no gradient, no texture, no cast shadow on the background. Each character sits centered in its cell, FULL BODY with feet/underside and the parachute pants completely visible (the pants + kicks are the signature — never crop them), nothing touching cell edges. All **9 cells are used** — no empties on this sheet.

**COLUMNS = characters** (left to right: Sir Reginald Slugmore, Chompers the Cutworm, Baron von Beetle). **ROWS = poses** (row 1 idle, row 2 win, row 3 lose). Each character must read as the SAME design straight down his column — same body color, same pants, same gear, same face.

Row assignment (top to bottom):
- **Row 1 — IDLE:** `char/slug-slugmore-idle` | `char/cutworm-idle` | `char/june-beetle-idle`
- **Row 2 — WIN:** `char/slug-slugmore-win` | `char/cutworm-win` | `char/june-beetle-win`
- **Row 3 — LOSE:** `char/slug-slugmore-lose` | `char/cutworm-lose` | `char/june-beetle-lose`

Character accents this sheet: Slugmore lavender **#B06FE0**, Chompers neon orange **#FF7A18**, Baron metallic teal **#1FC7B0**. Portraits render small in the HUD (~72px card) and ladder (~48px tile), so keep silhouettes chunky and poses readable at thumbnail size.

## Wire

These nine cut PNGs feed `satellites/chaff-wars/index.html` as the crew B opponent art: the `-idle` sprite replaces the emoji draw on the opponent HUD card in the HUD pass (keep `st.em` as the emoji fallback when the PNG misses), and the same idle art fills that character's ladder row tile in `buildLadder` (`.fem` tiles). The `-win` and `-lose` sprites swap in on the result screen in place of the `go-emoji` glyph — opponent shows `-lose` when the player wins and `-win` when the player drops. All three poses are also the source frames for the future VS splash screen (player character left, opponent right, idle poses facing off).
