# Root Groups — Art Pack

> Sixteen tiles hide four secret threads — tap the four that belong together, watch each group bloom into its shelf, and the deviousest root of all drops a seed in your pouch.

**Genre:** NYT Connections remake (single-file HTML5, 540×960 portrait stage — but this game is **fully DOM-based, not canvas-drawn**: the board is a CSS-grid of 16 live-text tile divs (20 in Tangle), solved groups become tinted DOM banner rows above the board, and every screen is styled DOM — there is no canvas anywhere). Tap **four tiles** that share a hidden thread, Submit; a correct group **blooms** into a colored banner, a wrong guess **wilts** and spends one of **four guess buds**. Groups run **easy to devious** across tiers 1-4 (1-5 in Tangle), each tier marked by a toggleable **tier symbol** (🌱 🌿 🍀 🌸 ⭐ — the game's real colorblind aid, `SET.sym`). A **Hint** costs 3 Dew and gold-pins one tile to its group; solving the hardest **root group** drops a feral seed into your pouch. Four modes: **Grove Groups** (endless), **Daily Roots** (date-seeded, streak), **Zen Sort** (no fail, no earn), **Tangle** (5 groups, expert). A 40-category bank with a conflict-rejecting generator guarantees a unique solution, and the **Category Compendium** logs every category you have ever untangled.

_The game already ships and plays procedurally (`satellites/root-groups/index.html`) — this art is an optional visual upgrade **and** the cosmetics library for the in-game customization economy. Mechanics, the grid, guess buds, tier order, hint costs and every threshold are IDENTICAL across every theme below; only the skin changes. Critical constraint for a word game: **every tile label, group name and button caption is live DOM text** — the art supplies letterless plates, banners, pins and blooms underneath it, so NOTHING in any sheet may contain text or letterforms._

## Pick a look — theme direction

⚖️ **Director note:** no forced-botanical house style. The mechanic here is pure word-sorting — nothing about a 4×4 category grid needs a garden — and the real category bank is only half botanical (Gems, Metals, Planets, Instruments, Wild Cats, Sea Life, Shapes, Numbers live right beside Flowers and Herbs). So the boldest option below leaves the garden entirely, and it happens to be the one the game's own cosmetics ladder was secretly designed for.

### 1. Midnight Curio ⭐ RECOMMENDED (polished, kid-friendly, non-botanical)
*A warm cabinet of wonders after closing time. The sixteen tiles are letterless specimen labels pinned to a brass-railed curio wall; finding a group slides its collection drawer shut with a satisfying bloom of lamplight; the Compendium is the museum's leather catalog and the root group is the deepest drawer — the one with a living root curled inside, still dropping seeds. Deep moss-black shadow, honeyed brass, soft lamplight; everything rounded, chunky, huggable — an attic of wonder, never spooky.*

**Why this one:** the game's REAL cosmetics ladder is literally a materials ladder — **Paper → Wood → Slate → Stained Glass** tile themes — and a curiosity cabinet is the one fiction where paper labels, wooden drawer fronts, slate plaques and leaded-glass panes are all native objects rather than skins. It is also the only fiction that honestly holds all 40 categories (a botany drawer AND a gem tray AND a planet chart AND an instrument case), where a garden theme has to shrug at half its own content. Integration risk is near zero: the game's locked palette IS the cabinet — gold `#c8a84b` is the brass, sage `#7ab356` the patina, cream `#e8dcc8` the lamplit labels, the moss-black screens the shadowed shelves. The five tier banners become five enamel drawer-fronts, the tier symbols become collector's pins (silhouette-first, so the colorblind toggle gets stronger, not weaker), and the Compendium finally looks like what it is: a catalog of curiosities. **All sheets below bake this look in.**

### 2. Seed Packet Bench (alt, cozy — the botanical option)
*A moonlit potting bench: the tiles are vintage letterless seed-packet faces laid on dark felt, correct groups sprout into a window-box banner, the root group tips a real seed into a burlap pouch.* Warmest and most native to the game's own copy ("bloom", "root", "seeds your garden"), and the cheapest emotional fit for the Lucid Winds portal — but it keeps the game inside the botanical lane the Director wants offered a way out of, it flatters only half the category bank, and paper/wood/slate/stained-glass tile themes have to be explained as bench clutter instead of belonging. Best held as the flavor source for the default Paper theme and the Soft Bloom style, which stay gently botanical inside the Curio look anyway.

### 3. Tangle & Twine (alt, cozy-craft — the basket option)
*A weaver's table: tiles are quilted fabric patches tacked to a corkboard, hidden threads literally connect each group, and solving pulls a colored thread taut until the row cinches into a woven band — the portal's 🧺 basket glyph made whole.* Charming, tactile, and the "untangle the threads" metaphor is the strongest read of the actual puzzle verb — but thread-and-patch detail fights legibility at 64px tile height (the ONE thing a word grid cannot compromise), the craft fiction still stumbles on Planets and Metals, and it is the least premium of the three on a portal card. Keep the taut-thread cinch as inspiration for the solve animation, and a future "Quilt" tile theme would slot straight into the existing `THEMES[]` array.

**Recommendation: Midnight Curio.** It is the non-botanical jump the Director asked to see, the only fiction that fits ALL 40 real categories, and — decisively — the only look where the shipped cosmetics ladder (Paper/Wood/Slate/Stained Glass) reads as four genuine objects of desire instead of four CSS gradients. It rides the locked midnight palette for near-zero integration risk, and it borrows Seed Packet Bench's warmth for the bloom moments so the game still feels like Lucid Winds. **All sheets below bake this look in.**

## Cosmetics economy

Every Root Groups cosmetic is earned by **PLAYING** — no loot boxes, no randomized purchases, nothing that changes a board, a category, a tile or a payout. Everything is a **KNOWN unlock at a KNOWN threshold** printed right on its locked wardrobe card (`renderWard()` shows each locked card's `t` requirement string). Two cosmetic families, both already wired in `localStorage` key **`rootgroups_save`**:

- **Tile themes** (`SAVE.tileTheme`, `THEMES[]`) — the material of the sixteen tiles: *Paper* (starter, free) → **Wood** (solve **10** boards) → **Slate** (solve **30** boards) → **Stained Glass** (solve **60** boards). Gate: `SAVE.boardsSolved` — every won board in ANY mode counts, Zen included (Zen pays no Sunbeams but its wins still increment the counter).
- **Bloom styles** (`SAVE.bloomStyle`, `BLOOMS[]`) — the flourish when a group solves: *Soft Bloom* (starter, free) → **Burst** (**5 flawless** boards) → **Gilded** (**15 flawless** boards). Gate: `SAVE.noMissSolves` — wins with zero mistakes (`G.mistakes===0` at `endBoard(true)`), the mastery faucet.

Both gates run through one check (`unlockedC()`, threshold-only, no purchases). **Honest wiring note:** the wardrobe equip/persist loop is fully live today, but `SAVE.tileTheme` / `SAVE.bloomStyle` are not yet consumed by any renderer — wiring this pack's sheets is what adds the consumption (tile-plate swap on `.cell`, solve-FX variant in `solveGroup()`). Three more collection layers already persist free in the same save and cost the art pack nothing: the **Category Compendium** (`SAVE.solvedCats{}`, 40 categories, the completable set sheet 02 dresses up), the **seed pouch** (`SAVE.pouch`, +1 per root-group solve), and the **Daily Roots streak** (`SAVE.streak`); `SAVE.tangleWins` is also tracked and sits ready as a future cosmetic faucet — no new fields needed.

**Currency lanes stay locked:** Dew is the in-run assist currency only — earned **+tier** (1-5 💧) per solved group in `solveGroup()`, spent **3 💧 per Hint** (free spend in Zen) — it buys NOTHING in the wardrobe, and no cosmetic ever costs Dew or Sunbeams. Sunbeams remain the portal earn: **1 per solved group**, **+2 per solved board (+3 flawless)**, hard-capped **12/run** (`runEarn` in `earn()`) and **30/day** (`_sbCapEarn`, key `sw_sb_rootgroups`); Zen pays 0 by design. Skins are purely visual — they never touch the generator, tier order, guess budget, hint cost or payouts.
