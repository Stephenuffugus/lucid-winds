# Season Sway — Art Pack

> A visitor lays a card on the midnight table — swipe it left or right, keep Sun, Rain, Soil and Wildlife in balance, and the little plant you tend becomes a keepsake bloom and a poem.

**Genre:** Reigns-style card-swipe stewardship (single-file HTML5; gameplay is a 540×960 portrait canvas inside a scaled stage, menus/results are DOM screens). A 40-card deck of garden visitors — Koi, Fox, Owl, the four Season Spirits, even The Beholder — each poses a dilemma; swiping **left or right** applies that choice's deltas to four meters (**Sun ☀ · Rain 🌧 · Soil 🌱 · Wildlife 🦋**, clamped 0–100). If any meter empties or overflows, the garden goes to rest. The season turns every **6 cards** and quietly drags one meter (Spring pushes Wildlife up, Summer/Autumn/Winter pull Rain/Soil/Sun down), with pressure and entropy rising as seasons stack. A **Living Keepsake** plant grows mid-screen from your run's dominant lean; every finished run mints a keepsake flower + a procedural haiku into your local **Grove**. Four modes: The Keeper's Year 🃏, Daily Almanac 📅 (date-seeded), Seed Vow 🎯 (18 cards, grow a promised bloom), Zen Tending 🍃 (no-fail). Colorblind-safe by design: each meter has a fixed position, its own pictographic icon and a code-drawn letter/name label; choice previews are ▲/▼ **shapes**, not just colors.

_The game already ships and plays procedurally (`satellites/season-sway/index.html`) — this art is an optional visual upgrade **and** the cosmetics library for the in-game customization economy. Mechanics, deck text, meter math, swipe thresholds and the shape-first colorblind cues are IDENTICAL across every theme below; only the skin changes._

## Pick a look — theme direction

⚖️ **Director note:** no forced childish botanical house style. One honest constraint here: the 40 card speeches are **code-owned garden fiction** (a koi asks about the pond, a fox about the warren), so the theme freedom lives in the *rendering and the deck-object world* — the table, the card stock, the material language — not in swapping the cast. Three directions below, cozy → mature, with a verdict.

### 1. Pressed-Flower Almanac (cozy)
*A keeper's scrapbook by lamplight: soft watercolor visitors like pressed-and-pasted journal illustrations, linen card stock, petal and fern inclusions in the paper, warm candle-glow on a wooden night table.* Warmest and most native to the Lucid Winds greenhouse feel, and watercolor is forgiving to generate. But it is exactly the cozy-botanical lane the Director asked to move past, it reads soft/young on a portal card, and loose watercolor edges cut poorly against a magenta knockout.

### 2. Gilded Almanac ⭐ RECOMMENDED (polished, mature-leaning, kid-friendly)
*An antique ink-and-gilt oracle deck on a midnight table. Every visitor is a small engraved-ink miniature with flat gouache fills and one gilt-foil glint — friendly woodcut plates from a keeper's almanac, framed by fine gilt borders on warm card stock. The world around the cards is a hushed, lamplit card-table nocturne.* This is the deck-object direction the game is literally named for — the 🃏 mode button, "Daily Almanac," "Seed Vow" — so the fiction of *drawing cards from a fortune deck* carries the theme away from cute-garden rendering without fighting one line of code-owned copy. Ink miniatures stay crisp at the 64–90px scale the card face gives a portrait, dark contour lines survive the magenta knockout cleanly, and the light card stocks the code already ships (`#e8dcc8` linen, `#cfe0b8` vellum, `#c8b8e0` dusk, `#f0d79a` gilt) ARE antique paper colors — the cosmetics family becomes four collectible printings of the same deck for free. Mature enough to feel like a real object, friendly enough for a kid (every animal gets a kind face; The Beholder is wondrous, never creepy).

### 3. Stained Glass Seasons (bold, most polished)
*Visitors as luminous leaded-glass panels; the four seasons as rose-window washes; meters as glowing glass vials in a dark chapel of leaves.* The most striking screenshot of the three and a true material departure. But it fights the code: the card face must stay a **light** field because the engine draws dark ink text (`bk.ink`) directly onto `bk.col` — dark luminous glass under code-drawn dark text is a legibility trap — and heavy glow bleeds into the magenta key. Hold it in reserve as a future premium card-back printing rather than the base look.

**Recommendation: Gilded Almanac.** It is the natural reading of the game's own nouns (almanac, vow, deck), the biggest step away from childish-botanical that still honors the code-owned garden copy, the safest technically (ink lines on light stock = clean knockout, readable text, tiny PNGs), and the cosmetics economy falls out of it elegantly — four printings of one deck, four gardens to lay it on. **All sheets below bake this look in.**

## Cosmetics economy

Everything is earned by **PLAYING** — no loot boxes, no randomized purchases, nothing that touches the deck, the meter math or the swipe threshold. Every unlock is a **KNOWN threshold shown on its locked wardrobe card** (the code renders `it.desc` on the card). Two cosmetic families and four earn faucets, all already wired in `localStorage` key **`seasonsway_save`**:

- **Card backs** (`PROG.back`, `BACKS[]` — these restyle the card stock the dilemma is printed on): *Linen* (starter, free) → **Moss Vellum** (best run reaches **6 seasons**, `PROG.bestSeasons`) → **Dusk Card** (finish **10 runs**, `PROG.runs`) → **Almanac Gilt** (**5-day** Daily Almanac streak, `PROG.streak`).
- **Gardens** (`PROG.table`, `TABLES[]` — the full-screen table backdrop behind play): *Midnight* (starter, free) → **Meadow** (best run reaches **12 seasons**, `PROG.bestSeasons`) → **Emberfall** (finish **20 runs**, `PROG.runs`) → **Frostglass** (keep **3 Seed Vows**, `PROG.bestVow` — it increments once per vow kept).

Four faucets: **single-run season mastery** (`PROG.bestSeasons`), **cumulative runs** (`PROG.runs` — the showing-up faucet), the **Daily streak** (`PROG.streak` — lapsed-friendly return loop), and **vows kept** (`PROG.bestVow` — mode mastery). `ownedC()` also honors `PROG.owned{}` for an optional manual grant without touching earn logic; the equipped picks live in `PROG.back` / `PROG.table`. The **Grove** (`PROG.blooms[]`, capped at 240 seeds, last 60 displayed) is the keepsake collection every run feeds — a pride surface, not a gate. Cosmetics are **purely visual**: `curBack()`/`curTable()` only recolor the card and backdrop. Sunbeams stay the earn currency only (`_sbCapEarn`, **30/day** cap under `sw_sb_seasonsway`; Keeper's Year ≤ 12/run, Daily pays on first finish of the day only, Seed Vow pays 12 kept / ≤ 6 broken, Zen pays nothing).
