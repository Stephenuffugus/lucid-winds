# Sheet 04 — Effects

**PATCH-REQUIRED wiring:** FX render in the `FX` loop in `render()` keyed on
`f.k`: 'ring' (bell), 'pop' (balloon), 'cut' (scissors), 'nudge'/'tap' (the One
Nudge ripple). Each is a short 3-5 frame burst; author frames left to right and
step by `f.l` (life counter). Win screen confetti gears are a new decoration for
`s-over` (drop-in DOM/canvas, no engine change).

background for cutout. A sprite sheet, 4 rows x 4 columns, each cell 192x192
