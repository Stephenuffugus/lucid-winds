(attic/blueprint/sunset/copper in `THEMES`). Target 540x790 in game — generate
at 1080x1580 portrait and downscale; keep the lower 40px readable as FLOOR and
the top corners quiet for HUD chips. One image per theme, generated as four
SEPARATE portrait images (not a grid), all on their own full canvas, NO magenta
needed (full-bleed). Keep the middle third low-contrast so parts and chalk trail
stay readable.
