# Leaf Fit — Art Pack

> Drag a leaf sprig onto the midnight trellis, watch a whole row light up green, and clear it in a shower of pollen — bank enough Nectar and a Bloom saves the run.

**Genre:** Woodoku / block-fit puzzle (single-file HTML5 canvas, 540×960 portrait; DOM overlay screens for title / wardrobe / grove / results). A 9×9 board with 3×3 boxes; three polyomino **sprigs** deal into a tray and you drag them on — a ghost shows the landing spot and a pulsing green **pre-clear glow** paints any row, column or 3×3 patch that will complete. Cleared leaves drift into the **Nectar** meter on the right edge; a full meter arms the golden **🌸 Bloom** button, which deals three fresh sprigs — the rescue you earned, never bought. Four modes: **Bloom Run** (classic), **Daily Trellis** (seeded, streak), **Zen Trellis** (free Blooms, ends only when you quit), **Gauntlet** (pre-scattered leaf-litter + 70% nectar gain). Every sprig species reads by **MOTIF and symbol as well as color** — six species carry six fixed colorblind glyphs (dot / star / heart / diamond / hex / plus) — so the board stays clear for colorblind players.

_The game already ships and plays procedurally (`satellites/leaf-fit/index.html`) — this art is an optional visual upgrade **and** the cosmetics library for the in-game customization economy. The grid, piece shapes, weights, hitboxes, scoring and the six symbol cues are IDENTICAL across every theme below; only the skin changes._

## Pick a look — theme direction

⚖️ **Director note:** no forced childish botanical house style. The mechanic is a block puzzle (Woodoku's own heritage is a woodcraft table, no garden required) — but this game's code-owned copy is leaf-locked ("drag leaf sprigs onto the midnight trellis", "Nectar", "Bloom", wardrobe sections literally named *Leaf sets* and *Trellises*). So the options below range cozy-botanical → polished glass → fully non-botanical woodshop, all kid-friendly.

### 1. Leadlight Conservatory ⭐ RECOMMENDED (polished, glowing, keeps the copy honest)
*A Victorian glasshouse at midnight. The sprig tiles are luminous **stained-glass leaf panes** — jewel-lit from behind — set into a dark **leaded trellis lattice** with warm brass fittings. Placing a sprig is setting glass into a window; clearing a line is lantern-light flooding through a finished panel; the Nectar meter is a slim brass-and-amber vial; a Bloom is the whole conservatory glowing warm for a heartbeat.*

**Why this one:** it is the biggest visual lift that keeps every code-owned string true — the leaves stay leaves, the trellis becomes the leadlight lattice, Nectar/Bloom become light — while leaving the soft childish garden look completely behind. Glass is glow-native to the locked midnight palette (`#0d100c` board, `#8ec462` pre-clear pulse, gold `#c8a84b` → cream `#ffe9a8` full-nectar flare all read as light through glass, so the engine's existing glow code becomes the theme for free). Jewel tiles pop hard on a dark field at thumbnail size, the six species stay distinct by vein-motif AND symbol (colorblind rule), and the craft feel (leaded glass, brass) reads mature without ever being scary. Integration cost is near zero: same dark board, same six species hues, same gold accents. **All sheets below bake this look in.**

### 2. Pressed Meadow (alt, cozy — the botanical option, upgraded)
*A midnight herbarium: soft papercut / gouache pressed-leaf tiles pinned to a twine-and-lath garden trellis, pollen motes drifting, warm and gentle.* Warmest and most native to Lucid Winds, and the cheapest to read for the youngest players — but it is exactly the house botanical lane the Director wants to move past, and matte paper tiles glow less on a dark board than glass, so clears and the Nectar flare land quieter.

### 3. Timber Inlay (alt, mature-neutral — fully non-botanical)
*The honest Woodoku heritage: warm carved hardwood blocks with visible grain dropping into a walnut marquetry tray; clears sand away in a puff of shavings; brass inlay lines mark the 3×3 boxes.* Handsome, gender-neutral, very "craftsman premium" — but it fights the code-owned leaf/Nectar/Bloom copy head-on (wood shavings are not pollen), warm brown boards fight the locked midnight palette, and it is the least kid-magnetic of the three. Hold it in reserve as a future unlockable trellis + leaf-set pairing rather than the base theme.

**Recommendation: Leadlight Conservatory.** Keeps every code-owned string honest while fully escaping the childish-garden look, is glow-native to the real palette and the engine's existing pulse/glow draws, reads jewel-bright at thumbnail size, and stays warmly kid-friendly. Borrow Pressed Meadow's softness for the Grove keepsakes; keep Timber Inlay as a future cosmetics drop.

## Cosmetics economy

Every Leaf Fit cosmetic is earned by **PLAYING** — no loot boxes, no randomized purchases, no pay-to-win, nothing that changes the grid, the piece bag, scoring, Nectar gain or the Sunbeam payout. Everything is a **KNOWN unlock at a KNOWN threshold** shown right on the locked wardrobe card (`it.desc`). Two cosmetic families and three earn faucets, all already wired in `leaffit_save`:

- **Leaf sets** (`PROG.leaf`, `LEAFSETS[]`, six glass colors each): *Meadow* (starter, free) → **Ember** (best score ≥ **2,500**) → **Frost** (**80** total line clears) → **Orchid** (**5-day** Daily streak).
- **Trellises** (`PROG.board`, `BOARDS[]`, board cell + grid colors): *Willow* (starter, free) → **Slate** (best score ≥ **1,200**) → **Loam** (**40** total line clears).

The three faucets: **best score** (`PROG.best` — set by Bloom Run / Daily / Gauntlet; Zen never writes it), **total clears** (`PROG.totalClears` — every row/column/box cleared in ANY mode counts, Zen included), and the **Daily streak** (`PROG.streak`, the lapsed-friendly return loop; `PROG.bestDaily` / `lastDaily` / `dailyDone` back it). All persist in `localStorage` (`leaffit_save`); equipped choices live in `PROG.leaf` / `PROG.board`; `PROG.owned{}` (keys like `leaf1`, `board2`) allows an optional manual grant without touching the earn logic, and `curLeaf()` / `curBoard()` fall back to the starter if an equipped item is not owned. Alongside the wardrobe, good runs grow **Grove keepsake flowers** (`PROG.blooms`, seeded, capped 240 stored / last 60 shown): `floor(clears/6) + 1` extra for a best-combo ≥ 3 (Zen caps at 2 per run) — a collection reward, not a purchase. Sunbeams stay the earn/collection currency only (`_sbCapEarn`, 30/day cap in `sw_sb_leaffit`, ≤12/run at the call sites). **Optional soft-coin (convenience only, not required):** the portal's existing **Dew** — never Sunbeams — could early-unlock a cosmetic slightly ahead of its threshold via `PROG.owned`, but every item stays fully earnable free by playing, so the wall is a showing-up wall, not a paywall.
