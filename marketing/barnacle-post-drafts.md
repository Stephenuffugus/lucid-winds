⛔ **The code is `THEBARNACLE69` and it goes to one man.** Not in a caption, not in a
comment, not in a code round up, not in a directory submission. The whole joke only
works while nobody else can open him. If it leaks, mint a replacement with
`node scripts/make-code.js WORD` and swap the two numbers in the `CODES` table.
Anybody who already unlocked him keeps him, because ownership lives in `PROG.chars`.

⛔ **Never word it so it sounds like he is involved.** You made a costume as a tribute
and you are keeping the code for him. That is charming. "In partnership with" and
"featuring" are the things to avoid, and you never need either to tell this joke.

⛔ **Say it is free with no ads early.** It is the most persuasive fact you have and
people scroll past before they reach it.

## When people ask for the code, and they will

> Sorry. That one is his. Everything else in there is free.

Or, if you want to be helpful with it:

> That one is his and only his. But there are five other costumes behind codes that
> anyone can find, and every critter in the game is earned by playing.

Do not soften and hand it out to the first person who asks nicely. The joke is the
scarcity, and the moment one other person has it the post stops being true.

## If he or anyone near him actually replies

Keep it short and do not pitch.

> This genuinely made my week. The code is THEBARNACLE69 and it is yours to hand out
> however you like, or to sit on forever. Either is a good outcome for me.

Then let him do whatever he wants with it, including nothing.
