# INTERNATIONAL OUTREACH — regions, outlets, portals, and the rules of engagement

Compiled 2026-07-29 from live research. Companion to `/press/` (send THAT link,
never a google-redirect-wrapped URL — the Jul 24 batch went out with mangled
`google.com/url?q=` links).

## The strategy (learned the hard way this week)

1. **Pitch a GAME, never "the platform."** Jupiter Hadley's exact words:
   "this isn't a game. It's a platform." Every pitch leads with ONE game
   fitted to that outlet, portal link second.
2. **Disclose AI plainly, choose targets, never debate.** The press kit's
   "How the games are made" section is the standing answer. Anti-AI outlets
   (IndieGamesPlus and most Western hobby-curator circles) are a hard no —
   do not spend sends there. Business-side and regional outlets mostly do
   not care.
3. **Portals deliver PLAYERS; press delivers legitimacy.** In PH/SEA/LATAM
   especially, distribution portals and communities are where browser games
   actually get played. Press second.
4. **Send clean links** with `?from=<slug>` attribution (already live) so we
   learn which region converts.

## ⛔ Two constraints before anything ships

- **CrazyGames / Poki / GameDistribution monetize with THEIR ad SDK** and
  CrazyGames wants 2-month exclusivity for the better rev share. The studio
  rule is NO ADS. Putting games there means ads around/in our games and
  possibly exclusivity. ⛔ Stephen's call per game — candidates would be a
  few self-contained arcade titles as "ambassadors" (the portal link in the
  credits), never the flagship. GameMonetize/Kizi (already emailed) have the
  same shape.
- **China: Firebase and Google domains are blocked.** Accounts, cloud sync,
  sunbeams, and payments are all dead there, and Google Fonts breaks pages.
  Games would half-work at best. DEPRIORITIZE China until a self-hosted
  fallback exists; Taiwan is the Chinese-language market we can serve today.

- ZNT Productions showcases (BR/LATAM) — watch windows.
