# Sky Wolf Studio currency + retention (Stephen's 7/16 question)

**The question:** "In game currency: build for Sky Wolf. Seeds or dew? What gets people
coming back? What is our game lacking that these games have?"

## What the biggest games have that keeps people returning

Looking at the games Penny and Jessie actually play (Playhop/Poki hits, plus the
Wordle/Duolingo pattern):

1. **A reason to come back TOMORROW, not just play longer today.** Streaks, daily
   puzzles, daily gifts. Missing a day costs something you can see.
2. **Visible progress that never resets.** Level counters, collection pages,
   world maps with checkmarks.
3. **Something to spend on that shows.** Skins and cosmetics others (or you) can see.
4. **Short sessions that end with "one more."** The daily is beatable in 3 minutes.

## What Sky Wolf already has

- Sunbeams already ARE the studio currency: earned in every game, travel with you,
  feed Lucid Winds plants. That is a genuinely unusual, strong hook.
- Dailies now exist in many games separately (Hues, Daily Trio, Merge & Blast,
  Super Slice, Dewball, Silt, Word Sprout...).
- Per-game coins exist in a few games (Hues borders, Nectar Drop mastery cosmetics).

## What we're actually lacking (the honest answer)

Not a currency. **A front door that makes the return visit a ritual.** Today the
portal is a shelf of games; nothing on it says "here's what's waiting for you today"
or "you're on a 6-day streak." The retention layer exists but is scattered inside
individual games where nobody sees it.

## Options for the Director

**A (recommended): No new currency. Crown sunbeams + build the Daily Hub.**
- Portal top strip: today's dailies across the studio (Hues #N, Trio #N, Slice
  daily...), each with a done-check, plus a studio-wide streak counter.
- Daily streak pays sunbeams (small, capped, server-side later like everything else).
- Zero new economy lanes, zero drift risk (we already have a two-source sunbeam
  drift problem parked — a second currency doubles that class of bug).

**B: Add "Seeds" as a portal-cosmetic soft currency.**
- Earned only from the Daily Hub streaks/quests; spent only on portal-level cosmetics
  (profile plant badge, portal themes, name flair). Never buys gameplay.
- Pro: a visible spend-sink that isn't LW-entangled. Con: second wallet to sync,
  second thing to explain, and Jessie already questioned why Hues coins exist.

**C: Dew goes studio-wide.**
- Not recommended: Dew is LW's wild-tending lane and its sinks (slots, skips,
  boxes) are balanced for LW. Exporting it breaks the locked economy lanes.

**Recommendation: A.** The gap is ritual, not wallet. If A works and people ask
"what do I DO with all these sunbeams outside LW," that's the moment to consider B.
