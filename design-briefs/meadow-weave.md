# Meadow Weave — Revamp Brief

**Revives:** Dorfromantik (Toukana, 2022) — draw a hex tile, rotate it, place it so its terrain edges match its neighbors; complete regions and hit quests for score; calm, endless, no fail-you-just-run-out-of-tiles

## The essence (protect this)

The quiet dopamine of the perfect fit — you turn a tile in your fingers, find the one rotation where forest meets forest and river meets river all the way around, and set it down with a soft click. No clock, no threat, just a slowly growing patchwork you're proud of and a tile counter you're gently trying not to let hit zero. Protect the draw-rotate-place rhythm, the edge-match satisfaction, the "one more tile" calm, and the honesty that you never lose so much as *finish*.

## Signature upgrade

THE LIVING DIORAMA — in Dorfromantik a completed region just scores and sits there, flat. Here, the moment you close off a region (a full meadow, a ringed pond, a whole hedgerow) it **comes alive**: the grass ripples, the water shimmers and catches the moon, fireflies rise off a finished field, a finished grove's canopy sways, and a tiny companion wanders in to live there. And every completed biome **presses a keepsake** — it drops a **feral seed** straight into your Wild pouch (reusing the existing feral system), scaled by how big and how clean the region was. This is the definitive hook because it turns a pretty-but-inert score board into a garden that literally breathes and *pays* you: the calm placement loop now grows a living, animated diorama you keep, and the biomes you complete feed the Lucid Winds collection meta instead of just a number. A Dorfromantik where your finished map isn't a screenshot, it's a habitat.

## Dated friction to kill

- Flat, static "done" regions: the original's completed areas are just colored hexes with a checkmark — no life, no payoff beyond points. Ours animate and drop a seed.
- Score is the only reward: a great map earns a number and nothing you keep. Ours feeds feral seeds + a diorama gallery + cosmetics.
- Quest opacity: flag-quests ("a forest of exactly 11") can feel like guesswork about whether you can still satisfy them. Ours shows a soft live projection of each open quest's best-case reachable size so a quest death feels chosen.
- Fiddly rotation on touch: rotating a hex by repeated taps is slow. Ours: a thumb-flick or a rotation ring, plus a "suggest best rotation" ghost that a beginner can lean on and a master ignores.
- Endgame drift: late Dorfromantik boards sprawl until you lose track of open edges. Ours: a gentle "open edges" pulse and a recenter/zoom that keeps the frontier readable at 540px.
- No session shape: it's one long meditative sprawl with no natural daily bite. Ours adds a seeded Daily Meadow and quick Quest boards.
- Overwhelming first minute: a blank hex sea with no guidance can stall a new player. Ours opens with a 3-tile guided placement that teaches edge-matching wordlessly.

## Game-feel spec

- Place: the tile lifts under your thumb with a soft shadow, snaps to the nearest legal hex slot with a magnet ease, and lands with a woody "tock"; matched edges flash a short green seam and add a rising chime per matched side (0 matches = neutral, all 6 = a little fanfare).
- Rotation: a flick or a tap-ring spins the hex in 60° steps with a satisfying detent; a faint ghost shows the best-scoring rotation for anyone who wants the training wheel.
- Region-complete: the closed region blooms to life left-to-right — grass sways, water shimmers, a village's windows light, a firefly puff rises — then a feral seed pops out and arcs to the pouch counter with a warm resolve chord.
- Quest flags: a small banner tile you can place; while its region is open it shows a live count and a soft target ring; completing it showers extra tiles + a rarer seed, failing it just closes quietly (no punish sting).
- Tile scarcity tension: the tile-bag counter breathes; matched placements refund tiles (the core economy), so a clean streak visibly refills your bag and a sloppy one drains it — the only "pressure" in the game and it's gentle.
- Camera: pinch/drag pan with inertia; a double-tap recenters on the active frontier; open edges glow faintly so you always know where you can build.
- No fail buzzer, no clock: running out of tiles eases into a calm "the meadow rests" summary with your map gently spotlit; tap to keep it as a diorama or start again.

## Onboarding & difficulty

The first board hands you three tiles and a single glowing legal slot with a ghost showing the matching rotation — you place forest-to-forest, water-to-water, field-to-field and *see* the edge seams reward you, learning the whole game in under fifteen seconds with zero text. Tile refunds start generous so early boards can't stall. The mastery ceiling is pure optimization, never twitch: (1) tile scarcity tightens as biome variety climbs (rail and village edges are harder to satisfy), (2) quest flags demand precise region sizes that force you to plan several tiles ahead, (3) chasing giant single-biome regions for the fattest feral seeds means resisting easy small closes, and (4) seasonal boards change which edges are plentiful. Anyone can weave a pretty meadow; only a planner threads a 30-hex river through four quests without stranding an edge.

## Modes

- Endless Weave (main) — the calm core: place tiles, complete regions, keep the bag alive as long as you can, and grow one big living map. Where the biggest dioramas and rarest seeds come from.
- Daily Meadow — one date-seeded tile sequence everyone gets that day: fair, comparable, pays a guaranteed daily Sunbeam + a seasonal seed scaled by login streak. The retention spine.
- Quest Weave — a board seeded thick with flag-quests; a scoring push for players who want objectives and a leaderboard, not just serenity.
- Zen Grove — infinite tiles, no scarcity, no score: pure decoration, build the meadow of your dreams with no pressure at all. Kid- and wind-down-safe; earns no Sunbeams.

## Botanical identity

You're weaving a Lucid Winds night-meadow one hex at a time, and every terrain is a garden habitat rather than generic countryside: sage grassland, moonlit ponds, bramble hedgerow, wildflower field, mossy stone path, and lantern-lit hamlet, each edge readable by texture and silhouette as well as color (colorblind-native). Completed regions animate into living dioramas and draw in one of the 85 companions to inhabit them — a koi surfaces in a finished pond, a toad settles in a hedgerow, a heron wades a closed marsh — reusing companion art wholesale. The whole board re-tints through the four seasons (reusing Spring #E8A0BF, Summer #D4A843, Autumn #D4842A, Winter #A0C4E8), and the feral seeds you press from finished biomes drop into the same Wild pouch you already own. Deep near-black table, sage tiles, gold Sunbeams, cream UI — it sits natively in the midnight garden.

## Retention hook

Daily Meadow is the spine: one shared seeded board a day, a guaranteed Sunbeam, and a seasonal feral seed whose rarity rides your login streak (miss a day, the streak and the seed's rarity reset). Endless Weave feeds two collections at once — the feral-seed pouch (every completed biome presses one, so the Wild/greenhouse economy advances every session) and a Diorama Gallery of your best finished maps, which doubles as the unlock wall for cosmetics: tile-art sets (papercut, stained-glass, embroidered-felt), table backdrops, and biome palettes unlock at known thresholds of biomes-completed and best-map scores. Sunbeams follow the portal standard (30/day/game, 12/run cap). The compounding pull: the streak protects your daily seed, every calm session presses more seeds and prettier dioramas, and the gallery shows off what you've grown.

## Build notes

Single-file vanilla ES5 canvas, no frameworks — fits the portal satellite pattern. Core engine is a hex grid (axial coords), a tile with 6 typed edges, legal-placement + best-rotation scoring, and flood-fill region detection to know when a biome closes. Heavy reuse: the 4-season palette skins the board, the 85-companion art populates finished dioramas, and the existing feral-seed/Wild-pouch system receives the pressed seeds (no new economy — it plugs into the collection meta you already have). Genuinely new code is modest: the hex placement/rotation UI with touch flick, the region-close flood fill + "living diorama" animation layer (cheap looped sprite shimmer, cap active animators for thermals), the tile-bag refund economy, and the date-seeded daily RNG (mulberry32 on the date). Watch perf on the diorama animations — pool and cap them, pause off-screen regions. Deterministic daily seed keeps Daily Meadow fair. ⛔ Shares the calm-placement lane with Plot Bloom (Islanders) — ship one, let it breathe before the other.
