# Super Slice 3D: the art review of Astra's handoff (23 Oct 2026)

Seven reviewers (one per category) judged every file in `Super_Slice_Dev_Handoff.zip` from prepared sheets (3x3 repeats,
props on grey and near black, the slab crops the game shows, fruit and halves split, every knife with its alpha box).
Every verdict below that is not a plain "use" is either applied by `satellites/slice-3d/tools/cut_art.py` (marked APPLIED)
or left for the part that uses the file (marked LATER). Files not listed here were judged "use" as delivered.

## Fourteen of the sixteen tiles have no real seams, and all eight wall_rock tiles can go in 

Fourteen of the sixteen tiles have no real seams, and all eight wall_rock tiles can go in as they are. Only lantern_night/ledge has a significant problem: a baked left-to-right lighting gradient (column means fall from 75 to 52, about 30%) plus vertical specular bands. These show as stripes every tile in the 3x3 and need flattening before import. Two ledges need small fixes: clockwork_well/ledge doubles its brass rail and clips rivets at the Y wrap (crop rows 489-511, then resize back to 512), and deep_tide/ledge has a faint grain hairline at the X wrap (offset by 256px and heal). All three will show as patterns up a wall hundreds of units tall, though fog and the viewing angle should soften them. Two content notes: orchard_loft wall_rock is wood planking, not rock (it fits the world), and mossy_canyon wall_rock has the tops of its blocks lit from above.

- **worlds/lantern_night/ledge.jpg** (use-with-fix, major): Purple plank floor with a baked left-to-right gradient. - **worlds/clockwork_well/ledge.jpg** (use-with-fix, minor): Dark planks banded by brass rails and riveted plates. ## All 32 knife sheets can go in as they are

All 32 knife sheets can go in as they are. Each one is a single knife lying horizontal with the blade left and the grip right. Measured alpha principal axes all fall within about ±1.5 degrees, so no rotation or mirroring is needed. Every sheet, icon and trail has clean RGBA alpha, and no file has art clipped at the canvas edge (checked on all four borders). The biggest in-game risk is size: the bboxes vary from about 593 to 758 px wide and 2.5 to 4.3 in aspect. Very slim skins (combo_conductor, midnight_katana) will look thin, and the long ones (icicle, sir_spatulot, wallbreaker_pick) depend on the per-skin length scaling being right. The icons are diagonal hero shots, which is fine for icons; about eight differ in detail from their sheets (grip colour or blade shape) but are clearly the same skin. Every trail reads as a streak with a bright head on the left tapering right; groundskeeper and obsidian_flake are fainter than the rest.
