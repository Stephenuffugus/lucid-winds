The same answers as the Steam survey (store/jimothy-steam/CONTENT_RATING.md). Violence: yes, mild cartoon: Jimothy gets bumped by traffic and flops over, stars spin, you restart; no weapons, no blood, no injury shown. Alcohol: one reference: one hidden landmark collectible is "A Rainier Tallboy Tab", a pull tab lying in the street as litter; nobody drinks anything. 6. After the first upload

Send Claude Google's app signing key

Google's help says it is under Protected with Play, then Play Store distribution, then Go to Play app signing, in the App signing key section: the SHA-256. Not the Upload key (that one starts 77:B7:89:10). Paste it to Claude and the Jimothy app opens without an address bar. If your screen looks different, send a screenshot.
