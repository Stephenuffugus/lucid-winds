## Full description

The dryer opens and a real heap of socks tumbles onto the folding table. Dig through it with one thumb, find each
sock's twin, roll the pair into a ball and flick it into the basket. Odd socks wait in the Odd Bin, and when a mate
turns up in a later Load, the two of them meet.

Laundry Day has no timer and nothing to fail. Misses stay on the table and cost nothing. Rush is the same pile with a
clock, streaks and four small powers, for the days you want one.

Every sock is generated by the game, so no two are alike, and the Drawer keeps every design you have ever folded.
Pegs hang on the Clothesline as you play; nothing is bought, nothing is undone. Lint and Quarters buy baskets, dryers,
room decor and radio stations, all cosmetic.

Made for one thumb and a quiet evening. Color vision modes for deuteranopia, protanopia and tritanopia, a pattern
first option so lookalike socks never differ by color alone, a tap to toss alternative to flicking, and a reduce
motion setting. Your save lives on your device and can be exported as a file.

No ads. No purchases inside the game. No account. Works offline after the first load.

From Sky Wolf Studio, a one person studio.
