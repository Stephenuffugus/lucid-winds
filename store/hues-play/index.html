5. After the first upload

Send Claude Google's app signing key

Google's help says it is under Protected with Play, then Play Store distribution, then Go to Play app signing, in the App signing key section: the SHA-256. Not the Upload key (that one starts 1E:FF:D1:19). Paste it to Claude and the Hues app opens without an address bar. If your screen looks different, send a screenshot.
