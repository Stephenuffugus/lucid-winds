How to play

🌸
Goal: touch whatever IS WIN. In most gardens that is the bloom.
👆
Move: swipe or tap the arrows. Everything that IS YOU walks together, and walking into a word tile pushes it.
🧱
Rules: three word tiles in a row make a rule, like STONE IS STOP. A green line glows under every rule that is working.
✨
The trick: push a word out of its row and that rule switches off. Line words up and a new rule switches on. The garden obeys instantly.
Deeper roots
🖐
Word tiles always push. Objects only push when a rule says IS PUSH.
🌊
IS SINK swallows whatever touches it, and the water goes too. IS DEFEAT bites anything that is YOU.
🪙
A golden word is gripped by roots. Step on the root knot somewhere on the board to loosen its grip.
↩
Undo takes back every move, all the way to the start. There is no way to truly ruin a puzzle.
🌰
Each garden hides one seed on a harder path. Collect them for wardrobe keys.
