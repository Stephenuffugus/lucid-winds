CREASE · Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
Crease is played on your phone. It collects nothing about you and sends nothing about you anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no purchases inside the game.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game remembers lives on your device only, in local storage:

  • Your settings and your progress, under the one key lw:crease:save: sound, reduced motion, high contrast and whether every mode is shown, and the specimens you have earned for your shelf.

None of it leaves your device, and none of it names you. To erase it, open the settings (the gear) and tap Clear this game twice, or clear the storage of the Crease app (Android Settings, Apps, Crease, Storage, Clear storage), or on the web clear this site's data in your browser. There is no copy anywhere else.

Network requests the game makes

The game's own files come from lucidwinds.com, together with a few files it shares with the studio's other number games, the same way any web page loads its files. That server sees an ordinary file request from your device and nothing about you or your play. After the first load the game keeps its own copies of its files on the device, so it plays with no connection. Every sound in the game is made on the device.

Permissions

The game asks for no device permissions. No location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts.

The Google Play app and the web

The Google Play app and the web page at lucidwinds.com run the same game and do the same things.

Children

Crease collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version is posted at this address.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about anything the game does with data, by email: stephen@skywolfstudio.com
