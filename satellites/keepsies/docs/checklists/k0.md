# K0 on a phone, ten things to try

The builder cannot run this. Stephen runs it when he wakes; his notes become the feel pass.
Open `lucidwinds.com/satellites/keepsies/` once Fable has deployed the branch, or the codespace port.

K0 is deliberately small: one shooter on dirt, no ring game yet. What is being asked here is whether the
world feels like a place and whether the marble feels like a marble, not whether it is a game.

1. The title card comes up before anything happens, and it tells you what to do in one line. Read it. Is the
   instruction clear enough that you would know what to do without being told twice.
2. Tap PLAY. It should be an easy target for a thumb without looking. If you had to aim, say so.
3. Drag one finger left and right. The camera should walk around the marble smoothly with no jump when
   you first touch and no drift when you let go.
4. Try to drag the camera underneath the ground. You should not be able to; it stops at the dirt. If you
   ever see the world from below, that is a bug and it is the one worth reporting first.
5. Pinch in until the marble fills the screen. Does it still look like glass at that size, or does it go
   plastic. Then pinch out. Does the horizon still look like a horizon.
6. Tap the dirt. The marble should go, roll, slow down and stop the way a marble does, not the way a ball
   in a game does. The question that matters: does it stop too soon, too late, or about right.
7. Turn the volume up and tap again. You should hear it. One impact, glassy, short. Is it the sound a
   16 mm glass marble makes on packed dirt, or is it a beep.
8. Tap several times quickly. Nothing should stutter, and the sound should not pile up into mush.
9. Put the phone down for a minute with the game open, then come back. It should carry on, not lurch
   forward as if catching up on the time it missed.
10. Rotate to landscape and back. Nothing should be cut off or stretched, and the marble should still be
    where you left it.

Write what you felt in `PLAYTESTS.md`, in your own words. The two questions the build is actually asking:
does the marble weigh anything, and does the ground feel like ground.
