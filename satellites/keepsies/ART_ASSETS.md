# Keepsies: art assets

Almost everything in this game is drawn by code on purpose (DESIGN §10: 60 of the 65 marbles are shader recipes, the four grails and the First Marble are sculpts; the dirt ring, the HUD, the decals, the debris and every sound are procedural). The list below is what code cannot make well, in the order the build needs it. Nothing here blocks a phase: every item has a procedural placeholder that ships until the real piece lands.

Format: wiring line, in-game size, shape law, then a prompt to paste. Colours on FF00FF where a cut-out is needed. Never claim any of it is hand painted.

## Prompts for item 2, the grail figures. Written 2026-09-04 after looking at `docs/shots/k2-inspect-grail.png`.

What the look showed: the stand-in for a grail is the epic swirl recipe, a grey blue sphere that reads as
a small moon. At 140 px on the turntable it has no figure and no silhouette, and nothing on the card says
which grail it is except the name. So the one law for all four sculpts: **the figure must read as a
silhouette at 140 px**, which means one big shape, limbs held close, no thin parts that vanish under a
sphere's refraction.

Route: Meshy image to 3D needs ONE clean reference image per figure, then `tools/forge3d/meshyfit.py`
fits it into the 22 mm clear sphere. ⛔ `meshy_api.py` persists task ids now (`meshy-tasks.json`); if a
run is killed, rerun the same command and it resumes rather than paying twice. Balance was 2640 on
2026-09-04. The figure fills about 70 percent of the sphere. Nothing here is hand painted.

Reference image spec, all four: one figure alone, three quarter view from slightly above, centred,
filling the frame, on a flat mid grey `#808080` background (Meshy reads a clean ground better than
magenta), soft even studio light, no ground shadow, no text, no sphere, no base. 1024 square.
Sculptural, matte, muted colour, the detail in the silhouette not the surface.
