KEEPSIES · Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
Keepsies is played on your phone. It collects nothing about you and sends nothing about you anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no purchases inside the game.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game remembers lives on your device only, in local storage:

  • Your game, under keepsies.save.v1: your player profile (which hand you shoot with, your aim calibration, level, experience, league and the techniques you have learned), the marbles you own and the story of each one (who it was won from or lost to, and when), your showcase, the game's own Sunbeams wallet, your bags, trophies, open buy back offers and daily chores. The game's Sunbeams are play money inside Keepsies; they cannot be bought and are worth nothing outside the game.
  • How many times the aiming dial's cue has been shown, under kp_dial_cue, and a count of today's Sunbeams under sw_sb_keepsies, which nothing reads.
  • The studio's music: which of its songs you have unlocked by playing, and how long and on how many days you have played, under sws_game_unlocks and keys that begin sws_music. If you use the music player, it also keeps its volume, the last song and any playlist you make, under sws_music_state and sws_playlists. These are counted on the device and decide when the next song unlocks.

None of it leaves your device, and none of it names you. If your browser refuses local storage, the game keeps your game for this visit only. To erase it all, clear the storage of the Keepsies app (Android Settings, Apps, Keepsies, Storage, Clear storage), or on the web clear this site's data in your browser. There is no copy anywhere else.

Network requests the game makes

The game's own files, its 3D engine and its art come from lucidwinds.com, the same way any web page loads its files. That server sees an ordinary file request from your device and nothing about you or your play. After the first load the game keeps its own copies of its files, so it plays with no connection; the statue figures are kept once a match has used them. Your opponents are played by the game on your device; there is no online play.

The game loads the studio's music script (/music-unlocks.js) and, when you open the music player, its player (/music-player.js), both from lucidwinds.com. The player plays nothing until you ask it to. When you play a song it streams from lucidwinds.com, or, for some older public domain recordings, from archive.org (the Internet Archive). Those servers see an ordinary request for the song and nothing about you. Music needs a connection.

Sharing and vibration

Share makes a card of a marble or of a match on the device and opens your phone's own share sheet, or saves the picture, only when you tap it. A card carries no name or identifier of yours. The game sends it nowhere; you choose where it goes. The game buzzes your phone at moments in play where the phone allows it; that needs no permission and sends nothing.

Permissions

The game asks for no device permissions. No location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts.

The Google Play app and the web

The Google Play app and the web page at lucidwinds.com run the same game and do the same things.

Children

Keepsies collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version is posted at this address.
