Bloomzap
Sky Wolf Studios
Race the rival gardener to grow seven words from a shared bed of letters. Make a word they already grew and you zap it clean off the trellis. Hunt the words they'll never think of.

Pick your rival

First to grow seven words wins the bed.

How to play

🔤
Tap the shared letter tiles to spell a word (3–5 letters), then Plant it onto your trellis.
🌿
First gardener to seven words wins the bed. Both of you draw from the same eight letters.
⚡
Plant a word the rival already grew and it gets ZAPPED off their trellis — and yours to make. The word is gone for good. That's your way to slow them down.
🕵️
Careful — they can zap you too. So hunt the odd, rare words they won't find, and only spend a turn zapping when it really counts.
☀️
Win beds to earn Sunbeams for your garden. Tougher rivals pay more.
