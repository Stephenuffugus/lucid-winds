## 4. Every CONFIG number that moved from spec, and why

The ore table, strata table, resource table, node weights, shrine numbers and upgrade
costs are all **verbatim from HANDOFF section 3**. What moved:

1. **`BRACE_SHRUG_PER_LEVEL: 0.25` (new).** This is the biggest deviation and it exists
   because section 3 contradicts itself. 200 meters is about 44 nodes. At the specced band
   hazard rates, with The Glass dealing 2 integrity, the expected damage on the way to 200m
   is **13.4 integrity against a maximum of 6 braces**. Nobody could ever reach 200m, so the
   ">60 percent for a full kit" bound was unreachable by construction, not by tuning.
   Section 3.6 says a hazard costs integrity "unless prevented by gear" but section 3.7
   ships no gear that prevents one, so the Brace track now does the job the spec's own
   sentence describes: each level also gives a 25 percent chance the timbers hold. Three
   levels means three hazards in four bounce off. It lands the depth bound at 63.8 percent,
   leaves the unupgraded rate at 0.0 percent, and turns Brace from the weakest track into a
   real purchase. Every hazard rate and damage value is untouched.
2. **`VEIN_COUNT_W: [[1,20],[2,35],[3,45]]`.** Spec says a vein holds 1 to 3 ore and does
   not weight them. Skewed toward 3 so the pack fills at a rate that makes greed lethal.
3. **TUNE values resolved by the sweep** (spec gave "pocket 25 to 40" as a starting point and
   nothing at all for caches): `pocketAirBase 30 + 0.22/depth`, `pocketLampBase 34 + 0.30/depth`,
   `cacheBase 26 + 1.35/depth` with a 0.7 to 1.3 roll, `heartChance 0.34` on an eligible deep vein.
4. **Mining cost = ore tier ticks, drill minus one, floor one** (the plan's ruling on the
   spec's dead ended "2 ticks"). A tick is one depth of descent at the current rock, so it
   costs 1.4 air on the Wet Shelf and 3 lamp in the Dark Seam.
5. **Bargain 6 "halve weight, lose half value"** is implemented as *the bottom of the bag
   spills until the pack is at or under half its weight*. Discrete ore cannot be split
   exactly in half, so it spills oldest first, which is neutral (it neither hands the player
   a free junk purge nor robs their best ore) and the card shows the exact ore, the exact
   value and the exact weight before they accept.
6. **Bargain 9 "teleport up 15"** clamps at 1 meter. Otherwise a shrine at 12m is a free
   ascent that skips the entire cost of the game.
7. **Bargain 1 and 8 clamp to the caps**, and a bargain whose air cost would zero you out is
   refused with the reason shown rather than quietly killing you.
8. **Full clear costs 15,253 cash, not the "approximately 11,400" in section 3.7.** That is
   simply what `base x 2.1^level` sums to over the six specced tracks. Nothing moved, the
   spec's own arithmetic was off.

## 5. Honest misses

1. **Cautious banks 61.4 percent of Optimal, not 35 to 50.** The reason is structural and
   worth Stephen seeing: at the specced numbers **the pack, not the air, is the binding
   constraint** for most of a run. A 40 to 60 kilo pack fills after three or four veins,
   around 25 to 40 meters, and at that point both a careful player and a perfect player go
   home with a full bag. Air only becomes the real master when the player starts dropping
   ore to trade up, which is exactly the drop gold for beryl move the design is built
   around. The gap widens as the pack grows. Closing it to 50 percent means either a
   smaller pack cap or a steeper `weight/20` ascent term, both specced numbers, so I left
   them and reported it.
2. **Runs to full clear: 216 cautious, 123 optimal, against a target of about 25.** Also
   structural. A mid kit run banks a mean of 100. To clear 15,253 in 25 runs a player must
   bank 610 a run, and the ore table cannot produce that: the best possible 60 kilo pack is
   beryl at 18.75 a kilo, about 1,100, and no real run mines a pure beryl pack. Getting to
   25 runs means multiplying ore values by roughly six, which re prices every number in
   section 3.5. I raised cache values as far as felt honest (a cache is meant to be the safe
   play reward, not the income) and left it. **This is a Director call:** 120 to 200 runs is
   a normal roguelite meta length, so the shipped number may simply be right and the spec's
   estimate wrong.
3. **A run is 1.0 minutes, not 4 to 8.** One run *cannot* be 4 to 8 minutes at the specced
   air table: 180 air caps a round trip at about 90 meters, which is about 20 nodes and 25
   decisions. So a SITTING is modelled as 4 runs plus the shop between them
   (`RUNS_PER_SESSION 4`, `SEC_SHOP_VISIT 18`), which lands at 5.0 minutes, inside the
   window. The model is printed with the table so it is arguable rather than hidden.
4. **The depth 200 bound is measured with a dedicated Diver policy** (never mines, dives
   until the air is gone). Greedy, Cautious and Optimal all reach 0.0 percent because they
   carry weight, and a depth record run in this game is a suicide run by design (section
   3.4 tracks depth and cash as two separate leaderboards). The sweep prints the depth 200
   rate for all three policies as well, and they are all 0.0 percent, which is the honest
   number for a cargo run.

## 6. What shipped

**SIM (pure, headless, DOM free):** deterministic node columns generated per shaft from the
seed alone, lazy but order independent (`mixSeed(seed, i)` per node), so a node's contents
can never depend on what the player did. Node to node movement, ascent as one commitment,
the AIR TO SURFACE readout computed by `ascentCost()` and shown at all times, mining priced
by ore tier, a hard weight cap with a readable block reason, one tap drops, all twelve
bargains, five strata plus an unbounded Below, hazards resolved at arrival so bargain 12 can
raise the rate mid run, lamp blindness (not death) at zero, Assay lookahead, run resume.

**Craft, from the plan's signature list:**

- **The air margin tone.** Two sine oscillators at D3 through a compressor. Silent above a
  30 percent margin, fading in below it, the second oscillator detuning up to 62 cents as
  the margin closes so the beat frequency gets genuinely uncomfortable. Everything pitched
  in the game is quantized to D minor (`quantD`), including pickaxe pitch by ore tier,
  Heartstone's sub octave D1 boom, and every UI blip.
- **The odometer** is the biggest element on screen, real rolling digit strips, with the all
  time depth record drawn as an etched line in the shaft and a clean chime plus a haptic the
  moment you pass it.
- **Strata transitions** recolor the shaft and stamp the band name once.
- **The greed ledger:** cargo drawer sorted by value for weight, ratio shown per item, one
  tap drop, and a quiet glint on a drop with no toast and no text.
- **Live decision support:** every action button carries its own price. DESCEND shows air,
  lamp, distance and what is down there. MINE shows its tick cost at this rock. ASCEND shows
  the climb. The player computes greed, never arithmetic.
- **Shrine bargains as full screen cards** with the exact numbers, ACCEPT in gold and WALK
  AWAY in sage, both 52px tall, and a refusal reason when a bargain is unaffordable.
- **Loss screens itemize the manifest** and name the worst thing left behind:
  "The beryl is still down there. 87m." Bargains taken appear in the run log underneath.
- **The company logbook**: last ten runs, one line each, plus lifetime stats.
- **Seed links** (`?seed=`), share strings with no dashes, daily shaft with a fixed loadout
  and a streak counter, input log replay of your best run watchable from the logbook.
- **Options panel:** sound, volume, haptics, reduced motion override, the margin tone
  toggle, an ascend confirmation, the seed with a copy link button, and an exit.
- **Embed protocol** (`window.SWS_EXIT`, `{sws:'ready'}` at parse and on load) with three
  findable exit buttons: the X on the run screen, the X on the surface, and one in options.

**Touch targets** (CSS, rendered at 375x667): DESCEND, MINE, ASCEND 60px tall; CARGO 52px;
all `.btn` 52px; drop buttons 78x48; icon buttons 48x48; shop buy buttons 92x52; the volume
slider 48px tall. Nothing under 48.

**Service worker:** copied from `bandits-box`, every cache constant renamed to the
`deepwell-` prefix, the activate handler only ever deletes keys starting `deepwell-`, the
recorded audio branch removed (every sound here is synthesised), navigations refetch with
`cache:'no-cache'` and fall back to a real Response. `SHELL_VERSION` and the registration
`?v=` are both `20260816a` and must move together.

## 8. The single next thing I would do

Sweep the pack cap. The two missed bounds share one root cause, and it is measurable in
about fifteen minutes: the pack fills before the air ever gets frightening, so a careful
player and a perfect player play nearly the same game. Drop `PACK_BASE` from 40 to about 28
and re run the sweep. If the Cautious to Optimal ratio falls toward 50 percent and Optimal's
banked mean rises (because trading up rather than filling up becomes the only way to profit),
then the game's central decision moves from "is my bag full" to "can I afford to carry this
out", which is the game section 3.1 says it wants to be.
