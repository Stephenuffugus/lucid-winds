How to play

●
The part every mode shares. Tap anywhere to flip the knife with a spin, and hold to keep flipping. The blade cuts, the handle bonks. Fruit splits open where the blade passes and pays a combo, while a handle-first hit just knocks you about and drops the combo.
▶
SUPER SLICE 3D, the normal run. You travel to the RIGHT along the ground. Brown wood is solid: hop walls, land on crates, bounce off mushroom caps. Pink crystals end the run on contact, so treat them as walls, not bumps. The run finishes at the score wall: stick it HIGH and blade first for the multiplier.
▲
SUPER SLICE WALL CLIMB, one wall and no level ceiling. Same flipping rules as the normal run, but there is no course: you flip straight UP the face of one enormous wall and the height you stick at IS your score. Every run gets the whole ladder, all 34 bands up to x900, so your level never caps you and only your throw decides where you stop. On the climb screen you can switch to Be the Blade: hold and circle your finger and the knife spins with you, and handle bounces off the wall ride that spin upward. Glancing blade touches scrape off, so only a committed blade angle sticks.
▼
SUPER SLICE FREE FALL, which plays differently. You are falling down a shaft, not travelling along the ground, and you steer by holding the LEFT or RIGHT side of the screen to drift that way. Carve down through the tall brown slab stacks: each one you cut opens in a burst of colour and chains your combo. When a dive has stacks with a gold band on top, they are its Clean Slice line: carve every slab of them and land on the floor to earn the Clean Slice badge. Every fifth level ends at a boss over a net: fall onto it blade first and keep the blade down, and each time it hops the net lobs you back onto it (the net aims for you, so a nudge toward it never carries you past). Three hits and it opens, and it pays more the fewer times you miss: no miss x5, one miss x3, more x2. Cut a rope or miss three lobs to skip it, which costs the Clean Slice. Keep the handle pointed at the side walls: a handle bounce is clean, but a blade into the wall sticks you and ends the dive. Pink crystals down here shove you rather than kill. Free Fall comes in two versions: authored levels, and the endless drop where the only score is how deep you get. The endless drop grows busier and faster the deeper you go, until no thumb can keep up.
