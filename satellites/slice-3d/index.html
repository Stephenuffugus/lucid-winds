SUPER SLICE 3D
Sky Wolf Studios
Fall through towers of color. Every stack you slice splits open and spills its insides. Tap left or right to flip the blade that way. Walls bounce, they never grab. Land dead center on the golden pad for the big multiplier.

How to play

1
The blade falls on its own. Tap the LEFT side of the screen to flip left, the RIGHT side to flip right. Each flip hops and steers the blade.
2
Slice every colored stack you touch. Slabs split open where the blade passes and spill their bright insides. Every slice grows your combo.
3
Grey stone cannot be cut. It bounces you back up and resets the combo. Walls bounce too, and never grab the handle.
4
The golden pad waits at the bottom. Land in the center ring for x3 on your whole run. Deeper levels, taller towers.
