SUPER SLICE 3D
Sky Wolf Studios
Flip the knife through a sunny 3D world and slice the fruit, it splits right open. Journey: run the forest counter and stick the score wall high. Freefall: dive the pit, keep your blade off the walls or you stick and fall, and land the bullseye floor blade down.

How to play

1
Tap anywhere to flip. Every tap hops the knife forward with a spin. Hold to keep flipping. Resting on the ground is fine, tap to get moving again.
2
Slice everything soft. Fruit splits open where the blade passes and spills its bright insides. Combos grow your score.
3
Brown wood is solid. Hop over walls, land on crates, and bounce off the sides. Mushroom caps are springy. Pink crystals knock you back and break the combo.
4
The score wall ends the run. Stick it HIGH and blade first: the higher your blade lands, the bigger the multiplier on everything you cut. Handle hits bounce you back for one more try.
5
Freefall mode drops you down a deep pit. Hold the LEFT or RIGHT side of the screen to drift that way and flip the knife. The walls bounce you back and forth.
6
Aim the blade at fruit and it cuts. But keep the handle toward the walls: touch your blade to a wall and it sticks, and the dive is over. Land blade down on the bullseye floor to finish, closer to the center for a bigger multiplier.
