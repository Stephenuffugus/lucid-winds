SUPER SLICE 3D · Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
Super Slice 3D is played on your phone. It collects nothing about you and sends nothing about you anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no purchases inside the Google Play app.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game remembers lives on your device only, in local storage:

  • Your progress, under keys that begin s3d_: your level and best runs (s3d_prog), your settings (s3d_set), the Slivers you have earned (s3d_slivers), the knives you own and the one you use (s3d_skins), the things you have found (s3d_dex) and today's Daily dive and its result (s3d_daily).
  • A count of today's Sunbeams, under sw_sb_slice3d. Only the web copy does anything with it (see below).
  • The studio's music: which of its songs you have unlocked by playing, and how long and on how many days you have played, under sws_game_unlocks and keys that begin sws_music. If you use the music player, it also keeps its volume, the last song and any playlist you make, under sws_music_state and sws_playlists. These are counted on the device and decide when the next song unlocks.

None of it leaves your device, and none of it names you. To erase it all, clear the storage of the Super Slice 3D app (Android Settings, Apps, Super Slice 3D, Storage, Clear storage), or on the web clear this site's data in your browser. There is no copy anywhere else.

Network requests the game makes

The game's own files and its painted art come from lucidwinds.com, the same way any web page loads its files. That server sees an ordinary file request from your device and nothing about you or your play. After the first load the game keeps its own copies of what it has fetched, so it plays with no connection. The Daily dive is drawn from the date on your device; no server is asked.

The game loads the studio's music script (/music-unlocks.js) and, when you open the music player, its player (/music-player.js), both from lucidwinds.com. The player plays nothing until you ask it to. When you play a song it streams from lucidwinds.com, or, for some older public domain recordings, from archive.org (the Internet Archive). Those servers see an ordinary request for the song and nothing about you. Music needs a connection.

Permissions

The game asks for no device permissions. No location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts.

The Google Play app and the web

The Google Play app does everything above and nothing more. When you play in a web browser at lucidwinds.com instead, everything above still holds, with two optional extras the Play app does not have:

  • Sunbeams. Playing earns Sunbeams, the currency of our game Lucid Winds. They are counted in your browser's local storage (under sws_pending_sunbeams and related keys). If you have signed in to Lucid Winds in the same browser, the Sunbeams you earned are added to that account through our server (hosted on Google Firebase), which records your Lucid Winds account identifier and the number earned. Without a Lucid Winds sign in, nothing is sent. To do this the web copy loads Google's Firebase code from www.gstatic.com.
  • The feedback form. On the web, a feedback button opens a form. What you type, and the game's name and version, go to our server so we can read it. Only what you choose to send is sent.

Children

Super Slice 3D collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version is posted at this address.
