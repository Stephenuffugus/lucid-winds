Snakes & Ladders
Roll the die and race up the garden. Land at the bottom of a wooden ladder and your pawn climbs straight up. Land on a garden snake and you slide back down. First to land exactly on square 100 at the top wins.

How to play

1
The goal. Be the first pawn to land exactly on square 100 at the very top of the garden.
2
Rolling. On your turn, tap the big ROLL button (or tap the die). It spins and stops on a number from 1 to 6, and your pawn hops that many squares.
3
Ladders climb up. Land on the bottom of a wooden ladder and your pawn climbs all the way to the top. Free progress.
4
Snakes slide down. Land on a snake's head and you slide down its body to the tail, back toward the start.
5
Land exactly. You must finish on 100. If a roll would carry you past it, you bounce back the extra steps.
6
Winning. First pawn to reach 100 wins. Play the computer, or share one device and take turns with a friend.
