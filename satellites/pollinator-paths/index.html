A calm game of air traffic control, played in a night meadow.
Draw flight paths with your finger. Bees, butterflies and hummingbirds each want their own flower, and no two wings may ever meet.

How the meadow works

The goal. Land every flier on its matching flower pad and keep your three hearts.
The move. Touch a flier, drag a line, and end it on the pad that shows the same silhouette.
The signs. A safe landing chimes for 10 points. Two wings touching costs a heart, and at zero hearts the shift ends.
The finer points
👆
Touch a pollinator and draw. It follows your line. End the line on its matching flower pad and it glides home.
🐝
Three fliers, three pads. The bee flies a solid line, the butterfly a dashed one, the hummingbird a dotted one. Shapes match shapes, so the pads are silhouettes, never just colors.
💥
Two fliers touching is a dazed puff and a lost heart. Three hearts and the shift is over. Redraw a path any time, right up to the last second.
💮
Cross-Pollination: glowing blossom rings drift across the meadow. Steer two DIFFERENT species through the same ring moments apart and it bursts into a bonus bloom.
✿
Six bonus blooms fill a bouquet, and a full bouquet presses a keepsake flower into your Grove.
🌙
Zen Meadow never takes a heart, fliers just bounce apart. Practice the busy skies there.
