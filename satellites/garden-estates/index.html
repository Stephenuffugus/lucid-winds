Garden Estates
Sky Wolf Studios
Roll around the garden, buy plots, grow greenhouses, and charge rent. Complete a color set to build. Be the last gardener with coins in the pot. You play against friendly computer rivals.

How to play

1
The goal. Be the last gardener with coins. Buy plots, grow greenhouses, and collect rent until your rivals run dry.
2
Your turn. Tap Roll Dice. Your pawn walks that many spaces. Every time you pass the green Gate, the bank pays you $200.
3
Empty plot. Tap Buy to own it, or Pass to leave it. Own both plots of one color and you have a full set.
4
A rival's plot. You pay them rent. A full set and taller greenhouses mean much bigger rent.
5
Build. Own a full color set, then tap either of those plots on the board to raise a greenhouse. Build them evenly across the set.
6
Compost corner. Land on Go To Compost and you are sent to rest. Roll doubles or wait it out to get back on the path.
7
Cards. Step on a Weather or Almanac space and fate deals you a bonus or a bill.
8
Out of coins. If you cannot pay, your greenhouses are sold and your plots change hands. When one gardener is left, that gardener wins.
