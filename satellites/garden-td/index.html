How to Play

Goal. Pests march along the path toward your compost bin. Stop them before they reach the end. Each one that gets through costs a Leaf 🍃. Lose all your Leaves and the level is over.

Plant friends. Tap a dashed soil spot to open the tray, then pick a tower. They cost Seeds 🌱, which you earn by shooing pests and clearing waves.

Upgrade & sell. Tap a planted friend to upgrade it, change who it targets, or sell it back for most of your Seeds.

Know your friends. Marigolds shoot single pests. Cacti and puffballs hit crowds. The beehive is your answer to flyers. The sundew slows everything down. The pitcher melts armor. The sunflower snipes the big ones.

Earn Sap 🍯. Every level pays Sap you spend in the Potting Shed on skins and critters. Keep more Leaves to earn three of them per level.
