TOFU · Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
TOFU is played on your phone. It collects nothing about you and sends nothing about you anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no purchases inside the Google Play app.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game remembers lives on your device only, in local storage:

  • Your progress, under keys that begin tofu.: the dishes you have opened (tofu.progress), your best time on each (tofu.best), whether sound is off (tofu.mute) and whether you have seen the pool hint (tofu.hintPool).
  • A count of today's Sunbeams, under sw_sb_tofu. Only the web copy does anything with it (see below).
  • If you open the level workshop, the level you are drawing, under tofu.lab.

None of it leaves your device, and none of it names you. To erase it all, clear the storage of the TOFU app (Android Settings, Apps, TOFU, Storage, Clear storage), or on the web clear this site's data in your browser. There is no copy anywhere else.

Network requests the game makes

The game's own files come from lucidwinds.com, the same way any web page loads its files. That server sees an ordinary file request from your device and nothing about you or your play. After the first load the game keeps its own copies of what it has fetched, so it plays with no connection. Every sound in the game is made on the device.

Copying

In the level workshop, COPY puts the level you drew on your clipboard as text, only when you tap it. The game sends it nowhere.

Permissions

The game asks for no device permissions. No location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts.

The Google Play app and the web

The Google Play app does everything above and nothing more. When you play in a web browser at lucidwinds.com instead, everything above still holds, with one optional extra the Play app does not have:

  • Sunbeams. Playing earns Sunbeams, the currency of our game Lucid Winds. They are counted in your browser's local storage (under sws_pending_sunbeams and related keys). If you have signed in to Lucid Winds in the same browser, the Sunbeams you earned are added to that account through our server (hosted on Google Firebase), which records your Lucid Winds account identifier and the number earned. Without a Lucid Winds sign in, nothing is sent. To do this the web copy loads Google's Firebase code from www.gstatic.com.

Children

TOFU collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version is posted at this address.
