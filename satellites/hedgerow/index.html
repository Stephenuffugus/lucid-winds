How to play

Fence the garden pests into a corner and reclaim the bed. A Sky Wolf remake of the classic JezzBall.
Objective: reclaim 75% of every ground without losing all your leaves.
1
Tap the bed to grow a hedge wall. It sprouts both ways until it hits an edge or another hedge, splitting the bed in two.
2
Use the ⇆ / ⇅ button to choose whether the next hedge grows across or up-and-down.
3
Any patch of bed with no pests in it once a hedge closes gets reclaimed and planted. Reclaim 75% to clear the ground.
4
If a pest touches a hedge while it is still growing, the hedge wilts and you lose a leaf. Lose them all and it is over.
5
Clear grounds to earn Sunbeams. A clean ground (no leaves lost) grows one back.
