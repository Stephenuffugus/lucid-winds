# BURR BLAST — Story & Canon
_The little defender of the Lucid Winds garden. Source of truth for names, worlds, and voice._

## Voice (read first)
Warm, small, and gently funny. This is a garden defending itself, not a war.
**Nothing dies.** Pests **puff away** into dandelion fluff; forts **come down**.
No blood, no death words. Keep lines short. Let pictures carry it. It should feel
like a pop-up storybook you can flick across.

---

## The hero — BRAMBLE
A young burdock sprout no taller than a thumb, with a seed-burr for a heart and a
forked twig for a slingshot. Wears a bottlecap hat tipped back. Scrappy, cheerful,
stubborn as a root. Self-appointed **Keeper of the Patch**. Speaks in bursts.

> "Big forts start small. Knock 'em down while they're small."

## The trouble — THE HUSK
Every spring the pests keep to themselves. Not this one. **The Weevil King** has
rallied the swarm and declared the whole garden his — *the Husk* — and set them
hoarding every seed and sap-drop behind rickety forts. If the pests hoard the
seeds, nothing new ever grows. Bramble won't have it.

## The pests (cast)
- **Aphids** — round, green, big-eyed foot-soldiers who sit in the forts. Not evil,
  just following orders and a little dim. Popped, they burst into a puff of fluff.
- **The Weevil King** — the boss. A fat snout-weevil in a crown of chewed gold-leaf,
  perched on a hoard. Pompous, greedy, secretly a coward.
- **World lieutenants** (flavor, optional art):
  - W1 **Sergeant Nettle** — a bossy aphid drill-sergeant.
  - W2 **Old Pebblejaw** — a stone-hauling beetle who walls things in.
  - W3 **The Glazier** — a lacewing who blows fragile dewglass panes.
  - W4 **Fuse** — a firefly "demolitions expert" guarding the blastpods.

## The seeds — Bramble's satchel (the 6 ammo types)
Bramble collects trick seeds from friendly plants along the way:
| Seed | From | Trick (tap in flight) |
|---|---|---|
| **Burr** 🌰 | a burdock | steady and true, no trick |
| **Split Seed** 🍃 | a dandelion | bursts into three |
| **Acorn** 🌳 | the old oak | heavy; slams straight down |
| **Puffball** 🍄 | a mushroom ring | pops a spore cloud |
| **Thornpod** 💥 | a bramble cousin | hot-tempered; explodes |
| **Boomerang Pod** 🪃 | a maple samara | curves back around |

## The four worlds (the arc + the level canon)
Each world opens with a one-line title card in Bramble's voice.

### World 1 — SPROUT MEADOW  _(twig forts · learn to sling)_
> "Flimsy little lean-tos. Good. We start easy."
Sergeant Nettle has the aphids throwing up twig lean-tos across the meadow.
Levels: First Sprout · Two Little Pests · The Lean-To · Rooftop Sitter · Under the
Table · Twin Huts · Little Wall · Meadow Finale.

### World 2 — STONEYARD  _(pebble walls · the Acorn arrives)_
> "Rocks are heavy. So hit 'em with something heavier."
Old Pebblejaw has walled the aphids in with pilfered pebbles.
Levels: Stone Roost · Heavy Table · Two-Stone Wall · Mixed Masonry · The Overhang ·
Fortress Row · Balanced Beam · Stoneyard Finale.

### World 3 — DEWGLASS HOLLOW  _(fragile glass · Split + Puffball)_
> "Glass looks tough. Glass is a liar."
By the pond, the Glazier's forts are all fragile dewglass — one good tap and they
sing and shatter.
Levels: Glass House · Spread the Seed · Panes and Posts · Puff the Cluster ·
Greenhouse · Hanging Garden · Shatterworks · Hollow Finale.

### World 4 — BLASTPOD BOG  _(blastpods + iron · Thornpod + Boomerang · the keep)_
> "Let the fort blow up the fort."
The Weevil King's keep, ringed in stolen blastpods and cold iron that won't break —
so make it work for you.
Levels: First Spark · Chain Reaction · Around the Corner · Bunker · Powder Keg ·
The Redoubt · Bogworks · **The Weevil King** (boss).

## The ending
Bramble topples the keep. The hoarded seeds spill and scatter on the wind, and the
garden bursts into new green. The King, dethroned, scuttles off swearing he'll
"be back next spring." (He will — that's **Endless**: the Husk never really ends,
and Bramble keeps the watch, patch after patch, deeper into the Bog.)

## The intro comic (6 panels)
Square panels, storybook art, one caption each. Plays once on first open; replayable
from a **Story** button on the menu.

1. **Dawn.** The garden, quiet, one bloom breathing. — _"The garden of Lucid Winds. Quiet. Growing."_
2. **The swarm.** Aphids marching in, hauling twigs and seeds. — _"Until the swarm came, and hoarded every seed."_
3. **The King.** The Weevil King on a throne of stolen seeds, gold-leaf crown. — _"The Weevil King called it his. He called it the Husk."_
4. **One sprout.** Close on Bramble, bottlecap hat, picking up a forked twig. — _"One sprout disagreed."_
5. **The stance.** Bramble loads a burr, pulls the sling back tight. — _"Bramble. Keeper of the Patch."_
6. **The shot.** The burr flies, a twig fort topples, aphids puff into fluff. — _"One fort at a time."_

## The ending card (1 panel)
The garden in full bloom, seeds on the wind, the King scuttling off small in the
corner. — _"The seeds are free. Grow on."_
