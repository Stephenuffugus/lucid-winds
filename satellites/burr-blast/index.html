How to play

Pull back the slingshot on the left and let go. The further you pull, the harder the burr flies. A dotted guide shows the arc.

The pests have thrown up rickety forts of twig, stone, dewglass and blastpod. Knock the structures down, crush the critters, and clear every pest to win.

Each burr has a trick. Tap the screen mid-flight to trigger it:

🌰 Burr · steady and true, no trick.
🍃 Split Seed · bursts into three.
🌳 Acorn · heavy; tap to slam downward.
🍄 Puffball · tap to blast a spore cloud.
💥 Thornpod · explodes on tap or on impact.
🪃 Boomerang Pod · tap to curve back around.

Fewer burrs used = more stars. Three stars fills the patch with gold. Blastpod crates chain, so line up your shot and let the fort do the work.
