HOW TO FLY

Left half of the field is your flight stick. Right half aims and fires. The 🎯 toggle fires for you so one thumb is enough.

Enemies read by shape and motion: moths wander, wasps chase, needles dash, the serpent only dies by its glowing head, thorn mines burst into needles, and gravity bulbs drag you close, so overfeed them with fire until they burst.

Kills drop pollen, and pollen raises your multiplier. Every kill also plants a flower. Flowers mature in eight seconds, and flying over a mature bloom charges your Bloom Bomb.

In Pacifist Run your petals are sealed. Fly through the gates to detonate them.
