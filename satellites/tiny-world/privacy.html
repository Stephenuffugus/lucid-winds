Pixel Petri · Privacy policy

Last updated 25 September 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
Pixel Petri does not collect any data about you, and does not send anything about you anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no purchases inside the game. After the first time it opens, the game keeps its own files and can open with no connection at all.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game remembers lives on your device, in your browser's storage for lucidwinds.com. The app from Google Play runs inside your phone's browser (usually Chrome), so its saves sit in that browser's storage for lucidwinds.com, the same place they sit when you play Pixel Petri at lucidwinds.com in that browser.

  • Your worlds are kept in the browser database (IndexedDB) named tw: a short row for each world (when it was made and last saved, its size, its day, how many creatures live in it, and a small picture of it), two full copies of each world's save, and which world was open last.
  • The Scrapbook is in the same database: the stickers you have earned, a picture of each first thing that happened, and the creatures you have named.
  • Small settings are kept in the browser's local storage, under these names:
    • tw_sound: sound, music, quiet surprises and vibration on or off.
    • tw_music: how long the game has been played, which unlocks songs, and which songs are unlocked or switched off.
    • tw_tray and tw_tray_folded: your favourite and recent things in the tray, and whether the tray is folded.
    • tw_news: how the news line is shown.
    • tw_firsts, tw_ufo_seen and tw_poured: which surprises you have already been shown once, so they are not shown again.
    • tw_counts: the moment the game was first opened on this device, how many days it has been opened on, and how many seconds after that first opening each of seven things first happened (placing something, poking a creature, lifting one, a surprise, opening the Scrapbook, Safe mode, a second day). They never leave the device and nothing reads them but the game itself.

The game asks the browser to keep this storage when the phone runs short of space. Uninstalling the app does not by itself delete it (Chrome may offer to clear the site's data when you do). Clearing lucidwinds.com's site data in the browser deletes all of it for good: there is no copy anywhere else.

Files you save. Save to file writes the open world into a .tinyworld file, and a Scrapbook postcard is saved as a picture. Each is saved on your device the way your browser saves any download, and goes nowhere else. Open a file reads only the file you choose, on your device.

Network requests the game makes

The game's own files (its page, its code, its pixel font and its icons) come from lucidwinds.com, and when music is on the songs are played from lucidwinds.com. Nothing is loaded from any other server. The site's host sees an ordinary request for each file, as it would for any web page, and the game adds nothing about you to it. With no connection, the recorded songs cannot play, so the game plays its own music instead.

If something goes wrong inside the game, it notes the error in the page's memory so it can carry on. That note is never stored and never sent.

Permissions

The app asks for no device permissions: no location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts. Opening or saving a world uses your phone's own file picker, and only the file you choose is read or written. On phones that can, the game gives a short buzz when something is placed, when a meteor lands and when the ground quakes, which needs no permission.

Children

Pixel Petri is made to be played by anyone, and we expect children to play it. It has no ads, no purchases inside the game, no accounts, no chat and no way to reach other players or be reached by them. There is nowhere in the game to type: a creature is named by choosing from a list. The game has no links that lead out of it. It collects nothing from anyone, children included, so we hold nothing about any child to share or delete. A parent who wants the saves gone can clear lucidwinds.com's site data in the browser.
