HOW TO PLAY

The goal. Your gold pawn ● starts at the bottom. Walk it to any square in the top row before the rival ▲ walks to the bottom row. First pawn home wins.

Your turn is one action. Tap a dotted square to step your pawn one space. Or plant a fence: tap 🧱 FENCE, tap a gap between squares, then tap ✓. You get 10 fences, so does the rival.

Fences slow, they never trap. A fence blocks walking for both pawns. A green preview means it is allowed. Red means refused, no fence may ever seal off a pawn's last path home. Pawns nose to nose jump straight over each other, or diagonally if a fence sits behind.

↷ The vault, once per game. Boxed in? Tap ↷ VAULT, then tap the orange square to climb one fence beside you. That fence becomes an open gate for BOTH pawns forever, so the door you open, your rival may use.

Modes. Duel is the full game against three wardens. Daily deals everyone one trapped puzzle, win it in the stated moves, the vault is usually the key. Blitz allows ten seconds a turn. Zen is a free sandbox that pays no sunbeams.
