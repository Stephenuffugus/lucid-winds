WARDIAN · Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
Wardian lives on your phone. It collects nothing about you and keeps nothing about you anywhere else. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no purchases inside the game.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game remembers lives on your device only, in local storage:

  • Your jar, under the key lw_wardian_v1: what grows in it and what you placed in it, the journal and the pouch, the time you last looked in, the last weather word and temperature if you let the jar listen to your sky (never your location), and your settings (sound, motion, tilt, weather, northern or southern seasons).
  • The studio's music: which of its songs you have unlocked by playing, and how long and on how many days you have played, under sws_game_unlocks and keys that begin sws_music. If you use the music player, it also keeps its volume, the last song and any playlist you make, under sws_music_state and sws_playlists. These are counted on the device and decide when the next song unlocks.

To pick northern or southern seasons for a new jar, the game reads your phone's time zone name on the device. It is not sent anywhere, and you can change the seasons in Settings.

Copy My Jar puts your whole jar on your clipboard as text, only when you tap it, so you can move it yourself. Bring It In reads such a text back.

None of it leaves your device, and none of it names you. To erase it all, clear the storage of the Wardian app (Android Settings, Apps, Wardian, Storage, Clear storage), or on the web clear this site's data in your browser. There is no copy anywhere else.

Your sky (off unless you turn it on)

Settings has a button, Let The Jar Listen To Your Sky. It is off at the start. If you tap it, your phone asks whether to share your location. If you allow it, the game rounds your position to about a kilometre and asks api.open-meteo.com, a free weather service, for the current weather there. That request carries the rounded position and nothing else about you. While the setting is on it asks again at most about once an hour. Your location is never stored and never put on a photograph. Tap the button again and the setting goes off and the saved weather is cleared; deny the location and the jar runs on the clock alone.

Network requests the game makes

The game's own files come from lucidwinds.com, the same way any web page loads its files. That server sees an ordinary file request from your device and nothing about you or your play. After the first load the game keeps its own copies of its files on the device, so it plays with no connection. Every sound in the game is made on the device.

The Music button opens the studio's music player. It plays nothing until you ask it to. When you play a song it streams from lucidwinds.com, or, for some older public domain recordings, from archive.org (the Internet Archive). Those servers see an ordinary request for the song and nothing about you. Music needs a connection.

Photographs

Photo draws a picture of your jar on the device, stamped with the date, the hour and the weather word. It is handed to your phone's share sheet, or saved as a download, only when you tap it. The game uploads nothing.

Permissions

Location, only if you turn on the sky, as above. Tilt, only if you turn it on: the jar then reads your phone's motion on the device to tip the jar, and some phones ask your permission first. Nothing is sent. No camera, no microphone, no contacts. On phones that support it, some moments give a short vibration.

The Google Play app and the web

The Google Play app and the web page at lucidwinds.com run the same game and do the same things.

Children

Wardian collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version is posted at this address.
