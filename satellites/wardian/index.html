WARDIAN

A tiny world that lives on your time

In 1829 a doctor named Nathaniel Ward left a moth chrysalis in a sealed jar with a little soil, forgot about it, and came back to find a fern growing. He had not watered it once. The jar had been raining on itself.

This one keeps his habit. Night falls in it when night falls outside your window. Nothing in it can die, get sick, or leave.
