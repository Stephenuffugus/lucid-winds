Get through the scene without anyone hearing you.

Set your phone flat on a table and use both hands.

  1. Hold both cheeks, the two lower pads. Pressure rises the whole time, and a cheek lifted in the quiet is heard.
  2. Let goLet go under the noise. When a bus, the organ or applause plays, lift both cheeks to vent and put them back before it ends. Lift right as it starts for a Perfect.
  3. Answer the hands, the two upper pads. One lights with what the scene wants. Touch it before its ring runs out and hold it until it lets go.

A missed call is a fidget, and the second fidget is a strike. Three strikes and the room knows. Later acts call both hands at once, even under the noise.
