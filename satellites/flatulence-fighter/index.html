How to play

Hold both pads to clench it in. Pressure climbs the whole time you sit there.

Each hand tires as its bar fills. Lift one hand to rest it, then swap before it slips.

When a cover noise hits, tap VENT to drop the pressure. Vent the instant the noise starts for a perfect.

Tap leaks to plug them. Cough makes your own cover, sip drains pressure, waft fans a fresh strike away.

Any sound the room hears is a strike. Three strikes and you are exposed. Outlast the acts and run up the score.
