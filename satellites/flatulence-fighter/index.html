The program notes

How to play

  1. Hold both pads to clench it in. Pressure climbs the whole time you sit there.
  2. VentVent under the noise. Pressure only drops while a cover noise plays. Vent the instant it starts for a Perfect.
  3. Tap a leak on the stage to plug it before it squeaks.

Each hand tires as its bar fills. Lift one hand to rest it, then swap before it slips. With one hand up the pressure climbs slowly; with both up it climbs fast, so a thumb can leave a pad for a moment.

The props on the stage: Cough fakes your own cover noise, hold Sip to drink the pressure down (you cannot clench while sipping), and Waft fans a fresh strike away for a moment.

Hold breath slows the rise. Let go before you gasp. A wobble creeps in: hold Lean left or Lean right against it.

Any sound the room hears is a strike. Three strikes and you are exposed. Outlast the acts and run up the score.
