Roof
Your move is in
  1. Every tile has a head (top row) and a tail (bottom row). Each cell carries 0–3 copper studs.
  2. Drag a tile up from your tray (in no-drag mode, tap a gold spot, then tap it again). Its head must cover the exposed tail of exactly one tile: straight (both cells) or staggered (one cell, the other over empty roof).
  3. Strength rule: every head cell must have at least as many studs as the tail cell it covers. A red cell means too weak.
  4. Heads can never be covered, so they're banked. Tails stay exposed until someone covers them.
  5. Tap a tray tile to flip it (head ⇄ tail; in no-drag mode the first tap picks it). Swipe it sideways to mirror it.
  6. No legal hang? You drop the tile below the roof to start a new branch.

Scoring: every visible stud of your color. Heads are safe and score ×1. A tail that's still exposed at the end and has weathered a full round (every other player had a turn since it was hung) catches the rain: ×2. A completely filled row (a course) gives +3 to whoever shows the most studs in it. Ties are shared wins.

Turn order snakes like a roofing crew working back along the roof: 1-2-3, 3-2-1, 1-2-3… so sometimes you hang two in a row.

A moss sprout marks a weathered tail. Cracked tails are ones you can cover this turn. A pulsing red edge means one of your tails is at risk. A +3 tag at the left edge marks a full row (a course) in the colour of the player it pays; a grey tie tag pays nobody.
