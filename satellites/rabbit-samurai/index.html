Rabbit Samurai
Sky Wolf Studios
A little bunny warrior hops through crate yards and burrow tunnels. Gather carrots, free three caged mice, fling off spinning porcupines with your rope, ride bounce pads and cannons up to high ledges, then grab the diamond at the end.

How to play

1
Get to the diamond. Each level is a small maze of platforms. Reach the sparkling diamond at the far end to clear it. An old mole rat pops up and gives you a thumbs up.
2
Move and jump. Hold the left and right pads at the bottom to run. Tap the big JUMP button to hop. On a keyboard use the arrow keys and the space bar.
3
Throw your rope. Press and hold anywhere on the screen near a spinning porcupine to grab on, then drag left or right to swing where you want. Let go to fling off (or tap JUMP).
4
Bounce and launch. Land on a green bounce pad to spring high. Step onto a cannon and it shoots you way up to reach the top ledges.
5
Free the mice. Three mice sit in little cages. Run into a cage to pop the mouse out. Freed mice follow you in a happy line.
6
Grab every carrot for a bigger score. Watch the spikes in the pits. Fall in and you lose a heart. Clear a level to earn sunbeams.
