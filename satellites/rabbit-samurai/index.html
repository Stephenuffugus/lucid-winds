Rabbit Ronin
Sky Wolf Studio
A rabbit ronin and a dagger on a vine. Swing the bramble canyons, slice through hedgehogs and crows at full speed, free the caged mice, and spend your carrots at the dojo shop on a longer vine, a mightier fling and a fourth heart.
Tap to continue

How to play

1
Get to the diamond. Each level is a small maze of platforms. Reach the sparkling diamond at the far end to clear it. An old mole rat pops up and gives you a thumbs up.
2
Move and jump. Hold the left and right pads at the bottom to run. Tap the big JUMP button to hop. On a keyboard use the arrow keys and the space bar. Clear The Crate Yards and you learn the AIR DASH: tap JUMP in the air to lunge forward.
3
Throw your vine. Hold the VINE button (or press anywhere on the field) and your dagger flies to the nearest glowing anchor. Drag left or right to pump the swing, release to fling. The ring shows which anchor you will catch.
4
Speed is your blade. Swing fast enough and you slice straight through hedgehogs and crows. Touch them slowly and they hurt you. You can also stomp a hedgehog from above.
5
Free the mice. Three mice sit in little cages. Run into a cage to pop the mouse out. Freed mice follow you in a happy line. Bounce pads and cannons launch you to the high ledges.
6
Carrots are coin. Every carrot you carry out of a cleared dojo goes into your bank. Spend them at the Dojo Shop: longer vine, mightier fling, swifter paws, a fourth heart. Big spike pits have no floor worth landing on. Swing them.
7
Dash to cut. Once you have cleared the first six dojos, the DASH button lunges you forward, blade out, on the ground or in the air. It needs a moment to recover, unless the dash connects: a kill cools it at once, so a line of enemies is a chain, and every link is worth more.
