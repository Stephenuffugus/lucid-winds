How to play

1
Each tile is split into four triangles, and each triangle holds a number from 0 to 9. The numbers are also color-coded.
2
Drag tiles from the tray into the bed. Two tiles may sit side by side only if the numbers on their touching edges match.
3
A mismatched seam glows red. Fill every cell with no red seams to solve the bed.
4
Stuck? A Nudge locks one correct tile in place. A hint-free solve earns a little more sun.
5
Solve beds to earn Sunbeams for your garden. Faster, bigger, hint-free solves earn more.
