Stop Motion
Sky Wolf Studios
Take a photo, move your object a tiny bit, take another. Onion skin shows a ghost of your last shot so you can line up the next one. Turn all your photos into a little movie you can play and share. Helpful tips show up as you go.

How it works

1
Prop your phone so it stays still. Point the camera at your object.
2
Tap Take Photo to snap a frame. Move your object a tiny bit, then snap again.
3
Turn on Onion skin to see a faded ghost of your last photo. Line up the next shot with it, then nudge your object.
4
Your frames fill the strip at the bottom. Tap a frame to select, then move it or delete it.
5
Tap Play to watch your movie. Slide Speed to make it faster or slower.
6
Tap Export to build a video you can share or save. No camera? Tap Import to add photos from your phone.
Tips for a great stop motion
+
Move your object in small steps. Small moves make smooth motion.
+
Keep the light steady so the film does not flicker.
+
More frames means smoother film. Aim for at least 12.
