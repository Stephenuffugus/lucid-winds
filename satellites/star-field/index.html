How to Play

Plant stars. Tap a cell to plant a star. Tap again to leave a dot that means no star here. One more tap clears the cell.
Win the sky. Place exactly one star in every row, every column, and every colored bed. The sky blooms the moment the last star lands.
Give them room. No two stars may touch, not even at the corners. Touching stars glow pink until you move one.
Helpers. ◈ auto-marks the cells a new star rules out. 💡 plants one correct star for you. ↺ deals a fresh sky.
