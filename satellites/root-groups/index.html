Root Groups
Sky Wolf Studios
A word puzzle like Connections, sort sixteen tiles into four hidden groups of four.
Tap four tiles that share a secret thread and watch each group bloom. The trickiest group seeds your garden.

How to play

1
Tap four tiles you think share a hidden thread, then tap Submit.
2
A correct group blooms and its name is revealed. Solve all four groups to clear the board.
3
Groups run easy to devious (one leaf to four). Watch for a tile that looks like it fits the wrong group.
4
Solve the trickiest root group to drop a rare seed in your pouch.
5
Hint locks one tile to its group for a little dew, so you are never stuck. Four wrong guesses ends the board gently.
