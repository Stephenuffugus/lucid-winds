Sky Wolf Studios
Build a course of clay traps, then drop poor little Clayton in. Every bonk, spin and boom pays out coins. Spend them on nastier gear. A cozy claymation ragdoll sandbox.

How to play

🔺
Tap a trap in the tray, then tap the room to place it. Drag any trap to move it; tap 🗑 then a trap to bin it.
🟢
Tap DROP and Clayton falls from the ceiling. Every hard bonk, saw-spin and explosion earns coins.
🪙
Spend coins in the tray to unlock the buzz saw, bombs and the big fan. Bigger danger, bigger payouts.
↻
Drop again any time for a fresh Clayton. Your traps stay put.
☀️
Rack up damage to earn Sunbeams for your garden.
