Seed Reel
Sky Wolf Studios
Spin a garden bed of seeds, bees and creatures. Neighbours help each other bloom. Every spin you get one free replant — move a single tile to line up a synergy — then harvest nectar to meet the season's Bloom Quota.

How to play

🎰
Spin to scatter your garden's tiles across the bed. Each tile is worth nectar.
🐝
Neighbours help each other. A Bee beside a Flower, Rain beside Seeds — adjacent friends pay bonus nectar. Watch for the glowing links.
✋
The Gardener's Hand: once per spin, tap a tile then tap a cell to move it. Line up a synergy before you harvest. The preview shows the new total.
🌱
Harvest, then draft one new tile into your garden. Every few spins a Bloom Quota comes due — make enough nectar or the garden goes to seed for winter.
🌟
Rare companions (Koi, Toad, the Beholder) are big earners. Pull one and build around it.
