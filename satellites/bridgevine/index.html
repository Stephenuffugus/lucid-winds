Sprout living pods, weave vine struts, and grow a bridge that holds its own weight all the way to the Sun Bloom.

Trellis Trials

Each trial hands you a small pouch of pods and one gap that wants crossing.

How Bridgevine works

1
Drag on the world to sprout a pod. It weaves vine struts to its two or three nearest neighbors. Two struts minimum, so build from what already stands.
2
The vine sags and swings under real weight. Struts show strain by color and notch marks, zero notches easy, one notch worried, two notches about to snap.
3
An overworked strut snaps. Anything cut off from the roots falls away, so triangles are your friends.
4
Dew Ballast: touch a hanging dewdrop with your vine to pouch it, then tap a strut to spend one dew and crystallize it. Crystal struts hold far more before breaking.
5
Reach the Sun Bloom and hold it for a slow breath. The load path blossoms and a keepsake is pressed into your Grove.
6
Stuck or snapped? The reset leaf replants the trial fresh, no penalty.
