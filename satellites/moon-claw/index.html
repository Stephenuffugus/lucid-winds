How to play

Read the pile before you spend a token.
☝
Hold anywhere and the claw glides across the cabinet. Let go and it drops where it hangs. That is the whole control.
⚖
The claw is honest. It grips with the same strength every single time. Whether a prize comes up is physics, not a payout table.
◎
Centre it over a plump body for a solid grip. Clip a limb and the prize hangs crooked, swings on the carry, and can slip at the worst moment.
☰
Buried prizes need digging. Every grab and every nudge moves the pile, so take the top ones first and open a path.
●
Five tokens a cabinet. One drop is one token, hit or miss.
✦
Some cabinets hold a golden koi at the very bottom of the pile. If you see a glint down there, it is real, and it is worth the dig.
A loose grip is not a lost grip. Watch how hard the prize is swinging on the way to the chute: a gentle sway makes it home, a wild one lets go.
