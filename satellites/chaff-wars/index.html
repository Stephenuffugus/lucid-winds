How to play

1
Seedpods fall two at a time. Move them with ◀ ▶, spin either way with ↺ ↻ or by tapping the board, and press DROP to slam a pod down instantly.
2
Line up four or more of the same color touching side to side or top to bottom and they pop. Pods above fall into the gap, so one clear can trigger the next for a chain.
3
Every chain drops grey Chaff on your rival. The bigger the chain, the more Chaff you send. Chaff never pops on its own, but it shatters when a colored group right next to it clears.
4
Send Chaff while Chaff is falling toward you and the two cancel out, so a well timed chain is also a shield. Whoever gets stacked up to the top first loses.
5
Clear your whole board for an All Clear, which loads a huge bonus onto your very next attack.
