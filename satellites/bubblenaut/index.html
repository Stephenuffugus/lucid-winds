BUBBLENAUT
Sky Wolf Studios
Trap runaway alien critters in bubbles, pop them, and collect the treasures of five strange worlds.

How to play

You are a bubblenaut on specimen duty. The critters got loose across five worlds.
Objective: clear every critter from the room to open the next one. Five rooms per world, five worlds.
◀▶
Move with the pads (or A and D). Fall off the bottom and you drift back in from the top.
⬆
Jump with the green button (or W). You can hop up through thin platforms.
◯
Bubble with the blue button (or S). A fresh bubble traps any critter it touches, then floats away.
✦
Pop a floating bubble by touching it. A trapped critter bursts into that world's treasure. Grab it before it fades. Bubbles popped together chain!
!
Leave a critter bubbled too long and it breaks out angry. Touching a loose critter costs a life.
