HUES · Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
The Google Play copy of Hues does not collect any data about you, and does not send anything about you anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no purchases inside the game. After the first load, the game plays with no network connection. Music needs one to play its songs. The copy on the open web has two optional extras, described in their own section below.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game remembers lives on your device only, in your browser's local storage:

  • Your scores and streaks (your best Endless run, your Daily results, best and streak), your coins, the borders you own and the one you have equipped, today's goals, the difficulty you picked and whether sound is on (the keys that start with hm.).
  • The songs you have unlocked for the Music player.

None of it leaves your device. Clearing the site's browsing data deletes all of it permanently; there is no copy anywhere else.

Network requests the game makes

The game's own files, and its songs when music plays, come from lucidwinds.com. Its three typefaces come from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, the same way any web page loads its files. Each of those servers sees an ordinary file request from your device and nothing about you or your play.

After the first load, the game keeps its own copies of its files, so it plays offline. Songs are played from lucidwinds.com when you listen and are not kept on the device, so music needs a connection.

Feedback

In the Google Play copy, the Feedback button opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and the game never sees it.

Sharing

Sharing your result happens only when you tap Share. It goes through your phone's own share sheet to the app you pick, and it carries your result (the round's colors and your score), nothing about you. The game never shares anything by itself.

Permissions

The app asks for no device permissions. No location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts.

The copy on the open web

When you play Hues in a web browser at lucidwinds.com instead of the Google Play app, everything above still holds, with two optional extras:

  • Sunbeams. Playing earns Sunbeams, the currency of our game Lucid Winds. They are counted in your browser's local storage. If you have signed in to Lucid Winds in the same browser, the Sunbeams you earned are added to that account through our server (hosted on Google Firebase), which records your Lucid Winds account identifier and the number earned. Without a Lucid Winds sign in, nothing is sent. To do this the web copy loads Google's Firebase code from www.gstatic.com.
  • The feedback form. On the web, Feedback opens a form. What you type, and the game's name and version, go to our server so we can read it. Only what you choose to send is sent.

Children

Hues is a general audience game and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version is posted at this address.
