JIMOTHY — SKIN 16: DR. JIMOTHY

MAKE TWO SHEETS FROM THIS DOCUMENT:  16a  and  16b
Nine frames on each sheet, eighteen in total. Label the files exactly 16a and 16b.

================================================================================================
THE CHARACTER
================================================================================================

Dr. Jimothy — one of the playable characters in Jimothy, a storybook hopper set on the
rainy streets of Seattle.

STANCE (locked — the same in every frame):
  Quadrupedal at rest on flat plantigrade palms; he rears onto his haunches or up on his
  hind legs in any frame where a forepaw has to carry something.

WARDROBE AND PROPS (locked — these appear in ALL EIGHTEEN frames, including the deaths):
  In all 18: navy mortarboard with a gold tassel off the viewer-left corner; navy gold-
  trimmed sash knotted at his right shoulder falling in a V across the chest; one firm cream
  diploma roll tied with a gold ribbon, carried held in a paw, bitten crossways in the jaws,
  clamped under an arm, or wedged through the sash — never set down, never out of frame.
  Three frame-specific held extras only: fry carton (eat), takeaway cup (coffee), black
  umbrella (umbrella).

HOW THIS BODY WORKS:
  He is the hero's body exactly: round and spherical, no visible neck, head perched high
  above the shoulders, long skinny limbs, and a SHORT STUBBY tail that never streams, ropes
  or curls out behind him. Raccoon hands are the whole point — flat plantigrade palms, five
  long spread fingers, heel down, never knuckles. The one real problem on this sheet is prop
  logistics: a frame cannot be four-square AND hold something, so whenever the diploma needs
  a hand he rears up, and whenever he must be four-square or running on all four limbs the
  diploma goes through the sash or into the jaws. The stubby tail cannot act as a tripod, so
  "reared up" here always means sitting back on the haunches or standing on both hind feet.

================================================================================================
RULES — THESE APPLY TO BOTH SHEETS, AND TO EVERY SHEET IN THIS PROJECT

  LAYOUT
  • NINE frames per sheet, laid out 3 across and 3 down, in the numbered order given below.
  • Background is flat magenta #FF00FF, edge to edge.
  • ⛔ Put a clear gap, or a white divider line, between every cell. NEVER let two frames touch.
    The cutter separates frames by finding the background between them; frames that touch get read
    as one object and come out sliced wrong.
  • Nothing may run off the edge of its own cell. Keep tails, wings, capes and effects inside.

  THE CAMERA — IDENTICAL IN ALL NINE
  • Three-quarter high angle looking slightly down, character facing the viewer.
  • The one exception is the sideways run frame, which is a side-on profile.

  SCALE — THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE ON THE SHEET
  • The character must be THE SAME SIZE in every frame. The game normalises each frame on its own,
    so a frame that is drawn bigger or wider than the others makes the character visibly change
    size mid-animation.
  • Target proportions: roughly as wide as it is tall, and never more than about 1.3 times wider
    than tall. If a pose wants to sprawl wider than that, tuck it in.
  • ⛔ Effects count as part of the frame. A big spray of water or a long trail of speed lines makes
    the game shrink the animal to fit them in. Keep every effect tight to the body.

  WHAT MUST NEVER BE PAINTED
  • ⛔ NO GROUND. No floor, no pavement, no puddle, no curb, no drain, no cast shadow, no scattered
    leaves or crumbs on the deck. The character is cut out and dropped onto the game's own street —
    anything painted on the ground survives the cut and floats there as grey scum.
  • ⛔ NO DETACHED PROPS. Anything not touching the body becomes its own separate sprite, or makes
    the character shrink. Props stay in a hand, a mouth, a pocket or against the body.
  • ⛔ NO READABLE TEXT. No lettering on badges, signs, newspapers, diplomas or paddles. Text is
    unreadable at game size, and one of these frames gets mirrored, which reverses it. Suggest
    print with texture instead.
  • ⛔ NO HOT PINK OR MAGENTA in the artwork itself — not in steam, glow, fabric or effects. Those
    tones get deleted along with the background. Keep effects warm, cool or neutral.

  STYLE
  • Match the existing Jimothy artwork: warm, funny, a little melancholy, rainy
    Seattle at night. Rich shadows, wet highlights.
  • Effects are painted INTO the frame — fire streaks, water bursts, circling stars, swirling
    bottlecaps. The game does not add them.
  • Deaths are funny-sad, never grim and never gory. Circling stars, not X-eyes.

SHEET 16a  —  NINE FRAMES, 3 ACROSS AND 3 DOWN, IN THIS ORDER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. IDLE
   In the game: standing, at rest — the default  ·  on screen constantly
   Standing four-square facing camera on flat spread palms, mortarboard dead level, chin a
   touch high, the rolled diploma wedged through the sash across his chest so no paw is
   borrowed. Gold tassel hangs still off the viewer-left corner, sash falls in a neat V,
   fine drizzle beading his shoulders.

2. SIT
   In the game: 4 seconds without input  ·  until the player moves
   THE QUIET BEAT: sitting up on his haunches with the diploma open flat across his lap
   and both paws resting on it, but his half-lidded eyes are aimed past the camera at
   nothing — the ceremony is over and nobody stayed. Tassel has drooped forward past his
   cheek and he has not bothered to move it; one shoulder rain-dark.

3. EAT
   In the game: 8 seconds without input  ·  until the player moves
   Sitting up on his haunches with a paper fry carton hugged to his chest in one paw, the
   other holding a single fry up between two fingers exactly like a piece of chalk mid-
   lecture, mouth open on a point nobody asked for. Diploma wedged through the sash with
   one stray fry balanced on its gold ribbon; tassel swinging in front of his eye and
   ignored.

4. CROUCH
   In the game: first quarter of every hop  ·  0.07s, hundreds of times a run
   Flattened low on all fours, haunches up, weight forward on spread plantigrade palms,
   mortarboard tipped down over his brow with the tassel dangling in front of his nose.
   Diploma bitten crossways low in the jaws, well below the eye line so his face still
   reads; eyes locked forward, dignity temporarily suspended.

5. LEAP
   In the game: middle half of every hop  ·  0.15s, hundreds of times a run
   Airborne on a compact upward diagonal — never stretched flat — front paws thrown
   forward with the diploma held ahead like a relay baton, hind legs tucked close, belly
   to the viewer, mouth open in a delighted bark. Sash and tassel kick back in short
   streaks that stay inside his own box; the mortarboard rides a hair clear of his head,
   still overlapping it.

6. LAND
   In the game: last quarter of a hop, plus a hold  ·  0.17s, hundreds of times a run
   Front paws slapped down flat with fingers spread, body low, weight absorbed through the
   shoulders, a tight ring of spray bursting off his own palms and hind feet and touching
   nothing else. Mortarboard slumped over one eye with the tassel across his face, diploma
   back through the sash, head coming up, whiskers forward.

7. RUN-R
   In the game: a sideways hop to the right  ·  0.28s
   Side-on quadruped bound to the right, body low and long, all four limbs in the stride,
   diploma wedged tight through the sash so no paw is stolen from the run. Mortarboard
   jammed down over the brow, tassel and sash blown flat back along his spine, water
   flicking off his hind paws in a short contact spray.

8. DASH-RUN
   In the game: hopping while the coffee dash is up  ·  0.19s
   Flat-out bound, limbs at full stretch, gold and cream speed streaks ripping past close
   along his flanks, eyes wide and ears pinned flat. The diploma has come untied in the
   sash and unrolled about a hand's length, the loose end curling back over his shoulder
   and no further — no paper tail leaves his box; tassel a rigid straight line behind him.

9. COFFEE
   In the game: STANDING STILL while the dash is up  ·  4.2s, or 7.0s on a Double Shot
   FEET PLANTED, NOT RUNNING: up on his hind legs vibrating in place, takeaway cup raised
   in one paw, diploma clamped under the opposite arm, mortarboard knocked askew with the
   tassel caught mid-whip in a full blurred circle around his head doing all the motion
   work. Pupils tiny in enormous eyes, fur bristled, jitter lines, warm fire licks and
   pale cream steam curling up around his own back feet — one corner of the diploma very
   slightly singed.

SHEET 16b  —  NINE FRAMES, 3 ACROSS AND 3 DOWN, IN THIS ORDER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. MAGNET
   In the game: standing while the Snacks magnet is up  ·  6.5s
   Up on his hind legs, diploma half-unrolled between both paws at chest height,
   mortarboard level again, mouth open in pure astonished delight that the world is
   bringing him things. Gold bottlecaps and fries spiral in on a tight gold-and-cream arc
   that overlaps his silhouette, two already landed in the curl of the paper, tassel
   lifted on the swirl.

2. UMBRELLA
   In the game: standing while an umbrella shield is held  ·  indefinite, until it takes a hit
   Up on his hind legs under a black umbrella whose canopy is only a little wider than his
   shoulders and tilted to keep clear of the frame edge, mortarboard perfectly dry and
   perfectly level beneath it, diploma pressed to his chest under the sash. Rain streaks
   off the rim into a short spray that touches his own feet — no puddle, no wet ring, no
   ground; small smug closed-mouth smile.

3. SHIELD
   In the game: the instant the shield eats a hit  ·  0.55s
   Braced low on his hind feet, both forearms up with the diploma held horizontally across
   his face like a professor blocking a thrown eraser, eyes squeezed shut, teeth gritted.
   A bright blue-violet energy dome flares tight around him with rain streaking off it;
   mortarboard knocked back on his crown, tassel flung upward.

4. SCARED
   In the game: a gull or crow is lining up a dive  ·  0.82 to 1.15s
   Up on his hind legs, both paws clutching the rolled diploma to his chest like a very
   small shield, head craned back and up at the sky. Eyes huge and white-rimmed, ears
   flat, mortarboard slid back but still hooked on his skull with the tassel standing bolt
   upright, drizzle lines slanting past close.

5. FLEE
   In the game: the street sweeper is closing in  ·  seconds at a time
   Quadruped sprint to the left, mouth open in panic, short warm-red speed streaks hugging
   his flanks, ears flat, stubby tail bottled out. Mortarboard jammed low with the tassel
   whipping forward past his cheek, diploma still wedged in the sash with only its ribbon
   end flapping — nothing tears loose and no marks or symbols in the air.

6. CHEER
   In the game: LEVEL CLEAR — the camera zooms in on them  ·  2.6s, the biggest moment in the game
   Up on his hind legs, one arm bent up with the diploma raised, the other paw open just
   below the mortarboard he has flicked a thumb's width clear of his crown — the cap still
   overlaps his own head, tassel whirling. Gold ribbon sparks and confetti burst in close
   around his shoulders, mouth wide open, rain lit gold.

7. KO
   In the game: death by traffic, gull or steam  ·  1.55s, camera leans in, light closes
   Flat on his back, all four legs in the air with palms up, dazed half-moon lids and a
   small satisfied smile, gold stars circling his head — wounded dignity, entirely pleased
   with himself. Mortarboard tipped upside down against his shoulder and still touching
   him, tassel draped over his arm, and the diploma has unrolled across his belly to
   reveal a child's crayon drawing of a round raccoon beside a gold seal and ribbon — no
   lettering.

8. DIZZY
   In the game: death by the street sweeper  ·  1.55s, same treatment
   Slumped back on his haunches, spiral eyes, tongue out, stars looping close around his
   head. The mortarboard has rotated a quarter turn so the tassel hangs dead in front of
   his nose and he is going cross-eyed at it; the diploma sags in a floppy bent arc from
   one slack paw, still gripped.

9. SPLASH
   In the game: death by water  ·  1.55s, same treatment
   The instant of the burst — soaked, arms flung up, mouth open in shock inside an
   exploding crown of bright white and cyan water that closes around his middle.
   Mortarboard gone to sodden cardboard folded over one ear, tassel plastered flat to his
   cheek, diploma limp and unfurling in one raised paw with the gold seal bleeding a soft
   halo — no ink lines, no letters.

NOTES FOR ME WHEN I CUT THIS
================================================================================================
• NO GROUND IN ANY OF THE 18. No wet asphalt, puddles, ripples, drying smears, cast shadows,
scattered fries or a dropped cap lying on the floor — the cutter keeps every non-magenta
island and they all survive as grey scum under a floating raccoon. Every effect must touch
him: spray off his own palms, steam around his own feet, water crown closing on his body.
• ENVELOPE. The mortarboard corners are the widest and highest point of his silhouette and
sit above the skull line, so leave clear margin all round every cell and let nothing touch a
divider. Every frame including effects must land between about 0.80:1 and 1.35:1 and inside
roughly 236x224px — the widest offenders were dash-run's paper tail and cheer's cap toss,
both now pulled back inside his box.
• PROP LEDGER — mortarboard, gold tassel, navy sash and cream diploma roll appear in ALL
EIGHTEEN. The diploma never leaves his body and never touches ground; it is a firm tight
roll in every frame except the three where the damage is the joke (dash-run half-unrolled,
dizzy floppy, splash limp). Held extras appear once each: fry carton (eat), cup (coffee),
umbrella (umbrella).
• NO READABLE TEXT ANYWHERE. The diploma is blank cream paper with a gold seal and ribbon —
never a word, never a date, never a crest with lettering. Ko's reveal is a wordless crayon
drawing. No '!!' marks in flee, no numbers, no badge type.
• COLOUR AT 59px. Navy board and sash against gold tassel and ribbon is the entire
silhouette read — keep those exact navy and gold values identical in all 18. Effects stay
pale cream, gold, warm red or bright blue-violet: pink steam, magenta water and blue-violet
domes get bitten apart by the magenta knockout.
• SCALE AND ANCHOR. Match his BODY to hero Jimothy's idle first, then add the cap on top —
he must be the same raccoon size as Jimothy and the same size as himself in all 18,
checkable by the head being the same pixel count every frame. Sprites are centre-anchored,
so keep his mass at the centre of the painted content; a high cap toss or a low spray ring
will push him off his lane line.
• Left-facing run: Must be painted separately — the tassel hangs off the viewer-left corner,
the sash knots at his right shoulder and falls in an asymmetric V, and the diploma sits
left-of-centre in the sash, so a mirror jumps all three sides mid-stride on hard props the
eye tracks; the engine's mirror fallback is not wired for fully animated characters anyway,
so a missing run-l falls through to the procedural blob.
