================================================================================================
THE CHARACTER
================================================================================================

The King of Hop — one of the playable characters in Jimothy, a storybook hopper set on the
rainy streets of Seattle. He is Jimothy the raccoon dressed as a stage dancer from another
era, and he takes it completely seriously. He is a RACCOON in a costume: a raccoon face, a
raccoon mask, raccoon paws. Never a human face, never a portrait of any real person.

STANCE (locked — the same in every frame):
  UPRIGHT IN ALL EIGHTEEN. He is a dancer and he never drops to all fours, not even in the
  athletic frames: crouch, leap, land, run-r, dash-run and flee are all DANCE MOVES performed
  on his hind legs. Weight on the balls of the feet, knees soft, spine long.

WARDROBE AND PROPS (locked — these appear in ALL EIGHTEEN frames, including the deaths):
  IN ALL 18: a black felt fedora with a black satin band, worn tipped low toward his RIGHT
  eye; ONE white sequined glove on his LEFT paw ONLY (frame-right when he faces the viewer)
  — the right paw is always bare raccoon paw, this asymmetry is the whole costume, never
  paint two gloves; a short black sequined jacket worn open, sleeves pushed a little up the
  forearm; a plain white V-neck tee under it; black trousers cut HIGH, ending well above the
  ankle; bright white sparkly socks filling that gap; plain black loafers. His short stubby
  ringed tail comes out under the jacket hem. Per-frame props only, never carried outside
  their frame: a plain paper takeaway cup (coffee), a compact black umbrella (umbrella), and
  the identical gold bottlecap in magnet, cheer, dizzy and splash.
  Sparkle is part of the wardrobe: tiny white and pale gold glints ON the glove, the socks
  and the jacket sequins only, always touching the fabric, never floating free.

HOW THIS BODY WORKS:
  He is Jimothy's own body — round and spherical with no visible neck, the head perched high
  on the shoulders, long skinny limbs, and a SHORT STUBBY ringed tail; never the long
  streaming trash-panda tail, in any frame. Five long spread fingers on each paw, and the
  hands are half of why this character works, so keep the gloved paw readable in every
  frame: it is the brightest thing on him. The fedora is a HARD prop sitting on a body that
  squashes and stretches, so re-draw it honestly in each pose rather than smearing it with
  the fur — a warped hat reads as a mistake instantly. His comic engine is that a round,
  damp street raccoon moves with total, unearned, razor-sharp precision, and never once
  acknowledges that anyone is watching. The hat brim hides one eye in every living frame;
  the eye you can see is calm, half-lidded and utterly certain.

================================================================================================
RULES — THESE APPLY TO BOTH SHEETS, AND TO EVERY SHEET IN THIS PROJECT
================================================================================================

  LAYOUT
  • NINE frames per sheet, laid out 3 across and 3 down, in the numbered order given below.
  • Background is flat magenta #FF00FF, edge to edge.
  • ⛔ Put a clear gap, or a white divider line, between every cell. NEVER let two frames touch.
    The cutter separates frames by finding the background between them; frames that touch get read
    as one object and come out sliced wrong.
  • Nothing may run off the edge of its own cell OR the edge of the sheet. Keep the hat, the
    raised arm, the jacket and every effect well inside. Leave a clear magenta margin all round.

  THE CAMERA — IDENTICAL IN ALL NINE
  • Three-quarter high angle looking slightly down, character facing the viewer.
  • The one exception is the sideways run frame (30a frame 7), which is a side-on profile.

  SCALE — THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE ON THE SHEET
  • The character must be THE SAME SIZE in every frame. The game normalises each frame on its own,
    so a frame that is drawn bigger or wider than the others makes the character visibly change
    size mid-animation. His HEAD must be the same size in all eighteen.
  • He is upright, so every frame is a little taller than it is wide. Never wider than it is tall.
    If a pose wants to sprawl (the spin, the deaths), tuck it in.
  • ⛔ Effects count as part of the frame. A big spray of water or a long trail of speed lines makes
    the game shrink the animal to fit them in. Keep every effect tight to the body.

  WHAT MUST NEVER BE PAINTED
  • ⛔ NO GROUND. No floor, no stage, no spotlight pool, no pavement, no puddle, no cast shadow.
    The character is cut out and dropped onto the game's own street — anything painted on the
    ground survives the cut and floats there as grey scum.
  • ⛔ NO DETACHED PROPS. Anything not touching the body becomes its own separate sprite, or makes
    the character shrink. The hat stays on his head or in his paw, always touching him.
  • ⛔ NO READABLE TEXT. No lettering anywhere.
  • ⛔ NO HOT PINK OR MAGENTA in the artwork itself — not in sparkle, glow, fabric or effects. Those
    tones get deleted along with the background. Sparkle is white, pale gold or cool blue-white.
  • ⛔ EXACTLY two arms, two legs, one tail, five fingers on each paw, in every frame. Count them
    before you finish each cell. ONE glove, on his LEFT paw.
  • ⛔ No microphone, no stage lights, no crowd, no music notes, no logos, no lettering.

  STYLE
  • Match the existing Jimothy artwork: warm, funny, a little melancholy, rainy
    Seattle at night. Rich shadows, wet highlights. Rain beads on the hat felt and the sequins.
  • Effects are painted INTO the frame — glide streaks, water bursts, circling stars, swirling
    bottlecaps. The game does not add them.
  • Deaths are funny-sad, never grim and never gory. Circling stars, not X-eyes.

SHEET 30a  —  NINE FRAMES, 3 ACROSS AND 3 DOWN, IN THIS ORDER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. IDLE
   In the game: standing, at rest — the default  ·  on screen constantly
   Standing upright facing the viewer in the held opening pose: feet together, one knee
   softly bent with that heel lifted, the bare right paw resting on his belt line and the
   gloved left paw raised to pinch the front of the hat brim. Head tipped down so the brim
   hides his right eye; the visible eye is half-lidded and certain. Rain beads on the felt,
   one small white glint on the glove. Perfectly still, like the moment before the music.

2. SIT
   In the game: 4 seconds without input  ·  until the player moves
   Off duty for once: sat back on his haunches with his knees up and the white socks
   showing, the fedora taken off and held against his chest in the bare right paw while the
   gloved left paw smooths the brim with two fingers. Both eyes visible for the only time on
   this sheet, soft and tired; the ears are a little flattened from a whole night under a
   hat. This is his quiet frame — nobody saw the last show and he did it perfectly anyway.

3. EAT
   In the game: 8 seconds without input  ·  until the player moves
   Sat the same way with the hat back on and a single street fry held delicately between two
   fingers of the BARE right paw, the gloved left paw held well clear and up, fingers spread,
   so that not one crumb touches the sequins. Eyes closed in approval, cheek full, a second
   fry tucked in the hat band like a feather. Still a raccoon eating garbage, just refusing to
   get it on the glove.

4. CROUCH
   In the game: first quarter of every hop  ·  0.07s, hundreds of times a run
   The wind-up, upright: both knees bent deep and turned in, heels lifted, weight on the
   balls of the feet, body coiled low over them. Bare right paw pressed to the crown of the
   hat to hold it down, gloved left paw swept low and back behind his hip with the fingers
   spread. Brim over one eye, the visible eye locked forward, jacket hem swinging.

5. LEAP
   In the game: middle half of every hop  ·  0.15s, hundreds of times a run
   Fully airborne and bolt upright in the high toe-point: legs together and straight,
   loafers pointed straight down so he seems to stand on nothing, jacket flared open off
   both shoulders. Gloved left paw thrown straight up overhead with the fingers spread and
   one hard white glint on it, bare right paw holding the hat to his head. Face perfectly
   calm — he is being lifted, not jumping. Stubby tail straight out behind.

6. LAND
   In the game: last quarter of a hop, plus a hold  ·  0.17s, hundreds of times a run
   The landing, held: dropped onto one knee with the other leg bent in front, body upright,
   head bowed so the brim covers both eyes, gloved left paw flat against his chest and the
   bare right paw flung out low to the side for balance. A tight white crown of street water
   bursts where the knee and loafer meet the ground and touches them — no puddle, no
   pavement, no shadow. Jacket still settling around him.

7. RUN-R
   In the game: a sideways hop to the right  ·  0.28s
   SIDE-ON PROFILE, FACING RIGHT — the one side-on frame. He is caught mid-glide in the
   backwards slide: the front foot flat, the back foot up on the point of its toe, both
   knees soft, torso leaning a little forward over the feet, stubby tail held short behind.
   He is sliding BACKWARDS, toward frame-left, while facing right — so three short pale
   glide streaks sit in FRONT of his toes on the right-hand side, touching the loafer, and
   there is nothing at all behind him. The gloved left paw is the far arm: raise it to the
   hat brim so the white glove shows clearly above his muzzle. Bare right paw swings loose
   and low. Nothing under him, no reflection, no shadow.

8. DASH-RUN
   In the game: hopping while the coffee dash is up  ·  0.19s
   The spin at full speed, still upright on the toe of one loafer: body twisted a half turn
   so we see him three-quarter from the front with the jacket whipped out flat around him
   like a bell, the other knee drawn up tight. Gloved left paw clamped on the hat, bare
   right paw tucked to his chest, brim blurred at its edge. Two short tight rings of pale
   gold speed line wrap his waist and knees and TOUCH him, with warm gold-orange fire
   licking off the spinning heel. Keep it narrow: the spin is a column, not a sprawl.

9. COFFEE
   In the game: STANDING STILL while the dash is up  ·  4.2s, or 7.0s on a Double Shot
   Standing dead still and failing at it: feet planted together, a plain scorched paper cup
   pinched in the BARE right paw at chest height with the gloved paw held away from it,
   fur bristling out at the cuffs and collar, the visible eye gone wide with a pinprick
   pupil. One heel is a tapping blur and the hat is level but vibrating. Pale cream steam
   rises off the cup, short warm gold jitter lines hug his shoulders, small gold-orange
   fire licks at both heels. The feet do not move; everything else wants to.

SHEET 30b  —  NINE FRAMES, 3 ACROSS AND 3 DOWN, IN THIS ORDER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. MAGNET
   In the game: standing while the Snacks magnet is up  ·  6.5s
   Upright with his weight on one hip, the gloved left paw held out at shoulder height,
   palm up, fingers spread, and the hat tipped back far enough that BOTH eyes show for once
   — wide, astonished, mouth open. A tight spiral of gold bottlecaps and fries curls in
   around the glove and down his arm, every piece overlapping his body, two caps already
   stacked in his open gloved palm. Joy that the world is coming to him; he never grabs.

2. UMBRELLA
   In the game: standing while an umbrella shield is held  ·  indefinite, until it takes a hit
   Standing upright with his ankles crossed, leaning his weight on a compact black umbrella
   held OPEN over his head in the gloved left paw, canopy no wider than his own body and
   tilted back so it stays well clear of the cell edge. The hat is still on underneath it.
   Bare right paw on his hip, visible eye half-lidded and faintly pleased with himself.
   Rain beads and runs off the canopy rim in a tight bright ring touching the fabric —
   nothing on the ground, no dry patch and no shadow.

3. SHIELD
   In the game: the instant the shield eats a hit  ·  0.55s
   The instant of impact: braced in a quarter turn away with his knees bent, the gloved left
   paw thrown up flat toward the blow with the fingers spread, the bare right paw holding
   the hat on. A blue-violet dome flares tight around him, just past the hat and elbows,
   with rain streaking off its skin and the glove lit hard white at the point nearest the
   hit. This is the one frame where the composure cracks: mouth open, visible eye squeezed
   shut. ⛔ Keep the WHOLE dome inside the cell with clear magenta all the way round it.

4. SCARED
   In the game: a gull or crow is lining up a dive  ·  0.82 to 1.15s
   Frozen bolt upright on the very tips of both loafers, knees locked together, both paws
   clamping the hat down onto his skull, head craned back and up so the brim lifts and both
   eyes show huge and round. Jacket hem and stubby tail both rigid. A hard gull-shaped
   shadow falls across his chest and face only — nothing cast anywhere below him.

5. FLEE
   In the game: the street sweeper is closing in  ·  seconds at a time
   The backwards glide at panic speed: he faces RIGHT, toward the thing chasing him, and
   slides hard toward camera-LEFT with his feet a frantic blur, torso leaning back, mouth
   open in real alarm. Gloved left paw pinning the hat down, bare right paw thrown out in
   front of him as if to say wait. Short red-gold speed strokes sit in FRONT of his toes on
   the right, touching the loafers, and two small exclamation strokes touch the hat brim.
   Keep him well clear of the cell edge.

6. CHEER
   In the game: LEVEL CLEAR — the camera zooms in on them  ·  2.6s, the biggest moment in the game
   The finish pose, held: up on the toes of both loafers, one knee kicked high, the gloved
   left paw punched straight up overhead with the fingers spread and a burst of small white
   and pale gold glints coming off the glove and touching it, the bare right paw sweeping
   the hat off and holding it out at arm's length but angled inward so it stays inside the
   cell. Both eyes visible, mouth wide in real, unguarded joy — the mask of cool is gone.
   A short shower of gold bottlecaps falls onto his shoulders, touching him the whole way.

7. KO
   In the game: death by traffic, gull or steam  ·  1.55s, camera leans in, light closes
   Flat on his back with his long legs straight up in the air, white socks and loafers
   crossed neatly at the ankle as if he planned it, jacket spread open beneath his
   shoulders. The fedora has slid down to cover his whole face and rests on his muzzle,
   still touching him; the gloved left paw lies flat on his chest with one finger still
   weakly raised, the bare right paw limp and open. Slow gold stars circle above the hat.
   The read is wounded dignity, not injury: the show went on, and then it stopped.
   ⛔ Exactly two legs in the air and exactly two arms. Count them.

8. DIZZY
   In the game: death by the street sweeper  ·  1.55s, same treatment
   He has finally spun one turn too many: sat down hard and crooked with his legs splayed,
   spiral eyes, tongue out, the hat knocked round sideways on his head with the brim over
   one ear, jacket twisted half off one shoulder. The gloved left paw is still held up in
   the air out of habit. Stars orbit the crooked hat; one gold bottlecap sits on his chest.
   One connected silhouette, nothing loose beside him.
   ⛔ Exactly two legs and exactly two arms. Count them.

9. SPLASH
   In the game: death by water  ·  1.55s, same treatment
   The instant of shock inside an exploding white crown of water that bursts up tight around
   his middle: soaked through with his fur plastered flat so he looks half his usual size,
   mouth wide, both eyes huge. The bare right paw grips the sodden hat to his head; the
   gloved left paw is thrust straight up out of the water, high and dry, the one thing he
   saved. One gold bottlecap rides the lip of the crown against his shoulder.
   ⛔ Exactly two arms. Count them.

NOTES FOR ME WHEN I CUT THIS
• Cut with scripts/cut_sheet.py 30a 30b --out assets/skins/kingofhop --contact ... and LOOK at the
  contact sheet. COUNT LIMBS in every cell first: this skin exists because seven frames elsewhere
  shipped with extra limbs. One glove, on his LEFT paw, in every frame except the mirrored one.
• run-l = mirror(run-r) as in every other pack, so the glove changes paws in run-l. Accepted,
  same as every asymmetric costume in the game.
• THE MOONWALK IS A CODE FLAG, NOT AN ART TRICK. run-r is painted facing RIGHT like every other
  pack, so the file rule (a file faces the way its name says) still holds. The character entry gets
  moonwalk:1, and for that one character heroPose shows run-l on a right hop and run-r on a left
  hop, so he travels backwards. ⛔ This is a named exception for one skin. The facing note in
  heroPose forbids general flips; do not touch it for anyone else.
• His glide streaks are painted in FRONT of his toes for that reason: once the frames are swapped
  they trail behind the direction of travel.
• He is upright in all 18, so every frame is taller than wide and drawDefault sizes by height.
  Check his HEAD is the same pixel height across the pack on the contact sheet.
• Never depink the shield frame. Sparkle on this skin is white and gold: if any pink shows up in
  the glints, that is key residue and depink is safe on those frames.
• LIKENESS: a raccoon in a stage costume, no real name, no face, no song title anywhere in the game,
  the store page or the patch notes. The code word MOONWALK is TAKEN (it unlocks Astronaut Jimothy
  and is the first soundtrack song); pick another word if this skin ever gets a code.
• Unlock lane is Stephen's call. Recommended: a SECRET, earned by hopping backwards N times
  (the up key is "back" in this game), which works the same on web and Steam with no code and no
  calendar. CONTENT-MAP.md must be updated in the same commit as the roster change.
