================================================================================================
THE CHARACTER
================================================================================================

Seagull — one of the playable characters in Jimothy, a storybook hopper set on the rainy
streets of Seattle.

STANCE (locked — the same in every frame):
  Bipedal bird: upright on two pale-orange webbed feet at rest, never on all fours — "four-
  square" is a quadruped word and does not apply to him in any frame.

WARDROBE AND PROPS (locked — these appear in ALL EIGHTEEN frames, including the deaths):
  No clothing at all — his costume is ONE stolen french fry: crosswise in the yellow bill in
  idle, sit, eat (mid-swallow), crouch, land, run-r, dash-run, shield and dizzy; pinned
  under his LEFT webbed foot in coffee, umbrella and magnet; detached but still overlapping
  him in cheer (tossed), scared (falling), ko (lying by his bill) and splash (streaming
  off); absent ONLY from leap, deliberately. Other props, all completely BLANK — no logo,
  letters or numbers, ever: a red-and-white fry carton (eat, magnet, flee, splash), a plain
  black umbrella (umbrella), a plain takeaway cup (coffee).

HOW THIS BODY WORKS:
  He has no hands, so every raccoon "hold" is re-solved three ways: the bill grips, a webbed
  foot pins, and anything too big to grip is bitten high on the shaft so it rests on the
  body. Wing arc is strict — up, out and forward-mantled over the crown only; a wing never
  crosses in front of the chest, never points, never holds and never props him up. He stands
  on two legs, so rest is a one-legged hunch or a folded crouch, never a lap and never all
  fours. Body lock from the four existing PNGs: tall and narrow beside Jimothy's sphere,
  slate mantle, black wingtips with white mirrors, yellow bill with the red gonys spot,
  pale-orange webbed feet, short stubby tail, dark eye — same head size in all eighteen
  frames.

================================================================================================
RULES — THESE APPLY TO BOTH SHEETS, AND TO EVERY SHEET IN THIS PROJECT

  LAYOUT
  • NINE frames per sheet, laid out 3 across and 3 down, in the numbered order given below.
  • Background is flat magenta #FF00FF, edge to edge.
  • ⛔ Put a clear gap, or a white divider line, between every cell. NEVER let two frames touch.
    The cutter separates frames by finding the background between them; frames that touch get read
    as one object and come out sliced wrong.
  • Nothing may run off the edge of its own cell. Keep tails, wings, capes and effects inside.

  THE CAMERA — IDENTICAL IN ALL NINE
  • Three-quarter high angle looking slightly down, character facing the viewer.
  • The one exception is the sideways run frame, which is a side-on profile.

  SCALE — THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE ON THE SHEET
  • The character must be THE SAME SIZE in every frame. The game normalises each frame on its own,
    so a frame that is drawn bigger or wider than the others makes the character visibly change
    size mid-animation.
  • Target proportions: roughly as wide as it is tall, and never more than about 1.3 times wider
    than tall. If a pose wants to sprawl wider than that, tuck it in.
  • ⛔ Effects count as part of the frame. A big spray of water or a long trail of speed lines makes
    the game shrink the animal to fit them in. Keep every effect tight to the body.

  WHAT MUST NEVER BE PAINTED
  • ⛔ NO GROUND. No floor, no pavement, no puddle, no curb, no drain, no cast shadow, no scattered
    leaves or crumbs on the deck. The character is cut out and dropped onto the game's own street —
    anything painted on the ground survives the cut and floats there as grey scum.
  • ⛔ NO DETACHED PROPS. Anything not touching the body becomes its own separate sprite, or makes
    the character shrink. Props stay in a hand, a mouth, a pocket or against the body.
  • ⛔ NO READABLE TEXT. No lettering on badges, signs, newspapers, diplomas or paddles. Text is
    unreadable at game size, and one of these frames gets mirrored, which reverses it. Suggest
    print with texture instead.
  • ⛔ NO HOT PINK OR MAGENTA in the artwork itself — not in steam, glow, fabric or effects. Those
    tones get deleted along with the background. Keep effects warm, cool or neutral.

  STYLE
  • Match the existing Jimothy artwork: warm, funny, a little melancholy, rainy
    Seattle at night. Rich shadows, wet highlights.
  • Effects are painted INTO the frame — fire streaks, water bursts, circling stars, swirling
    bottlecaps. The game does not add them.
  • Deaths are funny-sad, never grim and never gory. Circling stars, not X-eyes.

SHEET 9a  —  NINE FRAMES, 3 ACROSS AND 3 DOWN, IN THIS ORDER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. IDLE
   In the game: standing, at rest — the default  ·  on screen constantly
   Standing square on both pale-orange webbed feet, wings folded tight with the black
   primaries crossed over the stubby tail, neck drawn in against the drizzle. One stolen
   fry clamped crosswise in the yellow bill, dark eye locked dead on camera with
   absolutely no guilt in it — and no pavement under him.

2. SIT
   In the game: 4 seconds without input  ·  until the player moves
   The gull's own rest, not a loaf: up on ONE leg with the other foot drawn into his
   breast feathers, body hunched, rain beading along the mantle, eye half-lidded and
   looking at nothing in particular. The fry is still crosswise in his bill, unstarted —
   he is not eating it because when it is gone the day is over.

3. EAT
   In the game: 8 seconds without input  ·  until the player moves
   One webbed foot planted flat on the tipped blank carton, head thrown all the way back
   over his spine, bill wide, a whole fry going down vertically with the throat visibly
   bulging. Wings cracked a few inches for balance and one eye rolled down at the viewer
   mid-swallow — caught, and entirely unbothered; nothing scattered loose off the body.

4. CROUCH
   In the game: first quarter of every hop  ·  0.07s, hundreds of times a run
   Compressed low and forward, tarsi folded, breast almost down to his own toes, wings
   cracked open a few inches to unlock, tail cocked, neck thrust out and eye fixed far
   ahead. Fry still crosswise in the bill, with a first flick of grit and water kicking
   off the webbed toes and staying tight to the feet.

5. LEAP
   In the game: middle half of every hop  ·  0.15s, hundreds of times a run
   A WIDE, slightly swept glide-dive: span held horizontal and angled forward with the
   wrists swept back so the whole shape sits about 1.3 wide to 1 tall, body and head large
   and unmistakable dead centre, bill level, feet tucked back. Absolutely nothing carried,
   no shed feathers, no spray — this same PNG is the diving-gull hazard at 69px and
   mirrors by direction, so it must read as one clean incoming bird and carry nothing
   shaped like a pickup.

6. LAND
   In the game: last quarter of a hop, plus a hold  ·  0.17s, hundreds of times a run
   Feet thrown forward with the webbed toes splayed wide, wings cupped forward to air-
   brake, tail fanned down like a flap, head coming back up as the weight settles — and
   the fry still in his bill, which is the whole joke. A tight white-and-cyan burst bites
   up around his own feet only: no puddle, no ring on the floor, no pavement.

7. RUN-R
   In the game: a sideways hop to the right  ·  0.28s
   Side-on to the right in the ridiculous flat-out gull waddle-run: body horizontal, neck
   stretched forward like an arrow, both wings half-open for balance, orange feet a
   paddling blur, fry trailing horizontally from the bill. Deliberately symmetric —
   nothing side-specific in this frame, because the engine mirrors it to make run-l.

8. DASH-RUN
   In the game: hopping while the coffee dash is up  ·  0.19s
   The same waddle at absolute maximum, wings caught at the bottom of a full power-stroke
   and STILL not airborne — the bird who could just fly and somehow never does — feet
   skimming, eye wide, pupil a pinprick, fry pinned flat back along his cheek by the wind.
   Three short white speed streaks, a low spray off the heels and two shed down feathers,
   all held inside the idle box.

9. COFFEE
   In the game: STANDING STILL while the dash is up  ·  4.2s, or 7.0s on a Double Shot
   Feet planted and holding perfectly still while everything else vibrates: the takeaway
   cup pinched by its rim in his bill and tipped fully upside down, lid popped, the lot
   going down in one. Crown feathers straight up in a startled crest, pupil shrunk to a
   dot, pale blush-cream steam (never pink), short orange fire licks and jitter ticks
   right at his feet — and the fry pinned under his left foot even now.

SHEET 9b  —  NINE FRAMES, 3 ACROSS AND 3 DOWN, IN THIS ORDER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. MAGNET
   In the game: standing while the Snacks magnet is up  ·  6.5s
   Head back, bill wide in a delighted open-billed scream, wings half-raised and cupped
   forward as if gathering it all in, every feather fluffed with sheer astonishment — the
   best day of his life. Fries, gold bottlecaps and one small tumbling carton spiral in
   TIGHT, every piece overlapping his silhouette and none more than half a body width out;
   the fry stays pinned under his left foot.

2. UMBRELLA
   In the game: standing while an umbrella shield is held  ·  indefinite, until it takes a hit
   A cheap black umbrella bitten by the SHAFT high up, just under the canopy, so it roofs
   his skull like a hat with the handle dangling loose past his breast and rain sheeting
   off the rim in a short curtain that never leaves the canopy. Bone dry, wings folded,
   eye half-lidded and insufferably smug, fry pinned under his left foot because the bill
   is busy — nothing jammed into a grate, nothing on the floor.

3. SHIELD
   In the game: the instant the shield eats a hit  ·  0.55s
   His own solve and no other bird's: turned side-on into the weather with one shoulder
   driven straight into the blast, wings clamped hard to the body, head low, feet braced,
   fry held dead level in the bill and completely unmoved. A blue-violet dome flares
   around him with a hot white spot where the hit landed and rain raking off it — never
   violet, never dark.

4. SCARED
   In the game: a gull or crow is lining up a dive  ·  0.82 to 1.15s
   Even gulls have a gull: the hard-edged shadow of a much bigger, meaner one falls across
   his back, breast and half his face, painted only where it lands on his body. Neck fully
   periscoped, bill open in a squawk of pure indignation, eyes rolled up, wings half-
   lifted and shivering — and the fry has finally slipped, caught mid-fall an inch below
   his bill with one sharp exclamation tick beside his head.

5. FLEE
   In the game: the street sweeper is closing in  ·  seconds at a time
   Full panic sprint to the left, body low and horizontal, neck stretched, both wings
   hammering, webbed feet a blur — with the ENTIRE blank red-and-white carton clamped in
   his bill, because he grabbed the whole thing on the way out. Red-orange speed streaks
   and one or two shed feathers rake off his tail, kept tight to the body.

6. CHEER
   In the game: LEVEL CLEAR — the camera zooms in on them  ·  2.6s, the biggest moment in the game
   The gull long call, which belongs to him alone: chest thrown out, head thrown right
   back over the spine, bill wide in a full-throated shriek at the rain, wings held low
   and slightly out — never a raised V, that is the other birds. One fry flips end over
   end just above his crown and overlapping his head, a few chip fragments falling close
   beside him, warm gold rim-light on his outline instead of a background sunbreak.

7. KO
   In the game: death by traffic, gull or steam  ·  1.55s, camera leans in, light closes
   Down on his right side, right wing pinned under him, left flopped over his flank, both
   legs out together with the toes curled, bill hanging open. Eyes are swooning half-moon
   lids with small gold stars circling tight above his head, and the fry lies one beak-
   length from that open bill, uneaten — he went out reaching for it.

8. DIZZY
   In the game: death by the street sweeper  ·  1.55s, same treatment
   Folded down onto his rump with both legs splayed out in front like a dropped toy, wings
   limp at his sides, head lolling back, little pink tongue out and spiral eyes (spirals
   are dizzy's alone — no stars in this frame). The fry hangs half out of the drooping
   bill by one end, a wobble arc either side of his head, and the whole silhouette is
   deliberately nothing like ko's.

9. SPLASH
   In the game: death by water  ·  1.55s, same treatment
   Swept hard sideways by the burst: a bright blue-violet crown explodes at his chest and
   shoves him off downstream, body angled with the flow, one wing braced uselessly against
   it and the other thrown up, soaked through, eye locked on camera and deeply offended.
   The blank carton tumbles away just ahead of his bill with two fries in the spray,
   everything still overlapping him and clear of the frame edge — no water surface, no
   kerb, no grate anywhere.

NOTES FOR ME WHEN I CUT THIS
================================================================================================
• NO GROUND, IN ANY FRAME. The cutter is a magenta flood, so pavement, puddles, damp smears,
sweeper swirls, storm-drain grates, cast floor shadows and scattered fries all survive as
floating grey scum under a bird with nothing beneath him. Contact effects only: the burst at
his own feet, grit off his toes, spray off his heels.
• CENTRE-ANCHORED, not bottom-anchored — the old sheet note was wrong. The engine centres
the painted content on the lane point, so keep his body mass at the middle of every frame;
that is why the cheer toss sits just above his crown and the splash debris stays beside him
rather than streaming away.
• ENVELOPE: every frame, effects included, between 0.80:1 and 1.35:1 and inside about 1.2x
the idle box, with a clear magenta margin all round — nothing touches a frame edge or a
divider line. The one checkable test: his HEAD must be the same number of pixels in all
eighteen. If an effect shrinks the head, the effect is too big. Paint idle, leap and cheer
first and send those three for a cut before the other fifteen.
• leap HAS TWO JOBS — it is also the diving-gull hazard for the whole game, drawn at 69px
and flipped by travel direction. Wide swept glide, body centred, and no fry or food-coloured
object in it, or the game shows players a bird carrying the pickup and teaches them to chase
the thing that kills them.
• COLOUR LAW: the knockout eats the magenta family. Steam is pale blush-cream, the shield
dome is blue-violet like Jimothy's own. The carton stays plain red-and-white and BLANK — no
logo, no numbers, no lettering anywhere on the sheet.
• PALETTE AND SCALE LOCK from the four existing PNGs (idle, crouch, leap, land): slate
mantle, black wingtips with white mirrors, yellow bill with the red gonys spot, pale-orange
webbed feet, dark eye, short stubby tail. Match idle's head size exactly — he reads tall and
narrow next to Jimothy, never smaller. Shed feathers appear in dash-run and flee only, never
on the held frames.
• Left-facing run: mirror
