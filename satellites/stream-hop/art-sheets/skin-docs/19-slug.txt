JIMOTHY — SKIN 19: BANANA SLUG

MAKE TWO SHEETS FROM THIS DOCUMENT:  19a  and  19b
Nine frames on each sheet, eighteen in total. Label the files exactly 19a and 19b.

================================================================================================
THE CHARACTER
================================================================================================

Banana Slug — one of the playable characters in Jimothy, a storybook hopper set on the
rainy streets of Seattle.

STANCE (locked — the same in every frame):
  Prone gastropod — never bipedal, never four-square, no limbs at any point: one muscular
  foot on the deck with the front third reared up, and his body length always spent in a
  curve or an S so the head rides high and the painted box stays near-square.

WARDROBE AND PROPS (locked — these appear in ALL EIGHTEEN frames, including the deaths):
  No costume and nothing held in any frame — the constants in all 18 are his own body:
  banana-yellow gloss, the identical black leopard-spot map, the darker olive mantle saddle
  over the front third, the pneumostome painted as a same-tone dimple on the right flank,
  one long specular highlight down the back, two eyestalks plus two lip-feelers, and a SHORT
  glossy slime stub touching his tail at the same length every single frame.

HOW THIS BODY WORKS:
  The two eyestalks do every job an arm would do — pointing, splaying, flinching, cheering —
  but they are hydrostatic, fluid pressure with no skeleton, so they invert into the head at
  the lightest touch: they can never bear weight, never prop anything up and never hold.
  Props therefore only ever stick to his slime, lie against his flank, or drape over his
  back; nothing is gripped. The travelling muscle wave banding down his foot does every job
  a leg would do, so speed is sold by how tight and fast that banding reads, never by how
  long a trail is. He rears by lifting the front third off the deck, which means he is never
  four-square and never has a spare limb — there was never a limb to spare.

================================================================================================
RULES — THESE APPLY TO BOTH SHEETS, AND TO EVERY SHEET IN THIS PROJECT

  LAYOUT
  • NINE frames per sheet, laid out 3 across and 3 down, in the numbered order given below.
  • Background is flat magenta #FF00FF, edge to edge.
  • ⛔ Put a clear gap, or a white divider line, between every cell. NEVER let two frames touch.
    The cutter separates frames by finding the background between them; frames that touch get read
    as one object and come out sliced wrong.
  • Nothing may run off the edge of its own cell. Keep tails, wings, capes and effects inside.

  THE CAMERA — IDENTICAL IN ALL NINE
  • Three-quarter high angle looking slightly down, character facing the viewer.
  • The one exception is the sideways run frame, which is a side-on profile.

  SCALE — THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE ON THE SHEET
  • The character must be THE SAME SIZE in every frame. The game normalises each frame on its own,
    so a frame that is drawn bigger or wider than the others makes the character visibly change
    size mid-animation.
  • Target proportions: roughly as wide as it is tall, and never more than about 1.3 times wider
    than tall. If a pose wants to sprawl wider than that, tuck it in.
  • ⛔ Effects count as part of the frame. A big spray of water or a long trail of speed lines makes
    the game shrink the animal to fit them in. Keep every effect tight to the body.

  WHAT MUST NEVER BE PAINTED
  • ⛔ NO GROUND. No floor, no pavement, no puddle, no curb, no drain, no cast shadow, no scattered
    leaves or crumbs on the deck. The character is cut out and dropped onto the game's own street —
    anything painted on the ground survives the cut and floats there as grey scum.
  • ⛔ NO DETACHED PROPS. Anything not touching the body becomes its own separate sprite, or makes
    the character shrink. Props stay in a hand, a mouth, a pocket or against the body.
  • ⛔ NO READABLE TEXT. No lettering on badges, signs, newspapers, diplomas or paddles. Text is
    unreadable at game size, and one of these frames gets mirrored, which reverses it. Suggest
    print with texture instead.
  • ⛔ NO HOT PINK OR MAGENTA in the artwork itself — not in steam, glow, fabric or effects. Those
    tones get deleted along with the background. Keep effects warm, cool or neutral.

  STYLE
  • Match the existing Jimothy artwork: warm, funny, a little melancholy, rainy
    Seattle at night. Rich shadows, wet highlights.
  • Effects are painted INTO the frame — fire streaks, water bursts, circling stars, swirling
    bottlecaps. The game does not add them.
  • Deaths are funny-sad, never grim and never gory. Circling stars, not X-eyes.

SHEET 19a  —  NINE FRAMES, 3 ACROSS AND 3 DOWN, IN THIS ORDER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. IDLE
   In the game: standing, at rest — the default  ·  on screen constantly
   Front third reared up off the deck with the rest of him curled into a shallow S
   beneath, both eyestalks tall and splayed, the two lip-feelers tasting the rain, mouth a
   calm soft line. Wet gloss down the back, the short slime stub curled at his tail, and
   nothing else in the cell.

2. SIT
   In the game: 4 seconds without input  ·  until the player moves
   Off duty: body settled into a fat comma, mantle relaxed, both eyestalks drooped to half
   mast and lazily crossed at the tips, eyes half-lidded in the rain and mouth a small
   content line. A thin glossy collar of slime is pressed out tight around his own
   outline, no wider than a lip-feeler — the last one still out here, and perfectly happy
   about it.

3. EAT
   In the game: 8 seconds without input  ·  until the player moves
   Nose-deep in a fallen chanterelle stuck to his own chest by the slime, rasping a neat
   scalloped bite out of the cap, one eyestalk tipped down to watch the food while the
   other stays up on lookout. A soggy Pike Place fry is glued flat against his flank with
   one identical scallop already taken out of it, abandoned as too dry.

4. CROUCH
   In the game: first quarter of every hop  ·  0.07s, hundreds of times a run
   Gathered to spring: concertina'd to half his length and twice his height, ridged like a
   squeezed accordion, mantle flared, both eyestalks pulled halfway back into his head and
   aimed up-screen. The slime stub is bunched into a tight glossy pleat at his tail,
   loaded like a spring.

5. LEAP
   In the game: middle half of every hop  ·  0.15s, hundreds of times a run
   Fully airborne on a steep diagonal, arced belly-forward like a thrown banana with his
   nose up-screen and tail low so he fills the cell corner to corner, both eyestalks
   streaming back like ribbons, mouth open in undignified delight. The stub has snapped
   clean at his tail into a short curl and three flung dew droplets, all of it tucked in
   against his own outline.

6. LAND
   In the game: last quarter of a hop, plus a hold  ·  0.17s, hundreds of times a run
   A belly-flop: the front third splatted wide and flat with a tight ring of slime
   squirting out right at the contact edge, the back half still arriving in a visible
   ripple down his foot. Both eyestalks have whipped forward past his own face from the
   impact, eyes crossed for a beat, grinning anyway.

7. RUN-R
   In the game: a sideways hop to the right  ·  0.28s
   Side-on and travelling right in a low shallow S, the muscular wave banding down his
   foot like rolling light, eyestalks laid back streamlined, jaw set with total
   determination. The stub stays short at his tail and the motion arcs hugging his flank
   are honestly, comically tiny — this is his top speed.

8. DASH-RUN
   In the game: hopping while the coffee dash is up  ·  0.19s
   The same side-on sprint flat out: body drawn thin, the foot waves compressed into a
   blur of banding, eyestalks pinned straight back, eyes wide, mouth open. Warm amber
   friction licks and one pale cream steam curl come off the foot's contact edge while the
   tail stub throws a tight spray of droplets — every effect touching him.

9. COFFEE
   In the game: STANDING STILL while the dash is up  ·  4.2s, or 7.0s on a Double Shot
   Dead still and visibly vibrating: reared up on his back third with the foot planted,
   double-image blur on both eyestalks, pupils shrunk to pin dots, the whole front of him
   glossed espresso-dark because he crawled straight through the spill rather than
   carrying it. The tipped takeaway cup lies against his own flank giving off pale blush-
   cream steam, with short jitter lines at his mantle and warm amber fire licks flickering
   at the stub.

SHEET 19b  —  NINE FRAMES, 3 ACROSS AND 3 DOWN, IN THIS ORDER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. MAGNET
   In the game: standing while the Snacks magnet is up  ·  6.5s
   Reared up with his mouth open in a delighted round O and both eyestalks splayed wide in
   pure astonishment, because with him everything sticks: gold bottlecaps already glued
   along his back like sequins and one fry stuck flat to an eyestalk. Three more caps
   spiral in on short curved arcs that overlap his body, close enough to be about to land
   on him.

2. UMBRELLA
   In the game: standing while an umbrella shield is held  ·  indefinite, until it takes a hit
   He crawled UNDER it — a fallen big-leaf maple drapes over his back and mantle with its
   stem still hooked at the tip of his tail so it reads as deliberately dragged into
   place, the front edge lifted just enough that his head and both fully extended stalks
   poke out clean. Rain sheets off the leaf's drip tip in one bright stream, and one fat
   drop has got through onto his back, which he is plainly thrilled about: eyes half-
   lidded and smug.

3. SHIELD
   In the game: the instant the shield eats a hit  ·  0.55s
   The hit lands: both eyestalks snap fully inside his head leaving two dimples, the body
   clenched into a tight tense hump, mantle flared, mouth a shocked little slot. A bright
   blue-violet dome flares tight around him with rain streaking off it, and the maple leaf
   is still stemmed to his tail but flipped back over itself and crumpled.

4. SCARED
   In the game: a gull or crow is lining up a dive  ·  0.82 to 1.15s
   Coiled back tight with his front end craned up-screen, both eyestalks stretched to full
   periscope height and bent to point dead at the sky, pupils huge, one bead of slime-
   sweat wobbling on his mantle. The diving gull's shadow falls as a hard dark bar across
   his own back and mantle only.

5. FLEE
   In the game: the street sweeper is closing in  ·  seconds at a time
   Absolute maximum slug velocity, which is nothing: body stretched to its longest inside
   the same box, foot waves frantic, eyestalks streaming back with a bold red double
   exclamation mark sitting right on top of them, mouth open in panic, a fine mist of
   slime flung off his flank. The red speed streaks are so short they are the joke.

6. CHEER
   In the game: LEVEL CLEAR — the camera zooms in on them  ·  2.6s, the biggest moment in the game
   Reared as tall as he goes on the last third of his foot with the rest of him looped
   beneath for width, both eyestalks flung skyward in a wide V with the little feelers up
   too, mouth open in a whoop, back arched proud in warm gold light. The stub at his tail
   curls up into one short glittering silver loop — a victory streamer the size of a
   shoelace.

7. KO
   In the game: death by traffic, gull or steam  ·  1.55s, camera leans in, light closes
   Rolled foot-up and still gently rocking, pale glossy belly-foot to the sky with only
   the curve of his back touching down; all four tentacles flop backward and DOWN over his
   own upturned face, the two long ones drooping past his eyes like wet noodles and the
   lip-feelers splayed sideways. Eyes are two dazed little dots pointing in slightly
   different directions, mouth slack, three stars circling close above him, and one bright
   slime smear still glossing the flank he skidded on.

8. DIZZY
   In the game: death by the street sweeper  ·  1.55s, same treatment
   Sprawled in a loose S with both eyestalks crossed and tied in a soft overhand knot, a
   spiral in each eye, mouth slack in a woozy smile, his back striped with brush-bristle
   dust and street grit. The tail stub is corkscrewed into one tight turn and three stars
   circle close above his head.

9. SPLASH
   In the game: death by water  ·  1.55s, same treatment
   Caught inside the burst, not under it: soaked and water-darkened with a crown of pale
   cyan-white exploding around his middle, both eyestalks flung up and out in shock and
   his mouth a round startled O. His slime stub is dissolving into pale milky curls at his
   tail — the actually sad part, and he knows it.

NOTES FOR ME WHEN I CUT THIS
================================================================================================
• NO GROUND, EVER — in any of the 18. No pavement, no puddle, no cast shadow, no slime road,
no skid mark leading in from off-frame, no scattered litter. The engine paints its own
ground shadow under him; anything wet that you paint must physically touch his body or it
cuts out as a floating grey smear beneath a slug standing on nothing.
• THE SLIME STUB IS A LOCK. Same short length, touching his tail, in all 18 — the trail was
previously a road in idle, run-r, dash-run, flee, cheer and dizzy at six different lengths,
which changed the crop and therefore changed his size every frame. Speed lives in the
muscle-wave banding down the foot, never in track length.
• ENVELOPE, AND THE ONE CHECKABLE TEST: every cell including its effects must land between
0.80:1 and 1.35:1 and inside roughly 236x224px, and his HEAD must be the same number of
pixels in all 18. He is a long animal in a near-square box — spend the length in a curve or
an S with the front third reared, never as a straight horizontal bar, or he draws half-size
mid-animation.
• NOTHING TOUCHES A CELL EDGE OR A DIVIDER — keep a clear magenta margin all the way round.
Detached bits (the magnet caps, the leap droplets, the circling stars, the exclamation mark)
sit within half a body width AND overlap his bounding box, or the cutter emits them as their
own sprite.
• COLOUR LAW: the knockout eats the magenta family, so no hot-pink or magenta effects
anywhere. Steam pale blush-cream, the shield dome bright blue-violet ( At 59px on wet grey
lane art his banana-yellow is carried by the black spot map and the single specular stripe —
keep the darker mantle saddle so the head end still reads.
• WET GLOSS IN ALL 18, ko, dizzy and splash included; a matte slug reads as a dead slug
rather than a knocked-out one. Spot map, mantle saddle and the right-flank pneumostome stay
in identical places every frame, and the pneumostome is a same-tone dimple, not a
contrasting mark. No readable text on anything, and no X eyes — dazed dots plus stars for
ko, spirals for dizzy only. Once these 18 exist the engine stops compositing its stand-in
umbrella and dome, so the maple leaf and the cyan dome must be painted into their own cells.
• Left-facing run: mirror — his only asymmetry is the pneumostome, and painting it as a
same-tone dimple makes the flip invisible at 59px; note for the build that a full character
with no run-l.png falls through to the procedural blob, so either export the mirrored file
or add the mirror fallback before marking him full.
