Jumping Jimothy · Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
The Google Play copy of Jumping Jimothy does not collect any data about you, and does not send anything about you anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no purchases inside the game. After the first load, the game plays with no network connection. The studio soundtrack needs one to play its songs. The copy on the open web has some optional extras, described in their own section below.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game remembers lives on your device only, in your browser's local storage:

  • Your progress: Adventure levels cleared, best runs, bottlecaps, the costumes and critters you own and the one you wear, badges, the codes you have redeemed, and your settings (the keys that start with sh_).
  • That the Supporter Pack is yours. The Google Play copy includes it.
  • The songs you have unlocked for the Music player.

None of it leaves your device. Clearing the site's browsing data, or uninstalling the app, can delete all of it permanently; there is no copy anywhere else.

Network requests the game makes

The game's own files, and its songs when music plays, come from lucidwinds.com. Its typeface comes from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, the same way any web page loads its files. Each of those servers sees an ordinary file request from your device and nothing about you or your play.

After the first load, the game keeps its own copies of its files, so it plays offline. Songs are played from lucidwinds.com when you listen, so music needs a connection.

Redeem codes

Codes are checked on your device. In the Google Play copy, redeeming a code sends nothing anywhere.

Sharing

Sharing happens only when you tap Share. It goes through your phone's own share sheet to the app you pick, and it carries a line about the game and a link to its Google Play page, nothing about you. The game never shares anything by itself.

Controllers

If you plug in or pair a game controller, the game reads its buttons while you play. Nothing about the controller is stored or sent.

Permissions

The app asks for no device permissions. No location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts.

The copy on the open web

When you play Jumping Jimothy in a web browser at lucidwinds.com instead of the Google Play app, everything above still holds, with these optional extras:

  • A studio account. You can sign in with an email address and a password to keep your progress and the Supporter Pack across devices. The account is held by our server on Google Firebase, which stores your email address, an account identifier and a copy of your progress. Without signing in, nothing is sent.
  • Sunbeams. Playing earns Sunbeams, the currency of our game Lucid Winds. If you have signed in, the Sunbeams you earned are added to that account through the same server. To do this the web copy loads Google's Firebase code from www.gstatic.com.
  • The Supporter Pack. On the web you can buy it, or give a gift, through a card checkout run by Stripe or a crypto checkout run by NOWPayments. They handle your payment details; we receive the order and whether it was paid, never your card.
  • Feedback. On the web, Feedback opens a form. What you type, the game's version and your browser's name go to our server so we can read it. Only what you choose to send is sent.
  • Repair reports. If the web copy fails to start, the next start sends us a short technical note about where it stopped (no name, no account, nothing typed), so we can fix it.
  • Code reports. When a code is redeemed on the web, we receive the code, what it gave, a random tag made by your browser, and the first six characters of your account identifier if you are signed in.

Children

Jumping Jimothy is a general audience game and is not directed at children. The Google Play copy collects no personal information from anyone.
