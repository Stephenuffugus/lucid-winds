Help Jimothy the deformed Seattle raccoon cross the rainy city, level after level, to the greatest dumpster feast in town. Dodge the geese and rolling cans, ride the ferries, and grab every bottlecap.

How to play

👆
Tap to hop forward. Swipe left, right, or down to hop that way.
⌨️
On a keyboard, use the arrow keys or WASD.
🚗
The streets are full of geese, rats, rolling coffee cans, and rushing traffic. Time your hop through the gaps.
🌊
On the water, land on a floating pad (a dumpster lid, a pallet, a log, or a ferry) and ride it. Fall in and you sink.
🍺
Grab every bottlecap. Each clean hop grows your Bloom Trail, and reaching a safe curb banks it for a big score. One bad hop wilts the whole trail.
🗺️
Adventure crosses Seattle level after level. Clear a level to unlock the next and earn up to three stars: finish it, hit its flower goal, and grab its coin goal.
🥀
Don't dawdle. The wilt creeps up from behind. Keep moving forward.
☕
Power-ups drop on safe rows. Coffee is a wilt-proof dash, the Umbrella saves you from one hit, and Snacks pull bottlecaps in and pay double.
🐾
Your bottlecaps pile up in the Prize Bin. Spend them to pull a new Seattle critter to play as, and the bin never hands you the same one twice. A couple of them are not in the bin at all. You have to find those.
