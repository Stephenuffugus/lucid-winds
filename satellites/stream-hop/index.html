Stream Hop
Sky Wolf Studios
Hop across the midnight garden. Dodge the tumbling acorns, ride the drifting lily pads, and weave a Bloom Trail as you go. Every clean crossing banks a flower.

How to play

👆
Tap to hop forward. Swipe left, right, or down to hop that way.
⌨️
On a keyboard, use the arrow keys or WASD.
🌰
The log causeways have rolling acorns and hedgehogs. Time your hop through the gaps.
🪷
On the stream, land on a lily pad and ride it. Fall in the water and you sink.
🌼
Each clean hop grows your Bloom Trail. Reach the far bank to bank a flower and score big, but one bad hop wilts the whole trail.
🥀
Don't dawdle. The wilt creeps up from behind. Keep moving forward.
☕
Power-ups drop on safe rows. Coffee is a wilt-proof dash, Umbrella saves you from one hit, Snacks pull coins in and pay double.
