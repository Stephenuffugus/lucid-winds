TUMBLE · Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 September 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
TUMBLE does not collect any data about you, and does not send anything about you anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no purchases inside the game. After the first load, the game works with no network connection at all.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game remembers lives on your device only:

  • Your save (the socks in your Drawer and Odd Bin, your Lint and Quarters, your Clothesline pegs, your unlocks, your stats and your Daily results) is kept in your browser's IndexedDB database named tumble, with a copy in local storage in case the database is unavailable.
  • Your settings (color vision mode, pattern first, sound, music, vibration, reduce motion) are part of that same save.
  • The Settings screen can export the save as a text file you keep, and import one. That file goes where you put it and nowhere else.

None of it leaves your device. Clearing the site's browsing data, or Start over in Settings, deletes all of it permanently; there is no copy anywhere else.

Network requests the game makes

The game's code libraries (three.js and Rapier) are loaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net and its two typefaces from fonts.googleapis.com, the same way any web page loads its files. Those servers see an ordinary file request from your device and nothing about you or your play. After the first load, the game keeps its own copies and does not need them again. There are no other network requests.

Permissions

The app asks for no device permissions. No location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts. Vibration on a match or a basket uses the standard vibration feature and can be turned off in Settings.

Children

The game contains no ads, no purchases, no chat, no links to other players, and collects nothing from anyone, children included. It is a cozy sorting game for a general audience.
