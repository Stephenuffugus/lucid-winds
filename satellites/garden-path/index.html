Garden Path
Sky Wolf Studio
Race your mascot up a winding flower path to the Garden Throne. Draw a color card, hop to that flower. First one to the throne wins the garden. Play against the computer or a friend.

How to play

1
The goal is to be the first mascot to reach the Garden Throne at the very top of the path.
2
On your turn, tap the deck at the bottom to draw a garden card.
3
A card with one color hops you forward to the next flower of that color.
4
A card with two colors is a big jump to the second flower of that color.
5
A special spot card leaps you straight to a garden landmark, like Gnome Hollow or the Fairy Ring.
6
Land on a mud puddle or thorn patch and you are stuck, so you lose your next turn.
7
Play against the computer, or share the screen with a friend for two players.
