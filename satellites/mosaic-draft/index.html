HOW TO PLAY

A ceramic tile duel against a rival potter
Your goal: draft colored clay shards, line them up in rows, and fire them onto your wall to build the highest-scoring mosaic. Finish a full row across your wall to end the game. The potter with the most points wins.
1
Draft a kind. The five discs are kiln plates. Tap any shard on a plate to grab EVERY shard of that kind at once. The shards you leave behind slide down to the Table in the middle. You can also draft one kind straight from the Table.
2
Place them in a row. After you draft, your rows glow green. Tap a glowing row to drop the shards in. Row 1 holds 1 shard, Row 2 holds 2, up to Row 5. A row can only hold one kind at a time.
3
Watch the floor. Shards that do not fit the row, or that you tap onto the floor on purpose, land on the floor track and cost you points: −1 −1 −2 −2 −2 −3 −3.
4
The kiln fires. When every plate and the Table are empty, the round ends. Each full row fires one shard onto your wall and clears. Part-filled rows wait for the next round.
5
Score by connecting. A fired shard scores 1 on its own, or the length of every unbroken line it joins, counting across and down. Long connected runs score big.
6
✦ Chase the glaze. One glinting glazed shard hides in each round. Land it in a row that fires this round and that placement scores DOUBLE. Leave its row unfinished and the glaze cracks onto your floor. Kiln Rush hides three.
7
Win the wall. Complete any wall row all the way across to end the game. End bonuses: +2 a full row, +7 a full column, +10 for placing one kind five times. Highest score wins.
Controls: tap shards to draft  ·  tap a glowing row (or the floor) to place  ·  tap the firing card to continue  ·  ⌂ returns to the menu.
Modes: Duel Ladder beats Tam, then Mirela, then Kover  ·  Daily Kiln is one seeded puzzle a day  ·  Kiln Rush hides three glazes  ·  Zen Studio is relaxed and cannot be lost.
Every kind carries its own symbol, so nothing rides on color alone.
