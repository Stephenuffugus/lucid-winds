Berry Vine
Sky Wolf Studios
A crawling vine of berries winds toward the burrow. Fire from the seedpod, match three, and set off a cascade before the vine curls home. See your next berry, swap it, and charge a Pollen Burst so color luck never beats you.

How to play

🎯
Drag to aim the seedpod, release to plant a berry into the crawling vine.
🫐
Match three or more of the same berry to burst them. Touching ends collapse and can cascade.
🔁
Not the color you need? Tap the reserve berry to swap it into the pod.
✨
Bursts charge the Pollen Burst. When it glows, fire it to repaint a berry and its neighbors to your color.
🕳️
Don't let the vine reach the burrow. No harsh game over though, the vine just curls back.
🌈
Berries read by shape and color both, so they're clear for everyone. Turn on high-contrast in ⚙ for extra cues.
