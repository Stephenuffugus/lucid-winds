FOX & BASKET
Sky Wolf Studio
Guess the word before the fox reaches the picnic basket.
Every letter you miss lets the fox creep one step closer. Spell the whole word and he trots away hungry.
◀ Sky Wolf Studio Arcade
How to play
The goal
A word is hidden behind blank slots. Work out what it is, one letter at a time, before the fox reaches the basket.
How it plays
Tap a letter. If it is in the word, every copy of it appears at once. If it is not, the fox takes one step toward the picnic. Six wrong letters and he gets there.
The buttons
  • Tap any letter on the keyboard to guess it
  • A green key means that letter was in the word
  • A red key means it was not, and the fox moved
  • The word above the keyboard tells you its category
  • The arrow at the bottom left returns to the arcade
Where are you picnicking?
The further from home, the longer the words.
