How it grows

👆
Tap the soil to tend the seed and gather light. That is the whole start.
🕯️
Spend light to unlock lanterns, sprouts and helpers. Each one gathers on its own, so the grove keeps growing when your hands are still.
🌙
It grows while you are away too. Come back to a fuller grove and a warm welcome.
🌳
When the heartwood hardens you may plant a New Seed: the grove resets, but every seed makes the next grove grow faster, forever.
🌼
There is no losing here. Only a garden that fills in, a little at a time.
