How Sled Vine works

✏️
Draw lines with your finger. The seed-sled drops from its perch and rides whatever you ink, picking up speed downhill and flying off the ends.
◯
Steer the ride through every bloom gate (ring, diamond and star shapes), then into the waiting goal flower.
🌿
Living Trail: leaves sprout along your lines wherever the sled has ridden. Finish a trial and the whole trail blooms, pressing a keepsake into your Grove.
🌵
Thorns end the run. So does falling off the world. Your ink stays where you drew it, so adjust and ride again.
🧽
The eraser removes a whole line and refunds its ink. Trials have a fixed pot of ink, so spend it kindly.
📅
Daily Ride reshuffles one trail for everyone each day. Free Ride is a bottomless ink pot with nothing to lose.
