HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Drag anywhere along the bottom to move the paddle. On desktop the paddle follows your mouse; ← → or A / D work too.

🚀 Tap the field, press Space, or hit the big button to launch the ball.

🧲 The spot where the ball strikes the paddle sets its angle. Hit with the paddle's edge to send it sideways, the centre to send it straight up.

💊 Broken bricks sometimes drop capsules. Catch the good ones, dodge the black-and-red hazards.

🔗 Break bricks without touching the paddle to build a combo. Bigger combos pay more coins and climb the sound.

🪙 Coins stay in this game. Spend them in the Shop on paddle, ball, trail, brick & background skins.

🐛 Bricks read by shape, not colour: square = plain, cracked = tough, X = steel, ✳ = bomb, ~ = regrows, ● = slider, ? = mystery, ◆ = gem.
