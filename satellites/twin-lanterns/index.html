How to play

read this together
🏮
A path of glowing stones crosses the night garden between your two lanterns. Your lantern lights only your rows. Your partner's lights the rest.
✦
First, each of you gifts one stone: pick one glowing cell from your rows to show your partner. Choosing which one to give away is the whole art.
💭
Then each of you marks where you believe the path runs through the rows you cannot see. The path never breaks and never moves diagonally.
🌲
Hand the phone over when the screen says so, and no peeking at your partner's half.
🔥
Two or fewer wrong lanterns and the path lights: your streak grows. Zero wrong is a perfect night. The streak belongs to the pair, so come back tomorrow, together.
