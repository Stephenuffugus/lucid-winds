Dew Snip
Sky Wolf Studios
Snip the moonlit vines and let a bead of dew swing free — thread it through three glints of nectar and into the waiting sprout. Every drop you deliver blooms a keepsake flower in your Grove.

How to play

✂️
Swipe across a vine to snip it. The dew falls and swings under gravity.
🫧
Snip vines in the right order and moment to swing the dew toward the sprout.
✨
Sweep the dew through all three nectar glints for a perfect three-petal bloom.
🌬️
Tap a pollen bellows to puff a breeze and nudge the dew across gaps or up over hazards.
🥀
Keep the dew off the thorns and don't let it slip off the edge — but a miss just resets, no harsh game-over.
🌼
Feed the sprout and it blooms a one-of-a-kind flower into your Grove.
