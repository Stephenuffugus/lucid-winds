BEFORE YOU SET OUT

1. Your warden walks the clock loop and fights on their own. You never steer them.

2. Your job: tap a card in your hand at the bottom, then tap a glowing spot on the clock to place it. Groves heal, dens spawn allies, towers watch.

3. The loop is a real clock. The same card acts differently at DAWN, NOON, DUSK and NIGHT. Where you place it is the whole puzzle.

4. Retreat at the campfire to bank everything you carry. Fall in battle and you keep only a third.

HOW TO WARD

Your warden walks the loop on their own and fights whatever stands on it. You do three things: play land cards onto the loop or the meadow beside it, equip the gear that drops, and choose when to retreat.

The loop is a clock. Each lap is one day, quartered into DAWN, NOON, DUSK and NIGHT. Cards act differently by quarter: groves heal double at dawn, graveyards raise two skeletons at night, watchtowers see farther at noon. WHERE on the clock you place a card is the whole puzzle. Moon Wells and Sun Dials bend nearby tiles to their own hour.

Retreating at the campfire banks 100% of your supplies, anywhere else 60%, and falling in battle saves only 30%. Banked supplies buy permanent camp upgrades between expeditions.

Daily Loop is the same seeded run for everyone. Deep Loop is longer with ◆ elite monsters. Zen Watch cannot end you, banks nothing, and pays no sunbeams.
