Multiply!
Multiplication Chart
A big, colorful times table you can tap. Pick a chart size, then play rounds where you hunt for the right numbers, or fill in a chart that is missing pieces. Learn your tables the fun way.
Chart size
12 by 12 is the classic learning chart. It adds the elevens and twelves.

How to play

1
The goal. Get to know your times tables. Each row and column of the chart is one table, and every square shows two numbers multiplied together.
2
Pick a size. Choose 10, 12, or 15. Bigger charts have more numbers and are a bigger challenge.
Prompt Play
3
Read the task at the top, then tap every square that fits. Right squares glow green and score points. A wrong tap flashes red and breaks your streak.
4
Find them all to finish the round. Play 8 rounds. No wrong taps in a round earns a Perfect bonus.
Fill the chart
5
Drag the number tiles from the bottom into the empty squares. In Multiply you fill missing answers. In Divide you read the answers and drag the missing number to the top or side.
Controls
6
Tap + and − to zoom, or pinch with two fingers. Drag the chart to move around. Tap the fit button to see it all again.
7
How to win. Clear every round or fill the whole chart to earn sunbeams. Beat your best score and your longest streak.
