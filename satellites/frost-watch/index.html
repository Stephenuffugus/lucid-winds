HOW TO KEEP WATCH

The goal. Bloom bursts of warmth from three braziers to melt the falling frost before it reaches the rooftops.

A sleeping town, three braziers, and a sky full of falling frost. Tap anywhere and the nearest stocked brazier sends a warmth burst there. It blooms into a warm ring that melts every shard it touches. Each brazier holds limited embers per wave.

Know your shards by shape: ◆ straight shards fall true. ⋔ splitters fork into children partway down. ☽ seekers curve toward your warmest ground. ⬢ glaciers are slow and need two hits.

The Thaw Meadow: every ground segment you keep safe thaws greener wave after wave, and every thawed row raises your score multiplier. One shard strike refreezes that segment back to bare ice.

Long Watch is endless. The Daily Watch is ten waves, the same sky for everyone. Blitz is 90 dense seconds. Zen Watch can never harm the town, and pays no sunbeams.
