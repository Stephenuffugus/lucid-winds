The shapes are stations, and every traveler wants to reach a station with a matching shape. Draw colored lines between stations, let the shuttles carry everyone home, and do not let any station overflow.

Sound

How to play

1The white shapes (circles, squares, triangles) are stations. Tap a numbered color chip at the bottom, then drag from one station to another to draw a line between them. Drag from the end of a line to make it longer.
2Small shapes pop up next to a station. Those are travelers, and each one shows the station shape it wants to reach. Shuttles ride your lines and drop every traveler at a matching shape.
3If too many travelers pile up at one station, a red ring starts to fill around it. If the ring fills all the way, the game ends. Tap UNWEAVE, then a line end, to take that piece of line back.
4Lines can only cross the river on a bridge, and bridges are rare. At the end of each week you pick one gift: a new line color, a shuttle, bigger stations, or a bridge.
5Keep a station calm and busy for a whole week and it blooms: more room and bonus deliveries. Bloom every shape in one run to earn a keepsake flower for your Grove.
