How to Play

Steer
Arrow keys, WASD, or swipe. The vine keeps moving; you choose where it turns.
Grow
Eat seeds to grow longer and score. Eat quickly to build a streak for bonus seeds. Golden seeds are worth 3.
Survive
The run ends if the vine hits a wall, a garden bed, a thorn, or its own body. Press space or the ❚❚ button to pause.
Gardens
Classic is pure snake. Power Garden wraps at the edges and drops power-up blossoms. Windstorm gets faster and thornier. Grove adds maze walls.
Earn
Every run pays 🌼 Petals. Spend them in the Nursery Shop on new vines, seeds, and gardens. Clear daily missions for more.
