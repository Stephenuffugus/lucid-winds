Ripcord · Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
Ripcord plays entirely on your phone. It has no accounts and it collects nothing about you. There is no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and nothing to buy inside the game. Every battle is worked out on the device. After the first load the game plays with no network connection. Music needs one to play its songs.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game remembers lives on your device only, in your browser's local storage, under the key ripcord.save.v1:

  • Your progress: the ladder rung you have reached, the parts and looks you have won, and the modes you have opened.
  • Your top: the parts it is built from, its weights, its move and the tuning you gave each part.
  • Your records: counts such as matches and rounds played and how they were won.
  • Your settings: sound, vibration, reduced motion and the 3D battle switch, and which help screens you have seen.

The studio's shared music shelf also keeps, on the device, how long and on how many days you have played (to hand you songs as you go) and which songs you have unlocked, under the keys sws_music_progress and sws_game_unlocks.

None of it leaves your device. Clearing the app's or site's storage deletes all of it permanently, and Settings in the game has an Erase button that does the same for your progress. There is no copy anywhere else.

Network requests the game makes

The game's own files (the page, its pictures and the 3D models of the parts) come from lucidwinds.com, the same way any web page loads its files. That server sees an ordinary file request from your device and nothing about you or your play. After the first load the game keeps its own copies of what it has used, so it plays offline.

Music plays nothing until you ask it to. When you play a song it streams from lucidwinds.com, or, for some older public domain recordings in the studio's player, from archive.org (the Internet Archive). Those servers see an ordinary request for the song and nothing about you. Songs are not kept on the device, so music needs a connection.

Permissions

The app asks for no device permissions. It uses the phone's vibration for a short pulse on a heavy hit, which you can turn off in Settings. No camera, no location, no microphone, no contacts.

Children

Ripcord is a general audience game and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes
