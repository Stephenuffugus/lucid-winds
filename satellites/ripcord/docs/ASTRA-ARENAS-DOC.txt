You are making 15 pictures for a phone game called Ripcord, a spinning top battle game where two hand wound tops fight in a round dish. Every world is a real place where a ring could be drawn on the ground: dirt, chalk, stone, wood and iron, nothing neon, nothing that glows for its own sake. I will put your pictures straight into the game, so every picture must follow these rules exactly.

RULES FOR EVERY PICTURE

1. Pictures 1 to 8 are BACKGROUNDS: a tall portrait image, as tall as you can make it (2 by 3 or taller), seen from directly above, filling the whole frame edge to edge.
2. In every background the middle of the picture is a calm, empty, dark circle about 60 percent of the picture's width across, with nothing in it and darker than everything around it, because the game puts the fighting dish on top of it. All the detail lives along the top, the bottom and the sides. Keep the bottom strip quiet and low contrast, because a button sits there.
3. Pictures 9 to 15 are DISH FLOORS: a square image of a round spinning top arena seen from directly above, centred, the rail ring spanning about 90 percent of the picture's width, the rail band about one eighth of the ring's radius wide. Inside the ring the floor is flat and completely unmarked: no chalk, no lines, no circles, no pockets, no posts. Outside the ring a narrow apron darkening to near black at the corners.
4. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no border, no frame, no packaging, no hands, no people, no motion blur.
5. Lit from the upper left, a soft specular along the top edge, deep shadow on the lower right. Rich, believable painted surfaces in muted earthy colours, close to the colour codes given in each picture.
6. Nothing may look like a brand, a real product, a national flag or a religious symbol.

HOW TO WORK

Make the pictures one at a time, in order, each as its own image. Before each image write one short line: Picture N: its title. If you have to stop, stop after a finished picture; when I say continue, carry on with the next one. After picture 15, list any picture you think broke a rule so I can ask for a redo. Then, if you are able to, give me one zip file of all 15 images named picture-01.png to picture-15.png so I can download them in one click; if you cannot make a zip, just say so.
