Cosmic Cadets
Sky Wolf Studios
Tap to lift a Comet Cadet on the night wind. Thread the star-spires, and thread the center Star for a Perfect that ignites a keepsake. Build a Bloomstreak and fill your Sky Map as you drift through four changing skies.

How to play

👆
Tap anywhere (or press Space) to flap. Each tap lifts the cadet a little; let go and it drifts down.
✦
Fly through the gap between the star-spires. The cadet's glowing core is smaller than it looks, so near-misses live.
★
Thread the center Star of a gap for a Perfect: it ignites, time slows, and your Bloomstreak climbs.
🌌
The sky drifts through four phases as you go, and the wind picks up. Reading the gap stays a live skill.
🌟
Your best streak grows keepsake stars onto your Sky Map, and pays sunbeams. A soft landing, never a hard crash.
