Seed Flutter
Sky Wolf Studios
Tap to lift a dandelion seed on the night wind. Thread the reed gaps, and thread the center for a Perfect that blooms a flower on the stem. Build a Bloomstreak and grow a garden as you drift through the seasons.

How to play

👆
Tap anywhere (or press Space) to flap. Each tap lifts the seed a little; let go and it drifts down.
🌿
Fly through the gap between the reeds. The seed's soft body is smaller than it looks, so near-misses live.
🌸
Thread the center of a gap for a Perfect: the bud blooms, time slows, and your Bloomstreak climbs.
🍂
The meadow drifts through the four seasons as you go, and the wind picks up. Reading the gap stays a live skill.
🌼
Your best streak grows keepsake flowers into your Grove, and pays sunbeams. A soft landing, never a hard crash.
