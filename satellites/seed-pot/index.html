Seed Pot
Sky Wolf Studios
Drop seeds into the pot. Two of the same kind touch and grow into the next. Seed to sprout to bloom. Merge two Heirloom Blooms and a companion hatches. Just don't overflow the rim.

How to play

👆
Move your finger to aim, then tap or release to drop the seed into the pot.
🌱
Two of the same kind that touch merge into the next one up the growth ladder, and score more.
🦋
Merge two Heirloom Blooms and a companion hatches, and it clears a patch of the pot and pays big.
🌿
If seeds pile up over the overgrowth vine at the rim, you have a few seconds before the pot overflows. Classic: that ends the run. Zen: it just composts a loose seed, no death.
