Think Fast
Sky Wolf Studios
A blink-fast garden of tiny games. One word flashes, you do the thing, and the next one is already here. Keep the Bloom Meter climbing and do not run out of hearts as it all speeds up.

How to play

1
A big word flashes each round, like TAP! or SWAT! A small hint under the word tells you exactly what to do, like "Tap the fly" or "Drag the basket under the petal".
2
Do it before the gold timer bar above the field runs out. Most rounds are one tap, one drag, or one hold.
3
You have hearts. Miss a round and you lose one. Run out and the meadow rests.
4
Every clean win fills the Bloom Meter. Fill it and the whole meadow blooms for a bonus keepsake.
5
It speeds up as you go, with a tougher round every so often. How long can you keep up?
