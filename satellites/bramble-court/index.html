How the Court plays

🖐
You and your rival each bring 5 cards. Take turns placing them on the 3×3 court until nine squares are filled.
⚔️
Every card has four edge numbers. Place a card beside a rival card and if your touching edge is higher, their card flips to your side. Most cards at the end wins; the unplayed card counts for its holder.
🌱
Rich Soil: a fertile square gives its card +1 on every edge. A thorn square gives −1. The soil is visible before you place, so plan around it.
📜
Later courtiers bring house rules. Open shows their hand. Same flips rivals whose edges exactly match yours on two or more sides. Plus flips pairs whose edge sums match.
🏆
Your first win against each courtier lets you claim one of their cards, and every third win presses two wild seedling cards into your collection.
👁
Your cards wear a round sage frame with a leaf. Rival cards wear a sharp rust frame with a thorn. Shape tells the side, not just color.
