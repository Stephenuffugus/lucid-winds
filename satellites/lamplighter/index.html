HOW TO LIGHT A TOWN

The square is a grid of walkways and dark houses. Tap a walkway to set a lamp. A lamp shines up, down, left and right until a house blocks the beam. Light every walkway to finish.

Two rules of the guild: no lamp may shine on another lamp, and a numbered house must have exactly that many lamps against its walls. Broken rules show at once, clashing lamps get a red ✕ and an overfed house shows !

Tap again for a gray ✕ note (a spot you believe holds no lamp), and a third tap clears. Watch the painted town behind the grid, its windows kindle as you light the square and fireflies come out near the end.

Solve without hints and a keepsake lantern is hung in your Lantern Row. Every puzzle has exactly one solution. Zen Dusk has free hints, a gentle shake for wrong lamps, and pays no sunbeams.
