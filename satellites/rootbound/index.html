Garden beds

Free the bloom in as few slides as you can.

How to play

🌼
The golden bloom is the big 2×2 planter. Your job is to slide it down and out the gate at the bottom.
↔
Drag any planter into an empty space. Planters slide, they never lift or turn. Clear a path.
🚪
Get the golden bloom to the gate at the bottom-centre and it slips free. Bed solved!
↶
Undo a slide, reset the bed, or take a Nudge hint if the roots get tangled.
⭐
Fewer slides earns more stars and more Sunbeams. Finish near par for three stars.
