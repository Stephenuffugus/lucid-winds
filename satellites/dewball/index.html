How to Roll

You are a bead of dew. Everything smaller than you sticks.

What you do

Roll into anything smaller than your ball and it sticks, making you bigger. Things too big to grab shove you around instead. Grow first, then come back and swallow them.

How you win

Reach the goal size before the clock runs out to earn a star. Keep rolling past the goal for two or three stars and extra Sunbeams. Dream Meadow has no clock, just an endless drift.

Controls

Left thumb rolls. Right thumb turns the camera. Double-tap the right side to flip around. Tap the water drop when it glows to dash.
Keys: WASD roll, Q and E turn, R and F tilt, SPACE dash.
