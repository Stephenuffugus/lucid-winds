DEWBALL · Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
Dewball is played entirely on your phone. The Google Play app collects nothing about you and sends nothing about you anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no purchases inside the game.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game remembers lives on your device only, in your browser's local storage:

  • Your progress, under the key dewball_save: the stars, best sizes and keepsakes of each world, the worlds you have opened, your best balls, how many things you have rolled up, your best Dream Meadow size, the ball skins you have won and the one you use, today's Sunbeam count, and your settings (sound, camera assist, inverted camera, reduced motion, the tutorial seen).
  • Which of the studio's songs you have unlocked by playing, and how long and on how many days you have played, under keys that begin sws_music and sws_game_unlocks. They are counted on the device and decide when the next song unlocks.

None of it leaves your device. Clearing the app's or the site's storage deletes all of it permanently; there is no copy anywhere else.

Network requests the game makes

The game's own files and its 3D models come from lucidwinds.com, the same way any web page loads its files. That server sees an ordinary file request from your device and nothing about you or your play. After the first load the game keeps its own copies of what it has fetched, so the worlds you have played open offline.

Music plays nothing until you ask it to. When you play a song it streams from lucidwinds.com, or, for some older public domain recordings, from archive.org (the Internet Archive). Those servers see an ordinary request for the song and nothing about you. Music needs a connection.

Permissions

The app asks for no device permissions. No location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts.

The copy on the open web

When you play Dewball in a web browser at lucidwinds.com instead of the Google Play app, everything above still holds, with two optional extras the Play app does not have:

  • Sunbeams. Playing earns Sunbeams, the currency of our game Lucid Winds. They are counted in your browser's local storage. If you have signed in to Lucid Winds in the same browser, the Sunbeams you earned are added to that account through our server (hosted on Google Firebase), which records your Lucid Winds account identifier and the number earned. Without a Lucid Winds sign in, nothing is sent. To do this the web copy loads Google's Firebase code from www.gstatic.com.
  • The feedback form. On the web, Feedback opens a form. What you type, and the game's name and version, go to our server so we can read it. Only what you choose to send is sent.

Children

Dewball is a general audience game and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version is posted at this address.
