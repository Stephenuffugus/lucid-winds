How to play

Read this, then take your nine.
✦
Flick the dewball up the lane. A quick flick throws hard, a gentle flick throws soft, and the angle of your flick is the line the ball takes.
●
The rings pay 10 to 50. Land in a mouth and the ball drops home. Anything that misses rolls down to the front tray for 10.
◎
The two corner burrows pay 100. They want a full power roll on an edge line, and the gutter is waiting just wide of them.
⚖
Power picks how deep the ball lands. The 40 asks for a soft touch. Overthrow it and the back wall hands the ball down to the tray.
↻
Too soft and the ball rolls back to your hand, no ball spent. Too wide on the lane and it gutters for nothing.
★
Nine dewballs a round. The same flick always throws the same shot, so the lane can be learned. Rounds pay tickets for the trophy shelf, and every sunk hundred pays five extra.
Score past your best and the night remembers it.
