How Silt works

🎯
The goal. Paint silt, water, fire, and seed onto a pocket of living earth and let real physics take over.
👆
Pick an element chip, then draw on the world. Everything falls, flows, burns and grows by its own small rules.
🌱
Grow a garden: Soil drinks Water and turns dark. A Seed on damp soil sprouts a Vine, and a happy vine tips into a Bloom.
💓
The Garden pulse measures the living garden right now. Keep blooms alive and fires out. Holding a strong pulse is what earns sunbeams.
🔥
Fire eats Vine and Oil. Water quenches it into rising Mist, and mist cools on stone ceilings and rains back down.
🌸
Raise 5 blooms at once and the garden is counted. Every third garden presses a keepsake into your Grove.
🔷
New matter to play with: Silt heated by fire fuses into Glass. Lava flows, burns, and cools to Stone where water touches it. Ice and Snow melt to water. A bloom breathes Pollen that re-sows soil. Fire leaves Ash that damp soil drinks to grow again. Moss and glowing Spore creep over wet stone and glass.
🪨
The quiet ones: Stone and Glass never move and nothing eats them, so they make walls and floors. Silt falls and piles into slopes. Oil pools like water and catches fire fastest of anything in the satchel.
🌱
Your garden is saved. Leave and come back and it's right where you left it. Scenes start a fresh little world; the dock's Clear begins a new garden.
