How to Play

Drag to move your paddle and block the ball. When the machine misses, you score.

Win the match by reaching the target score first. Pick the target and difficulty in Free Play. Career levels set their own.

Curve the ball: flick your paddle sideways at the moment you strike. Spin bends the flight.

Power-ups: in Gauntlet arenas, steer the ball through a capsule. Whoever touched the ball last gets the boost.

Rewards: matches pay ⚡ Rally and 💎 Gems for the Skins shop. Everything is earned by playing.

The Arenas
🏓Classic: side paddles. Drag up and down to guard your goal line.
🔼Sky Duel: paddles on top and bottom. Slide left and right.
🌀Orbit: curved paddles on a ring. Circle around to cover your arc.
⚡Gauntlet: the classic court plus power-ups, bumpers and a moving block.
🛡️Survival: solo. One paddle, three walls, three lives. Rack up returns.
⁘Multiball: three balls at once under classic rules. Any ball can score.
🏆Career: The Gauntlet ladder. Win each level to unlock the next.
