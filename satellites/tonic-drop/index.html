HOW TO PLAY

Objective. You are an angry squirrel packing its winter stash. Paired acorns drop into the stash. Line up four or more of one kind in a row or column and they crack away. Clear every pest to fill the stash.

How to play. Each acorn kind also wears a shape so you never need color: ● disc, ▲ triangle, ◆ diamond. The grumpy pests at the bottom wear the same shapes. Box four of a kind in a line and the pests crack out with them.

Controls. ◀ ▶ slide the falling pair, ⟳ rotates it, ⤓ drops it fast. When one acorn loses its partner it falls on its own, and a crack can chain into more cracks for bonus caps. Every 8 cracks the next pair brings a ✦ wild acorn that counts as any kind.

Modes. Stash Run ramps 20 stashes, Daily Stash serves one seeded stash a day, Sprint races the clock, and Zen Grove never tops out and pays no sunbeams. The caps you earn buy skins in the Shop.
