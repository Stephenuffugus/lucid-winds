SPORE DRIFT
an ambient drift
You are a spore in deep still water. Tap to breathe out a puff and drift the other way. Absorb what is smaller. Slip past what is not.

Chambers

Ten still waters, each with one quiet problem.

How to drift

👆
Tap anywhere and your spore breathes a puff of itself toward the tap, drifting you the opposite way. Every puff costs a little of you. The puff floats free, and can be re-absorbed.
🫧
Touch a smaller mote and you slowly drink it in. Touch a bigger one and it drinks you. Size is everything and it is always changing.
⚠️
Anything bigger than you carries a pulsing ring and a notch mark, whatever its color. When you outgrow it, the ring fades. Watch the rings, not the hues.
🌊
The Season Current flows through every chamber and slowly turns through spring, summer, autumn and winter. Riding it is free travel. Masters drift more and tap less.
🎯
Each Chamber has one goal, written at the top. The bar shows how close you are. Losing too much of yourself ends the drift.
✿
Clearing a chamber for the first time blossoms your spore into a keepsake for the Grove.
