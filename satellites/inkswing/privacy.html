INKSWING · Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
Inkswing is played on your phone. It collects nothing about you and sends nothing about you anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no purchases inside the game.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game remembers lives on your device only, in local storage:

  • Your drawings and settings, under the key lw_inkswing_v1: the drawings you keep in the folio, the name for a poster's caption if you choose to type one, which rigs you have opened, your settings (hum, motion, tilt, the ink and nib you mixed) and the tips you have already seen.
  • How many times you have opened the game, under inkswing_launches. It is used only to keep the studio's song card off your first screen.
  • The studio's music: which of its songs you have unlocked by playing, and how long and on how many days you have played, under sws_game_unlocks and keys that begin sws_music. If you use the music player, it also keeps its volume, the last song and any playlist you make, under sws_music_state and sws_playlists. These are counted on the device and decide when the next song unlocks.

None of it leaves your device, and none of it names you. To erase it all, clear the storage of the Inkswing app (Android Settings, Apps, Inkswing, Storage, Clear storage), or on the web clear this site's data in your browser. There is no copy anywhere else.

Network requests the game makes

The game's own files come from lucidwinds.com, the same way any web page loads its files. That server sees an ordinary file request from your device and nothing about you or your play. After the first load the game keeps its own copies of its files on the device, so it plays with no connection. Every sound in the game is made on the device.

The game loads the studio's music script (/music-unlocks.js) and, when you open the music player, its player (/music-player.js), both from lucidwinds.com. The Music button opens that player. It plays nothing until you ask it to. When you play a song it streams from lucidwinds.com, or, for some older public domain recordings, from archive.org (the Internet Archive). Those servers see an ordinary request for the song and nothing about you. Music needs a connection.

Sharing and posters

SHARE opens your phone's own share sheet, or copies a link to your clipboard, only when you tap it. The link carries the swings that drew the drawing and nothing about you. Opening such a link redraws it on your own device. MAKE A POSTER draws an image on the device and hands it to your share sheet or saves it as a file; the caption carries the name you typed, if you typed one. The game sends none of this anywhere; you choose where it goes.

Permissions

The game asks for no device permissions on Android. No location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts. If you turn TILT on, the game reads the phone's motion sensor to lean the pendulum; that reading is used on the device while you play and is never stored or sent.

The Google Play app and the web

The Google Play app and the web page at lucidwinds.com run the same game and do the same things.

Children

Inkswing collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version is posted at this address.
