Jade Garden
Sky Wolf Studio
Match free pairs of botanical tiles to clear the garden. A tile is free when nothing rests on it and one side is open. A calm, unhurried Mahjong solitaire.

Choose a garden

Every garden is always solvable. Take a hint or undo any time you snag.

How to play

🀄
Tap two free tiles that show the same botanical to match them away.
🌸
Seasons (🌸🌻🍁❄️) match any other season, and Elements (💧☀️⛰️🌬️) match any other element, just like classic Mahjong's flowers and seasons.
✋
A tile is free only if nothing rests on top of it and its left or right side is open. Free tiles sit bright; blocked ones dim. Turn on Highlight free tiles to see them glow.
🌸
The Season tiles (gold frame) all match each other, and the Element tiles (silver frame) all match each other. Handy wilds.
🌿
Clear the whole garden to win. Snagged? Take a hint or undo, or reset the garden to try a different order.
☀️
Clear gardens to earn Sunbeams. Bigger gardens and faster clears earn a little more.
