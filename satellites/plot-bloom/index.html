How to play

Goal. Fill the plot with pieces that love their neighbors and score as high as you can.
Plant. Tap a piece in your hand, then tap an empty square. While a piece is picked up, every empty square shows the exact points it would earn there.
Neighbors. A square's neighbors are the 8 squares touching it, corners included. A new piece earns points from each neighbor it loves and loses points for each neighbor it clashes with. Pieces two or more squares away pay nothing.
🌷
Plan ahead. Points are paid once, the moment you plant. A planted piece still matters after that: it counts as a neighbor for everything planted beside it later. A pond planted today raises the numbers on the squares around it for the flowers, trees, and veg you plant tomorrow.
Grow. Points fill the bar at the top. When it fills, the plot grows and new kinds of pieces join your hand.

Who loves whom
🌷 Flowers love 🐝 hives, 💧 ponds and 🪑 benches.
🌳 Trees love benches and ponds but crowd other trees.
💧 Ponds feed 🥕 veg but crowd other ponds. 🌿 Hedges shelter veg.
🐝 Hives hate benches, and two hives quarrel.

Score 6 or more with one planting and it bursts into a Harmony Bloom, a keepsake flower for your Gallery.
