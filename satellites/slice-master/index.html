How to play

👆
Tap anywhere to flip. Every tap hops the knife forward with a spin. Resting on the ground is fine, tap to get moving again.
🥕
Slice everything you touch. Veggies pay money, crates burst open with coins, and the counter at the top keeps ticking.
🩷
Never touch the pink crystals. One touch ends the run and the level restarts.
🔩
Steel plates don't cut. Land on them, ride them, hop off them.
🎯
Every level ends at the score wall. Stick it high for up to ×32 on everything you sliced.
🪙
Money buys new knives in the shop. Every fifth level is a bonus kitchen stuffed with coins and nothing pink.
