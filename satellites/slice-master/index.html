Sky Wolf Studios
One tap flips the knife. Carve through the stacks, cut the crates open, never touch the floor — then stick the highest notch on the score wall.

Courses

carve every stack · stick the wall high

How to play

👆
Tap anywhere to flip. The knife hops and spins forward on its own.
🍉
Fall into a stack and carve. Slicing slows your fall, so you cut a channel right through the pile. Crates cut open and spill coins.
🔩
Steel plates don't cut — they bounce you high. Use them.
⚡
Never touch the floor. It ends the run on the spot.
🎯
The run ends at the score wall — notches are worth ×1 up to ×10 at the very top. Everything you sliced gets multiplied by where you stick.
