How to play

Land on the sun or on anything stuck and the piece sticks.

Sides never grab. Miss everything and it falls into the void.

Close a square ring. It breaks and the outside crushes in.

SLAM drops it and turns the whole gyre a quarter turn.

Drag to slide the piece. Tap to turn it.

Journey asks for more rings each level. Daily Gyre is the same run for everyone, once a day. Zen never ends. Tap the board to turn a piece.
