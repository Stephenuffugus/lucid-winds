How to play

1
Pieces fall from the top and STICK when they land on the golden core or on anything already stuck. Sides never grab, so a falling piece slides past the structure into deeper gaps. The floor and walls hold nothing: miss everything and the piece drifts into the void. Move with ◀ ▶, spin with ⟳ (or tap the board), nudge down with ▼.
2
Grow the structure from the core outward into square rings. Fill every cell of a ring and it erases, and everything outside crushes inward one ring. Chains score big.
3
SLAM drops the piece instantly and spins the whole gyre a quarter turn as it lands. This is how the gyre grows sideways and underneath: build a tower upward, then slam to swing it around the sun. Set a piece down gently and nothing spins.
4
Journey asks for more rings each level and speeds up. Daily Gyre is the same piece run for everyone, once a day. Zen never ends. Topping out just clears the sky.
