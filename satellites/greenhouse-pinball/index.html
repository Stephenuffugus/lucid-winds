Greenhouse
Pinball
Sky Wolf Studios
Keep the pollen bead alive on a living table. Every bumper you strike grows the plant — fill the growth ribbon to trigger Bloom Multiball. A bed of moss catches your first drain, so nothing feels cheap.

How to play

🚀
Tap to launch the pollen bead up the lane onto the table.
👆
Tap the left half of the screen for the left flipper, the right half for the right. (Keyboard: ← and → , or Z and /.)
🌻
Hit the flower bumpers to score and grow the plant. The green growth ribbon on the right fills as you go.
🌸
Fill the ribbon for Bloom Multiball — the table erupts with extra beads and huge score.
🌿
The moss net glows across the drain and bounces a doomed bead back — once per ball, and always on your last ball.
🪴
Classic: 3 balls. Zen: endless, drains just re-launch. Daily: one seeded table a day.
