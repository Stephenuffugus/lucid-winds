A whole pinball table sculpted in clay. Flip the eyeball down the lab bench, chomp the monster bumpers, knock the beakers flat, and feed the big mouth to light the MEGA MASH. A goo net catches your first drain, so nothing feels cheap.

How to play

🚀
Hold to charge the plunger, release to fire the bead up the lane — more charge, harder shot. (Keyboard: hold Space.)
⭐
A flower lights up as you launch — strike that lit bumper first for a Skill Shot bonus.
👆
Tap the left half of the screen for the left flipper, the right half for the right. (Keyboard: ← and → , or Z and /.)
🤚
Nudge the table to save a doomed bead — two-finger tap, or keys Q / E / W. But nudge too much and the table TILTS: flippers freeze and the ball is lost.
🌻
Hit the flower bumpers to score and grow the plant. The green growth ribbon on the left rail fills as you go.
🌷
Collect the five B·L·O·O·M standups to light the lock, then shoot the glowing Greenhouse ramp to lock a bead in the glasshouse. Three locks start Pollen Multiball: jackpots on every ramp, four of them light a Super Jackpot at the scoop, and a huge spinner rip adds a ball.
🌸
Fill the growth ribbon for a Bloom Rush: a 20 second frenzy where every switch pays a bonus. Refill the ribbon while it burns to extend it.
💫
The Heartvine ramp changes its exit after every pass (watch the gold flag), a quick double on the Fern ramp crosses over, and a charged Taproot kicks a doomed bead back up the left outlane. Roll both inlane gates to recharge it.
🌿
The moss net glows across the drain and bounces a doomed bead back — once per ball, and always on your last ball.
🪴
Classic: 3 balls. Zen: endless, drains just re-launch. Daily: one seeded table a day.
