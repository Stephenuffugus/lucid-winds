Meadow Weave

Turn each hex tile and set it down so meadow meets meadow, pond meets pond. Close off a region and it comes alive and presses a feral seed into your pouch. No clock, no losing. Just weave.

How to weave

🎯
The goal. Turn and lay hex tiles so meadow meets meadow and pond meets pond until the map comes alive.
🔄
Tap Rotate to turn the next tile. Each hex has six edges, each a kind of land.
👆
Tap a glowing empty slot to place it. Line up matching edges for points and a rising chime.
🌾
Close off a whole region of one land and it comes alive and drops a feral seed into your pouch.
🎋
Clean placements refund tiles to your bag. A tidy weave lasts longer.
🍃
You never lose, you just finish when the bag runs out. Keep the map you grew.
