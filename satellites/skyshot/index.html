Skyshot
Sky Wolf Studios
A slingshot sits in the night garden and fires straight up the sky. The buds up there will not hold still and the brambles keep turning, so read the rhythm and pick your moment.

How to play

Skyshot fires up the screen, not across it.
Press anywhere and drag down to pull the sling back. Drag further for more power, drag sideways to lean the shot.
The dotted guide shows the start of the arc only. The rest is yours to judge.
Pop every moonbud to clear the level. Buds walk, circle and swing, so aim where the bud is going.
Brambles turn and gates breathe open and shut. Wait for the gap, the clock never stops.
Seeds are limited. Clear the level at or under par for three stars. Run out of seeds and the level is lost.
On a keyboard: left and right to aim, up and down for power, space to fire.
