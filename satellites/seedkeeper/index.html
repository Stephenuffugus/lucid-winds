Seedkeeper
Sky Wolf Studios
Guide the little hedgehog through the hazard gardens. Gather the seeds, open the greenhouse gate, and slip out the far door. A cozy remake of Chip's Challenge.

The gardens

Each garden teaches something new.

How to play

🦔
Swipe or use the pad to walk the hedgehog one square at a time.
🌰
Gather all the seeds, then the gate opens. Reach the greenhouse door to finish.
🔑
Keys open the matching coloured doors. Push a stone planter into a pond to make a safe path.
🧿
Find the charm for each hazard: lily charm for ponds, ember charm for fire, frost charm to steer on ice, wind charm on gusts.
🐛
Pests move when you do. Touch one and you wilt, so read their paths. You can always retry or undo.
