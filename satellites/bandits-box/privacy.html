Bandit's Box · Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 August 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
Bandit's Box does not collect any data, and does not send anything anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. The app works with no network connection at all.

What is stored, and where

The app saves one item in your device's own browser storage, under the name bandit-set. It holds only your settings and preferences:

  • volume, and the sound and haptics toggles
  • accessibility choices such as quiet mode, calm mode and larger toys
  • your chosen colour theme
  • which toy you had open last, and any toys you pinned as favourites
  • an optional pop tally, which only counts if you switch it on yourself, and the label word you chose for it

This never leaves your device. We cannot see it. It is not backed up to us, not synced, and not shared with anyone.

What is not collected

No name, no email address, no account, no contacts, no location, no device identifier, no advertising ID, no photos, no microphone or camera access, and no usage or analytics data of any kind.

Deleting it

Open Settings inside the app and choose Start fresh. That deletes bandit-set immediately and completely. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Because nothing was ever sent to us, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and nothing to request.

Children

Bandit's Box is suitable for all ages and is designed to be safe for children. Because it collects no data at all, there is no personal information from a child or anyone else to misuse. There are no ads, no purchases, no links out of the app, and no way to communicate with anyone through it.

Third parties

None. The app contains no third party SDKs, no ad networks, no analytics providers and no payment processors. All of its sound is generated by the app itself as you play; nothing is downloaded.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a future version of the app ever collected anything, that would be stated here plainly before it shipped.
