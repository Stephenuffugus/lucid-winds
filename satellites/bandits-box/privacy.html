Bandit's Box · Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
Bandit's Box collects nothing about you and sends nothing about you anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. The toys work with no network connection at all; only the studio's music needs one.

What is stored, and where

The app saves its settings in your device's own browser storage, under the name bandit-set. It holds only your settings and preferences:

  • volume, and the sound and haptics toggles
  • accessibility choices such as quiet mode, calm mode and larger toys
  • your chosen colour theme
  • which toy you had open last, and any toys you pinned as favourites
  • an optional pop tally, which only counts if you switch it on yourself, and the label word you chose for it

The studio's music: which of its songs you have unlocked by playing, and how long and on how many days you have played, under sws_game_unlocks and keys that begin sws_music. If you use the music player, it also keeps its volume, the last song and any playlist you make, under sws_music_state and sws_playlists. These are counted on the device and decide when the next song unlocks.

None of this leaves your device. We cannot see it. It is not backed up to us, not synced, and not shared with anyone.

What is not collected

No name, no email address, no account, no contacts, no location, no device identifier, no advertising ID, no photos, no microphone or camera access, and no usage or analytics data of any kind.

Deleting it

Open Settings inside the app and choose Start fresh. That deletes bandit-set immediately and completely. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes it and the music keys too. Because nothing was ever sent to us, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and nothing to request.

Children

Bandit's Box is suitable for all ages and is designed to be safe for children. Because it collects no data at all, there is no personal information from a child or anyone else to misuse. There are no ads, no purchases, no links out of the app, and no way to communicate with anyone through it.

Third parties

None. The app contains no third party SDKs, no ad networks, no analytics providers and no payment processors. All of the toys' sound is generated by the app itself as you play.

Network requests the app makes

The app's own files come from lucidwinds.com, the same way any web page loads its files. That server sees an ordinary file request from your device and nothing about you. After the first load the app keeps its own copies of its files on the device, so the toys play with no connection.

The Music button opens the studio's music player. It plays nothing until you ask it to. When you play a song it streams from lucidwinds.com, or, for some older public domain recordings, from archive.org (the Internet Archive). Those servers see an ordinary request for the song and nothing about you. Music needs a connection.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a future version of the app ever collected anything, that would be stated here plainly before it shipped.
