MERGE & BLAST
Sky Wolf Studios
Tap a group of matching numbers and they blast into the next number up. Chase the big doubles: 512, 1K, 2K… how high can you grow?

How to play

👆
Tap two or more touching tiles with the same number. They blast away, and ONE tile of the next number up grows where you tapped.
🧮
2s make a 4, 4s make an 8… all the way into the thousands. Bigger groups score bigger.
🚀
Journey gives you a goal each level and counts every level you finish. Daily Grid is the same board for everyone, 30 taps, once a day. Zen just grows.
🔀
No possible group? The garden reshuffles itself for free.
