Flock the World — Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 August 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
Flock the World does not collect any data, and does not send anything anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no network requests to any third party. After the first load, the game works with no network connection at all.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game remembers lives in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  • ftw_run — your in-progress campaign, so you can resume it.
  • ftw_recs — lifetime records (runs, wins, best coverage, synergies found).
  • ftw_seen, ftw_tips — which one-time tips you have already read, and whether tips are on.
  • ftw_mute, ftw_playlist — sound preferences.

None of it leaves your device. Clearing the site's browsing data deletes all of it permanently; there is no copy anywhere else.

Permissions

The app asks for no device permissions. No location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts.

Children

The game contains no ads, no purchases, no chat, and collects nothing from anyone, children included. It is political satire about surveillance, written for a general audience.
