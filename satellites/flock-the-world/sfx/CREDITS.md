# SFX Credits — Flock The World

All sound effects in this folder are sourced from **Kenney.nl** asset packs.
Every Kenney pack is published under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0)** — public
domain, free for commercial use, no attribution required. The `License.txt`
inside each downloaded pack was verified to state "Creative Commons Zero, CC0"
at download time (2026-08-24).

Processing applied (ffmpeg): converted to mono 96kbps MP3, leading silence
trimmed, short fade-out at tail, peak-normalized to approx. -1 dB.
`buy_large.mp3` is `chips-stack-3` pitched down ~15% (asetrate resample) for a
heavier feel — a pitch shift of the CC0 recording, not a synthesized sound.
