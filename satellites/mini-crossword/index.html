HOW TO SOLVE

Tap a square to select its word, tap it again to switch between Across and Down. Type letters on the keyboard; the clue for your current word shows in the bar. Fill every white square correctly to win.

The ‹ › arrows jump to the previous or next word. Every square is checked in two directions, so a crossing letter you are sure of helps the word that crosses it.

Ink Streak: solve with no wrong letters and your streak grows, inking new pens and papers. One wrong letter breaks the streak for that puzzle. Zen turns on auto check (wrong letters glow red) and pays no sunbeams, so it never counts against your streak.

Daily is one shared puzzle a day, Endless deals fresh minis, Time Trial races the clock. Ink you earn buys cosmetics in the shop.
