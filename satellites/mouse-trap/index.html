Mouse Trap
Sky Wolf Studios
A little garden mouse is loose in your veg patch. Plant hedges one at a time to wall it in. After every hedge the mouse scurries one step toward the nearest open edge. Box it in on all sides before it slips out into the wild garden.

How to play

1
Goal. Trap the garden mouse so it has nowhere left to run. You win the moment every escape route is blocked.
2
Your move. Tap any empty tile to plant a hedge there. Hedges are walls the mouse cannot step through.
3
The mouse moves. Right after each hedge, the mouse takes one step along its shortest path toward the nearest open edge. A soft glow shows where it will run next.
4
You lose if the mouse reaches a tile on the outer edge and escapes into the wild garden.
5
You win when the mouse is fully boxed in with no path to any edge. Fewer hedges earns more stars.
6
Difficulty is set by how many hedges start on the board. Easy gives you a big head start, Hard gives you very few.
