Flower Press
Sky Wolf Studios
Clear the pressing pile by matching free pairs of blooms. A tile is free when nothing sits on it and one side is open. A cozy botanical Mahjong solitaire.

Choose a pile

Every pile is always solvable. Take a hint or reshuffle if you snag.

How to play

🌸
Tap two free tiles that show the same bloom to press them out of the pile.
✋
A tile is free only if nothing is stacked on top of it and its left or right side is clear. Free tiles look bright; locked ones are dimmed.
🌿
Clear the whole pile to win. Snagged? Take a hint, undo a press, or reshuffle the pile into a fresh solvable order.
☀️
Clear piles to earn Sunbeams. Bigger piles and faster, hint-free clears earn more.
