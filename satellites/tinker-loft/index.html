HOW TO TINKER

Every machine gives you a goal and a tray of parts. Tap a part, tap the loft to place it, drag to fine tune, then press RUN and watch honest physics do the rest.

Planks are ramps (TILT cycles their angle). Dominoes topple in chains. Fans blow sideways, balloons only rise, funnels catch and drop, seesaws tip under weight, and scissors cut the nearest string when something rolls over them.

The One Nudge: while the machine runs you may tap ONCE anywhere. The nearest moving piece gets a small shove toward your finger. Some machines are built around that single tap. Clear a bench without spending it for a ★ no-nudge star.

Daily Contraption is the same remix for every player with a streak to keep. Sandbox is a free build with every part. Zen Loft opens every bench with bottomless parts and endless nudges, and pays no sunbeams.
