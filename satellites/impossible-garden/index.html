How to Play

Turn
Tap a round knob to turn its vine arm. Each turn changes which gaps the vines bridge.
Walk
Tap any tile and the wanderer walks to it along connected paths. A vine that spans a gap joins the two sides, even when the angles look like they could never meet.
Bloom
Walk the wanderer onto the golden bloom to finish the garden. There is no way to lose, so take your time.
Modes
Journey opens gardens in order. Daily Path offers one garden each day. Free Play lets you wander any garden, with a glowing hint knob if you pause.
