Leaf Fit
Sky Wolf Studios
Drag leaf sprigs onto the midnight trellis. Fill a whole row, column, or 3×3 patch and it clears in a shower of pollen. Bank enough pollen and a Bloom saves you when the bed fills up. You only ever lose to your own play. Tap a sprig to turn it.

How to play

1
Drag a sprig from the tray onto the trellis. A ghost shows where it lands; green means a line will clear.
2
Tap a sprig, or the ⟳ Rotate button, to turn it a quarter turn before you plant it.
3
Fill any full row, column, or 3×3 patch to clear it. Clear several at once for a bigger combo.
4
Cleared leaves drift into Nectar. When it's full, tap Bloom to deal three fresh sprigs. Recovery you earned, never bought.
5
No countdown and no ads. A small clock under your score just counts your time. The bed only fills when nothing fits and your Nectar is empty.
6
Good runs grow keepsake flowers into your Grove and pay sunbeams.
