How Root Weave works

🎯
The goal. Drag the glowing bulbs until no two roots cross and feel the knot loosen into living green vine.
👆
Drag a bulb anywhere on the soil. Roots that cross render as dry gray gnarl with an ✗ at every crossing point. Roots that run clean turn to living green vine.
🧩
Every tangle is grown from a real flat weave first, then scrambled, so a perfect untangling always exists.
🪢
Taproots are anchored bulbs with a knot. They cannot be moved. Weave everything else around them.
🌸
Hold the weave clean for a breath and it blooms. Each bloom presses a root mandala keepsake.
🕯
In Zen Weave the Nudge candle is free: it briefly shows one bulb the way home. Zen is for unwinding and pays no sunbeams.
📅
The Daily Tangle is one shared board per day. Keep the streak alive.
