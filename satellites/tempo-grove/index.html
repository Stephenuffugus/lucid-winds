Stack sun and moon quads while a sweepline crosses on the beat of the soundtrack, clearing every square you have grown.

How Tempo Grove works

🟨
Quads of Sun (gold, solid dot) and Moon (indigo, hollow diamond) fall onto the field. Shift with ◀ ▶, spin with ⟳, slam with ⤓. You can also tap the field to spin and swipe down to slam.
◼
Grow any 2x2 of one kind and it lights up as a marked square. Bigger same-kind slabs mark as overlapping squares worth more.
📏
The sweepline crosses the field on the beat of the music. Marked squares it passes are cleared together, and the stack settles.
🌸
Bloom Squares: every cleared square drops a petal into the garden border around the field. Clear 4+ squares in one sweep for a Grove Burst. Fill the border and a keepsake is pressed into your Grove.
♫
The soundtrack is Stephen's own music plus a public domain classical library. The music sets the sweep tempo, and you can swap tracks any time.
☀
Clearing squares earns sunbeams in every mode except Zen.
