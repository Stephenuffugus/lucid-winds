# WINDUP, what is drawn and what is not

Nothing in this game is a painted asset. The box, the cloth, the paper, the
brass comb and the crank are all drawn by code at run time, because the strip
scrolls and the tines move and a picture of a music box cannot do either. The
only files that ship are the three icons and the portal tile, and both are
generated by tools in this folder.

## What ships

| File | What it is | Made by |
|---|---|---|
| `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-maskable-512.png` | the app icons: a punched strip feeding into a walnut box, and the crank | `node tools/icons.mjs` |
| `docs/thumb.png` | the portal tile, 512 square | `node tools/thumb.mjs` |
| `docs/shots/*.png` | the evidence, not assets | `node tools/shots.mjs` |
| `docs/shots/p0-tine.wav` | a middle C, the C above it, and seven notes of Twinkle, for Stephen's ear | `node tools/tinewav.mjs` |

`tools/thumb.mjs` refuses to write a tile without a brass comb across its middle
and a crank under it, measured fifth by fifth. `tools/tinewav.mjs`
autocorrelates the first two notes and refuses to write a file that does not
contain the pitches it claims.

## How the object is made to have weight

The plan's third step of P1 is to shoot the box and look at it, and to fix the
drawing before anything else if it reads as a rectangle with a circle. What is
there now, and why:

- **The cloth has a nap**: five hundred short strokes at seeded positions, so it
  never crawls between frames.
- **The walnut has grain that follows the board** and one knot in it, drawn as
  four concentric ellipses. Both are clipped to the case.
- **The brass has exactly one specular line** along its top edge. One is what
  makes metal read as metal; two make it read as plastic.
- **The paper runs edge to edge THROUGH the case** and hangs out of both sides,
  curling where it is free. This is the single strongest cue that the thing is a
  music box rather than a panel.
- **The case sits in its own shadow** on the cloth.
- **The crank is mounted on a walnut bracket** off the case's right shoulder,
  with a brass shaft turning in it.

## What would be worth painting

`plans/windup/ART-PACK-WINDUP.md` has the three sheets. In order of what would
change the most:

1. `box-layers.png`, the case, lid, comb and crank as four separate renders. The
   drawn case holds together but it is flat: a render with real wood and real
   brass would carry the whole screen.
2. `velvet.png`, a tiled cloth. The nap is convincing at a glance and thin up
   close.
3. `wraps.png`, three wrapping papers. The drawn stripes and stars are the
   weakest thing in the game to look at, and they are the first thing a person
   receiving a gift sees.
