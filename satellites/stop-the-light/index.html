How to play

Read this, then release the firefly.
✦
A firefly runs the ring. Tap anywhere to stop it. One tap, that is the whole control.
●
Stop it inside the gold band and you keep going. Stop it in the pale heart at the middle of the band and that stop pays triple.
⚖
After every good stop you choose. Bank keeps the sparks you are holding. Go again grows the pot and narrows the band.
✖
Miss the band and the whole unbanked pot is gone. That is the only way to lose it.
☼
You get three fireflies a run. Each one is one chain. Banking ends that chain and keeps the sparks, so spend your fireflies well.
∿
The ring does not run at one speed. It swells and eases in the same pattern all run. Read the rhythm rather than counting.
↻
From round five the band drifts as well, so you are stopping a moving light on a moving gap.
The firefly tires after four laps. If you never tap, that counts as a miss.
