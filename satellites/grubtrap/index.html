Grubtrap
Sky Wolf Studios
You're a field mouse in an overrun bed. Shove the stone planters to box the garden grubs into a corner until they curl up for good. A cozy remake of Rodent's Revenge.

How to play

🐭
Steer the mouse with the pad. Walk into a stone planter to shove it, and a whole row of them if there is room behind.
🐛
Box a grub in on all four sides with planters or the bed wall and it curls into a harmless seedball. Curl up every grub to clear the ground.
⚠️
Don't let a grub reach you. If one crawls onto your square you lose a whisker. Lose them all and it is over.
🌰
Seedballs become solid, so use them to help pen the next grub. Later grounds add more grubs and quicker ones.
☀️
Clear grounds to earn Sunbeams for your garden.
