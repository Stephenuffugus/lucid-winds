## THE GAME (so the art fits)
Sprout Dice is a **cozy botanical dice roguelike** — you roll a little handful of **plant dice**,
reroll, then place each die on a garden **pest** to attack, or on yourself to heal/shield. Thorns
strike, bark protects, blooms heal. You climb floors and fight bosses. Think *Slice & Dice /
Dicey Dungeons* but as a **midnight greenhouse**.
