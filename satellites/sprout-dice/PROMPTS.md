# SPROUT DICE — paste-ready ChatGPT image prompts

*ChatGPT's image model does best with **one subject per prompt**, not a grid of many. So generate
these **one at a time** — each block below is a complete prompt. Paste the STYLE line + one SUBJECT.
Ask for a **plain solid magenta `#FF00FF` background** (I key it out); if ChatGPT paints a scene
instead, add "nothing else in the image, plain flat magenta background, the subject floats centered."*

---

## STYLE (paste at the top of every prompt)
> Cozy storybook game art, hand-painted gouache with clean cartoon cel-shading, soft warm rim-light,
> a little glow. Midnight-greenhouse palette: deep near-black green, sage green, warm gold, cream.
> Menacing-cute, never grim. Plain flat **magenta #FF00FF** background, nothing else, subject centered
> with even padding, no text or labels.
