Sky Wolf Studio · Lucid Winds

How to Play

1
Each letter of a short verse was swapped for another. Tap a scrambled letter to select it everywhere.
2
Tap a letter on the keyboard to guess what it really is. Your guess fills every matching spot at once.
3
The little bar under a letter shows how often it appears. Common letters are usually E, T, A, O.
4
Stuck? Spend Dew to reveal one true letter. Tap Check to gently flag wrong guesses. There is no way to lose.
5
Crack the verse and its plant blooms into your Gallery, with a splash of Dew and Sunbeams.
