# AIRWORTHY, what is drawn and what is not

Nothing in this game is a painted asset. Every plane, room, prop and dial is
drawn by code at run time, because the plane is re folded from its spec on every
frame and a picture of a plane cannot be. The only files that ship are the three
icons and the portal tile, and both of those are generated by tools in this
folder rather than painted.

## What ships

| File | What it is | Made by |
|---|---|---|
| `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-maskable-512.png` | the app icons | `node tools/icons.mjs` |
| `docs/thumb.png` | the portal tile, 512 square | `node tools/thumb.mjs` |
| `docs/shots/*.png` | the evidence, not assets | `node tools/shots.mjs` |

`tools/thumb.mjs` will refuse to write a tile that has nothing in it, that has
the plane outside the crop, or that is over 150 KB. A tile of empty paper is
exactly what this game would produce by accident, so the tool measures the
picture it just made before it writes it.

## The two rooms

Both are drawn by `drawCourse` from `COURSES`, and every prop in them is drawn
from the same numbers the flight reads. The fan's jet is `windAt`'s fan field,
the thermal's column is its thermal field, the banner's gap is what `ringsHit`
measures and the mark on the floor is the zone the accuracy challenge scores
against. There is no decorative prop anywhere in either room, on purpose: this
is a game about reading the air, and a prop that lies about the air is worse
than no prop.

- **The Gym.** Beige, a floor ruled in metres, high windows, a painted stripe,
  roof trusses with lamps hanging off them, two school banners to fly between
  and a desk to land on.
- **The Backyard.** Sky and clouds that drift at half the camera's speed, a
  waist high fence with a hedge behind it, a box fan on a stand, a kettle grill
  with a column of warm air over it, two pegged shirts on the line, and a
  paddling pool.

## The plane

`drawPlaneAt` draws it in profile: a long triangle, a folded keel under it, the
crease down its spine and the elevator bent at the tail. It carries a `dark`
flag for the wind tunnel, where the room is nearly black and an ink outline
disappears. `drawPlanView` draws it from ABOVE for the hangar cards, because in
profile a wide wing and a narrow one are the same picture and every card looked
identical.

## What is missing

There is no painted art at all, and it would suit this game: a hand drawn gym,
a photographed sheet of paper, a real backyard. The code drawing is deliberately
plain and it holds together, but it is the thinnest part of the game to look at.
