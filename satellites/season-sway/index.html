Season Sway
Sky Wolf Studios
A card game about tending one little garden. Swipe each visitor's card left or right to answer, and keep Sun, Rain, Soil and Wildlife in balance for as many seasons as you can.

How to play

1
Goal: keep the four meters, Sun · Rain · Soil · Wildlife, near the middle. If any bar empties or fills all the way, the run ends.
2
How: swipe each visitor's card left or right to answer them. As you drag, arrows over the meters show which ones that choice will move.
3
Watch for: every 6 cards the season turns, and each season pushes one meter toward an edge. Balance ahead of it.
4
Keep: every finished run grows a keepsake bloom and a haiku in your Grove, and earns sunbeams.
5
Modes: The Keeper's Year runs until a meter breaks. Daily Almanac is one rewarded run per day, with the same cards for everyone. Seed Vow deals 18 cards and names one meter; answer in that meter's favor more than any other to keep the vow. Zen Tending never ends and earns no sunbeams, so just relax.
