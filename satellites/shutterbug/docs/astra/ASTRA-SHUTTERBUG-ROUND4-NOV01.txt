WHY THERE IS A ROUND 4
Stephen today: "I want to make shutterbug really good." Your rounds 2 and 3 were shot inside the game on a phone at 412, 360 and 320 px wide. Those shots found two kinds of problem:
- Some pieces do not read yet.
- Seven new jump weapons are in, and some of their effects are drawn bigger or smaller than the area they actually hit.
Round 4 is mostly fixes. Each fix replaces the key whole: the same key name, size and frame names unless a line here says otherwise.

CONTRACT (unchanged)
- The same 16 colours as your manifest's palette, exactly those hexes. Alpha 0 or 255 only.
- Sun (#f2d13f) only in the hero and the beacon.
- One sprite key per PNG strip, frames left to right, all the same size. Plain, distinct frame names: letters, digits and underscores, starting with a letter.
- A source grid per strip, and a manifest entry per file: file, sprite, w, h, frames, names, fps, anchor, sha256, notes.
- Anything drawn over the room keeps a one pixel Ink keyline wherever it uses a saturated colour.
- WEAPON EFFECTS have their own colour law, which the game checks. An Ink outline, then only Slate, Pewter, Ash, Paper and Glare. The colours they must avoid, and why:
  - Sun: the hero's.
  - Film: help platforms.
  - Brick and Magenta: danger.
  - Amber: loot.
  - Teal.
  So fire is drawn as white and grey flame (a Glare core with Ash and Pewter licks).
