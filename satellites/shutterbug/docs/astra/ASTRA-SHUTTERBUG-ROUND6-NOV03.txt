SHUTTERBUG, ART ROUND 6 FOR ASTRA (3 Nov 2026): TROPHIES, BUGS AND THE BUG'S WHY

Thank you for round 5. Its keys, bosses, monsters and terrain are in the game (live build sb-m1-15).

WHY ROUND 6
Stephen, after testing with Jessie: "What are you unlocking? We need something people are playing for, even if it's
just bragging rights." The game now has a Trophies and bugs screen off the title: 13 trophies with progress, and all
8 bugs and 4 frames of the prize ladder, the locked ones greyed, the next prize named ("Clear a house to win the Teal
bug"). The medals are plain CSS circles today, and nothing tells the player why the bug climbs. This round gives
both a face. (Also new in the game: a banner that teaches the wall jump. No art needed for it.)

CONTRACT (unchanged from round 5)
- The same 16 colours as your manifest's palette, exactly those hexes. Alpha 0 or 255 only.
- One sprite key per PNG strip, frames left to right, all the same size, plain frame names. A source grid per strip
  and a manifest entry per file: file, sprite, w, h, frames, names, fps, anchor, sha256, notes.
- Sizes are in native pixels; one cell is 16 px. Check everything at 320, 360 and 412 px wide.
- EXCEPTION FOR THIS ROUND: the trophy screen and the story cards are menu screens, not the room, so Sun (#f2d13f)
  and Amber may be used in them. Never in anything drawn over a room.

1. TROPHY MEDALS (highest: the screen exists and waits for them)
   The 13 trophies come in five families, and a family's later steps wear a better metal:
     rooms cleared (1, 10, 50, 100), houses cleared (1, 5, 10), wall jumps (25, 250), keys found (10, 100),
     clean clears, rooms with no hit (1, 10).
   a. trophy_medals, 16x16, frames: room_bronze, room_silver, room_gold, house_bronze, house_silver, house_gold,
      wall_bronze, wall_silver, key_bronze, key_silver, clean_bronze, clean_silver, locked.
      Each is a medal with the family's emblem inside: a tiny room or photo for rooms, a house for houses, the bug's
      wall kick for wall jumps, the round key for keys, an unbroken heart for clean clears. locked is an empty
      socket that reads as "not yet", calm and never sad. The game draws them at 2x (32 px) beside the name.
   b. fx_trophy_new, 32x32, frames a to d, 12 fps, played once: a small shine burst behind a medal when a new
      trophy is named on the room's clear card. Gentle; it must not read as a hit or an explosion.

2. THE BUG'S WHY (high: three wordless story cards)
   The game will set its own words under each card; please draw no text.
   a. story_cards, 144x96, frames camera, climb, print:
      camera: the bug lives inside the player's camera, peeking out of the lens.
      climb: a photo becomes a room, and the bug climbs it toward the ceiling light.
      print: the light reached, the photo develops into a print the bug carries home.
   b. Optional: story_bug_wave, 32x32, frames a and b, 6 fps: the bug waving from the lens, for the first card.

3. OPTIONAL, IF YOU HAVE TIME: THE ALBUM (not built yet; planned as the next carrot)
   Every cleared room becomes a print in an album, grouped by its biome.
   a. album_page, 160x220: an album page with 6 print corners (each holding a 40x48 print), no text.
   b. album_corner, 8x8: one photo corner, which the game repeats at each print.

One zip, round6, with the PNG strips, a source grid per strip, and the manifest. Name your notes NOTES.md and say
anything you chose differently and why.
