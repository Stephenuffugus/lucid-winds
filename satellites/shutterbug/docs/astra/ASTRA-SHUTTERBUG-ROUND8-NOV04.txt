SHUTTERBUG, ART ROUND 8 FOR ASTRA (4 Nov 2026): THE ROOM YOU CAN READ

Thank you for round 7. All of it is in the game (build sb-m1-19 and after): the sixteen bugs to win, the eight
frames, the album and platinum medals, the album covers, the Daily Room's badge, stamp and card. Your six store
frames wait for real captures. The game is on the store's doorstep.

WHY ROUND 8
Stephen played it, we looked at it together, and we agree on what is wrong. The hook works (a photo of your room
becomes the level, and the reveal is still the moment) and the meta is rich (album, trophies, bugs, Darkroom,
houses, the guardian, the Daily Room). The climb itself is where it goes flat. From the screens in this zip:
- Once the climb starts the photo is gone. The room is grey and blue masses with 2 px dashed strips on them. The cat
  tree, the table, the chairs: gone. You climb "a grey level", not your living room.
- The bug is a 12 px yellow speck, the enemies the same. At arm's length nothing reads: not the feet, not the landing
  edges, not what is about to hurt you.
- The climb has no shape. A column of film tabs straight up, a monster or two, no landing, no set piece, no "oh, the
  bookshelf part".
- A quarter of the screen, the control strip under the room, is blank paper.
- Pickups and hits are quiet.
Code (ours) will put the dimmed photo back behind the grid during the climb, give every room three floors with a
landing each, place set pieces on the photo's own furniture, add a checkpoint, lead the camera, and time the juice.
Your art is what all of that is drawn with. Sections are in priority order; each stands alone, so send them in
pieces if that is easier, and send section 1 first on its own if the rest takes longer.

CONTRACT (unchanged, plus one new size)
- The same 16 colours as your manifest's palette, exactly those hexes. Alpha 0 or 255 only.
- One sprite key per PNG strip, frames left to right, same size, plain frame names; a source grid per strip; a
  manifest entry per file: file, sprite, w, h, frames, names, fps, anchor, sha256, notes, context (room or menu_only).
- Sizes in native pixels; one cell is 16 px. Check at 320, 360 and 412 px wide.
- The hero's feet row holds Ink; Sun lives only in the hero and the beacon. Anything drawn over a room keeps the
  room's colour laws.
- NEW: the hero grows. The bug becomes 16 wide by 24 tall (it was 12 by 16). Its hitbox stays 12 by 16: the extra
  pixels are drawn around it (2 px each side, 8 px above), the feet row stays the bottom row and stays Ink, the
  anchor is the bottom centre. Same silhouette, same character, same poses, just drawn big enough to read on a
  phone. New keys carry the suffix _big so the old ones keep working until every look has arrived.
