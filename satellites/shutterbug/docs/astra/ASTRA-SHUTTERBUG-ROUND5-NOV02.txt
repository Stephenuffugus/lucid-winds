SHUTTERBUG, ART ROUND 5 FOR ASTRA (2 Nov 2026)

Thank you for round 4. It arrived whole (19 strips, 76 frames, your VALIDATION passed) and it is in: the sized weapon effects, the quieter controls, the biome air, the Halo glyph, the crusher, the moth elite and the speck.

WHY ROUND 5 IS BIG
Stephen: "we have potential to really make this cool with the bosses, weapons, monsters, we can make diverse terrain and improve the gameplay."
The game is growing in five directions at once, and every one of them needs art:
- every room gets an objective: the exit is LOCKED and the bug collects keys to light it
- a boss for every biome, not only the clay guardian
- a second monster for every biome, so rooms stop feeling the same
- the dive weapons and charms get their own effects
- terrain pieces that change how a room plays
Sections are in priority order. If you can only do part, do it from the top. Every section stands alone, so you can send them in pieces (round5a, round5b) if that is easier.

CONTRACT (unchanged from round 4)
- The same 16 colours as your manifest's palette, exactly those hexes. Alpha 0 or 255 only.
- Sun (#f2d13f) only in the hero and the beacon.
- One sprite key per PNG strip, frames left to right, all the same size. Plain, distinct frame names: letters, digits and underscores, starting with a letter.
- A source grid per strip, and a manifest entry per file: file, sprite, w, h, frames, names, fps, anchor, sha256, notes.
- Anything drawn over the room keeps a one pixel Ink keyline wherever it uses a saturated colour.
- WEAPON EFFECTS keep their own colour law: an Ink outline, then only Slate, Pewter, Ash, Paper and Glare. Never Sun (hero), Film (help platforms), Brick or Magenta (danger), Amber (loot) or Teal.
- Sizes are in native pixels. One cell is 16 px. The bug's box is about 12x16. The game draws the room at about 2.5x to 3x on a phone, so check everything at 320, 360 and 412 px wide.
- THE SHAPE LAW (new, from round 4's shots): danger is pointed and red, loot is round and Amber, help is Film. A weapon effect must not look like a trap, an enemy, or a pickup. Round 4's kick spray looked like a spike trap and the ember mote looked like an enemy: that is the mistake to avoid.

1. KEYS AND THE LOCKED EXIT (highest priority: this is the next feature built)
   Each room hides 1 to 3 keys on the photo's own furniture (bookcase tops, cabinet tops, window sills). One of them is usually guarded by monsters, or dropped by an elite. The exit beacon stays dark until the bug holds every key, and lights a little more with each one.
   a. key, 16x16, frames a, b, c, d, 8 fps, loops: the key resting where it was placed, with a slow glint or turn. It is loot, so Amber with an Ink keyline is right. It must not read as a speck (round, small) or a scroll at 360 px: give it a clear key silhouette, a bow and a bit.
   b. key_get, 32x32, frames a to e, 12 fps, played once: the key collected, flying up or bursting toward the HUD. Bug centre at local (16,16).
   c. key_drop, 16x16, frames a, b, c, 12 fps, played once: an elite dies and drops its key, which bounces once before it rests (then the game switches to `key`).
   d. hud_key, 9x9, frames empty and full: the counter in the top bar, drawn as many times as the room has keys (so 1/3 shows one full and two empty). It must match hud_heart's weight so the two rows sit together.
   e. beacon_locked, the beacon's own size, frames locked_0, locked_1, locked_2, locked_3: the beacon dark and sealed (locked_0), then a third, two thirds and fully charged as keys arrive. Sun is allowed here because it is the beacon. locked_3 should hand over cleanly to fx_beacon_light from round 3.
   f. fx_beacon_refuse, 32x32, frames a, b, c, 12 fps, played once: the bug touches the exit without every key. A short "not yet" shake or flicker of the seal. It must be gentle, not a hit.
   g. key_guard_mark (optional), 8x8, frames a and b: a tiny mark above a cluster that holds a key, only if you think the guarded key needs a hint. Say in your notes if you think it does not.

3. THE BOSSES (high: one boss per biome)
   Room 5 of a house run has a boss. The boss's BODY is cut from the player's own photo (the largest saturated patch, eroding cell by cell as it is hit), so you do not draw the body. You draw what each boss DOES, and the pieces that frame it. Every attack has a telegraph of at least 0.8 s, so each one needs a warning frame before it hits.
   a. Shared pieces for every boss:
      - boss_weak, 16x16, frames a and b, 6 fps: the mark on a cell the bug can hit. It must read on any colour, because the body is the photo.
      - boss_hurt, 16x16, frames a and b: a flash laid over a cell the moment it is struck.
      - boss_outline_h and boss_outline_v, 8x8 tiles: the ring drawn round the body during its 2 s intro (the game repeats them round the edge).
      - hud_boss, 96x8, frames frame and fill: a health bar across the top. The game draws the fill at any width.
      - card_boss_name, 144x24, frame base: a plate under the boss for its name in the intro. Clear text area 120x12 in the middle.
   b. Attic (Moth Mother): the shed already exists (boss_moth_shed, round 1). Nothing new unless you want a stronger telegraph: say so in your notes.
   c. Frost (Icefall): drops icicle volleys after a 3 s telegraph.
      - frost_warn, 16x16, frames a, b, c: a shadow or crack on the ceiling cell that is about to drop.
      - boss_icicle, 16x32, frames fall and shatter_a, shatter_b, shatter_c: the big icicle and its break on the floor.
   d. Deep (Tidepulse): pulses a ring that reaches 4 cells every 5 s.
      - pulse_warn, 32x32, frames a, b, c: the body swelling before a pulse.
      - pulse_ring, 144x144, frames a to f, 12 fps, played once: the ring expanding to 4 cells (64 px) from the centre, its outer edge at the 144 px frame edge on its last frame. Danger, so Teal and Ink are fine here, it is not a weapon.
   e. Overgrowth (Briarheart): grows thorn lines along nearby platforms.
      - thorn_warn, 16x8, frames a and b: a ripple of shoots along a platform top just before the thorns come.
      - thorn_line, 16x16, frames grow_a, grow_b, grow_c, full, wither: one cell of thorns standing on a platform top; the game repeats it along the platform.
   f. Ember (Spark Fountain): sprays sparks upward after a 2 s telegraph.
      - fountain_warn, 32x16, frames a, b, c: glow building at the body's top.
      - boss_spark, 8x8, frames a and b: one spark in the air, larger and brighter than the spark enemy so the two are not confused.
   g. boss_down variants (optional): fx_boss_down from round 3 works for all five. If you want each biome's boss to fall differently, send fx_boss_down_<biome> at the same 64x64 and six frames.
