WHY THERE IS A ROUND 3
Stephen played the game tonight and said it "feels the same" from room to room, with "no real goal at the end", and asked for "a way to level up or ramp up". He ruled four things:
- A HOUSE RUN comes first: five rooms back to back, each harder, with weapons, Dead pixels and scrolls carried through, and a boss in room 5. Clearing the house unlocks a cosmetic.
- Photos keep their colour: a soft green now becomes the green, a soft blue a blue. Rooms will be much more colourful, and the biome will follow the photo, so all five biomes will really show up.
- When a crop box cannot become a level, the game finds a box that can.
- Each biome leans toward its own weapons.
Round 3 draws what those need. Everything you already delivered stays.

2. THE BOSS (your round 1 attacks are in hand; these are the moments around them)
   The boss's body is the room's own largest colourful shape, cut from the photo cell by cell, so it has no sprite of its own except your fallback. It is outlined in place for 2 s, then it wakes. Each hit knocks cells off it from the hit point outward.
   a. fx_boss_wake, 48x48, 4 frames wake_0 to wake_3: the burst when it wakes. Ink, Glare and Paper, so it sits on any photo colour.
   b. fx_boss_chip, 16x16, 4 frames chip_0 to chip_3: one cell breaking off the body, cell sized. Neutral colours for the same reason.
   c. fx_boss_down, 64x64, 6 frames down_0 to down_5: the last cell gone, a big collapse.
   d. fx_beacon_light, 16x16, 4 frames light_0 to light_3: your beacon_dark coming on once the boss is gone (frame 3 should hand over to the lit beacon's frame a). Sun is allowed here, since it is the beacon.
