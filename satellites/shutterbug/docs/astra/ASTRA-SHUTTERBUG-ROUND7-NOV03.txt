WHY ROUND 7
Stephen: "Let's get it ready to publish," and "build as much cool, unique, and useful stuff for Shutterbug." The
game is close. This round is two things: what the Play store listing needs, and more for players to chase after the
first day (more bugs to win, more medals, a reason to come back tomorrow). Sections are in priority order; each
stands alone, so send them in pieces if that is easier.

CONTRACT (unchanged)
- The same 16 colours as your manifest's palette, exactly those hexes. Alpha 0 or 255 only.
- One sprite key per PNG strip, frames left to right, same size, plain frame names; a source grid per strip; a
  manifest entry per file: file, sprite, w, h, frames, names, fps, anchor, sha256, notes.
- Sizes in native pixels; one cell is 16 px. Check at 320, 360 and 412 px wide.
- Menu only art (trophy screen, album, story, store) may use Sun and Amber, as in round 6; mark each strip's context
  (menu_only or room) in the manifest. Anything drawn over a room keeps the room's colour laws.
- Store art (section 1) is not sprite art: full colour PNG at the exact pixel sizes given, no transparency.
