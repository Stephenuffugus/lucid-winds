SHUTTERBUG · Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
Shutterbug turns a photo of your room into a level, and it does that entirely on your phone. Your photo is never uploaded, never stored and never sent anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no purchases inside the game. After the first load the game plays with no network connection. Music needs one to play its songs.

Your photos

When you photograph a room or choose a photo, your phone's own camera or photo picker hands the picture to the game. The game reads it on the device to build the level: it shrinks the picture to a grid of 24 by 48 colours from its own 16 colour palette, and it builds the platforms, the enemies and the loot from that grid. The picture itself is let go as soon as the level is built. It is not saved by the game and it never leaves your device.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game remembers lives on your device only, in your browser's local storage, under the key shutterbug.v1:

  • The levels you played recently and the rooms of a house run in progress, each kept as its 24 by 48 grid of palette colours (not the photo).
  • Your progress: rooms and houses cleared, your Dead pixels, the weapons you have developed and the prints waiting in the Darkroom, the looks you have won and the one you wear.
  • Your settings: sound, music, the mirrored controls and reduced motion.

None of it leaves your device. Clearing the site's browsing data deletes all of it permanently; there is no copy anywhere else. The Settings screen can export your save as text, for you to keep or move yourself.

Network requests the game makes

The game's own files come from lucidwinds.com, the same way any web page loads its files. That server sees an ordinary file request from your device and nothing about you, your photos or your play. After the first load the game keeps its own copies of its files, so it plays offline.

Music plays nothing until you ask it to. When you play a song it streams from lucidwinds.com, or, for some older public domain recordings, from archive.org (the Internet Archive). Those servers see an ordinary request for the song and nothing about you. Songs are not kept on the device, so music needs a connection.

Sharing

Sharing a level happens only when you tap Share. It goes through your phone's own share sheet to the app you pick, and it carries the level's code (its grid of palette colours, which is what the level is made from), never your photo and nothing about you. The game never shares anything by itself.

Permissions

The app asks for no device permissions of its own. Taking a photo opens your phone's camera app through the system picker, and choosing one opens the system photo picker; the game sees only the one picture you take or pick. No location, no microphone, no contacts.

Children

Shutterbug is a general audience game and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.
