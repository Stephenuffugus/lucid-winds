CONDUIT

You are a fluid that runs on its own body.

Mass is your health, your reach, your size and your ammunition, all one number. Spend it and you are weak. Keep it and you are easy to see.

  • Drag anywhere to move.
  • Tap FLOW to lift the camera, only while you are still and unseen.
  • In FLOW, drag from a power source to a machine. Every tile of wire is a tile of you; pull it back and 75% comes home.
  • Wet the floor, electrify it, take what drops, and leave.
