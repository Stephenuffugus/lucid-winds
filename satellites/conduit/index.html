CONDUIT — phase 0

You are a fluid. Your mass is your health, your ammo, your reach and your body size, all at once. Spending it makes you weak. Keeping it makes you conspicuous.

  • Drag the left of the screen to flow.
  • FLOW lifts the camera so you can route power — only while still and unseen.
  • In flow, drag from a power source to a device. Every tile costs mass. Pulling it back returns 75%.
  • Wet the floor, then electrify it. Harvest what drops. Reclaim your wire. Reach the exit.
