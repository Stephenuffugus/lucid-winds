HOW TO PLAY

The Attic

The goal

Rummage up one of one fake vintage records, comics, tapes, toys and handhelds, and pray for factory sealed. Ten kinds of thing are buried up here and each one wears in its own way. Everything you keep goes on the shelf, and eight grails on your Want List are yours alone.

How it plays

A rummage costs one ticket, so a dig is a decision rather than a button you hold down. Five tickets arrive every day. A find in FINE or better pays one back, anything on your Want List pays three, and two things scrapped off the shelf make one more. When the tickets run dry, DUST OFF the old boxes and find some stubs.

The controls

  • TODAY’S FIND is free, once a day, and it is the same object for everybody
  • RUMMAGE digs, and the condition is revealed last
  • DUST OFF opens the grime panel: drag across it to wipe
  • Tap anything on the shelf to look at it again, or to scrap it
  • WANT LIST shows the eight things you are hunting
