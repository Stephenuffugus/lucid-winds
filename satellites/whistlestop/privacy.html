WHISTLESTOP · Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026 · Sky Wolf Studio
Whistlestop is played on your phone. It collects nothing about you and sends nothing about you anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no purchases inside the game.

What the game stores, and where

Everything the game remembers lives on your device only, in local storage:

  • Your progress, under the key lw_whistlestop_v1: the stars you earned on each puzzle, the rugs you built, your settings (sound, motion, trains bump or pass through), the tips you have already seen and how many cars your trains pull.
  • The studio's music: which of its songs you have unlocked by playing, and how long and on how many days you have played, under sws_game_unlocks and keys that begin sws_music. If you use the music player, it also keeps its volume, the last song and any playlist you make, under sws_music_state and sws_playlists. These are counted on the device and decide when the next song unlocks.

None of it leaves your device, and none of it names you. To erase it all, clear the storage of the Whistlestop app (Android Settings, Apps, Whistlestop, Storage, Clear storage), or on the web clear this site's data in your browser. There is no copy anywhere else.

Network requests the game makes

The game's own files come from lucidwinds.com, the same way any web page loads its files. That server sees an ordinary file request from your device and nothing about you or your play. After the first load the game keeps its own copies of its files on the device, so it plays with no connection. Every sound in the game is made on the device.

The Music button opens the studio's music player. It plays nothing until you ask it to. When you play a song it streams from lucidwinds.com, or, for some older public domain recordings, from archive.org (the Internet Archive). Those servers see an ordinary request for the song and nothing about you. Music needs a connection.

Sharing

Share This Rug copies a link to your clipboard only when you tap it. The link carries the layout of your rug and nothing about you. The game sends it nowhere; you choose where to paste it. Opening such a link builds that rug on your own device.

Permissions

The game asks for no device permissions. No location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts.

The Google Play app and the web

The Google Play app and the web page at lucidwinds.com run the same game and do the same things.

Children

Whistlestop collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version is posted at this address.
