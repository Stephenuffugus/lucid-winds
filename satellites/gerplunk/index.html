GERPLUNK

A lake at golden hour, a stone in your hand, and one flick. Throw across and up the water, and curl the wrist at the end for spin. Slide your thumb slowly first to turn the shore.

The plunk takes its name from the Stone Skipping and Gerplunking Club of Mackinac Island.
