Petal Slice
Sky Wolf Studios
Swipe to slice the tossed seed pods and blossoms in one stroke. Trace a glowing graft line through a cluster to weave a keepsake bloom. Just don't slice the thorny burrs.

How to play

✂️
Swipe across the tossed pods, berries, and blossoms to slice them. One stroke can cut many for a combo.
🌸
A glowing graft line links a cluster. Trace the whole line in one unbroken swipe to graft a keepsake bloom for a big bonus.
🌵
Don't slice the thorny burrs (they pulse red). Each one you cut wilts a petal.
🌼
Grove: you have 3 petals; lose them all and the run ends. Zen: no burrs, no fail, just slice. Daily: one seeded board a day.
