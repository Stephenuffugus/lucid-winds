Hexa Hive
Sky Wolf Studios
Drop honeycomb chips into the comb and match colors. Stack ten of a color and the cell pops in a shower of gold. Build a hive in a new land every level.

How to play

1
Tap a chip stack in your tray, then tap an empty comb cell to drop it in. Later trays arrive mixed, so clearing the top colour uncovers the one hiding under it.
2
When your chips touch a cell whose top color matches, all of that color slides together into one taller stack.
3
Stack ten of one color and it pops, clearing the cell and revealing any color hiding underneath.
4
Chain pops for big combos. In a level, pop enough stacks to finish the hive. Fill the comb with no room left and it is over.
🐝
From the fourth hive on, a tired worker naps on your tallest stack and it stops merging while she sleeps. Tap her to shoo her off, or pop a stack next door and the noise startles her away.
🌿
Zen mode is endless and gentle. Just pop honey and relax, and clear grounds to earn Sunbeams for your garden.
