Doodle Pad
Sky Wolf Studios
A calm free-draw pad. Pick a brush, a size, and a color, then draw whatever you like with your finger or mouse. Pens, pencils, markers, spray, and fun brushes that trail glitter, stars, and hearts. Fill shapes, change the paper color, undo any slip, and save your art as a picture.

How to use

1
Pick a brush from the row of tools. Pen and marker make smooth lines. Pencil and crayon add grain. Spray mists color. Glitter, Stars, Hearts, Bloom, and Flutter stamp a fun trail as you drag. The Eraser rubs your art away. The row scrolls sideways, so swipe it left to reach every tool.
2
Pick a size. The dots go from fine to thick. A bigger dot means a bigger brush.
3
Pick a color from the palette, or tap the rainbow swatch to mix your own exact color.
4
Draw right on the pad with your finger or mouse. Lines smooth out on their own.
5
Tap Fill, then tap inside a shape you closed off, to flood it with your color.
6
Tap the BG swatch to change the paper color behind your art.
7
Made a slip? Undo steps back and Redo puts it back. Clear wipes the whole pad.
8
Tap Save to keep your drawing as a picture. You earn a few sunbeams each day for saving.
