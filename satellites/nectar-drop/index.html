How to play

🎯
Drag to aim the launcher, then release to drop a pollen ball. It bounces off flowers and falls with gravity.
Pop every red bloom to clear the meadow. Reds glow so you can spot them.
Blue blooms are just for bouncing. Pop them for a few points.
Hit a green bloom to fire your gardener's power. Different gardeners do different things.
Purple blooms pay big bonus nectar.
🪣
The moving basket catches a falling ball for a free pollen.
🏆
Miss the basket and your ball drops into a bonus bin. The middle pays most. Land the last bloom's ball in a bin for a huge Nectar Rush.
🛡️
Garden bosses hide behind armored blooms. Hit them a few times to crack the shell.
