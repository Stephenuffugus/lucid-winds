How to play

🎯
Drag to aim the launcher, then release to drop a pollen ball. It bounces off flowers and falls with gravity.
Pop every red bloom to clear the level. Reds glow so you can spot them.
Blue blooms are just for bouncing — pop them for a few points.
Hit a green bloom to trigger a Bloom Burst — it pops every flower nearby.
Purple blooms pay big bonus nectar.
🪣
The moving basket at the bottom catches a falling ball for a free pollen.
