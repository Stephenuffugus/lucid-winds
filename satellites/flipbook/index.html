📓 How to make a flipbook
Goal: draw a little cartoon that moves.
  1. Draw a picture on the page.
  2. Tap the › arrow to flip to the next page. Your last drawing shows through faint so you can trace the next pose.
  3. Move your drawing a tiny bit on each new page.
  4. Press ▶ to play all your pages in a loop.
What the buttons do
  • Draw, Pages, and More are menus at the bottom. Tap one to open it, tap it again to tuck everything away so the whole page is clear.
  • 👻 Onion skin: see the page just before this one, faintly.
  • 1 Ghost (in Pages): show page one as a blue ghost so you can line up a smooth loop.
  • 8fps (in More): how fast it plays. Tap to change the speed.
  • ⛶ Cinema (in More): play big on a full black screen.
  • 🎙 Voice: record yourself talking over the cartoon.
  • 📤 Export saves it as a video. ↗ Share sends it to a friend.
  • ☀ Daily (in Pages): start with today's surprise drawing to trace.
  • ◻ Erase, ✕ Page clears the page, 📓 New starts a fresh book.
