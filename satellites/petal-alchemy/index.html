Start with Seed, Water, Sun, Soil, Air. Tap two to combine them and discover something new. Chain your way up through the whole garden, and coax out the rare True Blooms.

How to play

👆
Tap an element to load it, then tap a second one. If they take, a new element is born.
🔗
Tap the result to carry it straight into the next combine. Chain outward and the garden keeps growing.
🌸
A few deep, rare chains yield a True Bloom — a one-of-a-kind keepsake flower you keep.
📅
Daily Recipe sets one shared target to build from the five seeds; Bloom Hunt gives gentle quests.
🌫️
Two things that don't take just shimmer and wilt. Never a wrong move, never a buzzer.
