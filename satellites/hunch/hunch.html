HUNCH

draw it. the machine guesses. read its mind.

You get a thing to draw, a storm, a feeling, a bad pun. You get one minute. Then a real AI looks at your drawing and takes its best guess. Score by how close it gets.

1. A prompt appears, sometimes simple, often weird and abstract. There's no "correct" drawing.

2. Draw it before the timer runs out. Pen, colors, eraser. That's it. The constraint is the point.

3. The AI guesses blind · it can't see the prompt. Then it grades how close its guess was to your secret word.

4. Land a hit (50+), build your streak, each one multiplies your points. Miss, and the streak resets. The machine's wrong guesses are the best part.
