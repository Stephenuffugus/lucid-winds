LUCID WINDS — Privacy Policy

Effective: April 28, 2026 · Last updated: April 28, 2026

Lucid Winds is a botanical pattern-matching game playable in any modern browser and on the Pi Network. This policy explains exactly what data the game collects, why, and what choices you have. We tried to keep it short and human.

What we collect

  • Account email — handled by Firebase Authentication (Google) for signup and sign-in. We do not see or store your password.
  • GPS coordinates — only while the WILD tab is open. Used to drop and harvest plants on a real-world map. Your phone asks you for location permission first; you can refuse and most of the game still works (you only lose the WILD tab).
  • Pi Network user ID and payment records — only if you make a Pi payment. We receive a Pi user identifier from the Pi SDK and a transaction id from the Pi server. We never see your Pi wallet seed phrase.
  • In-game state (your "vault") — your greenhouse plants, nursery seeds, currencies (Sunbeams, Dew, fertilizer), and progress, stored in Firestore under your account so the game restores when you sign in on a new device.
  • Engagement counters — anonymous-ish numbers like how many plants you've grown or how many achievements you've unlocked, used to power the in-game Compendium and Trophies tabs.
  • Basic analytics — Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID G-XE58S4X6RX) records aggregate events such as "plant minted" or "tab switched". We do not link these events to your email.

Why we collect it

  • To run the game: keep your collection saved and syncing across devices.
  • To process Pi payments: confirm the payment with Pi's servers and grant the item.
  • To improve the game: understand which features get used, which are confusing.
  • To prevent cheating: our anti-spoof checks rely on knowing your GPS movement.

Third parties we use

Firebase (Google) — Auth, Firestore, Cloud Functions, hosting.

Pi Network — payment processing and Pi user identity.

Cloudflare — content delivery network in front of the site.

Google Analytics 4 — aggregate gameplay analytics.

Open-Meteo — public weather feed, no API key, no PII sent.

OpenStreetMap / CartoDB — map tiles for the WILD tab.

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertisers.

Your rights

  • Export — email stephenfurpahs@gmail.com from the address tied to your account and we'll send a JSON dump of your vault within 14 days.
  • Delete — same email address, ask for deletion, and we'll remove your account and all server-side data within 14 days. Some anonymized analytics may persist in aggregate.
  • Correct — most of your data is editable in-game. For account email changes, write to the same address.

Children

Lucid Winds is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed up, email us and we'll delete the account.

Security

Data is stored in Google's Firebase infrastructure, which is encrypted at rest and in transit (TLS). Firestore security rules restrict access so only you can read your vault. Cloud Functions verify your Firebase auth token before granting any Pi-paid entitlement.

Cookies and local storage

We use browser local storage to keep your session, settings, and an offline copy of your vault. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

Changes to this policy

If we materially change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update the date at the top and post a note in-game on first launch after the change. Continued use of the game after that note means you accept the updated policy.
