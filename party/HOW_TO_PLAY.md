# Whack Box: how to actually run a party night

Working names throughout. Stephen names things.

## The five minute version

1. **On the TV or a laptop plugged into it**, open
   `lucidwinds.com/party/host.html`. You get a menu of six games. Pick one.
2. The screen shows a **four character code** and the address for phones.
3. **Everyone else opens `lucidwinds.com/party/play.html`** on their phone,
   types the code and their name.
4. When enough people have joined, the START button lights up. Press it.
5. At the end: PLAY AGAIN, ANOTHER GAME (keeps the same room and code, so
   nobody rejoins), or END NIGHT.

⛔ **Today every room is one browser only.** The host tab and the phone tabs have
to be tabs of the SAME browser on the SAME machine. That is practice mode and it
is genuinely useful for trying every game, but it is not a couch. Real phones
need the five minute console switch on in `PARTY_CLOUD_SETUP.md`; the code side
is finished and waiting.

## The six games

| | what you do | players | about |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mothlight** | True or false, and the whole room watches your answer land, and you can change your mind until the timer dies. Being right when the crowd was visibly wrong pays more. | 3 to 8 | 8 min |
| **Firefly Futures** | Answer a yes or no about yourself, then the room bets on how many of you said yes. Nothing opens until the lanterns light. | 3 to 8 | 9 min |
| **Lifting Fog** | Something describes itself in four clues, worth 100 down to 25 as the fog lifts. One answer only, and a wrong guess locks you out. | 3 to 8 | 9 min |
| **First Frost** | Trivia knockout. The people you lose become the Frost and vote each round on how to wreck the next question. | 4 to 8 | 10 min |
| **Moongraft** | Everybody secretly draws one piece of the same plant. It assembles on the TV, gets a real Lucid Winds name and poem, and you all keep the card. No scores, no losers. | 3 to 8 | 9 min |
| **Same Soil** | One of you picks which of two things is more them. Everyone else guesses. The subject is always right about themselves. | 2 to 8 | 8 min |

**Picking one for a room:** Moongraft when nobody wants to be quizzed or there
are children. Same Soil when there are two of you, or when the room is people who
know each other. Mothlight when the room is loud. First Frost when it is
competitive and you have at least four. Firefly Futures for a mixed group. Lifting
Fog for people who like being clever.

## Things worth knowing

- **A phone that locks is fine.** Unlock it and it is back where it was, inside a
  second. Nobody retypes anything.
- **If the big screen dies**, phones say so and wait. Reopen the host on the same
  code and everybody comes straight back.
- **Everyone earns sunbeams**, winner and last alike. That is deliberate: a party
  game where losing pays nothing is a party game people stop joining.
- **Nothing needs an account** to play, and guests install nothing.
- **The big screen holds itself awake** while a room is open. Nobody touches the
  host during a party, which is exactly why it is the screen most likely to dim
  and sleep, so it asks the browser to keep it lit.
- **There is a sound toggle** in the top right of the host screen, and it
  remembers. Phones make no sound at all on purpose: they buzz instead, which
  only the person holding one can feel.

## If something is wrong

- **The START button will not light up.** Not enough players yet. The button says
  how many are needed. First Frost needs four, Same Soil only needs two.
- **A phone says the code does not answer.** In practice mode it must be a tab of
  the same browser as the host.
- **A red bar at the bottom of the screen.** The room could not connect. It says
  why, and `PARTY_CLOUD_SETUP.md` is the fix.
