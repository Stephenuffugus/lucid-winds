Privacy Policy

Lucid Winds — last updated April 30, 2026

Lucid Winds is a single-player botanical game with optional cloud sync and Pi Network payments. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, and what we do not do.

Who we are

Lucid Winds is built and operated by Stephen Furpahs as an independent developer. Contact: stephen@lucidwinds.com.

What we collect

  • Account email + password hash. Stored in Firebase Authentication so you can sign in across devices. Password is hashed by Firebase; we never see the plaintext.
  • Game state. Your greenhouse, nursery, wild plants, currencies, achievements, settings. Stored in Firebase Firestore at vaults/{your-uid}. Readable only by you (enforced by Firestore security rules).
  • Wild plant locations you choose to share. When you sow a plant on the real-world map, the latitude and longitude you tap and the plant's hash are written to a public collection so other players can interact with it. You can drop plants only at your own location.
  • Anonymous Pi user identifier. If you pay with Pi, the Pi Network SDK gives us a Pi-issued user ID and the payment ID. We use these to fulfill your purchase and reconcile with Pi's servers. We do not receive your Pi wallet address, your phone number, or your name.
  • Anonymous analytics. Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-XE58S4X6RX) records aggregate, non-identifying events such as "game completed" or "tab switched." It uses Google's standard cookies and is subject to Google's privacy policy.
  • Approximate device location. The Wild tab uses your browser's geolocation API to show your position on a map and to allow planting and foraging. Location is read only while the Wild tab is open and is not stored, except as part of plants you choose to drop.

What we do not collect

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not run third-party advertising.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
  • We do not custody your Pi. All payments flow through Pi Network's SDK and your Pi wallet.

How long we keep it

Account data lives as long as your account exists. If you want it deleted, email us at the address above and we will remove your vaults/{uid} document and Firebase Authentication record within 14 days.

Children

Lucid Winds is a general-audience botanical game. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Third parties we rely on

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and Firestore database. Firebase privacy.
  • Pi Network — payments. Pi privacy.
  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage analytics. Google privacy.
  • Open-Meteo — weather data for the climate damage system. No personal data sent. Open-Meteo terms.
  • OpenStreetMap / CartoDB — map tiles for the Wild tab. No personal data sent.
  • Stripe (optional) — credit card tips. Stripe handles all card data directly. Stripe privacy.

Your rights

You can request a copy of the data we hold about you, or request deletion, by emailing stephen@lucidwinds.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes meaningfully we will update the "last updated" date at the top and, where appropriate, surface a notice in the app.
