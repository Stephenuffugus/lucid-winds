How Lucid Winds Works

1. PLAY
Find patterns in card games. Each match earns Dew Drops.
2. BLOOM
Every 30 Dew Drops grows a unique plant. No two are alike — yours is one-of-one.
3. COLLECT
Plants live in your Greenhouse. Sort by rarity. Flip cards to see DNA + haiku.
4. BREED
Cross two plants to create a seed. Water it 3 days in the Nursery to bloom.
5. EXPLORE
Drop plants on a real-world map. Others can see and try to harvest them.
6. DEFEND
Choose a challenge game. Harvesters must beat it to claim your plant.
Every plant is a mathematically unique SVG artwork generated from your gameplay. No AI. No copies. Pure math + attention.
